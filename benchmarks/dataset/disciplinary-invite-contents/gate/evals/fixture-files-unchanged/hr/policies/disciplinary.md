# Disciplinary procedure

Owner: People team. Last reviewed: September 2026.

This procedure applies to every Fieldstone Ltd employee who has passed probation, for conduct and
for performance. A line manager first deals with minor issues informally, by talking them through
with the employee and agreeing what needs to change.

## 1. Investigation

When a manager believes that an employee may have committed misconduct, or that their performance
is still short of what is needed after informal support, the People team appoints an investigating
officer who is not the employee's line manager. The investigating officer meets the employee and
any witnesses, collects the documents and writes a report from
[the investigation report template](../templates/investigation-report.md). The report says whether
there is a case to answer. An investigation meeting only establishes the facts: no outcome is
decided at it.

## 2. Hearing

When there is a case to answer, a hearing manager who took no part in the investigation holds a
disciplinary hearing. The hearing manager writes to the employee at least five working days before
the hearing and sends them a copy of the investigation report and its evidence.

Hearings are held in person at Fieldstone House, 14 Canal Street, Leeds LS1 4AB, or by video call
when the employee asks for one. At the hearing the investigating officer presents the findings, the
employee answers them and may call witnesses, and the hearing manager adjourns to decide.

## 3. Companions

An employee may bring a companion to a disciplinary hearing or an appeal hearing: a colleague or a
trade union representative. The companion may speak for the employee and confer with them during
the hearing, but may not answer questions for them. If the chosen companion cannot come at the time
set, the employee may propose another time within five working days of it.

## 4. Outcomes

The hearing manager decides one of these outcomes and confirms it to the employee in writing within
five working days of the hearing.

| Outcome                  | Used for                                                        | Kept on file |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| No action                | the allegation is not upheld                                    |              |
| First written warning    | misconduct or performance that is not gross misconduct          | 6 months     |
| Final written warning    | serious misconduct, or more misconduct during a live warning    | 12 months    |
| Dismissal with notice    | more misconduct or poor performance during a live final warning |              |
| Dismissal without notice | gross misconduct                                                |              |

## 5. Gross misconduct

Gross misconduct is conduct serious enough to end the employment, even as a first offence, and it
can lead to dismissal without notice. Examples:

- theft, fraud or falsifying records;
- violence, or serious bullying or harassment;
- mishandling customer data, such as sending customer records outside Fieldstone's systems,
  including to a personal email account;
- working under the influence of alcohol or drugs;
- a serious breach of health and safety rules.

## 6. Appeals

The employee may appeal against any outcome, in writing, within five working days of receiving it.
A manager more senior than the hearing manager hears the appeal.
