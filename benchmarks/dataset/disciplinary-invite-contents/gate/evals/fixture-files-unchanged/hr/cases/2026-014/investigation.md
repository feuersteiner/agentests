## Background

On 16 November 2026 the outbound mail filter flagged three emails with spreadsheet attachments,
sent from Jamie Holt's work account to a personal Gmail account. IT passed the alert to HR, and
the case was opened the same day.

## Evidence

- The mail filter log for Jamie's work account, 1 to 16 November 2026.
- The three emails and their attachments.
- Notes of the investigation meeting with Jamie on 18 November 2026, signed by Jamie.
- A statement from Leanne Brook on the team's remote-working arrangements.

## Findings

1. On 3 November 2026 Jamie forwarded the renewals pipeline export, 412 customer contacts with
   business names, contact names, phone numbers, meter numbers and contract end dates, from his
   work account to his personal Gmail account.
2. On 9 November 2026 he forwarded an updated copy of the same export, with his call notes.
3. On 14 November 2026 he forwarded a spreadsheet of 38 customers' bank details, collected for
   direct debit set-up.

At the investigation meeting Jamie confirmed that the Gmail account is his and that he sent the
three emails. He said he wanted to work through the renewals backlog at home at weekends, and that
he has since deleted the emails. Leanne Brook confirmed that Jamie has a Fieldstone laptop with VPN
access for remote work. There is no evidence that the data went further than Jamie's account.

## Conclusion

There is a case to answer for gross misconduct under section 5 of the
[disciplinary procedure](../../policies/disciplinary.md): mishandling customer data by sending
customer records to a personal email account. I recommend a disciplinary hearing.

Priya Shah, HR Business Partner
