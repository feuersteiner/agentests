# Disciplinary hearing invitation template

Copy into the case folder and fill in the parts in square brackets.

---

Dear [employee name],

We would like to invite you to a disciplinary hearing on [date] at [time] in [location] to discuss
[reason for the hearing].

The meeting will give you the opportunity to respond, and after it we will write to you with the
outcome and any next steps.

If you have any questions, please contact [HR contact].

Kind regards,

[Manager name]
