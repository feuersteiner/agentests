# Disciplinary hearing invitation template

For the hearing manager. Copy this into the case folder as `hr/cases/[case number]/invite.md`,
replace every part in square brackets, and send it at least five working days before the hearing
with the investigation report and its evidence.

---

Dear [employee's first name],

**Disciplinary hearing: [day and date] at [time]**

I am writing to invite you to a disciplinary hearing at [time] on [day and date] in [room],
Fieldstone House, 14 Canal Street, Leeds LS1 4AB. [Or: by video call, at the link below.] I will
chair the hearing, [name of investigating officer] will present the findings of the investigation,
and [name] from the People team will take notes.

**The allegation.** The allegation is that [what the employee is alleged to have done or failed to
do, with each date or period, for example "on 3 and 9 March you shared your system password with a
contractor"].

**Possible outcomes.** If the allegation is upheld, the possible outcomes are [the outcomes that
apply: a first written warning, a final written warning, or dismissal]. [If the allegation could
be gross misconduct, add: "This could amount to gross misconduct, and one possible outcome is
dismissal without notice."] No decision has been made, and I will consider your response before
deciding.

**Your right to be accompanied.** You have the right to be accompanied at the hearing by a
colleague or a trade union representative. Please tell me who by [date]. If they cannot attend at
this time, you may propose another time within five working days.

The investigation report and its evidence are enclosed. Please send me any documents you want me
to consider by [date].

Yours sincerely,

[Hearing manager's name]
[Job title]
