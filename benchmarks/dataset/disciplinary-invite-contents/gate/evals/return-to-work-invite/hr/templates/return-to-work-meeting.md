# Return-to-work meeting invitation template

For line managers. Send this when an employee is coming back after five or more days of sickness
absence, and replace each part in square brackets.

---

Dear [employee's first name],

I hope you are feeling better. Welcome back. I'd like to invite you to a short return-to-work
meeting at [time] on [day and date] in [room], Fieldstone House.

It's an informal conversation: we'll talk about how you are, whether you need any changes to your
work or hours for a while, and anything that would help you settle back in. You don't need to
prepare anything.

Best wishes,

[Manager's name]
