# Annual leave

Owner: People team. Last reviewed: October 2026.

Every Fieldstone employee gets 25 days of annual leave a year, plus the eight bank holidays in
England. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## Booking leave

Book leave in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for a week or more, and at least two working
days ahead for a single day. Your line manager approves it within three working days. Bring any
clash with the team's cover rota to your next one-to-one meeting.

## Carrying leave over

You may carry up to five unused days into the next leave year. They must be taken by 31 March.
