# Disciplinary hearing invitation template

Copy this into the case folder as `hr/cases/[case number]/invite.md` and replace every part in
square brackets.

---

Dear [employee's first name],

I am writing to invite you to a disciplinary hearing at [time] on [day and date] in [room],
Fieldstone House, 14 Canal Street, Leeds LS1 4AB.

The hearing is about [reason].

If the matter is upheld, the possible outcomes are a first written warning, a final written
warning or dismissal. If it could amount to gross misconduct, dismissal may be without notice.

You have the right to be accompanied at the hearing by a colleague or a trade union
representative. Please tell me who by [date].

Yours sincerely,

[Hearing manager's name]
[Job title]
