# Case 2026-014: briefing note for the hearing manager

For Tom Whitfield, Head of Customer Operations, who chairs the hearing at 10:00 on Thursday
3 December 2026 in Meeting Room 2, Fieldstone House.

## The case

Priya Shah's investigation found that Jamie Holt, Renewals Coordinator, forwarded customer records
from his work email to his personal Gmail account on 3, 9 and 14 November 2026: the renewals
pipeline export on 3 and 9 November, and 38 customers' bank details on 14 November. Jamie admits
sending them and says he meant to work at home at weekends. There is no evidence the data went
further. Read the full report in `investigation.md` before the hearing.

## Running the hearing

1. Introduce everyone and explain the order of the hearing.
2. Priya presents the findings; Jamie or his companion may ask her questions.
3. Jamie gives his response. His companion may speak for him but may not answer for him.
4. Ask open questions: why the VPN laptop was not used, whether anyone told him the rules, and
   whether any copies are left.
5. Adjourn to decide. Do not announce an outcome in the room.

## Deciding

Section 5 of the procedure lists mishandling customer data as gross misconduct. Weigh Jamie's
explanation, his record and any mitigation before choosing an outcome from section 4, and confirm
it in writing within five working days.
