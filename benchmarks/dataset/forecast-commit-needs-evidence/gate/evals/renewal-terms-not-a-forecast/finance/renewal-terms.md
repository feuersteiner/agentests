# Renewal terms

_Corvid Systems finance, updated 6 August 2026._

How we price and paper Perch renewals.

- A renewal is priced at the current list price for the plan and the number of seats in use 60 days
  before the renewal date.
- A customer who commits to a two-year renewal gets 8% off both years. A three-year commitment
  gets 12% off.
- Seats can be reduced at renewal by up to 20% without a sales review.
- The renewal order form goes out 45 days before the renewal date, signed by the customer's
  economic buyer and by Corvid's deal desk.
