# Q4 2026 renewals

Corvid Systems: the contracts that come up for renewal between 1 October and 31 December 2026.
Notes copied from the CRM on 6 August 2026.

## Renewals

### Halvard Freight

$120,000 a year, renews 31 October. Owner: Lena Fischer.

- Customer since 2024; 85% of seats active.
- Ingrid Solberg, VP operations and economic buyer, said on the 3 August call that they will renew
  at $120,000 by 31 October if SAML single sign-on ships by 30 September.
- Renewal order form as last year; their procurement steps are known and Ingrid signs.

Category: Commit

### Morrow & Pike

$70,000 a year, renews 15 November. Owner: Tom Becker.

- Customer since 2023; Perch is used in all three of their offices.
- Carla Mendes, the CFO and economic buyer, confirmed in writing on 3 August that they will renew
  at $70,000 by 15 November.
- Renewals under $100,000 need no procurement review there; Carla signs the order form.

Category: Commit

### Northgate Clinics

$45,000 a year, renews 20 October. Owner: Aisha Khan.

- Customer since 2024, with 50 active users.
- Owen Hale, operations director and economic buyer, said on 28 July that they will renew at
  $45,000 by 20 October, provided their board approves next year's IT budget on 24 September.
- Owen signs the order form; no procurement review.

Category: Commit

### Sable Ridge Telecom

$160,000 a year, renews 15 December. Owner: Aisha Khan.

- Customer since 2022; active seats fell from 300 to 210 since March.
- Their new CIO, who will sign off the renewal, started in July and has not met us yet. Our
  champion says renewal is "very likely".
- Paper process unknown: their procurement team changed in June.

Category: Best Case

### Wrenfield Schools

$90,000 a year, renews 31 December. Owner: Tom Becker.

- Customer since 2023; all 12 schools use Perch.
- Gail Turner, head of IT and economic buyer, emailed on 30 July that they intend to renew at
  $90,000 by 31 December.
- The district moves to a new purchasing portal in October, and the steps to sign there are not
  known yet.

Category: Commit

## Outlook

Q4 renewals look strong: $325,000 of the $485,000 up for renewal is in Commit.

- **Commit, $325,000:** Halvard Freight, Morrow & Pike, Northgate Clinics and Wrenfield Schools
  have all told us they will renew.
- **Best Case, $160,000:** Sable Ridge Telecom; the champion expects a renewal, but usage is down.
