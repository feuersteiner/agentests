# Gift cards, online and at the till

Offer digital gift cards from your shop site in the amounts you set.

[Turn on gift cards](/settings/gift-cards)

## How it works

1. Choose the amounts you want to offer, from $10 to $250.
2. A reader buys a card and writes a message. Shelfwise emails it to the recipient on the date
   they pick.
3. The recipient redeems the code at checkout or shows it at your till. Staff look up the balance
   in the Shelfwise app.

## Good to know

- Cards do not expire.
- A card can be used across several orders until the balance is spent.
- Gift cards are part of the Growth plan.
