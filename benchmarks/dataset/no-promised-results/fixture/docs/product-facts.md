# Shelfwise product facts

Shelfwise is a storefront app for independent bookshops. A shop installs it, imports its
catalogue and gets an online shop on its own domain.

## Features

- **Online storefront.** A shop site with search, category shelves and a basket. Themes are
  edited in the browser.
- **Catalogue import.** Upload a spreadsheet of ISBNs; Shelfwise fills in titles, authors, covers
  and descriptions from the book data feed.
- **Pre-orders.** Shops list a title before publication day. Readers pay at checkout and the order
  is released on the publication date.
- **Click and collect.** Readers order online and pick up in the shop. Staff mark an order as
  ready and the reader gets an email.
- **Gift cards.** Digital gift cards in amounts the shop sets, redeemable online and at the till.
- **Events calendar.** A page of readings and signings, with ticketing for up to 200 seats.
- **Staff picks.** Shelves on the home page that booksellers curate, each pick with a short note.
- **Email-capture popup.** New in November 2026. A signup popup shown to visitors on the shop
  site. The shop sets the wording, the delay before it appears (5 to 60 seconds) and the pages it
  appears on. Addresses go to the shop's subscriber list in Shelfwise and can be exported as CSV
  or synced to Mailchimp. A visitor who closes it does not see it again for 30 days. Available on
  both plans.
- **Newsletters.** Growth plan only. Send email newsletters to the subscriber list from Shelfwise,
  with book blocks that pull the cover and price from the catalogue.
- **Abandoned basket reminders.** Growth plan only. One reminder email, sent 24 hours after a
  signed-in reader leaves books in the basket.
