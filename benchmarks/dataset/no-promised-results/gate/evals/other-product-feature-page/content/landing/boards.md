# Boards for your team's work

Create a board for each project, add columns for each stage and drag cards between them.

## What is on a card

- An owner, a due date and labels.
- A checklist, with progress shown on the card.
- Comments, with mentions that notify the person named.

In an independent 2026 test, boards loaded twice as fast as in two other tools we compared.

Boards are included in every plan, with a 30-day free trial.
