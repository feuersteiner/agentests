# Your booksellers' picks, on your front page

Build shelves on your home page and add a short note to each pick.

## How it works

1. Create a shelf and give it a name, such as "Bea recommends".
2. Add titles from your catalogue and write a line about each one.
3. Drag the shelves into the order you want.

Staff picks can help turn casual browsers into loyal regulars who keep coming back to your shop.

Staff picks are part of the Growth plan.
