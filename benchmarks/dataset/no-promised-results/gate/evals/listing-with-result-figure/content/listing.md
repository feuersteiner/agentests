# Shelfwise: the storefront app for independent bookshops

Bookshops that use Shelfwise increase their revenue by 30% on average in the first year.

## What you get

- An online shop on your own domain, with search and category shelves.
- Catalogue import by ISBN, with titles, authors and covers filled in.
- Click and collect, pre-orders, gift cards and an events calendar.
