# Write to your readers from Shelfwise

Send email newsletters to your subscriber list without leaving the app.

We cannot tell you what a newsletter will do for your sales, and we will not pretend to. Here is
what the feature does:

- Book blocks pull the cover, title and price from your catalogue.
- Schedule a send for a date and time.
- See how many emails were delivered and opened.

Newsletters are part of the Growth plan.
