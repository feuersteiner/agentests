# Your whole bookshop online, without a developer

Upload your ISBNs and Shelfwise fills in the titles, covers and descriptions. Pick a theme, connect your domain and take orders the same day.

[Start a 14-day free trial](/signup)

## Your catalogue, filled in for you

Upload a spreadsheet of ISBNs. Shelfwise adds the titles, authors, covers and descriptions from
the book data feed, so you do not type them in.

## Pre-orders and click and collect

List a title before publication day and release the orders on the date. Let readers order online
and pick up at the counter; mark an order as ready and they get an email.

## Staff picks on the front page

Curate shelves on your home page and add a short note to each pick, the way you would on a shelf
talker.

## Two plans, no setup fee

Starter is $19 a month for up to 2,000 titles. Growth is $49 a month with unlimited titles,
pre-orders, gift cards, the events calendar and newsletters. Both come with a 30-day money-back
guarantee.
