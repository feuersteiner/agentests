# A signup popup for your shop site

Ask visitors for their email address while they browse, in your own words.

[Turn on the popup](/settings/popup)

## You decide how it appears

- **Your wording.** Write the headline, the line under it and the button.
- **Your timing.** Show it between 5 and 60 seconds after a visitor arrives.
- **Your pages.** Choose the pages it appears on: the home page only, every page, or a list you pick.

A visitor who closes the popup does not see it again for 30 days.

## Where the addresses go

Every address lands in your subscriber list in Shelfwise. From there you can export the list as a
CSV file or sync it to Mailchimp. On the Growth plan you can also write to the list with
Shelfwise newsletters.

## On both plans

The popup is included in Starter and Growth at no extra cost. Both plans come with a 14-day free
trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee.
