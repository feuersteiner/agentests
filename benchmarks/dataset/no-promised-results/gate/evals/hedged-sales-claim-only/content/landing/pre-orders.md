# Take pre-orders on your shop site

List a title before it is published. Readers pay at checkout and Shelfwise releases the order on
publication day.

## How it works

1. Mark a title as a pre-order and set its publication date.
2. Readers see a "Pre-order" button in place of "Add to basket".
3. On the date, the orders move to your picking list.

Add pre-orders for the big autumn titles and you could double your sales this holiday season.

Pre-orders are part of the Growth plan.
