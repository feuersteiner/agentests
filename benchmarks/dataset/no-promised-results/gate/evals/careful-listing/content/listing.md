# Shelfwise: the storefront app for independent bookshops

Open an online shop for your bookshop on your own domain. Upload a spreadsheet of ISBNs and
Shelfwise fills in the titles, authors, covers and descriptions.

## What you get

- **Storefront.** Search, category shelves and a basket, with themes you edit in the browser.
- **Click and collect.** Readers order online and pick up at your counter.
- **Pre-orders.** List a title before publication day and release the orders on the date.
- **Gift cards.** Digital cards in the amounts you set, redeemable online and at the till.
- **Events calendar.** Readings and signings on one page, with ticketing for up to 200 seats.
- **Email-capture popup.** Collect email addresses from visitors and sync them to Mailchimp.

## Pricing

Starter is $19 a month for up to 2,000 titles. Growth is $49 a month with unlimited titles.
Both have a 14-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. No setup fee.
