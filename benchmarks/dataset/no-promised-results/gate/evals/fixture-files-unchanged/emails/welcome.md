Thanks for installing Shelfwise. Your 14-day free trial has started, and here is how to open your
shop:

1. **Import your catalogue.** Upload a spreadsheet of ISBNs and we fill in the titles, authors and
   covers. The [import guide](https://shelfwise.example/help/import) walks through it.
2. **Pick a theme.** Choose one and change the colours and fonts in the browser.
3. **Connect your domain.** Point your domain at Shelfwise, or use the free
   `yourshop.shelfwise.example` address.
