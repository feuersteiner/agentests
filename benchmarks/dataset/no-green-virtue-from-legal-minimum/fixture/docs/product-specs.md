# Saltmarsh product specifications

The product team's facts about each product. Copy takes its facts from here; prices are in
`data/products.json`. Last checked in November 2026.

Every product is made in small batches at our workshop in Wells-next-the-Sea, Norfolk, is
dermatologically tested and is vegan.

## Exfoliating Face Wash

- Size: 150 ml bottle with a pump.
- A daily foaming face wash for normal and oily skin. Rinse-off.
- Exfoliates with finely ground apricot shell. Also contains sea kelp extract and glycerin.
- Microbead-free (UK legal requirement for rinse-off products since 2018).
- Scent: light cucumber and sea fennel.
- How to use: massage onto damp skin, morning or evening, avoiding the eye area. Rinse off with
  warm water.
- Bottle: made from 100% recycled plastic.
- Refill pouch: 500 ml, enough to refill the bottle three times.

## Sea Kelp Body Wash

- Size: 250 ml bottle with a pump.
- A gentle everyday body wash with sea kelp extract and colloidal oat. Rinse-off.
- Scent: sea fennel.
- How to use: work into a lather on wet skin, then rinse off.
- Bottle: made from 100% recycled plastic.
- Refill pouch: 750 ml, enough to refill the bottle three times.

## Sea Buckthorn Hand Cream

- Size: 75 ml tube.
- A rich hand cream with shea butter and sea buckthorn oil, for dry and working hands. Leave-on.
- Scent: unscented.
- How to use: rub a little into dry hands as often as needed.

## Dry Shampoo Spray

- Size: 200 ml aerosol can.
- Soaks up oil at the roots between washes with rice starch. Leaves no white cast on light to
  medium hair; on dark hair, brush it through well.
- Scent: light sea salt.
- CFC-free propellant (required by law). Propellant: butane, isobutane and propane.
- How to use: shake well. Hold the can 20 cm from dry hair and spray in short bursts at the roots.
  Leave for two minutes, then massage in and brush through.
- Ingredients: Butane, Isobutane, Propane, Oryza Sativa Starch, Alcohol Denat., Parfum,
  Cetrimonium Chloride.
- Warnings, which must appear on the can: Extremely flammable aerosol. Pressurised container: may
  burst if heated. Keep away from heat, hot surfaces, sparks, open flames and other ignition
  sources. No smoking. Do not pierce or burn, even after use. Do not spray on an open flame or
  other ignition source. Protect from sunlight. Do not expose to temperatures exceeding 50°C. Keep
  out of reach of children. Avoid contact with eyes. Use only in well-ventilated areas.
