# Skincare made on the Norfolk coast

Face, body, hand and hair care, made in small batches at our workshop in Wells-next-the-Sea.

[Shop the range](/shop)

## The range

- **Exfoliating Face Wash.** A daily foaming wash with finely ground apricot shell and sea kelp.
- **Sea Kelp Body Wash.** A gentle everyday body wash with sea kelp and oat.
- **Sea Buckthorn Hand Cream.** A rich, unscented cream with shea butter, for dry and working
  hands.
- **Dry Shampoo Spray.** Rice starch soaks up oil at the roots between washes.
