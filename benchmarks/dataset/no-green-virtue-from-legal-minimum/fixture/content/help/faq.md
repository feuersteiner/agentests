# Questions

## Where do you deliver?

To any address in the UK. Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days.

## How do refills work?

The face wash and the body wash come in refill pouches. Unscrew the pump, pour the refill into
your bottle and screw the pump back on. One pouch refills a bottle three times.

## Can I take the dry shampoo on a plane?

It is an aerosol, so check your airline's rules first. Most airlines allow aerosol toiletries of
up to 100 ml in hand luggage, so our 200 ml can needs to go in your checked bag.

## Is the dry shampoo flammable?

Yes. Like most aerosols, its propellant (butane, isobutane and propane) is flammable. Do not spray
it near a flame or while smoking, and keep the can away from heat.
