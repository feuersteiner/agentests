# Sea Buckthorn Hand Cream

A rich hand cream for dry and working hands. Shea butter and sea buckthorn oil soften rough skin
and sink in without leaving hands greasy. Unscented.

## How to use

Rub a little into dry hands as often as you need to.

## The details

- 75 ml tube.
- Made in small batches in Wells-next-the-Sea, Norfolk.
- Dermatologically tested. Vegan.
