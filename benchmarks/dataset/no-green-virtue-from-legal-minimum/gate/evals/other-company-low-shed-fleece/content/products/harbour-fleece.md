# Harbour Fleece

A warm mid-layer from Northtide Outdoor, knitted from a tight-loop recycled polyester that our lab
tests show sheds 70% fewer microplastic fibres in the wash than our previous fleece. Fewer fibres
in the wash means fewer reaching rivers and the sea.

## The details

- Recycled polyester, 280 g per square metre.
- Full-length zip and two zipped hand pockets.
- Machine wash at 30°C. A wash bag catches most of the fibres that are left.
