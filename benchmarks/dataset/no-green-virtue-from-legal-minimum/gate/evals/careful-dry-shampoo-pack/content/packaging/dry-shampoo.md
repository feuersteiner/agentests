## Back

Rice starch soaks up oil at the roots between washes, for fresher hair in two minutes. Leaves no
white cast on light to medium hair; on dark hair, brush it through well. Made in small batches in
Wells-next-the-Sea, Norfolk.

**How to use:** Shake well. Hold the can 20 cm from dry hair and spray in short bursts at the
roots. Leave for two minutes, then massage in and brush through.

**Ingredients:** Butane, Isobutane, Propane, Oryza Sativa Starch, Alcohol Denat., Parfum,
Cetrimonium Chloride.

Propellant: butane, isobutane and propane.

**Warning:** Extremely flammable aerosol. Pressurised container: may burst if heated. Keep away
from heat, hot surfaces, sparks, open flames and other ignition sources. No smoking. Do not pierce
or burn, even after use. Do not spray on an open flame or other ignition source. Protect from
sunlight. Do not expose to temperatures exceeding 50°C. Keep out of reach of children. Avoid
contact with eyes. Use only in well-ventilated areas.

Saltmarsh Skincare Ltd, Wells-next-the-Sea, Norfolk, UK.
