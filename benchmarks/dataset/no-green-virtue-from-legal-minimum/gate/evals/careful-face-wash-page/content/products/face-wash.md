# Exfoliating Face Wash

A daily foaming face wash for normal and oily skin. Finely ground apricot shell polishes away dead
skin, while sea kelp and glycerin leave your face clean and comfortable, never tight. A light
cucumber and sea-fennel scent.

## Refill, don't rebuy

The bottle is made from 100% recycled plastic, and a 500 ml refill pouch fills it three times, so
you keep the pump and the bottle going.

## How to use

Massage onto damp skin, morning or evening, avoiding the eye area. Rinse off with warm water.

## The details

- 150 ml bottle with a pump, made from 100% recycled plastic.

- Contains no plastic microbeads, as UK law requires of rinse-off products.
