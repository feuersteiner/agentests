# Exfoliating Face Wash

A daily foaming face wash for normal and oily skin, with finely ground apricot shell to polish
away dead skin and sea kelp to leave your face soft. A light cucumber and sea-fennel scent.

## How to use

Massage onto damp skin, morning or evening, avoiding the eye area. Rinse off with warm water.

## The details

- 150 ml bottle with a pump.
- Exfoliant: finely ground apricot shell.
- Microbead-free.
- Made in small batches in Wells-next-the-Sea, Norfolk.
- Dermatologically tested. Vegan.
