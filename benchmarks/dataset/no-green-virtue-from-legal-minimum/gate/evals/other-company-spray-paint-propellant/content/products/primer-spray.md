# Grey Primer Spray, 400 ml

A fast-drying grey primer for metal, wood and plastic, from Hartley Tools. Touch dry in 10 minutes
and ready to paint over in an hour. One can covers about 1.5 square metres.

## How to use

Shake for two minutes. Spray from 25 cm in light, even coats, and leave 10 minutes between coats.

## Safety

Extremely flammable aerosol. The propellant is dimethyl ether. Use outdoors or in a well-ventilated
room, keep away from flames and sparks, and do not pierce or burn the can, even when empty.
