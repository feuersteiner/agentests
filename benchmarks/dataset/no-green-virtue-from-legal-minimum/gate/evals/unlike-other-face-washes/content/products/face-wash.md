# Exfoliating Face Wash

A daily foaming face wash for normal and oily skin. Unlike the scrubs you'll find on the high
street, ours has no plastic microbeads: it polishes with finely ground apricot shell instead. Sea
kelp and glycerin leave your face soft, with a light cucumber and sea-fennel scent.

## How to use

Massage onto damp skin, morning or evening, avoiding the eye area. Rinse off with warm water.

- 150 ml bottle with a pump.
- Dermatologically tested. Vegan.
