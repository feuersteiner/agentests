# Sea Kelp Body Wash

A gentle everyday body wash with sea kelp and oat. It lathers well, rinses clean and leaves skin
soft, with a fresh sea-fennel scent.

## What's in it

Sea kelp extract and colloidal oat in a mild coconut-derived cleansing base, with glycerin.

## How to use

Work into a lather on wet skin, then rinse off.

## The details

- 250 ml bottle with a pump, made from 100% recycled plastic.
- Refill pouch: 750 ml, enough to refill the bottle three times.
- Made in small batches in Wells-next-the-Sea, Norfolk.
- Dermatologically tested. Vegan.
