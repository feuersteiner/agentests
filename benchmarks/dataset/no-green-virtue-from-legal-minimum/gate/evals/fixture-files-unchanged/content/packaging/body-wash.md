A gentle everyday body wash with sea kelp and oat, made in small batches in Wells-next-the-Sea,
Norfolk.

**How to use:** Work into a lather on wet skin, then rinse off.

**Ingredients:** Aqua, Sodium Coco-Sulfate, Coco-Glucoside, Glycerin, Laminaria Digitata Extract,
Avena Sativa Kernel Flour, Sodium Chloride, Citric Acid, Sodium Benzoate, Parfum.

**Caution:** Avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse well with water.

Bottle made from 100% recycled plastic. Refill pouches at saltmarsh.example/refills.
