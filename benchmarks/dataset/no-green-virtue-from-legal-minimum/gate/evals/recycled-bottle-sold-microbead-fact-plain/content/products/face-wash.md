# Exfoliating Face Wash

A daily foaming face wash for normal and oily skin, with finely ground apricot shell and sea kelp.

## Lighter on the planet

- **A bottle made from 100% recycled plastic.** No new plastic goes into it.
- **Refill, don't rebuy.** A 500 ml refill pouch fills the bottle three times, so one bottle and
  pump last you for years.

## How to use

Massage onto damp skin, morning or evening, avoiding the eye area. Rinse off with warm water.

## Ingredients

Aqua, Sodium Coco-Sulfate, Glycerin, Prunus Armeniaca Seed Powder, Laminaria Digitata Extract,
Coco-Glucoside, Citric Acid, Sodium Benzoate, Parfum. No plastic microbeads (banned in UK rinse-off
products since 2018).
