# Harbour Breakfast Tea

A strong, malty breakfast blend of Assam and Kenyan teas from Brackenbury Tea Co., made to stand up
to milk.

## Why it's better for the bin

- **Plastic-free tea bags.** Most tea bags are sealed with polypropylene. Ours are folded and tied
  with cotton thread instead, so the whole bag goes in your food waste caddy.
- **Paper outer bag.** The bags come in a paper pouch inside the box, with no plastic film.

## How to brew

Pour freshly boiled water over one bag and brew for four to five minutes.
