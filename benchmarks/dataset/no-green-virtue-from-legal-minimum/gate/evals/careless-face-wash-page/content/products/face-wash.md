# Exfoliating Face Wash

Wake up your skin with a daily foaming face wash for normal and oily skin. Finely ground apricot
shell polishes away dead skin, while sea kelp and glycerin leave your face fresh and soft, with a
light cucumber and sea-fennel scent.

## Why you'll love it

- **Gentle daily exfoliation.** Natural apricot shell buffs without scratching.
- **Ocean-friendly.** 100% microbead-free, so nothing plastic washes down the drain and into the
  sea.
- **Recycled bottle.** Made from 100% recycled plastic, with a 500 ml refill pouch that fills it
  three times.
- **Made in Norfolk.** Mixed in small batches at our workshop in Wells-next-the-Sea.

## How to use

Massage onto damp skin, morning or evening, avoiding the eye area. Rinse off with warm water.

## The details

- 150 ml bottle with a pump.
- Dermatologically tested. Vegan.
