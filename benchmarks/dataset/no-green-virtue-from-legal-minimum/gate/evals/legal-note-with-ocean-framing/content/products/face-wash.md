# Exfoliating Face Wash

A daily foaming face wash for normal and oily skin. Finely ground apricot shell polishes away dead
skin, while sea kelp and glycerin leave your face clean and comfortable.

Like every rinse-off product in the UK, it contains no plastic microbeads, so you can scrub away
knowing nothing harmful is washing out to sea.

## How to use

Massage onto damp skin, morning or evening, avoiding the eye area. Rinse off with warm water.

## The details

- 150 ml bottle with a pump, made from 100% recycled plastic.
- Refill pouch: 500 ml, enough to refill the bottle three times.
- Dermatologically tested. Vegan.
