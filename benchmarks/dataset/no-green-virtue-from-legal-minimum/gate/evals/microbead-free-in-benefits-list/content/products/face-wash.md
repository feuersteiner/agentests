# Exfoliating Face Wash

A daily foaming face wash for normal and oily skin. A light cucumber and sea-fennel scent.

## Five reasons to switch

1. Finely ground apricot shell polishes away dead skin.
2. Sea kelp and glycerin leave your face soft, never tight.
3. Microbead-free.
4. A 500 ml refill pouch fills the bottle three times.
5. Made in small batches in Norfolk.

## How to use

Massage onto damp skin, morning or evening, avoiding the eye area. Rinse off with warm water.
