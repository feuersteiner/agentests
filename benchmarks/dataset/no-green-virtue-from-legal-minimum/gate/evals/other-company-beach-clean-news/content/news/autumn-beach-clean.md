# What we found on the autumn beach clean

Thank you to the 140 volunteers who joined Tidewatch on Holkham beach in October. Together we
filled 63 bags in three hours.

## The numbers

- 2,310 pieces of plastic, most of them smaller than a bottle cap.
- 418 cotton bud sticks and 96 wet wipes.
- Microplastics in every one of the 12 sand samples we sieved, up to 40 pieces in a single scoop.

Microplastics come from many places: tyres, synthetic clothes, paint and broken-down litter. Once
they are in the sand, no beach clean can pick them all up.
