# Never ask candidates about their pay history

Application forms, screening scripts, offer checklists and interview guides do not ask candidates about their current or previous pay, directly or indirectly; asking about pay expectations is allowed.
Pay includes base salary, bonus, commission, equity, allowances and benefits. Asking indirectly includes requesting a payslip, salary statement or tax document, asking what the candidate would give up by leaving, asking for expectations as an increase on what they earn now, and checking their pay with a current or previous employer.

Apply this to every hiring document you write or change, for the whole task, and check it again
before you commit.

## Why

- The EU Pay Transparency Directive (Article 5) says an employer shall not ask applicants about
  their pay history in their current or previous jobs.
- An offer anchored to past pay carries old pay gaps into the new job. Our offers follow the
  role's salary band and level, and the candidate's expectations, not what they earned before.
- "Current salary" is a stock field on forms and recruiter scripts, so it slips in by habit. An
  optional field or a soft "if you're comfortable sharing" still asks.

## How to handle pay instead

- Ask what the candidate is looking for: "What salary range are you looking for in this role?"
- Share the role's band when you know it, and ask whether it works for them.
- If a candidate brings up what they earn now, do not ask follow-up questions about it; go back to
  their expectations and the band.
- Offer checklists build the offer from the band, the level and the candidate's expectations.
  Reference checks confirm dates, role and performance, never pay.

## Gotchas

Each of these asks about pay history, even though it does not say "salary history":

- A "current salary", "current package" or "current OTE" field, required or optional.
- "What bonus or unvested equity would you be walking away from?"
- "What increase on your current salary would make you move?"
- "Please attach a recent payslip" or an annual income statement.
- "Verify last salary with the previous employer during references."
- "What would it take to match your current package?"

## Example

From an interview guide for a customer success manager.

Compliant:

```markdown
**Salary.** Share the range, €52,000 to €60,000 gross a year, and ask: "Does that range work for
you, and where in it would you expect to land?"
```

Violating:

```markdown
**Salary.** Ask what they earn now and what bonus they would give up, then offer about 10% more,
within the range.
```

## Before you commit

Reread every file you created or changed. Find each question, field, checklist item and instruction
that touches pay, compensation, bonus, equity, benefits, payslips or references. Rewrite any that
asks about, asks for proof of, or would reveal what the candidate is paid now or was paid before,
then check again.
