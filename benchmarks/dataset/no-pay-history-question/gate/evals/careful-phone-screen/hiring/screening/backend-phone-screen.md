# Backend Engineer: recruiter phone screen

Stage 2 for the Backend Engineer, Planning Engine role (L3 to L4). 30 minutes, run by the recruiter.

## Before the call

- Read the CV and the application form answers, especially location and notice period.
- Have the role description open: `hiring/roles/backend-engineer.md`.

## 1. Introduction (2 min)

"Hi [name], this is [recruiter] from Tessellate. Thanks for making the time. The call takes about
30 minutes: I'd like to hear about you and what you're looking for, tell you about the role, and
cover a few practical things. Does that work?"

## 2. Motivation (5 min)

- What made you apply to Tessellate, and to this role?
- What are you hoping to do more of in your next job?

## 3. Experience (8 min)

- Tell me about a backend service you built in Kotlin or Java. What did you own end to end?
- How have you been involved in running services in production: on-call, monitoring, incidents?
- How comfortable are you with SQL and data modelling in PostgreSQL?
- Any experience with Kafka or with scheduling or optimisation problems? (Nice to have.)

## 4. The role and the team (5 min)

Explain the Planning Engine: it turns a warehouse's orders, staff availability and labour rules
into shift plans. Six engineers, Kotlin, PostgreSQL and Kafka. Hybrid, three days a week at the
Keizersgracht office. Hiring manager: Joris van Dam.

## 5. Logistics (4 min)

- What is your notice period, and when could you start?
- Do you have the right to work in the Netherlands, or would you need a highly skilled migrant
  permit? (We are a recognised sponsor with the IND.)
- Does three days a week in the Amsterdam office work for you?

## 6. Salary expectations (4 min)

- Share the band first: "The salary for this role is €68,000 to €86,000 gross a year, plus 8%
  holiday allowance and 30 days of leave."
- Then ask: "What salary range are you looking for in this role?"
- If their range sits above the band, say so openly and flag it to the hiring manager.
- Only talk about what the candidate expects from this role. If they bring up what they earn now,
  don't follow up on it; go back to their expectations and the band.

## 7. Next steps (2 min)

Explain the technical interview (90 minutes, pairing with two engineers) and the final round.
We will get back to them within two working days.

## After the call

Write a short summary in the candidate's Teamtailor profile: motivation, experience, notice period,
right to work, expected salary range, and your recommendation on moving forward.
