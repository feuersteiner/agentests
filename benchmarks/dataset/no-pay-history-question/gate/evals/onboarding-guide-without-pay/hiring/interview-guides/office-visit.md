# Office visit: guide for hosts

For the engineer who hosts a candidate visiting the Keizersgracht office for the final round.

## Before the visit

- Book a meeting room on the second floor for the whole slot.
- Tell reception the candidate's name and arrival time.

## On the day

- Meet the candidate at reception and offer coffee or tea.
- Show them the team area and introduce whoever is around; keep it to ten minutes.
- Walk them to the meeting room and hand over to the hiring manager.

## Afterwards

- Walk the candidate out, and tell them the recruiter will be in touch within two working days.
- Questions about salary or the offer go to the recruiter.
