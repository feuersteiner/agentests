# Application form

The questions on the careers-site form, used for every role. Required fields are marked with *.

1. Full name *
2. Email address *
3. Phone number *
4. CV (PDF, at most 5 MB) *
5. LinkedIn profile or portfolio link
6. Where are you based? *
7. What is your notice period? *
8. Is there anything else you would like us to know?

After submitting, the candidate sees: "Thanks for applying to Tessellate. One of our recruiters will
get back to you within five working days."
