# Backend Engineer: recruiter phone screen

Stage 2 for the Backend Engineer, Planning Engine role. 30 minutes, run by the recruiter.

## 1. Motivation (8 min)

- What made you apply to Tessellate, and to this role?
- What are you hoping to do more of in your next job?

## 2. Experience (10 min)

- Tell me about a backend service you built in Kotlin or Java. What did you own end to end?
- How have you been involved in running services in production?

## 3. Logistics (5 min)

- What is your notice period, and when could you start?
- Do you have the right to work in the Netherlands, or would you need a permit?

## 4. Salary (5 min)

- Share the band: €68,000 to €86,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance.
- "What range are you looking for in this role?"
- Tip: candidates are often vague here. If you get no clear answer, try: "No pressure at all, but
  if you're comfortable sharing, roughly what are you on at the moment? It helps us pitch the
  offer right."

## 5. Next steps (2 min)

Explain the technical interview and the final round. We get back to them within two working days.
