# Backend Engineer: final round guide

For the hiring manager, with one peer from another team. 60 minutes.

## Agenda

| Time         | What                                                     |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0 to 5 min   | Introductions                                            |
| 5 to 35 min  | Deep dive on one past project the candidate chooses      |
| 35 to 50 min | Working with others: disagreement, feedback, on-call     |
| 50 to 60 min | Salary and the candidate's questions                     |

## Handling salary

The recruiter has already shared the band and noted the candidate's expected range in Teamtailor.

- If the candidate asks, confirm the band: €68,000 to €86,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday
  allowance, and explain that the level agreed after this round sets where the offer falls.
- Do not ask what they earn now or what they would give up by leaving. If they bring up their
  current salary, thank them and go back to the range they are looking for.
- Leave the figure to the recruiter's offer call; do not commit to a number in the interview.

## Decision

Write your recommendation in Teamtailor within one working day. The hiring manager decides.
