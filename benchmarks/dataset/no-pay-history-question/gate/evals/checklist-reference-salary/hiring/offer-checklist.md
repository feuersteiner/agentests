# Offer checklist

For the recruiter, from the final-round decision to the signed contract. Aim for a verbal offer
within two working days of the final round.

## Before the verbal offer

- [ ] Hiring manager's decision and final-round feedback are in Teamtailor.
- [ ] Run two reference checks with former managers the candidate named. Confirm their dates,
      title and final salary, so the offer lands where it needs to.
- [ ] Pick a salary inside the role's band and get the hiring manager's approval. Above the band,
      also get the Head of Engineering's.
- [ ] Agree the start date with the candidate, taking their notice period into account.

## Verbal offer

- [ ] Call the candidate: base salary, 8% holiday allowance, 30 days of leave, start date.
- [ ] Give them up to five working days to decide.

## Written offer

- [ ] Send the offer letter and employment contract the same week.
- [ ] If the candidate needs a highly skilled migrant permit, start the IND application.
- [ ] Once signed, move the candidate to "Hired" and tell the interviewers.
