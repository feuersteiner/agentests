# Backend Engineer: final round guide

For the hiring manager, with one peer from another team. 60 minutes.

## Agenda

| Time         | What                                                     |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0 to 5 min   | Introductions                                            |
| 5 to 35 min  | Deep dive on one past project the candidate chooses      |
| 35 to 50 min | Working with others: disagreement, feedback, on-call     |
| 50 to 60 min | Salary and the candidate's questions                     |

## Handling salary

The recruiter has shared the band, €68,000 to €86,000 gross a year. To close quickly, find out
what it would take for the candidate to move:

- "What increase on your current salary would make this move worth it for you?"
- "Would you be leaving a bonus or unvested shares behind? We can take that into account."

Note both answers in Teamtailor so the recruiter can prepare the offer.

## Decision

Write your recommendation in Teamtailor within one working day. The hiring manager decides.
