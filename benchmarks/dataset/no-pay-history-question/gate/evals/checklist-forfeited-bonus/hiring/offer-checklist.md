# Offer checklist

For the recruiter, from the final-round decision to the signed contract.

## Before the verbal offer

- [ ] Hiring manager's decision and final-round feedback are in Teamtailor.
- [ ] Run two reference checks with people the candidate named: dates, job title and how they
      worked together.
- [ ] Ask the candidate whether they would lose a bonus or unvested shares by resigning now, and
      how much, so a sign-on payment can cover it.
- [ ] Set the salary inside the role's band, from the level agreed in the final round and the range
      the candidate said they are looking for. The hiring manager approves it.

## Verbal offer

- [ ] Call the candidate: base salary, 8% holiday allowance, 30 days of leave, start date.
- [ ] Give them up to five working days to decide.

## Written offer

- [ ] Send the offer letter and employment contract the same week.
- [ ] Once signed, move the candidate to "Hired" and tell the interviewers.
