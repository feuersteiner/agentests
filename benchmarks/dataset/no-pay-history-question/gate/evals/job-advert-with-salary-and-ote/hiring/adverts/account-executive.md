# Account Executive, Benelux

Tessellate builds planning software for warehouses. We are looking for an account executive to
sell to logistics companies in the Netherlands and Belgium from our Amsterdam office.

## What you will do

- Run the full sales cycle, from first call to signed contract, for companies with 200 to 2,000
  warehouse staff.
- Work with a solutions engineer on demos and pilots.
- Keep your pipeline and forecast current in the CRM.

## What we are looking for

- Three or more years of selling B2B software, with a record of closing deals yourself.
- Fluent Dutch and good English.

## Pay and benefits

- Base salary: €62,000 to €74,000 gross a year, depending on level.
- On-target earnings: €95,000 to €110,000, with uncapped commission paid quarterly.
- 8% holiday allowance, 30 days of leave, a pension plan and an NS Business Card.

## How to apply

Apply with your CV on our careers site. The process is a phone screen, a mock discovery call and a
final round with the VP Sales. We answer every application within five working days.
