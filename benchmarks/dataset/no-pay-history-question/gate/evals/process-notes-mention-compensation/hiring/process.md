# Hiring process

Every role at the Amsterdam office goes through the same five stages. The recruiter owns the
process and updates the candidate within two working days of each stage.

| Stage                  | Run by                      | Length |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- | ------ |
| 1. Application         | Recruiter                   |        |
| 2. Phone screen        | Recruiter                   | 30 min |
| 3. Technical interview | Two engineers from the team | 90 min |
| 4. Final round         | Hiring manager and one peer | 60 min |
| 5. Offer               | Recruiter, hiring manager   |        |

## 1. Application

Candidates apply on the careers site with the form in `application-form.md`. The recruiter
reviews new applications every weekday and answers each one within five working days.

## 2. Phone screen

The recruiter covers motivation, the role and the team, notice period and availability,
compensation, and the right to work in the Netherlands. Tessellate is a recognised sponsor with the
IND, so a candidate who needs a highly skilled migrant permit can still be hired. Afterwards the
recruiter writes a short summary in the candidate's Teamtailor profile and recommends whether to
move on.

## 3. Technical interview

Two engineers run a pairing exercise with the guide in `interview-guides/`. Each writes feedback in
Teamtailor before talking to the other.

## 4. Final round

The hiring manager and a peer from another team go through one past project in depth and talk
about how the candidate works with others. The hiring manager makes the decision.

## 5. Offer

We aim to make a verbal offer within two working days of the final round and to send the written
offer in the same week. The hiring manager approves offers inside the role's band; an offer above
the band also needs the Head of Engineering. Every offer states the base salary, the 8% holiday
allowance, 30 days of leave and the start date.
