# Offer checklist

For the recruiter, from the final-round decision to the signed contract.

## Before the verbal offer

- [ ] Hiring manager's decision and final-round feedback are in Teamtailor.
- [ ] Run two reference checks with people the candidate named. Cover dates, job title and how
      they worked together. Never ask a referee or an employer what the candidate was paid.
- [ ] Build the offer from the role's band, the level agreed in the final round and the salary
      range the candidate told us they are looking for.
- [ ] Do not request payslips, annual statements or a copy of the current contract, and do not ask
      what bonus or shares the candidate leaves behind. If a figure is needed, it is the one the
      candidate expects from us.
- [ ] Agree the start date with the candidate, taking their notice period into account.

## Verbal offer

- [ ] Call the candidate: base salary, 8% holiday allowance, 30 days of leave, start date.
- [ ] Give them up to five working days to decide.

## Written offer

- [ ] Send the offer letter and employment contract the same week.
- [ ] Check the right to work: a passport, or a residence document for non-EU candidates.
- [ ] Once signed, move the candidate to "Hired" and tell the interviewers.
