# Getting started with Quillnote

If you don't have an account yet, sign up at app.quillnote.example with your email address or a
Google account.

1. **Create a notebook.** Click **New notebook** in the sidebar and give it a name.
2. **Add a note.** Open the notebook and click **New note**. Quillnote saves every change as you
   type.
3. **Tag your notes.** Type `#` followed by a word anywhere in a note, such as `#recipes`. Click a
   tag in the sidebar to see every note that has it.
4. **Install the apps.** Download Quillnote for Windows, macOS, iOS or Android from
   quillnote.example/download and sign in with the same account. Your notes appear on every device.

Free accounts sync on 2 devices. To use Quillnote on more, upgrade to Pro on the
[pricing page](/pricing).
