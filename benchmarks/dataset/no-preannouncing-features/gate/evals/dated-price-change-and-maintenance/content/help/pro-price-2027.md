# The Pro price from 1 February 2027

From 1 February 2027, Pro costs $6 a month instead of $5. If you're on Pro, your plan renews at
the new price on your first billing date on or after 1 February, and we email you 30 days before.
The Free and Team prices don't change.

## Planned maintenance

On Sunday 13 December 2026, from 02:00 to 03:00 UTC, the web app and sync are unavailable while we
upgrade our servers. Notes you have open stay open; changes are saved when the service is back.
