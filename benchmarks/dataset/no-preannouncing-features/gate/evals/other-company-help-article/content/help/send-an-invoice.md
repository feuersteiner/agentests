# Send an invoice

Tallybook emails the invoice to your client as a PDF when you click **Send**.

1. Open **Invoices** and click the draft you want to send.
2. Check the client's email address and the due date.
3. Click **Send**.

Invoices you haven't sent yet stay under **Drafts**. Until the client opens the email, the
invoice shows **Sent**; after that it shows **Viewed**. Tallybook emails the client a reminder
3 days before the due date, and you can see the current status of every invoice on the
**Invoices** page.
