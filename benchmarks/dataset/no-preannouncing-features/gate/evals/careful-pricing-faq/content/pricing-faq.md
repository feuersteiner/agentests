# Pricing FAQ

## How am I billed?

Every plan is billed monthly, by card (Visa, Mastercard or American Express) or PayPal. Your plan
renews on the same day each month, and we email you a receipt.

## What happens when I upgrade?

The new plan starts at once. You pay the difference for the rest of the current month.

## Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the month you have paid for. There are no refunds for
part of a month.

## What happens to my notes if I downgrade to Free?

All your notes stay. Only the 2 devices you used most recently keep syncing, and edit access on
notes you share turns into view-only.

## How does Team billing work?

Team costs $8.50 per member a month, on one invoice. A member added mid-month is charged for the
rest of that month.

## Is there a discount for students?

Yes. Students and teachers get Pro at half price with a school email address. Apply at
quillnote.example/education.

## Can I pay for a year at once?

No. Every plan is billed month by month.

## Is there a family plan?

No. Each person needs their own account. To work on notebooks together, invite each other with
edit access on Pro.
