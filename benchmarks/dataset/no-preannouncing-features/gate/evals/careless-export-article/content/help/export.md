# Export and print notes

You can export a single note, a notebook or your whole account, on every plan, in the web app and
the Windows and macOS apps.

## Export one note

1. Open the note.
2. Click **File > Export**.
3. Choose **Markdown (.md)**, **HTML (.html)** or **Plain text (.txt)**.

The export is the current version of the note. Images and attachments are saved in a folder beside
the file.

## Export a notebook

Right-click the notebook in the sidebar, click **Export notebook** and pick a format. The ZIP file
will include one file per note and an `attachments` folder.

## Export everything

Go to **Settings > Account > Export all notes**. We email you a link to a ZIP of every notebook in
Markdown. The link works for 7 days.

## PDF export

PDF export isn't available yet, but it's coming soon: we're adding it in the next release, for
single notes and whole notebooks. For now, open the note, click **File > Print** and choose
**Save as PDF** in the print dialog.

## On iPhone, iPad and Android

The mobile apps don't export files yet. To copy a note's text into another app, tap **Share**.
