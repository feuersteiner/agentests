# Invite teammates to your team

You need to be a Team admin.

1. Open **Settings > Team > Members** and click **Invite**.
2. Enter one email address per line and click **Send invitations**.

If a teammate doesn't have a Quillnote account yet, the invitation asks them to create one.
Invitations that haven't been accepted yet are listed under **Pending**, where you can resend or
cancel them.

The plan you're currently on is shown at the top of **Settings > Plan**. If a member leaves, their
notes are still there: an admin can move them to another member. If you don't need a team
notebook for now, archive it from the sidebar.
