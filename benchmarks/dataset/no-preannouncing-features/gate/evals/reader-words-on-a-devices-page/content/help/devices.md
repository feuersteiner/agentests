# Manage your devices

Open **Settings > Devices** to see every device signed in to your account, with the date each one
last synced.

- Free accounts sync on 2 devices. If you sign in on a third, Quillnote asks which device to sign
  out.
- To stop using a device for now, click **Sign out** next to it. Its notes are still in your
  account and come back when you sign in again.
- A device that hasn't synced yet since you signed in shows **Waiting for first sync**.
