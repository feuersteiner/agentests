# What happens when your connection drops

Quillnote saves every change to your account as you type, so your notes are the same on every
device.

If your connection drops while a note is open:

- The note stays open and you can keep typing. The cloud icon next to the title turns grey.
- When the connection comes back, Quillnote saves your changes and the icon turns blue again.
- If you close the note or quit the app before the connection comes back, the changes you made in
  the meantime are lost.

Until the connection is back, you can't open another note, create a note or search.
