# Export and print notes

## Export one note

1. Open the note.
2. Click **File > Export**.
3. Choose **Markdown (.md)**, **HTML (.html)** or **Plain text (.txt)**.

Images and attachments are saved in a folder beside the file.

## Save a note as a PDF

At the moment, Quillnote exports notes as Markdown, HTML or plain text, not as PDF. To get a PDF,
open the note, click **File > Print** and choose **Save as PDF** in the print dialog.
