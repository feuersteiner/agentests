# Does Quillnote work offline?

No. Quillnote needs an internet connection to open, create, search or edit notes, and notes are
not stored on your device for use without a connection.

## If your connection drops while a note is open

- The note stays open and you can keep typing. The cloud icon next to the title turns grey.
- When the connection comes back, Quillnote saves the changes you made in the meantime and the icon
  turns blue again.
- Changes that haven't been saved yet are lost if you close the note or quit the app before the
  connection comes back, so keep the note open until the icon is blue.

Until the connection is back, you can't open another note, create a note or search.

## Read notes without a connection

Before a flight or a trip, export the notebooks you need as HTML or Markdown (see
[Export and print notes](/help/export)) and open the files on your device. These copies don't
sync: changes you make to them don't reach Quillnote.
