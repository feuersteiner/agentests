# Share a note or notebook

You can share a single note or a whole notebook, by link or by inviting people by email.

## Share by link

1. Open the note, or right-click the notebook in the sidebar.
2. Click **Share > Copy link**.
3. Send the link. Anyone who has it can read the note or notebook, without an account.

## Invite people

1. Click **Share > Invite** and enter one or more email addresses.
2. Choose **Can view** or **Can edit**. Edit access needs the Pro or Team plan.
3. Click **Send**. Each person gets an email with a link to the note or notebook.

People you invite can read and edit, but comments on shared notes aren't supported yet.

## Stop sharing

Click **Share**, then **Turn off link** to disable the link, or click the name of a person and
choose **Remove**. They lose access at once.
