# Export responses

You can download a form's responses as a CSV or XLSX file.

## Steps

1. Open the form and go to **Responses**.
2. Click **Export**.
3. Choose **CSV** or **XLSX**.
4. Click **Download**.

## Large exports

An export with more than 10,000 rows is not downloaded in the browser. We email you a download
link instead. The link expires after 7 days.

## What the file contains

Each row is one response. The first columns are the submission time and the respondent's email
address, when the form collects it. The other columns follow the order of the fields in the form.
