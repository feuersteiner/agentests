Hi {{first_name}},

Welcome to Mapleform! Getting your first form live is quick and easy, and anyone can do it. No
technical skills needed.

1. **Pick a template.** Just head to **Forms > New form** and choose one of our 14 templates.
2. **Add your fields.** Simply drag fields in from the left panel.
3. **Hit Publish.** One click on **Publish** in the top right and you're live.
4. **Share it.** Grab your link from the **Share** tab in seconds.

That's all there is to it. You'll be collecting responses in no time!
