# Mapleform Help

Guides for building forms, collecting responses and working with your data.

## Getting started

Mapleform makes building a form easy. Create a form from **Forms > New form**, add the fields you
need from the left panel, then publish it and share the link. Publishing is effortless: the
**Publish** button is in the top right corner of every form.

- [Publish a form](publish-a-form.md)
