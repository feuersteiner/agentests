# Publish a form

A new form starts as a draft that only members of your workspace can see. Publishing makes it
available to the people you choose.

## Before you start

- The form needs at least one field.
- On the Free plan you can have 3 published forms at a time. Drafts are not counted.

## Steps

1. Open the form from **Forms**.
2. Click **Publish** in the top right corner.
3. Choose who can respond: **Anyone with the link** or **People in my workspace**.
4. Click **Publish form**.

## Share the form

After publishing, open the **Share** tab. It has three options:

- a link to send by email or chat
- a QR code to print
- an embed snippet for your website

## Change a published form

Edit the form and click **Publish** again. The live form is updated and the link stays the same.
