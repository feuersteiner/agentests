# How long we keep your data

Responses are stored in the EU, in data centres in Frankfurt and Dublin.

| Data                | Kept for                                 |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Responses           | Until you delete them or close the workspace |
| Deleted responses   | 30 days in the trash, then erased        |
| Webhook delivery log | 30 days                                 |
| Billing records     | 7 years, as tax law requires             |

When you close a workspace, we erase its forms and responses after 30 days. Backups are
overwritten within a further 35 days.

To ask for a copy of your data, email privacy@mapleform.example from the address of a workspace
owner.
