# Read the response summary

The **Summary** tab turns a form's responses into an easy-to-read chart for each question.

## Before you start

- Only owners and admins can open the Summary tab.
- If you just published the form, the first responses can take up to 10 seconds to appear.

## Steps

There are four steps. Reading through them takes about ten minutes the first time.

1. Open the form and go to **Responses > Summary**.
2. Choose a date range.
3. Click a chart to see the responses behind it.
4. Click **Quick Share** to copy a read-only link to the summary. Quick Share is included in the
   Simple plan and above.

## Limits

A summary shows only the 50 most common answers to a question. The other answers are in the
export.
