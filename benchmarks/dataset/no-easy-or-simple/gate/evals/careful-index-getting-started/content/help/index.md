# Mapleform Help

Guides for building forms, collecting responses and working with your data.

## Getting started

New to Mapleform? Work through these in order:

1. Create a form from **Forms > New form**. Pick one of the 14 templates or start with a blank form.
2. Add fields from the left panel. A form needs at least one field.
3. [Publish the form](publish-a-form.md) and choose who can respond.
4. Share the link, QR code or embed snippet from the **Share** tab.
5. [Export the responses](export-responses.md) as a CSV or XLSX file.

On the Free plan you can publish 3 forms and collect 100 responses a month.
