Welcome to Mapleform. Here is how to publish your first form.

1. Go to **Forms > New form** and pick one of the 14 templates.
2. Just drag the fields you want in from the left panel.
3. All you have to do now is click **Publish** in the top right corner and choose who can respond.
4. Open the **Share** tab for a link, a QR code and an embed snippet.

On the Free plan you can have 3 published forms and collect 100 responses a month.
