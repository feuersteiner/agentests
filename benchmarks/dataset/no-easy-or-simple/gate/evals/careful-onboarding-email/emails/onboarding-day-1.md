Hi {{first_name}},

Welcome to Mapleform. This email walks you through publishing your first form.

1. **Create the form.** Go to **Forms > New form** and pick one of the 14 templates, or choose
   **Blank form**.
2. **Add your fields.** Drag fields in from the left panel. A form needs at least one field before
   it can be published.
3. **Publish.** Click **Publish** in the top right corner and choose who can respond: **Anyone
   with the link** or **People in my workspace**.
4. **Share it.** Open the **Share** tab for a link, a QR code and an embed snippet.

Until you publish, the form is a draft that only members of your workspace can see. If you edit
the form later, click **Publish** again; the link stays the same.

On the Free plan you can have 3 published forms and collect 100 responses a month.

Reply to this email if you get stuck on any step.
