# Share a summary

Quick Share gives you a read-only link to a form's response summary. It is included in the Simple
plan and above.

## Steps

1. Open the form and go to **Responses > Summary**.
2. Click **Quick Share**.
3. Choose when the link expires: after 7 days, after 30 days or never.
4. Click **Copy link**.

To withdraw a link, open **Quick Share** again and click **Revoke**.
