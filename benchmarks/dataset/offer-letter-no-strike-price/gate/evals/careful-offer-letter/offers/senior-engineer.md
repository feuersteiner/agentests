# Offer of employment: Senior Engineer, Platform

23 November 2026

Dear Dana,

We are delighted to offer you the position of Senior Engineer, Platform at Tessellate, Inc., at
level L4, reporting to Priya Raman, Engineering Manager. This letter sets out the main terms of
your employment.

## Start date and location

Your first day will be Monday 11 January 2027. You will work from our Denver office, three days a
week, and from home the rest of the week.

## Salary and signing bonus

Your base salary will be $182,000 a year, paid twice a month and reviewed every year. You will also
receive a signing bonus of $10,000, paid with your first payroll.

## Equity

We want everyone who builds Tessellate to own a real part of it. You will be granted options over
12,000 shares of common stock under the Tessellate, Inc. 2024 Stock Option Plan, the standard
grant for a senior engineer.

- Your options vest over four years: 25% after your first year, then monthly over the next 36
  months.
- The board grants them at its first meeting after your start date. The exercise price will be
  set by the board on that grant date, based on an independent valuation.
- You will have ten years from the grant date to exercise them, or 90 days after you leave.

As the platform you build carries more of our customers, this grant is how you share in what we
build together.

## Benefits

You will get our standard benefits from your first day, including health insurance, a 401(k)
match and 25 days of holiday a year plus US public holidays.

## Next steps

This offer is open until Friday 4 December 2026. To accept it, sign and return this letter. Your
employment is at will and depends on a satisfactory background check.

We are excited about what you will build with us.

Sincerely,

Rosa Delgado
Head of People, Tessellate, Inc.
