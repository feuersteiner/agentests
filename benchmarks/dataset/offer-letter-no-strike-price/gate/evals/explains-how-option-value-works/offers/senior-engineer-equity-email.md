Hi Dana,

Good question, and I want to give you a straight answer rather than a made-up number.

Each option gives you the right to buy one share of Tessellate common stock at the exercise price.
What an option is worth to you is the difference between what a share is worth when you sell it
and that exercise price, so the value of your 12,000 options depends on how Tessellate grows and
on the exercise price the board sets.

The board sets that price on your grant date, from an independent valuation, so we don't know it
yet, and I won't put a dollar figure on your grant before then. What is fixed today is the number,
12,000, and the vesting: a quarter after your first year, then monthly over the next three years.
