# Benefits

Every full-time employee in the US gets these from their first day.

- Health: medical, dental and vision insurance. Tessellate pays 90% of the premium for employees
  and 75% for their dependants.
- 401(k): Tessellate matches contributions up to 4% of base salary.
- Holiday: 25 days a year, plus US public holidays.
- Parental leave: 16 weeks at full pay.
- Learning budget: $1,500 a year for courses, books and conferences.
- Equipment: a laptop of your choice and $500 for your home office.
