# Compensation policy

Last reviewed by the board on 2026-09-15.

Every role has a level, and every level has a salary band and a standard option grant. The hiring
manager proposes a level, and the VP of the function approves the level, the salary and the grant
before an offer goes out.

## Engineering levels

| Level | Title           | Base salary band     | Standard option grant |
| ----- | --------------- | -------------------- | --------------------- |
| L2    | Engineer        | $118,000 to $138,000 | 4,000 options         |
| L3    | Engineer II     | $140,000 to $165,000 | 7,000 options         |
| L4    | Senior Engineer | $168,000 to $195,000 | 12,000 options        |
| L5    | Staff Engineer  | $198,000 to $230,000 | 20,000 options        |

## Share options

- Options are granted under the Tessellate, Inc. 2024 Stock Option Plan.
- They vest over four years: 25% after the first year, then monthly over the next 36 months.
- The board grants a new hire's options at its first meeting after their start date.
- The exercise price of a grant is the fair market value of a share on the grant date, which the
  board sets from the latest independent 409A valuation.
- An employee has ten years from the grant date to exercise, or 90 days after leaving.

## Signing bonuses

A signing bonus of up to $15,000 needs the VP's approval and is paid with the first payroll.
