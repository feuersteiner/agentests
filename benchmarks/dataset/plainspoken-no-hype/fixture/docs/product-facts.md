# Product facts

The source for all Wrenmail copy. Last checked on 18 November 2026.

## What Wrenmail is

- An email-marketing tool for small online shops.
- Connects to Shopify and WooCommerce. Once a store is connected, Wrenmail reads its products,
  customers and orders.
- Runs in the browser. There is no mobile app.

## Plans

| Plan    | Price       | Contacts     | Emails a month |
| ------- | ----------- | ------------ | -------------- |
| Starter | €12 a month | up to 1,000  | up to 5,000    |
| Pro     | €32 a month | up to 10,000 | up to 50,000   |

- Both plans are billed monthly. A customer can cancel at any time and keeps access until the end of
  the paid month.
- Both plans start with a 14-day trial. No card is needed to start it.
- A customer can move between plans at any time. The change applies from the next bill.
- Shops with more than 10,000 contacts are not supported yet.

## In both plans

- Email editor: drag-and-drop blocks, 40 templates.
- Product blocks: pick a product from the connected store and the block fills in its name, photo and
  price.
- Segments: group contacts by what they bought, how much they spent or when they last ordered.
- Scheduled sends: pick a date and time for a campaign.
- Basic reports: opens and clicks for each email.

## In Pro only

- Abandoned-cart flow (released on 17 November 2026):
  - Sends a reminder email one hour after a shopper leaves the checkout with items in the cart.
  - An optional second reminder goes out 24 hours after the first.
  - The reminder lists the items left in the cart, with a link back to the checkout.
  - Stops as soon as the shopper places the order.
  - Only works when the shopper typed an email address at the checkout before leaving, or was
    logged in to the store.
  - The shop owner writes the reminder once, or starts from one of 3 templates.
  - Turned on under Automations > Abandoned cart. Off by default.
- Send-time optimisation:
  - For each contact, Wrenmail looks at the hours in which they opened emails over the last 90 days
    and sends the campaign at the hour they opened most often.
  - A contact with fewer than 5 opens in that period gets the hour most common across the whole
    list.
  - A campaign with send-time optimisation goes out over 24 hours, starting at the time the shop
    owner picks. It cannot be used for an email that must reach everyone at the same moment, such
    as a one-hour sale.
  - A tick box on the scheduling screen. Off by default.
- Revenue reports: the orders and revenue from shoppers who clicked an email and ordered within 5
  days.

## Support

- By email only, on working days.
- Starter: a reply within three business days.
- Pro: a reply within two business days.

## Not available

- SMS and WhatsApp messages.
- A/B tests of subject lines.
- Languages other than English in the app.
