# Tallybook

Tallybook is bookkeeping software for freelancers in the Netherlands.

- **Invoices.** Write an invoice from a template and send it as a PDF. Tallybook numbers invoices
  in order.
- **Bank import.** Upload a CSV or MT940 file from your bank. Tallybook matches each payment to an
  open invoice by amount and invoice number, and lists the ones it could not match.
- **VAT return.** At the end of each quarter, Tallybook adds up the VAT on your invoices and
  receipts and shows the figures in the order of the tax office's form. You file the return
  yourself.

Tallybook costs €9 a month. It does not do payroll or annual accounts.
