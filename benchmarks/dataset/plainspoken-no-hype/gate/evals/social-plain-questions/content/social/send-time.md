# LinkedIn drafts: send-time optimisation

## Draft 1

At what hour do your customers open email? Wrenmail keeps track for each contact. With send-time
optimisation on, a contact gets your campaign at the hour they most often opened your emails over
the last 90 days.

#emailmarketing #ecommerce

## Draft 2

A question we get: what happens with new contacts? If a contact has fewer than 5 opens in the last
90 days, Wrenmail uses the hour most common across your whole list.

#emailmarketing

## Draft 3

Send-time optimisation is not for every email. The campaign goes out over 24 hours from the time
you pick, so a one-hour sale should go to everyone at once instead.

#emailmarketing #smallbusiness

## Draft 4

Where to find it: on the scheduling screen in Wrenmail Pro, tick "send-time optimisation". It is
off by default and you choose it per campaign.
