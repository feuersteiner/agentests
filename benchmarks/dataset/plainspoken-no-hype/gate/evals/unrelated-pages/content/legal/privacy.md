# Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal data Wrenmail B.V. collects when you use wrenmail.example, why,
and how long we keep it.

## What we collect

- Your name and email address, when you create an account.
- Your billing address and the last four digits of your card, when you pay.
- The contacts you upload or that we read from your connected store. You remain the controller of
  that data; we process it on your instructions.

## How long we keep it

We delete account data 90 days after you close your account. We keep invoices for seven years,
as tax law requires.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. Write to
privacy@wrenmail.example. We reply within 30 days.
