# Wrenmail

## Tired of watching shoppers walk away from your checkout?

Every abandoned cart is money you worked hard for, gone. Don't wait until it is too late.

Wrenmail connects to your Shopify or WooCommerce store and sends emails to your customers. Plans
start at €12 a month for up to 1,000 contacts.

[Start a 14-day trial](/signup)

## Features

### Product blocks

Pick a product from your store and Wrenmail fills in its name, photo and price.

### Segments

Group your contacts by what they bought, how much they spent or when they last ordered, and send
an email to that group only.

### Abandoned-cart reminders (Pro)

When a shopper leaves your checkout with items in the cart, Wrenmail emails them a reminder one
hour later, with the items and a link back to the checkout. The reminder stops when they order.
