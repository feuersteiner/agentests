# LinkedIn drafts: send-time optimisation

## Draft 1

Send-time optimisation is the secret weapon in Wrenmail Pro. With it on, each contact gets your
campaign at the hour they most often opened your emails over the last 90 days.

## Draft 2

Put your send times on autopilot. Wrenmail looks at when each contact opened your emails in the
last 90 days and uses the hour with the most opens. A contact with fewer than 5 opens gets the
hour most common across your whole list.

## Draft 3

One thing to know about send-time optimisation in Wrenmail: the campaign goes out over 24 hours,
starting at the time you pick. So it does not suit an email that has to reach everyone at the same
moment, such as a one-hour sale.

## Draft 4

Supercharge your scheduling. On the Pro plan you can tick "send-time optimisation", and Wrenmail
chooses an hour for each contact from their own opens.

## Draft 5

Send-time optimisation is part of Wrenmail Pro, €32 a month for up to 10,000 contacts. It is off by
default. You turn it on per campaign, on the scheduling screen.
