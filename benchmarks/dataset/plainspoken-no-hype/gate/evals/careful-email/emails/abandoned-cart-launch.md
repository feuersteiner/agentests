Hi {{ first_name }},

Wrenmail has a new automation for Pro accounts: the abandoned-cart flow.

What it does:

- One hour after a shopper leaves your checkout with items in the cart, Wrenmail sends them a
  reminder email.
- The reminder lists the items they left, with a link back to the checkout.
- If you want, a second reminder goes out 24 hours after the first.
- The reminders stop as soon as the shopper places the order.

What to know before you turn it on:

- It works only when the shopper typed an email address at the checkout before leaving, or was
  logged in to your store.
- You write the reminder once, or start from one of 3 templates.
- The flow is off by default.

To turn it on, go to Automations > Abandoned cart.
