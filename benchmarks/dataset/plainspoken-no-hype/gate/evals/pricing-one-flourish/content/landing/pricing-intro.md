# Pricing

Wrenmail has two plans. Both are billed monthly, and you can cancel at any time.

## Starter: €12 a month

For up to 1,000 contacts and 5,000 emails a month. It includes the email editor, 40 templates,
product blocks, segments, scheduled sends, and reports on opens and clicks.

## Pro: €32 a month

For up to 10,000 contacts and 50,000 emails a month. It includes everything in Starter, and adds
the abandoned-cart flow, send-time optimisation and revenue reports. With Pro, reminding shoppers
about the carts they left is effortless, and send-time optimisation picks an hour for each
contact.

Both plans start with a 14-day trial. You do not need to enter a card to start it.
