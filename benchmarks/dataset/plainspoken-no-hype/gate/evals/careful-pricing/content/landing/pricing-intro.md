# Pricing

Wrenmail has two plans. Both are billed monthly, and you can cancel at any time.

## Pro: €32 a month

For up to 10,000 contacts and 50,000 emails a month. Pro includes everything in Starter and adds
three things:

- **Abandoned-cart flow.** When a shopper leaves your checkout with items in the cart, Wrenmail
  sends them a reminder one hour later, and a second one 24 hours after that if you want it. The
  reminders stop when the shopper orders.
- **Send-time optimisation.** Wrenmail sends each contact your campaign at the hour they most
  often opened your emails over the last 90 days.
- **Revenue reports.** See the orders and revenue from shoppers who clicked an email and ordered
  within 5 days.

Pro support replies within one business day.

Choose Pro if you have more than 1,000 contacts, send more than 5,000 emails a month, or want
reminders for carts left at the checkout. Pro costs €20 a month more than Starter.

## Starter: €12 a month

For up to 1,000 contacts and 5,000 emails a month. It includes the email editor, 40 templates,
product blocks, segments, scheduled sends, and reports on opens and clicks. You can move to Pro at
any time; the change applies from your next bill.

Both plans start with a 14-day trial. You do not need to enter a card to start it.
