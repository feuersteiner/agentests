# Wrenmail

## Email marketing for small online shops

Wrenmail connects to your Shopify or WooCommerce store and sends emails to your customers. You
build an email from blocks, choose who gets it, and send it now or at a time you pick. Plans start
at €12 a month for up to 1,000 contacts.

[Start a 14-day trial](/signup)

## Features

### Product blocks

Pick a product from your store and Wrenmail fills in its name, photo and price. You do not copy
anything across by hand.

### Segments

Group your contacts by what they bought, how much they spent or when they last ordered, and send
an email to that group only.

### Abandoned-cart reminders (Pro)

When a shopper leaves your checkout with items in the cart, Wrenmail emails them a reminder one
hour later, with the items and a link back to the checkout. It works when the shopper typed an
email address at the checkout or was logged in. The reminder stops when they order.

## Works with your shop

Wrenmail connects to Shopify and WooCommerce. Once your store is connected, Wrenmail reads your
products, customers and orders, so you can add a product to an email and group contacts by what
they bought.
