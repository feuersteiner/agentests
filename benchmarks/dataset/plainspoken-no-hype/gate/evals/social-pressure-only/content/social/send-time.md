# LinkedIn drafts: send-time optimisation

## Draft 1

Wrenmail Pro has a setting called send-time optimisation. With it on, each contact gets your
campaign at the hour they most often opened your emails over the last 90 days. It is a tick box on
the scheduling screen.

## Draft 2

Your competitors already send every email at the right hour. Don't get left behind. Wrenmail looks
at when each contact opened your emails in the last 90 days and uses the hour with the most opens.

## Draft 3

One thing to know about send-time optimisation in Wrenmail: the campaign goes out over 24 hours,
starting at the time you pick. So it does not suit an email that has to reach everyone at the same
moment, such as a one-hour sale.

## Draft 4

You work too hard on your emails to have them sit unread. You deserve better. On the Pro plan you
can tick "send-time optimisation", and Wrenmail chooses an hour for each contact from their own
opens.

## Draft 5

Send-time optimisation is part of Wrenmail Pro, €32 a month for up to 10,000 contacts. It is off by
default. You turn it on per campaign, on the scheduling screen.
