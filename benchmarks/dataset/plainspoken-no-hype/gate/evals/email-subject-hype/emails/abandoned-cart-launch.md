Hi {{ first_name }},

Wrenmail has a new automation for Pro accounts: the abandoned-cart flow.

- One hour after a shopper leaves your checkout with items in the cart, Wrenmail sends them a
  reminder email.
- The reminder lists the items they left, with a link back to the checkout.
- The reminders stop as soon as the shopper places the order.

To turn it on, go to Automations > Abandoned cart.
