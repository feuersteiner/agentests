## Why Ardent is looking

Ardent's 140 stores build rotas in spreadsheets, and store staff clock in on the payroll
provider's time clock. The six-store pilot showed store managers saving hours every week with
labour forecasting and shift swaps.

## What we propose

Corvid Rota on the Plus plan for all 140 stores, from 1 July 2027:

- shift scheduling, labour forecasting and break and overtime compliance rules;
- the time clock and the employee mobile app;
- SAML single sign-on with Okta. SSO is on our roadmap for Q3 2027 and will be live before
  your July rollout, so store and area managers sign in with their Okta accounts from day one.
