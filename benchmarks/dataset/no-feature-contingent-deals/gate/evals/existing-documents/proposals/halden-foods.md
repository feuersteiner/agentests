# Proposal: Corvid Rota for Halden Foods

_Prepared for Rosa Lindqvist, Director of Operations, by Sam Ortega, 2 September 2026_

## Why Halden is looking

Halden runs 35 grocery stores and builds every rota by hand in spreadsheets. Store managers spend
about six hours a week on it, and last-minute swaps happen by text message, so payroll often
learns about them too late.

## What we propose

Corvid Rota on the Core plan for all 35 stores:

- shift scheduling with templates for each store's opening hours;
- the time clock, on staff phones and on a tablet in each store;
- shift swaps and open shifts, approved by the store manager in the app;
- the employee mobile app, with email or Google sign-in.
