# Proposal: Corvid Rota for Ardent Retail

_Prepared for Dana Whitlock, VP Store Operations, by Sam Ortega, 24 November 2026_

## Why Ardent is looking

Ardent's 140 stores build rotas in spreadsheets, and store staff clock in on the payroll
provider's time clock. The six-store pilot showed store managers saving hours every week with
labour forecasting and shift swaps.

## What we propose

Corvid Rota on the Plus plan for all 140 stores, from 1 July 2027:

- shift scheduling, labour forecasting and break and overtime compliance rules;
- the time clock and the employee mobile app;
- sign-in with email or with Google accounts.

## Sign-in and SSO

We know Ardent's managers use Okta for their other tools. Corvid Rota does not offer single
sign-on today. SSO is on our roadmap with a target of Q3 2027; that is a target, not a committed
date, and this proposal does not depend on it. Until then, staff sign in with email or Google,
and we will keep Marcus updated as SSO progresses.
