## Terms

- Term discounts: 10% off every year of a two-year term, 15% off every year of a three-year term.
- Invoicing: annually in advance, net 30. On a term of two years or more, the customer may choose
  quarterly invoicing in advance instead, at the same price.
- Prices hold for the whole term.
