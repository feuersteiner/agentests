_Account notes, Sam Ortega (account executive), last updated 20 November 2026_

## Account

- Ardent Retail Group, Inc., 1800 Commerce Drive, Columbus, OH 43215.
- 140 home and garden stores across Ohio, Indiana and Michigan, about 6,500 store staff.
- Schedules today with spreadsheets and a time clock from their payroll provider.

## The deal

- Plus for all 140 stores, two-year term, so the two-year discount applies.
- Rollout to every store in July 2027, after the spring season. Subscription to start 1 July 2027.
- Quarterly invoicing in advance.
- Every invoice must show Ardent's purchase order number, ARD-PO-55821, or their accounts
  payable team rejects it.
