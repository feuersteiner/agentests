# Quillon on Larkspur Standard: summary for your legal team

_Prepared by Nadia Brooks, Larkspur Data, 20 November 2026. The signed order form is the
contract; this page summarises it._

## The service

- Larkspur Standard for 50 seats: event collection from Quillon's web, iOS and Android apps,
  funnels, retention charts, dashboards and alerts.
- Up to 20 million events a month, kept for 13 months, hosted in the US on AWS.
- Google sign-in.

## Price and payment

- $40,000 a year: 50 seats at $800 per seat per year, with no volume discount.
- Billed annually in advance, net 30, or quarterly in advance on request.

## Term and renewal

- One year from 1 January 2027.
- Renews at the then-current price, with an increase of no more than 7% and at least 60 days'
  written notice of any change; up to 90 days' notice can be agreed.

## Support and availability

- Email support from 9 to 6 ET on weekdays, with a first reply within one business day.
- Standard includes no uptime SLA and no service credits. Larkspur publishes its measured uptime
  on its status page: 99.95% over the last 12 months. That figure describes the past and is not a
  contractual commitment. An uptime SLA, 99.9% a month with service credits, is part of the
  Enterprise package.

## Data and security

- Larkspur's SOC 2 Type II report is available under NDA.
- Quillon can export its data at any time, and for 30 days after the contract ends.
