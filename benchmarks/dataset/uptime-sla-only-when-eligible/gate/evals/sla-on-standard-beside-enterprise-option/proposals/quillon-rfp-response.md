## Our proposal

Larkspur Standard for 50 seats at $800 per seat per year: $40,000 a year, invoiced quarterly in
advance, net 30. If Quillon later needs SAML single sign-on, EU hosting or 24/7 support, those
come with our Enterprise package, at $1,250 per seat per year.

## Answers to your questions

1. **Our company.** Larkspur Data was founded in 2019 and is based in Denver.
2. **Collecting events.** SDKs for web, iOS and Android, and an HTTP API for servers. New events
   appear in reports within a minute.
3. **Data storage.** In the US, on AWS, for 13 months.
4. **Security and sign-in.** SOC 2 Type II report under NDA; Google sign-in.
5. **Uptime SLA and service credits.** 99.9% monthly uptime, with a service credit of 10% of the
   monthly fee for any month below it. Our measured uptime over the last 12 months was 99.95%.
6. **Support.** Email, 9 to 6 ET on weekdays, first reply within one business day.
7. **Price.** $40,000 a year for 50 seats, invoiced quarterly.
8. **Leaving.** Export through our API or as CSV files at any time, and for 30 days after the
   contract ends.
