# Larkspur Data: response to Quillon's RFP for product analytics

_Valid until 7 December 2026_

Prepared for Hannah Lee, Procurement, and Rafael Ortiz, CTO, Quillon Inc., by Nadia Brooks,
Larkspur Data. 20 November 2026.

We propose Larkspur Standard for 50 seats, as your RFP asks. Our answers follow your questions in
order.

## 1. Our company

Larkspur Data was founded in 2019 and is based in Denver. We have run hosted product analytics for
seven years.

## 2. Collecting events

Our SDKs for web, iOS and Android send events from your apps, and our HTTP API takes events from
your servers. New events appear in reports within a minute.

## 3. Where your data is stored

In the US, on AWS, across three availability zones. Standard keeps your events for 13 months.

## 4. Security and sign-in

We hold a SOC 2 Type II report, which we share under NDA. Your team signs in with Google.

## 5. Uptime SLA and service credits

The Standard package does not include an uptime SLA or service credits. Our status page,
status.larkspurdata.com, publishes the uptime we measure: 99.95% over the last 12 months, November
2025 to October 2026, with every incident listed. That is our record, not a commitment.

An uptime SLA of 99.9% a month, with service credits, comes with our Enterprise package. For 50
seats, Enterprise would be $56,250 a year after a 10% volume discount, and we are happy to quote it.

## 6. Support

Email support from 9 to 6 ET on weekdays, with a first reply within one business day.

## 7. Price and invoicing

50 seats of Standard at $800 per seat per year: $40,000 a year, with no volume discount. We invoice
annually in advance, net 30, and can invoice quarterly in advance instead, as you ask.

## 8. Getting your data out

You can export your data at any time through our export API or as CSV files, and for 30 days after
the contract ends.
