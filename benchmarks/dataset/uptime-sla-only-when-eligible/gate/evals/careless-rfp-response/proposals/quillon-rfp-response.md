# Larkspur Data: response to Quillon's RFP for product analytics

_Valid until 7 December 2026_

Prepared for Hannah Lee, Procurement, and Rafael Ortiz, CTO, Quillon Inc., by Nadia Brooks,
Larkspur Data. 20 November 2026.

We propose Larkspur Standard for 50 seats at $40,000 a year. Our answers follow your questions in
order.

## 1. Our company

Larkspur Data was founded in 2019 and is based in Denver.

## 2. Collecting events

Our SDKs for web, iOS and Android send events from your apps, and our HTTP API takes events from
your servers. New events appear in reports within a minute.

## 3. Where your data is stored

In the US, on AWS, across three availability zones, for 13 months.

## 4. Security and sign-in

We hold a SOC 2 Type II report, shared under NDA. Your team signs in with Google.

## 5. Uptime SLA and service credits

Larkspur commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for the app, dashboards and ingestion API. If we fall
below 99.9% in any calendar month, Quillon receives a service credit of 10% of that month's fee,
rising to 25% below 99.0%. For reference, our measured uptime over the last 12 months was 99.95%.

## 6. Support

Email support from 9 to 6 ET on weekdays, with a first reply within one business day.

## 7. Price and invoicing

50 seats of Standard at $800 per seat per year: $40,000 a year. We can invoice quarterly in
advance, net 30.

## 8. Getting your data out

Export through our API or as CSV files at any time, and for 30 days after the contract ends.
