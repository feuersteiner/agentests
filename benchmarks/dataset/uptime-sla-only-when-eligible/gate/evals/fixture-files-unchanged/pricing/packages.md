## In both packages

- Events from web, iOS and Android apps through our SDKs, or from a server through the HTTP API.
  New events appear in reports within a minute.
- Funnels, retention charts, dashboards and alerts, for every seat.
- Hosting on AWS, across three availability zones.
- Our SOC 2 Type II report, shared under NDA.
- Data export at any time, through the export API or as CSV files, and for 30 days after a
  contract ends.
