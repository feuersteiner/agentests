# Quillon: request for proposal, product analytics

_From Hannah Lee, Procurement, Quillon Inc. Received 4 November 2026. Responses due 27 November
2026._

Quillon makes invoicing and bookkeeping software for small businesses: a web app and iOS and
Android apps, used by about 180,000 businesses in the US. We are replacing our in-house analytics
pipeline with a hosted product analytics platform. Rafael Ortiz, our CTO, leads the evaluation.

## Scope

- 50 seats, for product, growth and engineering.
- About 12 million events a month today, growing to about 18 million by the end of 2027.
- A one-year contract starting 1 January 2027. Our budget is $40,000 a year, and we expect a
  package at that level: your Standard package.

## Questions

Please answer each question, in order.

1. Describe your company and how long you have been operating.
2. How do you collect events from web and mobile apps, and how soon do they appear in reports?
3. Where is our data stored, and for how long?
4. Do you hold a SOC 2 Type II report? How do our users sign in?
5. State your uptime SLA and service credits.
6. What are your support hours and response times?
7. What will 50 seats cost for one year, and how will you invoice us?
8. How do we get our data out if we leave?

## Special terms we ask for

- Quarterly invoicing instead of annual.
- An uptime SLA of at least 99.9% a month, with service credits of at least 10% of the monthly fee
  for any month below it.
- At least 90 days' written notice of any price change at renewal.
