# Campaign calendar

## Spring sale 2027

- Runs from Monday 15 March to Sunday 28 March 2027.
- The three best sellers are on sale. Their sale prices are set by the `sale_net` entries in
  `data/prices.yaml`; the checkout switches to them by itself on the first day, with no code.
- Email goes to every customer who opted in to offers, on the morning of 15 March.
- Orders placed by Sunday 21 March arrive in time for Easter (28 March).
