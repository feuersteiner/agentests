# Delivery

## Where we deliver

We deliver to every UK address, including the Highlands and Islands, Northern Ireland and the
Channel Islands.

## How long it takes

Each product has its own lead time in the lab, from 2 to 5 working days. Once your order leaves
the lab, standard delivery takes 2 to 3 working days. Standard delivery is free on every order.

## Express delivery

You can choose express delivery at checkout for £4.95 per order. Your parcel then arrives the
working day after it leaves the lab. Express delivery is optional.

## Tracking

We email you a tracking link when your order leaves the lab.
