# Plans and prices

Shelfwise is the storefront app for independent bookshops. Prices are for businesses and exclude
VAT at 20%.

| Plan    | Price              | Titles    |
| ------- | ------------------ | --------- |
| Starter | £19 a month + VAT  | 2,000     |
| Growth  | £49 a month + VAT  | Unlimited |

Both plans come with a 14-day free trial. There is no setup fee.
