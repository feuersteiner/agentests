# Refunds and reprints

If a print arrives damaged or the colours are wrong, email us a photo within 30 days. We reprint
it and post it to you at no charge.

If you would rather have your money back, we refund the full amount you paid to your card.
Refunds under £50 reach your account in 3 working days; larger refunds can take 5.

We cannot refund an order because of a typing mistake in your own caption, but we will reprint it
at half price.
