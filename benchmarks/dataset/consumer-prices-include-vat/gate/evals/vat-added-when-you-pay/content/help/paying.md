# Paying for your order

## How can I pay?

We take Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

## When do you take the payment?

We take the payment when you place your order. If we cannot print one of your photos, we refund
it to your card the same day.

## Is VAT included?

No. VAT at 20% is added to the prices on our product pages when you pay.
