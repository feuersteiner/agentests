# How ordering works

1. Upload your photos from your phone or computer.
2. Choose a product and check the preview.
3. Pay by card or Apple Pay.

## What you pay

The prices on our product pages are for the product itself. A £1 packaging fee is added to each
item at checkout, on top of the price shown.

Standard delivery is free on every order.
