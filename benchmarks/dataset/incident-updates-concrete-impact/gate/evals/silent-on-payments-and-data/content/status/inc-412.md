**Update, 10:25 UTC**

Since 09:10 UTC, product photos and collection thumbnails have not been loading on about 18% of
stores. Shoppers on those stores see grey boxes where the photos should be, and the product editor
shows broken thumbnails.

Checkout is working normally and orders are coming in as usual.

We're rolling back the change to our image service that caused this. Next update by 11:00 UTC.
