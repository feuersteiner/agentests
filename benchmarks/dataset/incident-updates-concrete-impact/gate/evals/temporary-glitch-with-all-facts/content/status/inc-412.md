**Update, 10:25 UTC**

Since 09:10 UTC, product photos have not been loading on about 18% of stores; shoppers see grey
boxes where the photos should be. Checkout, orders and payments are working normally, and no data
has been lost.

We're rolling back the change to our image service that caused this. It's only a temporary glitch
and everything will be back to normal soon. Next update by 11:00 UTC.
