**Update, 14:40 UTC**

Since 14:05 UTC, card payments are failing at checkout on many stores: shoppers see "Payment could
not be completed" after entering their card. Wallet and bank transfer payments still go through.

Orders placed before 14:05 UTC are not affected, and failed card payments have not been charged.
No store data has been lost.

We're investigating the cause with our card payment team. Next update by 15:15 UTC.
