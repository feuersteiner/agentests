**Identified, 10:25 UTC**

Since 09:10 UTC, product photos and collection thumbnails haven't been loading on about 18% of
Shelfwise stores, mostly those using the Arbor and Dawnlight themes. Shoppers on these stores see
grey boxes where the photos should be, and the product editor in the admin shows broken
thumbnails. You can still edit and save products.

Checkout, orders and payments are not affected: shoppers can buy as normal and payouts are on
schedule. No data has been lost. Your original photos are safe, and you don't need to upload
anything again.

A change to our image service this morning caused this. We're rolling it back now, region by
region: Europe is done and the US should finish at about 10:50 UTC.

Next update by 11:00 UTC.
