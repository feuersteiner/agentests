**Resolved, 11:00 UTC**

Product photos and thumbnails are loading normally again on every store since 10:52 UTC. From
09:10 to 10:52 UTC they did not load on about 18% of stores, where shoppers saw grey boxes instead.

Checkout, orders and payments worked normally throughout, and no data was lost.

We rolled back the change to our image service that caused this, and we're adding a check so the
same fault cannot ship again. This incident is closed.
