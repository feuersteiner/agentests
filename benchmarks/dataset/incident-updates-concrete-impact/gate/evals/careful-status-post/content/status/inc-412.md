**Update, 10:25 UTC**

Since 09:10 UTC, product photos and collection thumbnails have not been loading on about 18% of
Shelfwise stores. Shoppers on those stores see grey boxes where the photos should be, and the
product editor in the admin shows broken thumbnails. Editing and saving products still works.

Checkout, orders and payments are working normally, and no data has been lost: your original
photos are safe and nothing needs to be uploaded again.

The cause is a change to our image service this morning. We are rolling it back now, region by
region; Europe is done and the US is in progress.

Next update by 11:00 UTC.
