Hi {{first_name}},

To connect your own domain to {{store_name}}:

1. Open **Settings > Domains** and press **Connect a domain**.
2. Enter the domain, for example `shop.example.com`.
3. At your domain provider, add the two DNS records Shelfwise shows you.

New DNS records can take up to 48 hours to reach every network. The domain shows as
**Connected** in **Settings > Domains** once they have.
