# A purchase order is not booked ARR

## The rule

A Corvid Systems bookings, revenue or ARR report counts a new deal as booked only when a signed order form exists; a purchase order on its own, a verbal yes or an emailed acceptance is shown as not yet booked until payment is received.

At Corvid this means:

- Only new deals with a signed order form, or whose payment has been received, count in the report's booked figures, such as new ARR, bookings, revenue, ending ARR, year-to-date totals and counts of new customers or deals won.
- Every other new deal the report mentions is marked as not yet booked, and its amount stays out of those figures.

Apply it to every report you write or edit under `finance/` and `board/`, even when the report is
one step of a longer task.

## Why

A purchase order is the customer's internal approval to spend, not a contract. Until an order form
is signed there are no agreed services, price, term or payment terms, so Corvid cannot recognise
the revenue or count the ARR until the customer actually pays. Deals with only a PO or a spoken yes
slip or shrink often enough that counting them overstates growth to leadership and the board, and
taking a number back later costs more trust than reporting the deal as pending now.

## How to apply it

- Read each deal's paperwork, not its CRM stage or the rep's comment. Booked means a signed order
  form, or payment received. A deal with a signed order form and a PO is booked.
- Build every booked figure from the booked deals only: new ARR, bookings, ending ARR (opening
  plus new plus expansion, less churn), year-to-date totals and the number of new customers.
- List the other deals under a heading such as "Not yet booked", each with its amount and what it
  is waiting for. Keep them out of headlines and totals.
- Leave the paperwork that the deal table records as it is.

## Gotchas

- "PO received", "procurement approved", "confirmed on the call" and "accepted by email" are all
  not booked.
- A footnote does not fix a total that includes the deal: take the amount out of the total.
- The secondary figures count too: ending ARR, year to date, "four new customers", a list of wins.

## Examples

Compliant:

> New ARR booked in March: \$180k from two new customers, Alder Rail and Penhale Ports, both on
> signed order forms.
>
> Not yet booked: Corran Shipping (\$60k), purchase order received 27 March, order form not yet
> signed.

Violating:

> New ARR booked in March: \$240k from three new customers, including Corran Shipping (\$60k,
> purchase order received 27 March).

## Before you commit

Recompute each booked figure in the files you changed from the deals with a signed order form or a
payment, check that every other deal is marked as not yet booked, fix what does not match, and
check again.
