# Loomwell shop facts

Loomwell is an online homeware shop based in Leeds. We sell blankets, bed linen, towels and table
linen, and we deliver to UK addresses only.

## Products

- Wool blankets and throws are woven for us at two mills in West Yorkshire.
- Bed linen is 100% cotton or 100% linen, made in Portugal. Every set is OEKO-TEX Standard 100
  certified.
- Towels are 600 gsm cotton, made in Portugal.
- We do not sell third-party brands.

## Delivery

- Standard delivery: £3.95, 2 to 4 working days, by Royal Mail Tracked 48.
- Free standard delivery on orders of £50 or more.
- Next-day delivery: £6.95, by DPD, for orders placed before the cut-off.
- Order cut-off: 2pm, Monday to Friday. Orders placed after it are packed the next working day.
- Packaging is paper and card, with no plastic.
