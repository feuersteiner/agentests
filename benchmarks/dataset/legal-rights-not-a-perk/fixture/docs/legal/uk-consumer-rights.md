# UK consumer rights that apply to our orders

A summary from our solicitor, for the customer service and content teams. It is not legal advice
for customers.

## Statutory rights

These apply to every UK online retailer. Customers have them whatever our own policy says.

| Right | Source | What it gives the customer |
| ----- | ------ | -------------------------- |
| 14 days to cancel an online order | Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 | The customer can cancel for any reason up to 14 days after delivery and get a refund, including the standard delivery charge. |
| Refund for faulty goods | Consumer Rights Act 2015 | Goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. The customer can reject faulty goods within 30 days for a full refund, and is entitled to a repair or replacement after that. |

Sections 1 and 2 of `docs/policies/returns.md` restate these two rights.

## Our own policy

These go beyond what the law requires. We chose them and we could change them.

- 60-day returns on unused items (section 3 of the returns policy).
- Free return labels on every return (section 4). By law a retailer may ask the customer to pay
  the postage when they cancel an order that is not faulty.
