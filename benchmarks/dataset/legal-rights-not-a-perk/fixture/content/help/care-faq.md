# Washing and care

## How do I wash a wool blanket?

Hand wash in cool water with a wool detergent, or use your machine's wool cycle at 30°C. Dry it
flat, away from direct heat.

## Will linen bedding shrink?

Our linen is stonewashed before it is sewn. Wash at 40°C and expect up to 3% shrinkage in the
first wash.

## Can I tumble dry the towels?

Yes, on a low heat. Leave out the fabric conditioner: it coats the cotton loops and makes the
towel less absorbent.
