# Delivery

## Where do you deliver?

To UK addresses only, including the Highlands and Islands, Northern Ireland and the Channel
Islands.

## How much is delivery?

Standard delivery is £3.95 and takes 2 to 4 working days. It is free on orders of £50 or more.
Next-day delivery is £6.95.

## When will my order be sent?

Orders placed before 2pm, Monday to Friday, are packed the same day. Orders placed later are
packed the next working day.

## How do I track my order?

We email a tracking link when your order leaves the warehouse. Standard orders come by Royal Mail
Tracked 48 and next-day orders by DPD.

## Can I change my delivery address?

Yes, until the order is packed. Email hello@loomwell.example with your order number and the new
address.
