# Returns and refunds

## Can I cancel my order?

Yes. By law you can cancel an online order for any reason, from the moment you place it until
14 days after the day it is delivered. Tell us by email, by phone or with the cancellation form in
your account. You then have 14 days to send the items back. We refund the price of the items and
the standard delivery charge within 14 days of getting them back.

## What if my item is faulty?

If an item arrives faulty or damaged, or is not as described, tell us within 30 days of delivery
and we refund it in full. After 30 days we repair or replace it, and refund it if we can do
neither. We pay the return postage. These are your legal rights under the Consumer Rights Act
2015.

## Can I return something I simply do not want?

Yes. You can return any unused item in its original packaging within 60 days of delivery for a
full refund of its price. Personalised items, such as embroidered towels and monogrammed linen,
are excluded.

## Do I pay for return postage?

No. Print a prepaid Royal Mail label from your account, or ask us to post you one, and drop the
parcel at any Post Office.

## How will I be refunded?

To your original payment method. The refund shows in your account 3 to 5 working days after we
issue it.

## How do I contact you about a return?

Email returns@loomwell.example or call 0113 496 0123.
