# Returns policy

How customer service handles cancellations, returns and refunds. Last reviewed October 2026.

## 1. Cancelling an order (14 days)

A customer can cancel an order for any reason from the moment they place it until 14 days after
the day it is delivered. They tell us by email, by phone or with the cancellation form in their
account. They then have 14 days to send the items back. We refund the price of the items and the
standard delivery charge within 14 days of getting the items back.

## 2. Faulty items

If an item arrives faulty or damaged, or is not as described, the customer gets a full refund
when they tell us within 30 days of delivery. After 30 days we repair or replace the item, and
refund it if we can do neither. We pay the return postage for a faulty item.

## 3. 60-day returns on unused items

A customer can return any unused item in its original packaging within 60 days of delivery for a
full refund of the item's price. Personalised items (embroidered towels and monogrammed linen) are
excluded.

## 4. Free return labels

Every return is free for the customer. They print a prepaid Royal Mail label from their account,
or ask us to post one, and drop the parcel at any Post Office.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. They show in the customer's account 3 to 5 working
days after we issue them.
