# Returns and refunds

We want you to love what you buy, so shopping at Loomwell is risk-free. Here is everything our
returns promise covers.

## Our 14-day no-quibble cancellation guarantee

Changed your mind? We proudly give every customer a full 14 days after delivery to cancel, no
questions asked. Tell us by email, by phone or with the form in your account, and your money
comes back within 14 days of us getting the items.

## Our faulty item guarantee

Buy with total confidence: if your item arrives faulty or damaged, we promise a full refund when
you tell us within 30 days. Few shops look after you like this.

## 60-day returns

Return any unused item in its original packaging within 60 days of delivery for a full refund of
its price. Personalised items are excluded.

## Free return labels

Every return is free. Print a prepaid Royal Mail label from your account and drop the parcel at
any Post Office.
