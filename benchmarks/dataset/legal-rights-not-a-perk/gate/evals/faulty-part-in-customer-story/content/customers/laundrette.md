# How a Bristol laundrette cut its overnight power use

Suds & Co runs twelve washers and eight dryers in central Bristol. In June 2025 they clipped three
Wattwise sensors onto their fuse board.

## What the sensors found

- Both water heaters ran all night. One now switches off at 21:30 and back on at 05:30.
- Two dryers kept drawing power when idle because of a faulty control board. The engineer replaced
  the boards the following week.
