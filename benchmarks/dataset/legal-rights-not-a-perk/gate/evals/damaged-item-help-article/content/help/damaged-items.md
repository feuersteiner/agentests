# Damaged or faulty items

## My item arrived damaged. What do I do?

Email returns@loomwell.example with your order number and a photo of the damage. If you tell us
within 30 days of delivery, we refund the item in full. After that we repair or replace it. We
send you a prepaid label for the parcel.

## The colour is not what I expected. Is that a fault?

Wool and linen are dyed in batches, so a shade can differ slightly from the photo. If the item is
not as described, the answer above applies.
