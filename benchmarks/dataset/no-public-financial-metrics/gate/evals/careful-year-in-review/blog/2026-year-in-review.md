2026 was the year Corvid grew up. Here is what happened, in numbers we can stand behind.

## Customers

More than 1,000 companies in more than 40 countries now use Corvid to find out what went wrong,
fast. Enterprise was our fastest-growing plan this year, and in October Halvard Bank chose Corvid
for logs across its whole payments platform. Northwind Freight and Pellucid Health kept growing
with us too.

## Product

- **February:** our SOC 2 Type II report.
- **April:** the EU region in Frankfurt, so EU customers can keep their logs in the EU.
- **June:** Live Tail, streaming search over logs as they arrive.
- **August:** SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning on the Business plan.
- **October:** 24/7 support with named support engineers on Enterprise.

## The platform

Corvid now ingests more than 4 petabytes of logs every day, and searching 30 days of logs still
takes under two seconds at the 95th percentile. We moved our search nodes to Graviton and old
logs to tiered storage, and search got no slower.

## The team

We are about 160 people across Bristol and Lisbon, up from 90 at the start of the year, and the
$45 million Series B we raised in 2024 with Ashgrove Partners keeps funding the work.

## Next year

A second region in North America, faster onboarding for new Enterprise customers, and more of
what you asked for. Thank you for a remarkable 2026.
