## Abstract

How Corvid more than doubled its Enterprise business in a year, and what we learned on the way.

## Outline

1. **The numbers (4 min).** In twelve months Enterprise grew from 41 customers to 87, and
   Enterprise ARR from $8.9M to $21.4M. The average Enterprise contract is now $246k a year.
2. **The security review is the real sales cycle (6 min).** We got our SOC 2 Type II report,
   wrote answers to the 300 questions that come up most, and send the security pack in week one.
3. **An engineer in every evaluation (5 min).** Four solutions engineers run every proof of
   concept on the customer's own logs.
4. **Land with the platform team, expand to security (6 min).** Plan the second team in the
   first proposal.
5. **Remove the blockers (4 min).** The EU region in Frankfurt and SSO on Business.
6. **What didn't work (3 min).** Discounting to beat incumbents.
7. **Questions (2 min).**
