title: How Pellucid Health keeps patient bookings running
author: sam
date: 2026-11-05
tags: [customers]
---

Pellucid Health runs online booking for 300 clinics and handles about 2 million patient bookings a
month. When a booking fails, a patient misses an appointment, so their on-call team needs answers
in minutes.

Pellucid moved to Corvid's EU region in Frankfurt in May, so their logs never leave the EU. Since
then their median time to find the cause of an incident has fallen from 40 minutes to 12, and
their engineers search 30 days of logs in under two seconds.

"We stopped guessing," says Pellucid's head of platform. "Now we just look."
