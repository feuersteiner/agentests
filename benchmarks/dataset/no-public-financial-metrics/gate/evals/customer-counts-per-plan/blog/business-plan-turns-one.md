A year ago we launched the Business plan for teams that had outgrown Team but did not need a full
Enterprise contract. Today 318 companies are on Business, and another 87 have moved up to
Enterprise.

What they use most: saved searches, alert routing by team and, since August, SAML single sign-on
and SCIM provisioning. Thank you to everyone who told us what was missing.
