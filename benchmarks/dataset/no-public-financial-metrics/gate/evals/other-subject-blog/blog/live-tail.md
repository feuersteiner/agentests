title: Introducing Live Tail
author: tom
date: 2026-06-12
tags: [product, announcements]
---

Customers kept telling us the same thing: when production is on fire, waiting for an index to
refresh is too slow. Live Tail is our answer. Open any search, press Live, and new log lines that
match stream in as they arrive, usually within a second of being written.

Live Tail is available on every plan today. Filters, highlights and saved searches all work, and
you can pause the stream, scroll back and pick up where you left off.
