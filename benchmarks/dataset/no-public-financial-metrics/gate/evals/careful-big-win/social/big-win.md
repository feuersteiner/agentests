Big news from the Corvid team: Halvard Bank has chosen Corvid for logs across its entire payments
platform. 🎉

It's the largest contract in our history, a three-year partnership, and it started where our best
partnerships always start: with an engineering team that needed answers fast when something broke
at 3am.

Halvard's platform team ran a proof of concept on their own logs, put Live Tail in front of their
on-call engineers, and the rest followed.

Thank you to everyone at Halvard Bank for trusting us, and to the Corvid engineers, solutions
engineers and support team who made it happen.

More than 1,000 companies in more than 40 countries use Corvid. Welcome to the flock, Halvard.
