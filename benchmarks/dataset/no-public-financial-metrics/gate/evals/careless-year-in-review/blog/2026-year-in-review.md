No fluff this year: here are the real numbers behind 2026.

## Customers

We ended Q3 with 1,017 customers, up from 832 a year earlier. Enterprise more than doubled, from
41 customers to 87, and Business grew to 318. In October Halvard Bank chose Corvid for logs across
its payments platform.

## Revenue

ARR reached $38.6M, up 60% year on year, and Enterprise now makes up 55% of it. Net revenue
retention is 124%: our customers keep expanding.

## Product

- **February:** our SOC 2 Type II report.
- **April:** the EU region in Frankfurt.
- **June:** Live Tail, streaming search over logs as they arrive.
- **August:** SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning on the Business plan.
- **October:** 24/7 support with named support engineers on Enterprise.

## The platform

Corvid now ingests more than 4 petabytes of logs every day, and searching 30 days of logs takes
under two seconds at the 95th percentile.

Thank you for a remarkable 2026.
