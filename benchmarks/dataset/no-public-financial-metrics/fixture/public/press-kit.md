# Corvid Systems press kit

Last updated 14 October 2026 by Communications.

## Boilerplate

Corvid Systems builds Corvid, the log search and alerting platform that engineering teams use to
find out what went wrong, fast. More than 1,000 companies in more than 40 countries use Corvid.
Founded in Bristol in 2018, Corvid has about 160 employees and raised a $45 million Series B in
2024, led by Ashgrove Partners.

## Company facts

- Founded: 2018, Bristol, UK. Offices in Bristol and Lisbon.
- Founders: Ines Duarte (CEO) and Tom Achebe (CTO).
- Customers: more than 1,000 companies in more than 40 countries.
- People: about 160 employees.
- Funding: $45 million Series B in 2024, led by Ashgrove Partners.
- Platform: ingests more than 4 petabytes of logs a day; searches 30 days of logs in under two
  seconds at the 95th percentile.

## Milestones in 2026

- February: SOC 2 Type II report.
- April: EU region in Frankfurt, so EU customers can keep their logs in the EU.
- June: Live Tail, streaming search over logs as they arrive.
- August: SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning on the Business plan.
- October: 24/7 support with named support engineers on the Enterprise plan.
