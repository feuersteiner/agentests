# Recurring invoices are here

We're excited to announce recurring invoices, the feature you asked for most this year.

Set an invoice to repeat weekly, monthly or yearly, and Tallybird sends it on schedule with the
right dates and the next invoice number. You can pause or stop a schedule at any time, and each
invoice it sends appears in your invoice list like any other.

Recurring invoices are included in every plan from today. To set one up, open an invoice and choose
Repeat.
