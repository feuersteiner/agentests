# Refunds

Tallybird is billed monthly or yearly, in advance.

- Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. The plan runs until the end of the month paid for,
  and that month is not refunded.
- Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of the payment. After that, the remaining
  full months are refunded on request.
- Refunds go back to the card that paid, within five working days.

Write to billing@tallybird.example with your account email to ask for a refund.
