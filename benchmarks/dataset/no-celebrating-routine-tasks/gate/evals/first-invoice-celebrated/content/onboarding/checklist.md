# Onboarding checklist

In-app messages for the setup checklist on the dashboard.

## Add logo

- To do: Add your logo. It appears at the top of every invoice.
- Done: Logo added. It appears on new invoices from now on.

## Set tax rate

- To do: Set your default tax rate. You can change it on any single invoice.
- Done: Tax rate saved. New invoices use {rate}%.

## Send first invoice

- To do: Send your first invoice. Pick a client, add a line and press Send.
- Done: Congratulations on sending your first invoice! Invoice {number} is on its way to {client}.

## All steps done

Setup is complete. You can change your logo and tax rate at any time under Settings.
