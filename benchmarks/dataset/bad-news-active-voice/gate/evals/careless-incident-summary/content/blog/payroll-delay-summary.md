date: 2026-11-24

## Summary

On Thursday 19 November, a technical issue affected the processing of salary payments.
Approximately 1,240 payments for 212 employers were delayed by one day.

## What happened

Following a scheduled release at 11:20, an error occurred in the job responsible for submitting
payroll runs. As a result, runs due on 19 November were not submitted at 15:00. The issue was
identified at 17:30 and a fix was deployed at 18:10.

All affected payments were sent at 06:00 on Friday 20 November and were confirmed by 09:10. No
payments were lost or duplicated.

## Next steps

- Additional test coverage has been added.
- Monitoring will be introduced from 26 November so that similar issues are detected sooner.
- Bank charges or late-payment fees incurred as a result of the delay will be refunded. Claims can
  be sent to support@ledgerline.example until 18 December.
- Affected employers will receive a credit for November's subscription fee by 30 November.

We apologise for the disruption this caused.
