---
title: Service disruption on 3 March
date: 2027-03-04
---

On 3 March, Tallyhub was unavailable for some customers between 09:10 and 11:40. Invoices could
not be sent and bank feeds were delayed.

The disruption was caused by a configuration change. The change was rolled back and service was
restored at 11:40. Additional safeguards have been put in place.

We apologise for any inconvenience.
