title: Your payroll year-end checklist
date: 2026-12-01
author: Priya Nair, Product

The tax year ends on 5 April. Here's what to check before then.

- Give every leaver a leaving date, so their P45 goes out.
- Check your employees' tax codes against the latest ones from HMRC.
- Send your final submission for the year on or before your last pay day. HMRC can charge a
  penalty if it arrives late.

Ledgerline creates each employee's P60 for you after the final run, and your employees can download
them from the employee portal.
