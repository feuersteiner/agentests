---
title: Pension reports are here
date: 2026-10-28
author: Priya Nair, Product
---

From today, Ledgerline builds your workplace pension report for you after every payroll run.

Open **Reports > Pensions** to see what you and each employee paid in, by provider. You can
download the report as a CSV file for your pension provider, or connect your NEST account and we
send it for you.

Pension reports come with every plan at no extra cost. Tell us what you think at
feedback@ledgerline.example.
