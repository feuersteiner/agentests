# Driftlog opens an EU data region

**Lisbon, 6 October 2026.** Driftlog, the observability tool for web backends, today opened a
data region in Frankfurt. Customers can choose to store their logs and traces in the EU when they
create a workspace.

A workspace in the EU region keeps its logs, traces and alert history in Frankfurt. Account and
billing records stay in the US region. Existing workspaces can ask support to move; a move takes
about two working days and no data is lost.

"A third of the teams that talk to us are in Europe, and many of them asked where their data
would live," said Priya Raman, CTO of Driftlog. "Now they can pick."

The EU region is available on the Team and Business plans at no extra charge.

## About Driftlog

Driftlog is an observability tool for web backends, with logs, traces and alerts in one place.
