# Trace replay is here

Trace replay records a failed request and lets you step through it afterwards, span by span.
Driftlog is the first observability tool to bring replay to production traces.

At each span you see the timing, the request and response payloads, and the log lines written
while the span was open. Trace replay is included in the Team and Business plans.
