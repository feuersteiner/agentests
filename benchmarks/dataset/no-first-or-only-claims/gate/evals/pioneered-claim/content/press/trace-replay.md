# Driftlog launches trace replay

**Lisbon, 1 December 2026.** Driftlog today released trace replay, which records a failed
request and lets an engineer step through it afterwards, span by span.

Trace replay is included in the Team and Business plans.

## About Driftlog

Driftlog pioneered replay debugging for production traces. Its observability tool brings logs,
traces and alerts together in one place. The company is based in Lisbon.
