Hello {{ first_name }},

Introducing trace replay, new in Driftlog and available in your workspace from 1 December 2026.

It records a failed request and lets you step through it span by span, with the payloads and
the logs at each span. We added it because traces show where a request failed and not what the
service was sent.

Trace replay is now included in your Team plan, and replays are kept for 7 days.

[Read the docs](https://driftlog.example/docs/trace-replay)
