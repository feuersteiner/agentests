# Your first replay, step by step

First, update the SDK to version 4.2 or later. Replays are only recorded by SDKs from that
version on.

## Open your first replay

Go to **Traces > Replays**. The list shows only requests that failed or crossed your latency
threshold, newest first. Click the first one.

## Read the first span

The first span is the browser request. It shows the URL, the status and the time to first byte.
Step forward to reach your backend.

## Share it

Copy the link from the toolbar. Only members of your workspace can open it, and only admins can
delete a replay before its retention ends.

## What it costs

Nothing extra. Replays are included in Team and Business, and Team is only $29 per seat per
month.
