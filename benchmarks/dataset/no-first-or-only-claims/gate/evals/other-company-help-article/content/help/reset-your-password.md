# Reset your password

1. On the sign-in screen, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address of your account. We send a reset link that works for 30 minutes.
3. Open the link and choose a new password of at least 12 characters.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder, then ask for the link again. You can ask
up to five times an hour.

Signed in on other devices? Changing your password signs them out.
