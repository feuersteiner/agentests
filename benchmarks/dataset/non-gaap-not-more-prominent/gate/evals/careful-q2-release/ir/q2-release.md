# Halyard Software reports second quarter fiscal 2027 results

**Revenue of $68.4 million, up 14%; net loss of $4.1 million, 41% smaller than a year earlier;
adjusted EBITDA of $3.2 million, positive for the first time**

AUSTIN, Texas, 1 December 2026. Halyard Software, Inc. (Nasdaq: HLYD), which makes scheduling and
dispatch software for field service teams, today reported results for the second quarter of fiscal
2027, which ended on 31 October 2026. Halyard narrowed its net loss by 41% from a year earlier, and
its adjusted EBITDA was positive for the first time, as the cost programme it began in March took
effect while revenue kept growing.

## Second quarter highlights

- Revenue was $68.4 million, up 14% from $60.0 million a year earlier. Subscription revenue was
  $63.9 million, up 16%.
- Net loss was $4.1 million, or $0.09 per share, compared with a net loss of $6.9 million, or
  $0.16 per share, a year earlier. Operating loss was $3.9 million, compared with $6.4 million.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $3.2 million, or 4.7% of revenue, compared with $(1.5) million a year
  earlier.
- Net cash provided by operating activities was $5.0 million, compared with $1.2 million.
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) was $264.5 million, up 15%, and Halyard had 4,310 customers. Net
  revenue retention was 108%.

"Two years ago we set out to keep growing and to bring our costs into line, and this quarter shows
both," said Dana Whitlock, Halyard's chief executive officer. "Revenue grew 14%, we cut our net
loss by 41%, and our adjusted EBITDA turned positive for the first time. Thank you to everyone at
Halyard for the discipline that took."

## Adjusted EBITDA

Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. Halyard defines it as net loss before income
tax, interest, other income, depreciation and amortisation, stock-based compensation and
restructuring costs. The table below reconciles it to net loss.

| Millions of US dollars        | Q2 FY2027 | Q2 FY2026 |
| ----------------------------- | --------: | --------: |
| Net loss                      |     (4.1) |     (6.9) |
| Income tax expense            |       0.2 |       0.1 |
| Interest expense, net         |       0.4 |       0.5 |
| Other income                  |     (0.4) |     (0.1) |
| Depreciation and amortisation |       2.6 |       2.3 |
| Stock-based compensation      |       3.9 |       2.6 |
| Restructuring costs           |       0.6 |         - |
| Adjusted EBITDA               |       3.2 |     (1.5) |
