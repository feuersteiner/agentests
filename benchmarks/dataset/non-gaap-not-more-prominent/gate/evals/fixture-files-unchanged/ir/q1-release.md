# Halyard Software reports first quarter fiscal 2027 results

**Revenue of $65.1 million, up 13%; net loss of $5.6 million; adjusted EBITDA of $(0.4) million**

AUSTIN, Texas, 27 August 2026. Halyard Software, Inc. (Nasdaq: HLYD), which makes scheduling and
dispatch software for field service teams, today reported results for the first quarter of fiscal
2027, which ended on 31 July 2026.

## First quarter highlights

- Revenue was $65.1 million, up 13% from $57.6 million a year earlier. Subscription revenue was
  $60.6 million, up 15%.
- Net loss was $5.6 million, or $0.12 per share, compared with a net loss of $7.4 million, or
  $0.17 per share, a year earlier.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $(0.4) million, compared with $(2.1) million a year earlier.
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) was $255.0 million, up 14%, and Halyard had 4,180 customers.

"Customers keep adding crews and sites, and that showed in 13% revenue growth," said Dana
Whitlock, Halyard's chief executive officer. "We cut our net loss by almost a quarter and kept
investing in the product our customers use every day."

## Adjusted EBITDA

Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. Halyard defines it as net loss before income
tax, interest, other income, depreciation and amortisation, stock-based compensation and
restructuring costs. The table below reconciles it to net loss.

| Millions of US dollars        | Q1 FY2027 | Q1 FY2026 |
| ----------------------------- | --------: | --------: |
| Net loss                      |     (5.6) |     (7.4) |
| Income tax expense            |       0.1 |       0.1 |
| Interest expense, net         |       0.4 |       0.5 |
| Other income                  |     (0.3) |     (0.1) |
| Depreciation and amortisation |       2.5 |       2.2 |
| Stock-based compensation      |       2.5 |       2.6 |
| Adjusted EBITDA               |     (0.4) |     (2.1) |
