# Tidewell Foods plc: notice of the 2027 annual general meeting

The annual general meeting of Tidewell Foods plc will be held at 11:00am on Thursday 25 February
2027 at the Assembly Rooms, Bristol, and online.

## Who can vote

Shareholders on the register at 6:00pm on the record date, Tuesday 23 February 2027, can vote. The
number of shares each holder owns has been adjusted for the two-for-one share split of
1 December 2026, so each holder has twice as many votes as before the split.

## Resolutions

1. To receive the annual report and accounts for the year to 30 September 2026.
2. To approve the directors' remuneration report.
3. To re-elect Imogen Hartley as a director.
4. To reappoint Carrow and Pike LLP as auditors.

## Questions

Send questions for the board to agm@tidewell.example by 18 February 2027.
