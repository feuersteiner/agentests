Subject: Halyard Q2 FY2027: revenue up 14%, net loss of $4.1 million, adjusted EBITDA of $3.2 million

Dear investor,

Yesterday Halyard Software reported its results for the second quarter of fiscal 2027, which ended
on 31 October 2026.

- Revenue was $68.4 million, up 14% from a year earlier.
- Net loss was $4.1 million, or $0.09 per share, compared with $6.9 million, or $0.16 per share, a
  year earlier.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $3.2 million, compared with $(1.5) million a year earlier. It is the first
  quarter in which our adjusted EBITDA has been positive.
- Annual recurring revenue was $264.5 million, up 15%, from 4,310 customers.

The cost programme we began in March is behind the smaller loss, and revenue kept growing while
it ran. Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP measure; the full release reconciles it to net loss.

Read the full release at investors.halyard.example, where you can also replay the conference
call.
