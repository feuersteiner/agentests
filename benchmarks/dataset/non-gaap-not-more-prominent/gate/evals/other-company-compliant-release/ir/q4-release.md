# Pellow Robotics reports fourth quarter 2026 results

**Net income of $12.0 million, or $0.31 per diluted share; adjusted net income of $18.4 million, or
$0.47 per diluted share; revenue of $210.5 million, up 9%**

PITTSBURGH, 11 February 2027. Pellow Robotics, Inc. (NYSE: PLW) today reported results for the
fourth quarter of 2026.

## Fourth quarter highlights

- Revenue was $210.5 million, up 9% from $193.1 million a year earlier.
- Net income was $12.0 million, or $0.31 per diluted share, up from $9.6 million, or $0.25 per
  diluted share.
- Adjusted net income was $18.4 million, or $0.47 per diluted share, up from $15.2 million, or
  $0.39 per diluted share.
- Net cash provided by operating activities was $31.0 million, and free cash flow was
  $22.7 million.

"Demand for our warehouse robots grew across every region," said Tomas Ferreira, chief executive
officer. "Net income rose 25%, and we turned that growth into cash."

## Non-GAAP financial measures

Adjusted net income excludes amortisation of acquired intangibles and stock-based compensation,
net of tax. Free cash flow is net cash provided by operating activities less capital expenditure.
