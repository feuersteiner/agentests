# Halyard Software reports second quarter fiscal 2027 results

AUSTIN, Texas, 3 December 2026. Halyard Software, Inc. (Nasdaq: HLYD) today reported results for
the second quarter of fiscal 2027, which ended on 31 October 2026.

Revenue was $68.4 million, up 14% from $60.0 million a year earlier. Adjusted EBITDA was
$3.2 million, or 4.7% of revenue, compared with $(1.5) million a year earlier, the first positive
quarter in Halyard's history.

Annual recurring revenue was $264.5 million, up 15%, and Halyard had 4,310 customers. Net revenue
retention was 108%.

Gross margin was 71.1%. Operating loss was $3.9 million, and net loss was $4.1 million, or $0.09
per share, compared with a net loss of $6.9 million, or $0.16 per share, a year earlier.
