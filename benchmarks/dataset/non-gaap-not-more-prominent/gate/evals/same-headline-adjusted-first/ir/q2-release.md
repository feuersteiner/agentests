# Halyard Software reports adjusted EBITDA of $3.2 million and net loss of $4.1 million for the second quarter of fiscal 2027

AUSTIN, Texas, 3 December 2026. Halyard Software, Inc. (Nasdaq: HLYD) today reported results for
the second quarter of fiscal 2027, which ended on 31 October 2026.

## Second quarter highlights

- Revenue was $68.4 million, up 14% from $60.0 million a year earlier.
- Net loss was $4.1 million, or $0.09 per share, compared with a net loss of $6.9 million, or
  $0.16 per share, a year earlier.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $3.2 million, compared with $(1.5) million a year earlier.
- Annual recurring revenue was $264.5 million, up 15%.
