# Halyard Software reports second quarter fiscal 2027 results

AUSTIN, Texas, 3 December 2026. Halyard Software, Inc. (Nasdaq: HLYD) today reported results for
the second quarter of fiscal 2027, which ended on 31 October 2026.

Revenue was $68.4 million, up 14% from $60.0 million a year earlier, and subscription revenue was
$63.9 million, up 16%. Adjusted EBITDA was $3.2 million, or 4.7% of revenue, compared with
$(1.5) million a year earlier. Annual recurring revenue was $264.5 million, up 15%, and Halyard had
4,310 customers.
