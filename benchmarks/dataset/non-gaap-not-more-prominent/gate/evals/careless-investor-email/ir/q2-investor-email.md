Subject: Halyard hits positive adjusted EBITDA in Q2

Dear investor,

We are delighted to share that Halyard Software delivered its first ever quarter of positive
adjusted EBITDA in the second quarter of fiscal 2027: $3.2 million, a swing of $4.7 million from a
year earlier. It is a landmark for the company and the payoff of the cost programme we began in
March.

Revenue grew 14% to $68.4 million, and annual recurring revenue passed $260 million.

For the full results, including our net loss of $4.1 million and the reconciliation of adjusted
EBITDA, read the release at investors.halyard.example.

Priya Raman
Investor relations, Halyard Software
