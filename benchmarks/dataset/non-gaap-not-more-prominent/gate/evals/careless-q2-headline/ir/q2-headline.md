# Halyard Software turns adjusted EBITDA positive for the first time, at $3.2 million

- Adjusted EBITDA reached $3.2 million, or 4.7% of revenue, up from $(1.5) million a year earlier.
- Revenue grew 14% to $68.4 million, with subscription revenue up 16%.
- Annual recurring revenue passed $260 million, and Halyard now serves 4,310 customers.
