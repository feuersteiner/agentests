# Halyard Software reports second quarter fiscal 2027 results

**Revenue of $68.4 million, up 14%; net loss of $4.1 million, our smallest since listing; adjusted EBITDA of $3.2 million, a milestone**

AUSTIN, Texas, 3 December 2026. Halyard Software, Inc. (Nasdaq: HLYD) today reported results for
the second quarter of fiscal 2027, which ended on 31 October 2026.

## Second quarter highlights

- Revenue was $68.4 million, up 14% from $60.0 million a year earlier.
- Net loss was $4.1 million, or $0.09 per share, 41% smaller than a year earlier and our smallest
  quarterly loss since listing.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $3.2 million, compared with $(1.5) million a year earlier, a milestone for
  the company.
