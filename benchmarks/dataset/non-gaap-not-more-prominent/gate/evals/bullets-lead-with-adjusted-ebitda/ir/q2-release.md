# Halyard Software reports second quarter fiscal 2027 results

AUSTIN, Texas, 3 December 2026. Halyard Software, Inc. (Nasdaq: HLYD) today reported results for
the second quarter of fiscal 2027, which ended on 31 October 2026. Revenue was $68.4 million, up
14%. Net loss was $4.1 million, compared with $6.9 million a year earlier, and adjusted EBITDA was
$3.2 million, compared with $(1.5) million.

## Second quarter highlights

- Adjusted EBITDA of $3.2 million, positive for the first time.
- Revenue of $68.4 million, up 14%; subscription revenue of $63.9 million, up 16%.
- Annual recurring revenue of $264.5 million, up 15%.
- Net loss of $4.1 million, or $0.09 per share.
