# Halyard Software delivers its first quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA

**Adjusted EBITDA of $3.2 million marks a milestone; revenue grows 14% to $68.4 million**

AUSTIN, Texas, 1 December 2026. Halyard Software, Inc. (Nasdaq: HLYD), which makes scheduling and
dispatch software for field service teams, today reported results for the second quarter of fiscal
2027, which ended on 31 October 2026. For the first time in its history, Halyard's adjusted EBITDA
was positive, a milestone that reflects the discipline of the cost programme the company began in
March.

## Second quarter highlights

- Adjusted EBITDA was a record $3.2 million, or 4.7% of revenue, compared with $(1.5) million a
  year earlier.
- Revenue was $68.4 million, up 14% from $60.0 million a year earlier. Subscription revenue was
  $63.9 million, up 16%.
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) passed $260 million, reaching $264.5 million, up 15%, and Halyard
  had 4,310 customers.
- Net revenue retention was 108%.

"This is the quarter we have worked towards for two years," said Dana Whitlock, Halyard's chief
executive officer. "Positive adjusted EBITDA is a milestone for Halyard, and it is the result of
real discipline across the whole team. We got there while still growing revenue 14%."

## Other financial results

Gross margin was 71.1%, compared with 69.0% a year earlier. Net loss was $4.1 million, or $0.09
per share, compared with a net loss of $6.9 million, or $0.16 per share, a year earlier. Net cash
provided by operating activities was $5.0 million.

## Adjusted EBITDA

Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. Halyard defines it as net loss before income
tax, interest, other income, depreciation and amortisation, stock-based compensation and
restructuring costs. The table below reconciles it to net loss.

| Millions of US dollars        | Q2 FY2027 | Q2 FY2026 |
| ----------------------------- | --------: | --------: |
| Net loss                      |     (4.1) |     (6.9) |
| Income tax expense            |       0.2 |       0.1 |
| Interest expense, net         |       0.4 |       0.5 |
| Other income                  |     (0.4) |     (0.1) |
| Depreciation and amortisation |       2.6 |       2.3 |
| Stock-based compensation      |       3.9 |       2.6 |
| Restructuring costs           |       0.6 |         - |
| Adjusted EBITDA               |       3.2 |     (1.5) |
