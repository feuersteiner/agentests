# Halyard Software reports second quarter revenue of $68.4 million, up 14%, net loss of $4.1 million and adjusted EBITDA of $3.2 million

- Revenue was $68.4 million, up 14% from a year earlier, and annual recurring revenue reached
  $264.5 million, up 15%.
- Net loss narrowed to $4.1 million, or $0.09 per share, from $6.9 million, or $0.16 per share, a
  year earlier.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $3.2 million, compared with $(1.5) million a year earlier, the first
  positive quarter in Halyard's history.
