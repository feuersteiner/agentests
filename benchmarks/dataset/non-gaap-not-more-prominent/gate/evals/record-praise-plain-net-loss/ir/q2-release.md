# Halyard Software reports net loss of $4.1 million and record adjusted EBITDA of $3.2 million

AUSTIN, Texas, 3 December 2026. Halyard Software, Inc. (Nasdaq: HLYD) today reported results for
the second quarter of fiscal 2027, which ended on 31 October 2026.

## Second quarter highlights

- Revenue was $68.4 million, up 14% from $60.0 million a year earlier.
- Net loss was $4.1 million, or $0.09 per share.
- Adjusted EBITDA was an exceptional $3.2 million, a milestone for the company and the strongest
  result in Halyard's history.
