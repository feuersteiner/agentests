# Q2 FY2027 board summary

## Headlines

- Adjusted EBITDA of $3.2 million, our first positive quarter.
- Revenue of $68.4 million, up 14% year over year.
- ARR of $264.5 million, up 15%; 4,310 customers; net revenue retention of 108%.

## Commentary

The cost programme started in March has done what it set out to do: adjusted EBITDA moved from
$(1.5) million a year ago to $3.2 million, and the last severance payments fell in this quarter.
Revenue growth held at 14% throughout.
