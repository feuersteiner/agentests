# Privacy policy

Ferngate Analytics Ltd ("Ferngate") provides dashboards for retail teams. This policy explains how
Ferngate handles the personal data of its customers and website visitors.

## What we collect

We collect your name, work email address and company when you create a Ferngate account, billing
details when you subscribe, and usage data when you use the dashboards.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal data for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this policy, and for
as long as required by law. Invoices are kept for 6 years.

## Contact

Write to privacy@ferngate.example with any questions.
