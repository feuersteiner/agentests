# How long do you keep my data?

We keep your personal data only for as long as we need it to provide Chalkline to you and to meet
our legal, tax and accounting obligations. When we no longer need it, we delete it or anonymise it.

If you would like us to delete your data sooner, you can close your account in Settings or write to
privacy@chalkline.example. For more detail, see our [privacy notice](/privacy).
