# How long does Boxwell keep my files?

- Files in your Boxwell drive: until you delete them, plus 90 days in the Bin.
- Your Boxwell account details: 1 year after you close your account.
- Invoices: 6 years, because tax law requires it.
- Support tickets: 5 years after the ticket is opened.
- Usage analytics: 26 months.
