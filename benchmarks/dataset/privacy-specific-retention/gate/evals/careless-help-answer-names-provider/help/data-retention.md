# How long do you keep my data?

Chalkline stores your boards and account details with Amazon Web Services in London, and our
support conversations live in Zendesk. Both keep your data only for as long as Chalkline needs it
to run the service, and for as long as the law requires after that.

When you close your account, we start removing your data from these services. If you want a copy
of your data first, export your boards from Settings.
