# Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal data Chalkline Ltd ("Chalkline", "we") collects when you use the
Chalkline web app at app.chalkline.example or visit chalkline.example, what we use it for, who else
receives it, how long we keep it and what your rights are. Our apps for iOS and Android have their
own notice.

## Who we are

Chalkline Ltd makes Chalkline, an online whiteboard for remote teams. We are registered in England
and Wales, at 14 Wapping Road, Bristol BS1 4RW, and we decide what personal data is collected
through the web app and this website and what it is used for. You can reach our privacy team at
privacy@chalkline.example.

## What we collect and why

### Your account

When you sign up, you give us your name, your email address and a password; we store a hash of the
password, never the password itself. Your workspace holds your boards and everything on them:
sticky notes, drawings, images and comments. We use this account data to sign you in, to show your
boards to the people you share them with, and to send you emails about your account, such as
password resets.

### Billing

If your workspace is on a paid plan, its owner gives us a billing contact, a company name and, for
companies in the EU, a VAT number. We use them to issue invoices and to keep a record of each
payment.

### Support

When you write to support@chalkline.example, your messages and our replies are kept together as a
support ticket, so that anyone on the support team can pick up the conversation.

### Product analytics

The web app records product analytics events, such as "board created" or "timer started", with your
account ID and the date and time. We use them to see which features are used and where people get
stuck. We do not use them for advertising.

## Who receives your data

- Our hosting provider stores all account and board data in its data centre in London.
- Our email delivery provider receives your name and email address to send our emails.
- Our accounting software provider holds our invoices and payment records.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

## How long we keep your data

We keep your personal data only for as long as we need it to provide Chalkline, for the purposes
described in this notice and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. After that, we delete it
or anonymise it.

## Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to
restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. To use any of these
rights, email privacy@chalkline.example; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the
Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

## Changes to this notice

When we change this notice we update the date at the top. If a change affects data you have already
given us, we email every workspace owner before the change takes effect.
