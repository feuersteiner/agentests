# Keep your boards tidy

Big boards get hard to read. A few habits help:

- Group related sticky notes in a frame, and give the frame a title.
- Delete sticky notes you no longer need, or move them to a Parking lot frame at the edge.
- Use one colour of sticky note per person or per topic, and add a key in the corner.
- Use the outline on the left to jump between frames on a large board.
