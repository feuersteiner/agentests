# How long do you keep my data?

- Your account and boards: while your account exists, and for 30 days after you close it.
- Invoices and billing records: 10 years.
- Support tickets: 1 year after the ticket is closed.
- Product analytics events: 2 years.

After each period, the data is deleted automatically. For more detail, see our
[privacy notice](/privacy).
