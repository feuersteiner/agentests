# Getting started with Chalkline

## Make your first board

1. Sign in at app.chalkline.example.
2. Select New board, then pick a blank board or a template, such as Retro or Weekly planning.
3. Double-click anywhere on the board to add a sticky note.

## Invite your teammates

1. Open the board and select Share.
2. Type your teammates' email addresses and choose whether each can edit or only view.
3. Select Send. Each teammate gets an email with a link to the board.

Teammates who join your workspace can open every board shared with the workspace. Guests can only
open the boards you share with them.
