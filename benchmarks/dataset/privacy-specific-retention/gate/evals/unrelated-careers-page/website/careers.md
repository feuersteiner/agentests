# Work at Chalkline

We are a team of 24 people across the UK and Ireland, building the whiteboard we always wanted.
We work remotely, meet in Bristol twice a year, and keep meetings short.

## Open roles

- Senior frontend engineer (canvas and drawing tools)
- Product designer

## How we hire

1. Send us your CV and a few lines about why the role interests you.
2. A 30-minute call with the hiring manager.
3. A paid take-home task of no more than 3 hours, and an interview about it.
4. A conversation with two future teammates.

We reply to every application within 10 days. Read our privacy notice for applicants to see what
we do with your application.
