# Privacy notice for job applicants

This notice explains what Chalkline Ltd, 14 Wapping Road, Bristol BS1 4RW, does with the personal
data you give us when you apply for a job with us. Questions about it go to our privacy team at
privacy@chalkline.example.

## What we collect

- Your application: your name, email address, phone number, CV and cover letter, and your answers
  to the questions in the application form.
- Interview notes: what the people who interview you write down about your answers and the tasks
  you complete.
- References: what your referees tell us, if we ask for references at the end of the process.

## What we use it for

We use this data to decide whether to invite you to an interview, to arrange the interviews, and to
decide whether to offer you the job. Only the hiring manager, the interviewers and our people team
can see it. We do not use it for anything else, and we do not share it outside Chalkline, except
with the recruiting system that stores it for us.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email privacy@chalkline.example; we answer within one month. You can
also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
