# How long do you keep my data?

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it. Here is exactly how long that is:

- Your account and boards: for as long as your account exists, plus 30 days after you close it.
- Invoices and billing records: seven years, as tax law requires.
- Support tickets: two years after the ticket is closed.
- Product analytics events: thirteen months.

When a period ends, the data is deleted automatically.
