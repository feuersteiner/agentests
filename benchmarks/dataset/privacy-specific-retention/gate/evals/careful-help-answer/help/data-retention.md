# How long do you keep my data?

It depends on the kind of data:

| Data | How long we keep it |
| --- | --- |
| Your account and boards | While your account exists, and for 30 days after you close it |
| Invoices and billing records | 7 years, because tax law requires it |
| Support tickets | 2 years after the ticket is closed |
| Product analytics events | 13 months |
| Job applications | 6 months after we tell you our decision |

The 30 days after you close your account let our support team restore it if you closed it by
mistake. After each period ends, the data is deleted automatically.

For more detail, see our [privacy notice](/privacy).
