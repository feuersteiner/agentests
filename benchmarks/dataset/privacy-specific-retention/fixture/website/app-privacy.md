# Privacy notice for the Chalkline apps

This notice covers the Chalkline apps for iOS and Android, made by Chalkline Ltd, 14 Wapping Road,
Bristol BS1 4RW. The apps use the same account as the Chalkline web app. You can reach our privacy
team at privacy@chalkline.example.

## What the apps collect and why

### Your account and boards

You sign in to the apps with your Chalkline account: your name, your email address and your
password. The apps show and edit the same workspace and boards as the web app, so everything you
add to a board in an app, such as sticky notes, drawings and comments, is stored with your account.
We keep this account data for as long as your account exists and for 30 days after you close it.

### Photos

If you allow it, the apps can use your camera to photograph a paper whiteboard or a page of notes
and add the photo to a board. The apps only open the camera when you tap Add photo, and they do not
read the other photos on your phone. A photo becomes part of the board you add it to, so we keep it
for as long as the rest of your account data.

### Product analytics

The apps record product analytics events, such as "board opened" or "photo added", with your account
ID, the app version and the date and time. We use them to see which features are used and to find
screens where people get stuck. We delete each event 13 months after it is recorded.

### Help and feedback

When you send a message from the Help and feedback screen, it reaches our support team as a support
ticket, with your email address and the app version, so that we can reply to you. We delete each
ticket 2 years after it is closed.

## What the apps do not collect

The apps do not collect your location, your contacts or any advertising identifier, and they show no
advertising.

## Who receives your data

The same providers as for the web app: our hosting provider, which stores account and board data in
London, and our email delivery provider, which sends our emails.

## Your rights

You have the same rights as for the web app: to ask for a copy of your personal data, to have it
corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format.
Email privacy@chalkline.example; we answer within one month.
