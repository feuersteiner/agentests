# Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal data Chalkline Ltd ("Chalkline", "we") collects when you use the
Chalkline web app at app.chalkline.example or visit chalkline.example, what we use it for, who else
receives it, how long we keep it and what your rights are. Our apps for iOS and Android have their
own notice.

## Who we are

Chalkline Ltd makes Chalkline, an online whiteboard for remote teams. We are registered in England
and Wales, at 14 Wapping Road, Bristol BS1 4RW, and we decide what personal data is collected
through the web app and this website and what it is used for. You can reach our privacy team at
privacy@chalkline.example.

## What we collect and why

### Your account

When you sign up, you give us your name, your email address and a password; we store a hash of the
password, never the password itself. Your workspace holds your boards and everything on them:
sticky notes, drawings, images and comments. We use this account data to sign you in, to show your
boards to the people you share them with, and to send you emails about your account, such as
password resets.

### Billing

If your workspace is on a paid plan, its owner gives us a billing contact, a company name and, for
companies in the EU, a VAT number. We use them to issue invoices and to keep a record of each
payment.

### Support

When you write to support@chalkline.example, your messages and our replies are kept together as a
support ticket, so that anyone on the support team can pick up the conversation.

### Product analytics

The web app records product analytics events, such as "board created" or "timer started", with your
account ID and the date and time. We use them to see which features are used and where people get
stuck. We do not use them for advertising.

## Who receives your data

- Our hosting provider stores all account and board data in its data centre in London.
- Our email delivery provider receives your name and email address to send our emails.
- Our accounting software provider holds our invoices and payment records.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

## How long we keep your data

We keep each kind of personal data for the period set out below. The periods come from our internal
retention schedule, which our privacy team reviews every June. For each kind of data we explain what
it includes, how long we keep it, why we keep it for that long, and how it is deleted at the end.

### Account data

**What it includes.** Your name, your email address, the hash of your password, your workspace and
every board in it, with its sticky notes, drawings, images and comments.

**How long.** For as long as your account exists, and for 30 days after it is closed. Your account
is closed when you delete it in Settings, or when the owner of your only workspace removes you from
it.

**Why.** People sometimes close an account by mistake. For 30 days after closing, our support team
can restore the account in full, with every board.

**How it is deleted.** When the 30 days are over, our nightly clean-up job deletes the account and
every board in its workspace from our database.

### Invoices and billing records

**What it includes.** Each invoice, with the billing contact, the company name, the VAT number, the
plan and the amount, and the record of each payment.

**How long.** 7 years.

**Why.** Accounting and tax law require us to keep invoices and payment records for 7 years, and
HM Revenue & Customs can ask to see them at any time during that period. Closing your account does
not shorten it: we keep the invoices of a closed workspace for the full 7 years.

**How it is deleted.** Our accounting software deletes each invoice and payment record when it
reaches 7 years old.

### Support tickets

**What it includes.** The messages you send to our support team, our replies, and any screenshots or
files you attach.

**How long.** 2 years after the ticket is closed. A ticket is closed when our support team marks it
as solved.

**Why.** When you write to us again about the same problem, the earlier conversation helps us solve
it faster, and most problems that come back do so within two years.

**How it is deleted.** Our help desk software deletes each ticket, with its attachments, 2 years
after it was closed.

### Product analytics events

**What it includes.** Each event the web app records, with your account ID, the feature and the date
and time.

**How long.** 13 months from the day the event is recorded.

**Why.** Thirteen months lets us compare each month's use of a feature with the same month a year
earlier.

**How it is deleted.** Every night, our analytics database deletes the events that have become older
than 13 months. The events are not copied to any other system.

## Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to
restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. To use any of these
rights, email privacy@chalkline.example; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the
Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

## Changes to this notice

When we change this notice we update the date at the top. If a change affects data you have already
given us, we email every workspace owner before the change takes effect.
