# Think together, wherever you are

Chalkline is an online whiteboard for remote teams. Sketch a plan, run a retro or map a customer
journey on one shared board, and watch your teammates' cursors move as they work.

## What teams use it for

- Planning sessions and sprint retros
- Workshops with sticky notes, voting and timers
- Diagrams, flows and wireframes

## Plans

Chalkline is free for up to three boards. The Team and Business plans add unlimited boards, guest
access and admin controls.
