# Send an invoice

Ledgerline sends invoices by email as a PDF file with a payment link.

1. Select **Invoices**, then **New invoice**.
2. Choose the client. Ledgerline fills in their billing address and payment terms.
3. Add a line for each product or service, with its quantity and price.
4. Select **Send**.

The client receives the invoice within a few minutes. You can see whether they opened it in the
invoice's **History** tab. To send a reminder, open the invoice and select **Remind client**.
