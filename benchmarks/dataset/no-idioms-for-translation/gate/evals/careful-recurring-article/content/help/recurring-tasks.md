# Recurring tasks

A recurring task repeats on a schedule. When you mark it as done, Orbitly creates the next one.
Recurring tasks are available on all plans.

## Make a task repeat

1. Open the task and select its **Due date** field.
2. Select **Repeat**.
3. Choose how often the task repeats:
   - **Daily**
   - **Weekly**, on the days you choose
   - **Monthly**, on a day of the month or on a day such as the second Tuesday
   - **Yearly**
4. Choose when the series ends: never, after a number of times, or on a date.
5. Select **Save**.

## What happens when you mark it as done

Orbitly creates the next task with the next due date and the same assignee. The checklist starts
again with every item cleared. Comments are not copied to the new task.

## Public holidays

Workspace admins can add the company's public holidays in **Settings > Calendar**. When a
recurring task would fall on one of these days, Orbitly moves it to the next working day.

## Change or delete a recurring task

When you edit or delete a recurring task, Orbitly asks whether the change applies to this task
only or to this task and all future tasks.
