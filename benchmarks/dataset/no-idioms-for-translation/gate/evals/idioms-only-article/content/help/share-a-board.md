# Share a board

Sharing a board keeps everyone in the loop and on the same page.

1. Open the board and select **Share**.
2. Enter the email addresses of the people you want to add.
3. Select **Send invitations**.

As a rule of thumb, share a board with everyone who needs to keep tabs on its tasks. People you
add can see and edit every task, so think twice before you add someone from outside the project.
