# Invite teammates

You need the Admin or Member role to invite people to a board.

## Invite a member of your organization

1. Open the board and select **Share**.
2. Enter one or more email addresses.
3. Select **Send invitations**.

Each invitation is valid for 7 days. Members can see and edit every task on the board.

## Invite a guest

Guests are people outside your organization, such as clients or contractors.

1. Open the board and select **Share**.
2. Select **Invite a guest** and enter the guest's email address.
3. Select **Send invitation**.

Guests see only the boards you share with them, and they cannot create boards. Guest access is
available on the Team and Business plans.
