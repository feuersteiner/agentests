# Flightline plans

Flightline is sold per seat, on annual contracts billed yearly in advance. A seat is one engineer
who uses Flightline. Prices from 1 January 2027.

| Plan       | Price per seat per year | Minimum seats |
| ---------- | ----------------------: | ------------: |
| Team       |                    $240 |            10 |
| Business   |                    $420 |            50 |
| Enterprise |                    $600 |           200 |

## What each plan includes

All plans include unlimited pipelines, Corvid-hosted runners within the fair-use limit, and
self-hosted runners.

- **Team**: email support in business hours, first response within 1 business day.
- **Business**: everything in Team, plus single sign-on, deployment approvals, email and chat
  support in business hours with a first response within 4 business hours for urgent issues, and a
  named customer success manager.
- **Enterprise**: everything in Business, plus audit log export, email, chat and phone support
  around the clock with a first response within 1 hour for urgent issues, and a named customer
  success manager.

## Training

Every user on every plan can take the self-paced courses in Flightline Academy at no extra cost:
Flightline basics, Writing pipelines, Release management and Administering Flightline.
