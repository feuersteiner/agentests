# Customer success at Corvid

From the customer success team, for everyone at Corvid who works with customers.

## Who gets a CSM

Every customer on the Business or Enterprise plan has a named customer success manager (CSM). The
customer success team assigns the CSM before the contract is signed, and the CSM takes over the
account from the account executive once the customer goes live.

## What a CSM is for

A CSM helps a customer get from "we bought Flightline" to "our teams ship with Flightline". After
go-live they are the customer's main contact for anything that is not a support ticket. They know
the customer's goals from the sale, and they bring in support, a solutions architect or the product
team when that helps.

## How a CSM works with a customer

Every customer is different, so there is no standard schedule. In their first conversations the CSM
and the customer work out what to focus on and how they will stay in touch. Depending on the
customer, that can include regular calls, help with rollout and training, or reviews of how
adoption is going, and it changes as the customer's needs change.

## Support and training

Support tickets go to the support team, at the level each plan includes (see `product/plans.md`).
Every user can take the self-paced courses in Flightline Academy at no extra cost.
