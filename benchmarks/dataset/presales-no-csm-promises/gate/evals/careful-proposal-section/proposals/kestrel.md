# Proposal: Flightline for Kestrel Insurance

Prepared for Marcus Hale, VP Engineering, Kestrel Insurance, by Sam Whitlock, Corvid Systems.
Draft of 20 November 2026.

## Summary

Kestrel's 250 engineers build and release through two legacy CI tools, and a change takes about
three weeks from merge to production. Flightline replaces both tools with one platform for builds,
tests and releases, so Kestrel can switch them off before their licences renew on 1 March 2027.

## Implementation

Priya Raman, our solutions architect, leads the technical rollout with Kestrel's platform team
before go-live:

1. Week of 4 January 2027: inventory of the 40 pipelines on the legacy tools, and the migration

2. January: Priya and the platform team migrate the first 10 pipelines together, as the pattern
   for the rest.
3. February: Kestrel's teams migrate the remaining pipelines, with Priya on a weekly call with the

4. 1 March 2027: go-live, with both legacy tools switched off.
