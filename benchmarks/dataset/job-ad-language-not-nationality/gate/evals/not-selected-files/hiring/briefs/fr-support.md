# Brief: Support Specialist, French-speaking customers

From Aoife Brennan, Support Lead, 18 November 2026.

## Why we're hiring

We now have 140 customers in France, Belgium and Switzerland, and French tickets wait nine hours
for a first reply on average, because Margaux is the only person on the team who answers them. The
new specialist takes the French queue with her and joins the English rota on Fridays.

## The job

- Answer tickets in French and English in Zendesk, by email and chat, 09:00 to 17:30 CET.
- Run screen-share sessions to fix connector set-ups with customers' IT teams.
- Read logs and run simple SQL to find out why data from a machine or ERP stopped arriving.
- Write help-centre articles in French, and translate the most-read English ones.
- Report bugs to engineering in Linear, in English.

## Who I want

- Two or more years in technical support for a software product.
- Comfortable with logs, APIs and basic SQL. Manufacturing experience is a bonus.
- French native speaker, please. Customers in Paris and Lyon notice straight away.
- Good written English: the product team and all our tools work in English.

## Package

- €40,000 to €46,000 a year, depending on experience.
- Remote anywhere in the EU, with a team week in Leeds twice a year.
- 28 days of holiday, a €1,000 home-office budget, share options.
- Reports to me.
