# Account Executive, DACH

Munich or remote in Germany · Sales team · €85,000 to €100,000 base, €170,000 to €200,000 OTE

## About Larkspur Data

Larkspur Data connects the machines, ERP and quality systems of mid-sized manufacturers, so that a
plant manager sees scrap, downtime and open orders in one place. We are 85 people in Leeds and
Munich, and 260 factories in the UK, Germany, Austria, Switzerland and France run on our platform.
Our working language is English.

## The role

Germany, Austria and Switzerland brought in 38% of our new business this year. As our second
account executive for DACH, you will own new business with Mittelstand manufacturers: family-owned
companies with 200 to 2,000 people, mostly in Bavaria, Baden-Württemberg and North Rhine-Westphalia.
You will report to Henrik Albers, Head of Sales EMEA.

## What you'll do

- Own new business with Mittelstand manufacturers in Germany, Austria and German-speaking
  Switzerland, from the first meeting to the signed contract.
- Work with our SDR on prospecting and with a solutions engineer on demos and pilots.
- Run pilots of six to eight weeks and negotiate with plant managers, IT and procurement.
- Carry a quota of €900,000 in new annual recurring revenue.
- Travel about 30% of the time, to customer sites and trade fairs such as Hannover Messe and SPS.

## What you bring

- Four or more years closing B2B software deals, ideally with manufacturers.
- Six-figure deals closed through a pilot and procurement.
- Fluent German (C1 or above): you will run discovery calls, pilots and contract negotiations
  in German, and you know how Mittelstand companies buy.
- Good working English: deal reviews, Salesforce notes and our internal meetings are in English.

## What we offer

- Base salary of €85,000 to €100,000, with on-target earnings of €170,000 to €200,000 and
  uncapped commission.
- Munich office, or remote in Germany with two days a month in Munich. You need the right to
  work in Germany.
- A company car or a BahnCard 100, 30 days of holiday and share options.
