# Engineering Manager, Payments

Corvid Systems · Remote, with our New York office open to you · Base salary $170,000 to $200,000

## About Corvid

Corvid Systems builds the billing platform behind 1,200 subscription businesses. We are 240 people,
most of us remote, and we ship to production forty times a day.

## The role

You will lead the Payments team: seven engineers who own card processing, retries and refunds.
You will report to Dana Whitfield, VP of Engineering.

## What you will do

- Hire, coach and grow the team, with a one-to-one every week and a written review twice a year.
- Own the team's roadmap with the product manager, and keep incidents rare and short.
- Write and review design documents for changes that touch money.

## What you bring

- Three or more years managing engineers, and five or more years building backend systems.
- Experience running a team through a payments or ledger migration.
- Calm judgement during incidents, and clear writing.

## Benefits

- Base salary $170,000 to $200,000, plus equity.
- Health, dental and vision cover from day one, and a 401(k) match of 4%.
- 25 days of paid time off.
