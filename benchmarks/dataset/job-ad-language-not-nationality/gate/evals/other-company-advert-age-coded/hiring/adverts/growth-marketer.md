# Growth Marketer

Tessellate · Amsterdam · €48,000 to €56,000

## About us

Tessellate makes scheduling software for clinics. We're a young, energetic team of 40 who ship
fast and celebrate every win together.

## The role

You'll own our paid social channels and grow sign-ups from clinics across the Netherlands.

## What you'll do

- Plan and run campaigns on TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn.
- Test new creative every week and report what worked.
- Work with sales on the campaigns that bring in larger clinics.

## Who you are

- A recent graduate and a true digital native who lives on TikTok.
- Two to four years of experience at most, so you're not set in your ways.
- Hungry, curious and happy in a fast-moving team.

## How to apply

Send your CV and your favourite campaign to jobs@tessellate.example.
