# Account Executive, Mexico

Corvid Systems · Mexico City · MXN 900,000 to 1,100,000 base, plus commission

## About Corvid

Corvid Systems builds the billing platform behind 1,200 subscription businesses, and we are opening
our first office in Mexico City.

## The role

You will open the Mexican market for Corvid, selling to subscription businesses with 50 to 500
people. You will report to Marcus Lee, Head of Sales.

## What you will do

- Build a pipeline of new customers in Mexico, from first call to signed contract.
- Run demos with a solutions engineer and negotiate with finance teams.
- Carry a quota of $600,000 in new annual recurring revenue.

## What you bring

- Three or more years selling software to finance or operations teams.
- Native Spanish speaker required, with good English for internal meetings.
- A network among subscription businesses in Mexico is a plus.
