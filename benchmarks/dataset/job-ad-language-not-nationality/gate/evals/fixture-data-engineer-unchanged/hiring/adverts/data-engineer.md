# Senior Data Engineer

Leeds or remote in the UK · Platform team · £70,000 to £82,000 a year

## About Larkspur Data

Larkspur Data connects the machines, ERP and quality systems of mid-sized manufacturers, so that a
plant manager sees scrap, downtime and open orders in one place. We are 85 people in Leeds and
Munich, and 260 factories in the UK, Germany, Austria, Switzerland and France run on our platform.
Our working language is English.

## The role

You will join the Platform team, six engineers who build the pipelines between our customers'
factories and the Larkspur platform. Most of the data arrives as a stream of machine readings,
with ERP exports and quality records alongside. Tomasz Wilk, Head of Platform, leads the team.

## What you'll do

- Build and run the ingestion pipelines that bring machine, ERP and quality data into the platform.
- Make the pipelines recover on their own when a factory's connection drops for hours or days.
- Design the tables that the dashboards and the alerting service read from.
- Review code, share the on-call rota (one week in six) and help new engineers find their feet.

## What you bring

- Five or more years building data pipelines that run in production, in Python and SQL.
- Experience with a stream processor, such as Kafka, Flink or Kinesis.
- You care about data quality and can explain a trade-off to someone who is not an engineer.
- Experience with industrial protocols such as OPC UA or MQTT is a bonus, not a requirement.

## What we offer

- £70,000 to £82,000 a year, depending on experience, and share options.
- 28 days of holiday plus bank holidays.
- £1,500 a year to spend on courses, books and conferences.
- Hybrid working: the Leeds office two days a week, or fully remote within the UK.
