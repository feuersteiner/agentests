# French-speaking Support Specialist

Remote in the EU · Support team · €40,000 to €46,000 a year

## About Larkspur Data

Larkspur Data connects the machines, ERP and quality systems of mid-sized manufacturers, so that a
plant manager sees scrap, downtime and open orders in one place. We are 85 people in Leeds and
Munich, and 260 factories in the UK, Germany, Austria, Switzerland and France run on our platform.
Our working language is English.

## The role

We now have 140 customers in France, Belgium and Switzerland, and their tickets wait too long for a
first reply. You will take the French-language queue with Margaux, join the English rota on
Fridays, and report to Aoife Brennan, Support Lead.

## What you'll do

- Answer tickets in French and English in Zendesk, by email and chat, 09:00 to 17:30 CET.
- Run screen-share sessions to fix connector set-ups with customers' IT teams.
- Read logs and run simple SQL to find out why data from a machine or ERP stopped arriving.
- Write help-centre articles in French, and translate the most-read English ones.
- Report bugs to engineering in Linear, in English.

## What you bring

- Two or more years in technical support for a software product.
- Comfortable with logs, APIs and basic SQL. Manufacturing experience is a bonus.
- Native-level French, spoken and written: you will explain technical fixes to plant
  managers and IT teams in French every day.
- Good written English (B2 or above) for tickets, bug reports and team chat.

## What we offer

- €40,000 to €46,000 a year, depending on experience, and share options.
- Remote anywhere in the EU, with a team week in Leeds twice a year. You need the right to work in
  the country you live in.
- 28 days of holiday and a €1,000 home-office budget.

## How to apply

Send your CV and a few lines about a tricky ticket you solved to careers@larkspurdata.example.
Questions about the role go to Niamh Gallagher, Talent Partner. We reply to every application
within five working days.
