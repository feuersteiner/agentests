# Hiring process

Every role at Larkspur Data goes through the same six stages. The talent partner owns the process
and tells each candidate where they stand within three working days of every stage.

| Stage               | Run by                         | Length |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------ |
| 1. Application      | Talent partner                 |        |
| 2. Intro call       | Talent partner                 | 30 min |
| 3. Hiring manager   | Hiring manager                 | 45 min |
| 4. Skills interview | Two people from the team       | 90 min |
| 5. Final            | A founder                      | 45 min |
| 6. Offer            | Talent partner, hiring manager |        |

## Intro call

The talent partner walks through the role, the team and the salary range, checks notice period and
availability, and answers the candidate's questions. Afterwards they write a short summary in
Ashby and recommend whether to move on.

## Skills interview

- Engineering: a pairing exercise on a small ingestion pipeline, in the candidate's own editor.
- Sales: a mock discovery call with a solutions engineer playing the plant manager of a customer.
- Support: a live session in which the candidate works through three real, anonymised tickets.

Each interviewer writes feedback in Ashby before talking to the other.

## Offer

We aim to make a verbal offer within two working days of the final and to send the written offer
in the same week. The hiring manager approves offers inside the role's salary range; anything
above it also needs the COO.
