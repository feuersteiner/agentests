# Brief: Account Executive, DACH

From Henrik Albers, Head of Sales EMEA, 16 November 2026.

## Why we're hiring

Germany, Austria and Switzerland brought in 38% of our new business this year, and Lena is the only
account executive covering them. She keeps the enterprise accounts. The new AE takes the
Mittelstand: family-owned manufacturers with 200 to 2,000 people, mostly in Bavaria,
Baden-Württemberg and North Rhine-Westphalia.

## The job

- Own new business with Mittelstand manufacturers in Germany, Austria and German-speaking
  Switzerland, from first meeting to signed contract.
- Work with our SDR on prospecting and with a solutions engineer on demos and pilots.
- Run pilots of six to eight weeks and negotiate with plant managers, IT and procurement.
- Quota: €900,000 of new annual recurring revenue a year.
- About 30% travel: customer sites, and trade fairs such as Hannover Messe and SPS.

## Who I want

- Four or more years closing B2B software deals, ideally with manufacturers.
- Has closed six-figure deals that went through a pilot and procurement.
- Need a native German speaker, ideally German, to sell to Mittelstand companies. They buy from
  people like them.
- Good English: deal reviews, Salesforce notes and all our internal meetings are in English.

## Package

- Base salary €85,000 to €100,000, on-target earnings €170,000 to €200,000, commission uncapped.
- Munich office, or remote in Germany with two days a month in Munich.
- Company car or a Bahncard 100, 30 days of holiday, share options.
- Reports to me.
