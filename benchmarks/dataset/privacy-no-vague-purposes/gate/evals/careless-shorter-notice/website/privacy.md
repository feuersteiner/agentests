# Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. Here's the short version of how Tessellate B.V. (Utrecht, the
Netherlands) handles your data. Questions? Email privacy@tessellate.example.

## What we collect

Your account details (name, email, password), the content of your boards, billing details, the
messages you send to support, technical data such as your IP address and device, and your email
address if you sign up for our newsletter.

## How we use it

We use your data to provide and improve Tessellate, to keep your account secure, to bill you and
to communicate with you, including about product news if you have signed up for it.

## Who we share it with

Trusted providers that help us run Tessellate, such as our hosting, payments and email partners.
We never sell your data.

## How long we keep it

As long as your account is active, and longer where the law requires it.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or export your data at any time. Just email
privacy@tessellate.example. You can also complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
