# Newsletter privacy note

When you sign up for the Tessellate newsletter, we collect your name, your email address, your
company, your job title and the pages you read on our website, and we use this information to send
you the monthly newsletter, to recommend board templates, to count which articles are read and to
plan our sales calls.

Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link. When you use it, we delete your details within 30 days.

Questions go to privacy@tessellate.example.
