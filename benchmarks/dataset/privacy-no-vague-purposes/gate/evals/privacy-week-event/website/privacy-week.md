# Privacy Week talk: privacy reviews on a whiteboard

On Thursday 28 January, our privacy lead Ilse Verbeek gives a 30-minute online talk on how product
teams can run a privacy review on a Tessellate board, from mapping a new feature to writing the
questions for legal.

Some seats are still free, and the talk might run a little over, so keep an extra ten minutes in
your calendar. You could also bring a feature your own team is working on: the last part of the
talk is an open review of two features from the audience.

Book a seat at tessellate.example/events.
