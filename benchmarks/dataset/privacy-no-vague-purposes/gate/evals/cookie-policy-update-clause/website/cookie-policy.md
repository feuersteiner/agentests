# Cookie policy

Last updated: 1 December 2026

This page lists every cookie tessellate.example and app.tessellate.example set, and what each one
is for.

| Cookie | What we use it for | When it is set | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tsl_session` | Keeps you signed in to the web app | When you sign in | After 14 days |
| `tsl_csrf` | Stops other websites from submitting our forms in your name | When a page with a form opens | When you close the browser |
| `tsl_analytics_id` | A random ID that links the pages and features one browser uses, so that we can count how many people use each feature | Only after you click "Accept analytics" on the banner | After 13 months |

We set no advertising or social media cookies. You can change your analytics choice with the
"Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

We may update this policy when we add or remove a cookie. The date at the top shows when it last
changed.
