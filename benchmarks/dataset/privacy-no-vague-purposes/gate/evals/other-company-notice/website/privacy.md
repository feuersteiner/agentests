# Privacy

Fernhill Bikes rents out electric bikes in Bristol through the Fernhill app. This page says what we
do with your personal data. Fernhill Bikes Ltd, 4 Cheese Lane, Bristol, is responsible for it.

## Your account

We use your name, email address and phone number to set up your account, to send you a receipt
after each ride and to text you a code when you sign in on a new phone.

## Your rides

While a bike is unlocked, it sends us its GPS position every 30 seconds. We use these positions to
work out the price of the ride, to find a bike that has been left outside the service area, and to
show you the route of each ride in the app. We delete ride positions 12 months after the ride.

## Payments

Your card details go straight to Adyen, which charges the card after each ride. We see only the
last four digits, to show you which card was used.

## Damage reports

If you report a damaged bike, we use your photos and description to send a mechanic and to decide
whether the damage happened during your ride.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, at privacy@fernhill.example.
You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
