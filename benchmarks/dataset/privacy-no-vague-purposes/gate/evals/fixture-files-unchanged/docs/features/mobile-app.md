# Mobile app

Feature note from the mobile team. Internal, never published.

Ships: Tuesday 1 December 2026, version 1.0, for iOS 17 and later and Android 12 and later.

## What it does

- Open, view and edit every board you can open on the web.
- Add photos to a board from the photo library or the camera.
- Push notifications for mentions, replies and board invitations, each turned on or off in
  Settings > Notifications.
- Offline mode: boards opened in the last 7 days stay readable without a connection, and edits sync
  when the phone is back online.

## Onboarding research

New users see five onboarding screens. On the first one they can tap "Help us improve onboarding"
to let us record those five screens. The privacy details are in privacy/data-inventory.md.
