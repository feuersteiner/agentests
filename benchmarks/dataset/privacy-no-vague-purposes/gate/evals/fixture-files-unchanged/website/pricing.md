# Pricing

All prices are per user per month, billed monthly, excluding VAT.

| Plan | Price | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | €0 | Up to 3 boards and 5 members |
| Team | €8 | Unlimited boards, up to 50 members, templates and search |
| Business | €15 | Everything in Team, plus single sign-on, audit log and priority support |

Pay yearly and get two months free on Team and Business.

Questions about plans or invoices? Email billing@tessellate.example.
