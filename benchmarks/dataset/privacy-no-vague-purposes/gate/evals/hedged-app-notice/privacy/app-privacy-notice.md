# Tessellate mobile app privacy notice

Last updated: 1 December 2026

This notice covers the Tessellate app for iOS and Android, made by Tessellate B.V. in Utrecht.
Questions go to privacy@tessellate.example.

## Photos

When you add a photo to a board from your photo library or camera, we upload that photo to the
board so that the people who can see the board can see it.

## Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we may use your phone's push token to send you notifications about
mentions, replies and board invitations.

## Crash reports

When the app crashes, we might collect a crash report with your device model, operating system
version, app version and the technical trace of the error, and use it to find and fix the crash.

## Onboarding recordings

If you tap "Help us improve onboarding", we may record some of the onboarding screens and watch
the recordings to find the steps where people get confused, so that we can change them.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, by emailing
privacy@tessellate.example.
