# Tessellate mobile app privacy notice

Last updated: 1 December 2026

Tessellate B.V. respects your privacy. This notice explains how we collect and use information when
you use the Tessellate app for iOS and Android.

## Information we collect

- Account information, such as your name and email address.
- Content you add to boards, including photos from your camera or photo library.
- Device information, such as your device model, operating system and push token.
- Usage information, such as crash reports and how you interact with the app.

## How we use your information

We may use the information we collect to:

- provide, maintain and improve the app;
- personalise your experience;
- send you notifications and other communications;
- understand how the app is used, for research and analytics purposes;
- develop new products and features.

## Sharing

We may share some information with trusted service providers who help us run the app.

## Your rights

You can contact privacy@tessellate.example to exercise your data protection rights.
