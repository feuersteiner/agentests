# Tessellate mobile app privacy notice

Last updated: 1 December 2026

This notice covers the Tessellate app for iOS and Android. It is made by Tessellate B.V.,
Oudegracht 112, 3511 AV Utrecht, the Netherlands. Questions go to our privacy lead, Ilse Verbeek,
at privacy@tessellate.example.

The app uses your Tessellate account and boards in the same way as the web app, as described in
our main privacy notice at tessellate.example/privacy. This notice covers what is different on your
phone.

## Photos

When you add a photo to a board from your photo library or camera, we upload that photo to the
board so that the people who can see the board can see it. The app reads only the photos you pick.
Photos are kept as board content, until you delete them.

## Push notifications

If you allow notifications, your phone gives the app a push token. We use it to send you the
notifications you turned on in Settings > Notifications: mentions, replies and board invitations.
We do not use it for marketing. We delete the token when you sign out or turn notifications off.

## Crash reports

When the app crashes, it sends us a crash report with your device model, operating system version,
app version, the screen that was open and the technical trace of the error. We use crash reports
to find and fix the crash. A report is linked to your account ID and nothing else, and we delete it
after 90 days.

## Onboarding recordings

On the first onboarding screen you can tap "Help us improve onboarding". If you do, the app records
the five onboarding screens as you go through them, and we watch these recordings to find the
steps where people get confused, so that we can change them. Recording stops after the last
onboarding screen, recordings are not used for advertising, and we delete them after 60 days.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing
privacy@tessellate.example. You can also complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
