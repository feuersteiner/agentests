# Tessellate

The online whiteboard for product teams. Plan a roadmap, run a retro or sketch a flow together,
then keep the board as the record of what you decided.

## Features

- **Boards:** an endless canvas for sticky notes, documents, images and comments.
- **Templates:** start from one of 40 templates for retros, roadmaps, user journeys and planning.
- **Search:** find any sticky note or comment across every board in your workspace.
- **Summaries:** Tessellate learns from your boards to personalise your experience, and may suggest
  templates that suit the way your team works.

## Get Tessellate

Use Tessellate in any modern browser at app.tessellate.example, or on iOS and Android.
