# Privacy at sign-up

When you create a Tessellate account, we ask for your name, your email address and a password.

**Your account.** We use your email address to sign you in and to send password resets and
security emails, and your name to show your workspace who you are. We store your password only as
a hash.

**Measuring the sign-up form.** If you accept analytics cookies, a random ID in a cookie lets us
count at which step of this form people stop, so that we can remove the steps that lose the most
people.

**What we do not do.** We do not use your sign-up details to develop new services, for research
purposes or to personalise advertising, and we do not sell them.

The full privacy notice is at tessellate.example/privacy.
