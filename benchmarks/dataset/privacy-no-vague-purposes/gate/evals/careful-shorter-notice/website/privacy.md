# Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Hi! This is how Tessellate B.V. (Oudegracht 112, Utrecht, the Netherlands) handles your personal
data when you use the Tessellate web app and this website. Questions? Write to our privacy lead,
Ilse Verbeek, at privacy@tessellate.example.

## What we use and why

| Your data | What we do with it |
| --- | --- |
| Name, email address, password (stored as a hash) | Create your account, sign you in, send password resets and security emails. Never used for marketing. |
| Workspace members and roles | Show members to each other and decide who can open and edit each board. |
| Board content | Show your boards to the people they are shared with and build your workspace's search index. Staff only open a board when you ask support to. Never used for advertising. |
| Billing contact, company name, VAT number | Issue invoices and charge your subscription. Stripe handles your card; we only see its brand and last four digits. |
| Support emails | Answer you. Once a month we tag tickets by topic to count the five most common problems. |
| IP address, browser and device at sign-in | Email you when your account signs in from a new country, and lock it for 15 minutes after 10 failed attempts. |
| Newsletter address (only if you sign up) | Send the monthly newsletter. Unsubscribe from any issue. |

## Who else gets it

Amazon Web Services stores everything in Frankfurt. Stripe gets billing and card details to charge
you. Postmark gets your name and email address to deliver our emails. We never sell your data or
share it with advertisers.

## How long we keep it

Account data: life of the account plus 30 days. Boards: until you delete them, plus 30 days in
backups. Invoices: 7 years (Dutch tax law). Support emails: 2 years after the ticket closes.
Sign-in records: 90 days. Newsletter address: until you unsubscribe.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to how we
use it, or take it with you. Email privacy@tessellate.example and we answer within a month. You
can also complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

## Changes

If a change affects data you have already given us, we email workspace owners 30 days ahead.
