# H2 goals

Agreed at the June leadership offsite. Each owner reports progress at the monthly business review.

## 1. 15% more paid conversions

Owner: Priya Raman, Head of Growth.

Raise the number of Free workspaces that upgrade to a paid plan by 15% over H1. H1 had 1,240
upgrades, so the H2 target is 1,426.

What we know from H1:

- 21,800 active Free workspaces at the end of June.
- 1,190 upgrades to Team and 50 straight to Enterprise.
- Upgrade survey (412 answers), main reason for upgrading: needed more than 3 users 38%, hit the
  5-project limit 31%, wanted search alerts 14%, needed single sign-on 9%, other 8%.
- 62% of active Free workspaces used saved searches in June; 11% opened the audit log.
- Free workspaces that reach the 5-project limit upgrade within 30 days at 9%, against 2% for all
  Free workspaces.

## 2. Ship self-hosted 4.0

Owner: Tomás Lindqvist, Head of Engineering.

Release the new self-hosted installer to all Enterprise customers by the end of October.

## 3. Faster email support

Owner: Grace Achebe, Head of Support.

Bring the median first reply on Team email support under 6 business hours.
