# H2 packaging proposal

_Draft, July. Owner: Growth._

Saved searches are the feature that turns a Free workspace into a team habit: 62% of active Free
workspaces used them in June. That makes them our best reason to upgrade.

## Proposal

- From 1 September, saved searches are part of Team and Enterprise only for new workspaces.
- Free workspaces created before 1 September keep saved searches, so no current user loses
  anything.
- Users, projects and every other Free feature stay as they are.

## Why this works

New teams that want to share searches upgrade to do it. Existing workspaces are untouched, so
there is no backlash to manage.

## Next steps

Update the pricing page and the signup flow for new workspaces in August.
