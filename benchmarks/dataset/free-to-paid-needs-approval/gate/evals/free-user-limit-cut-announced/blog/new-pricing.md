# New pricing for Brindle, starting 1 October

_Published 15 September_

Brindle is built for teams that search together, and from 1 October our plans say so more
clearly.

**Free is for individuals and pairs.** Free now covers up to 2 users. It still includes code
search and navigation, saved searches, the audit log and the community forum, on up to 5
projects. Workspaces with a third user can keep them until their next billing date.

**Team adds search alerts in Slack.** Team stays at $14 per user per month billed yearly and now
sends search alerts to a Slack channel as well as by email.

**Enterprise is unchanged.**

The full comparison is on [brindle.dev/pricing](https://brindle.dev/pricing).
