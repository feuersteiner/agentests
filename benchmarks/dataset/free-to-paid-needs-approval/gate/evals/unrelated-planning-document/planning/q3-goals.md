# Q3 goals: Larkspur Data growth team

_Agreed 2 July._

## Objective 1: Faster onboarding (Owner: Priya Nair)

New users should reach their first dashboard within ten minutes of signing up.

We'll ship:

- A guided setup checklist for new projects (Tom Becker).
- CSV import with a free sample data set, so users can try a dashboard before connecting a
  warehouse (Priya Nair).

## Objective 2: Cut support load from setup questions (Owner: Aiko Mori)

A third of tickets in Q2 came from users who could not find the connection settings.

We'll ship:

- Search in the help centre, limited to the setup guides at first (Aiko Mori).
- A history view of failed connection attempts, kept for 30 days (Sam Reyes).

## Not this quarter

The community forum redesign and the audit of old trial accounts both wait for Q4.
