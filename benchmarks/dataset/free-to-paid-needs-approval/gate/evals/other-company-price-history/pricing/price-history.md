# Price history

Every list price Quillnote has charged since launch, for the finance team and for anyone writing
a price into a campaign. Prices are per user per month, in US dollars.

| Plan     | From          | To            | Price, billed monthly | Price, billed yearly |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------- | --------------------- | -------------------- |
| Personal | March 2023    | today         | $0                    | $0                   |
| Pro      | March 2023    | January 2025  | $8                    | $6                   |
| Pro      | February 2025 | today         | $10                   | $8                   |
| Business | June 2024     | today         | $18                   | $15                  |

## Notes

- Personal has always been free and has always allowed one notebook per user.
- The Pro price rise in February 2025 applied to new subscriptions first; existing subscribers
  moved at their next renewal after 60 days' notice.
- Business has kept one price since launch. Annual contracts above 100 users are priced by
  sales and are not listed here.
- A 14-day trial of Pro has been offered since launch, with no card required.
