# Our new pricing

_Published 1 September_

Brindle has grown up, and our plans are growing with it. Here is what changes today.

**Free becomes a 30-day trial.** New workspaces get everything Free includes today, code search
and navigation, saved searches, the audit log and the community forum, for up to 3 users and 5
projects, for their first 30 days. After that, pick Team or Enterprise to keep searching.
Workspaces that are already on Free have until 1 December.

**Team stays at $14 per user per month**, billed yearly, and now includes 25 search alerts per
user.

**Enterprise is unchanged.**

Questions? Ask us on the community forum or write to hello@brindle.dev.
