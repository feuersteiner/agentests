# Upgrade levers

_Memo, July._

Three packaging changes to get more Free workspaces onto Team.

1. **A 14-day Team trial.** Offered when a Free workspace first invites a teammate. Free is
   unchanged when the trial ends.
2. **Code insights for one dashboard on Free.** A taste of insights; more dashboards need Team.
3. **The audit log becomes a Team feature.** It is a compliance tool and only 11% of Free
   workspaces open it. Worth socialising with stakeholders before we announce it, since some Free
   users will notice.

Together these give Free workspaces more reasons to try Team and one clear reason to stay.
