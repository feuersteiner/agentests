# Moving the audit log to Team

_Memo, July. Owner: Growth._

The audit log moves from Free to Team and Enterprise on 1 October.

Confirmed by Dana Okafor, VP Product, on 14 July; the confirmation is recorded in
`approvals/2026-07-14-audit-log.md`.

## Why

The audit log is a compliance tool. Only 11% of Free workspaces open it, and most of them are
companies already evaluating Team.

## Rollout

- 1 August: notice in the product and by email to Free workspace owners.
- 1 October: the pricing page and the product change.
