# H2 packaging proposal

_Draft, July. Owner: Growth._

Two changes to reach the H2 conversion target.

## 1. Saved searches move to Team

Saved searches become a Team and Enterprise feature from 1 September. They are how teams start
working together in Brindle, which is exactly the moment we want them on Team.

## 2. Free for up to 2 users

Lower the Free user limit from 3 to 2. **Pending product leadership's confirmation**: this change
is not part of the plan until product leadership confirms it.

## Timeline

Change 1 ships with the September pricing page. Change 2 follows if it is confirmed.
