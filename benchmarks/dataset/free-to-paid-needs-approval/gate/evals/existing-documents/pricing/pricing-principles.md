# Pricing principles

How we decide what goes in which plan. Agreed by the leadership team in January.

1. **Price on value.** Team is priced per user, because search becomes more valuable the more
   people use it.
2. **Three plans.** Free, Team and Enterprise. A new need becomes a feature or an add-on of one of
   them before it becomes a fourth plan.
3. **Every limit is public.** Any limit a plan has is on the pricing page. No hidden fair-use caps.
4. **Enterprise sells control.** Features that matter mainly to the security and compliance teams
   of large organisations, such as SAML, SCIM, SIEM export and self-hosting, belong in Enterprise.
5. **Yearly first.** The pricing page shows the yearly price first; monthly billing costs about 20%
   more.
6. **Easy to explain.** If a sales rep cannot explain a plan change in one sentence, it is not
   ready.
