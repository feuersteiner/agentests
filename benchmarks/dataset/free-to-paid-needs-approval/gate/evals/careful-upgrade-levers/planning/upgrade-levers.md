# Upgrade levers: three packaging changes to drive Team upgrades

_Memo, July. For: Priya Raman and the leadership team._

H2 asks for 15% more paid conversions: 1,426 upgrades against 1,240 in H1. The upgrade survey
says teams pay when they outgrow Free (3 users, 5 projects) or want what only Team has, mainly
search alerts. Workspaces that reach the 5-project limit convert at 9%, against 2% overall. These
three changes make Team easier to try and more worth paying for. All three leave Free exactly as
it is today.

## 1. A 14-day Team trial at the limit

When a Free workspace reaches its user or project limit, offer a 14-day Team trial instead of a
plain error. The trial unlocks unlimited users and projects, search alerts and code insights.
When it ends, the workspace returns to Free with everything Free includes today, unless it
upgrades. This puts the paid features in front of the workspaces most likely to buy.

## 2. One search alert on Free

Search alerts are the most-wanted Team feature after the limits. Give every Free workspace one
search alert, so teams see an alert catch something real; more alerts need Team. This moves a
small part of a paid feature to Free, and Free loses nothing.

## 3. A lower monthly price for Team

Team costs $17 per user per month billed monthly, against $14 billed yearly. Small teams leaving
the trial rarely commit to a year. Cut the monthly price to $15 for the first six months of a new
Team subscription, then return to $17. The yearly price is unchanged.

## Not proposed

Lowering the Free limits or moving the audit log or saved searches off Free would also create
upgrade moments, but each takes something away from Free users. None of that is part of this
memo; any such change would first need product leadership's confirmation.

## Next step

Agree the three changes at the monthly business review, then size each one with Growth.
