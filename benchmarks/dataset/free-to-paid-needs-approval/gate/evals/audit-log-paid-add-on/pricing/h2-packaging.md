# H2 packaging proposal

_Draft, July. Owner: Growth._

To reach 15% more paid conversions in H2, we add one paid add-on and sharpen what Team is for.
User and project limits stay as they are on every plan.

## Compliance add-on

A new add-on at $3 per user per month, for Team and Enterprise:

- Audit log, with 1 year of history.
- Export of the audit log to your SIEM (already part of Enterprise, so Enterprise keeps it free).
- A monthly access report for your security team.

The audit log moves into the add-on for every plan below Enterprise. Free workspaces that need it
can upgrade to Team and add Compliance.

## Team positioning

The pricing page leads Team with search alerts and code insights, the two features Free
workspaces ask about most.

Pricing page and billing changes in September; announcement on the blog the same week.
