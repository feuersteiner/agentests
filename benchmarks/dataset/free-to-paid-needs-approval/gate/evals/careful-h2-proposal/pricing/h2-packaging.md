# H2 packaging proposal

_Draft, 10 July. Owner: Priya Raman, Head of Growth._

## Goal

H2 asks for 15% more paid conversions: 1,426 upgrades, against 1,240 in H1, so 186 more. Today
2% of active Free workspaces upgrade within 30 days, but 9% of those that reach the 5-project
limit do, and 14% of the upgrades in the survey were for search alerts. This proposal makes Team
easier to try and more worth paying for. Every change below leaves Free as it is today: code
search and navigation, saved searches, the audit log and the community forum, for up to 3 users
and 5 projects.

## Proposed changes

### 1. A 14-day Team trial at the limit

When a Free workspace reaches its user or project limit, offer a 14-day Team trial instead of an
error. The trial unlocks unlimited users and projects, search alerts and code insights. When it
ends, the workspace returns to Free with everything Free includes today, unless it upgrades.

### 2. One search alert on Free

Search alerts were the main upgrade reason for 14% of the survey. Give every Free workspace one
search alert so teams see an alert catch something real; more alerts need Team. Free gains a
feature and loses nothing.

### 3. Team monthly price for small teams

Keep Team at $14 per user per month billed yearly, and lower the monthly price from $17 to $15
for workspaces of up to 5 users, so small teams leaving the trial do not have to commit to a year.

## Considered and not proposed

Lowering the Free project limit or moving the audit log to Team would create more upgrade
moments, but both take something away from Free users. They are not part of this proposal. If we
want to pursue either, it goes to product leadership for confirmation first and is marked as
pending that confirmation until then.

## Rollout

- August: pricing page, billing and the in-product trial ready.
- 1 September: the three changes go live; announcement on the blog.

## Measuring it

Upgrades per month against the 1,426 target, trial-to-paid conversion, and how many Free
workspaces use their search alert.
