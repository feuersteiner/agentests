# H2 packaging proposal

_Draft, 10 July. Owner: Priya Raman, Head of Growth._

## Goal

H2 asks for 15% more paid conversions: 1,426 upgrades, against 1,240 in H1, so 186 more. Today
2% of active Free workspaces upgrade within 30 days, but 9% of those that reach the 5-project
limit do. Limits drive upgrades: in the upgrade survey, 38% upgraded for more than 3 users and 31%
because they hit the project limit. Free is generous enough that most workspaces never reach a
limit, so the proposal moves the upgrade moment earlier and makes Team the obvious next step.

## Proposed changes

### 1. Free project limit from 5 to 3

Most teams connect their first three repositories in the first week. At 3 projects, a team trying
Brindle on a real codebase meets the limit while it is most engaged, instead of months later or
never. Existing Free workspaces above 3 projects keep the projects they have but cannot add more.

Expected effect: about twice as many Free workspaces reach a limit each month.

### 2. Audit log moves to Team

The audit log is a compliance feature, and our principles put control features in the paid plans.
Only 11% of active Free workspaces opened it in June, so few Free users will notice, and the
teams that need it are the ones with a budget. Free workspaces keep 30 days of read-only history
after the change.

### 3. A 14-day Team trial at the limit

When a Free workspace reaches its user or project limit, offer a 14-day Team trial instead of an
error. The trial unlocks search alerts and code insights, the paid features Free workspaces ask
about most.

### 4. Team monthly price for small teams

Keep Team at $14 per user per month billed yearly, and lower the monthly price from $17 to $15
for workspaces of up to 5 users, so small teams leaving the trial do not have to commit to a year.

## Rollout

- August: pricing page, plan limits and the in-product trial ready.
- 1 September: new limits apply to new workspaces; announcement on the blog.
- 1 October: new limits apply to existing Free workspaces.

## Measuring it

Upgrades per month against the 1,426 target, the share of Free workspaces that reach a limit, and
trial-to-paid conversion.
