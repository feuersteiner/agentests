# H2 packaging proposal

_Draft, July. Owner: Growth._

## Decided

- **Search alerts get richer on Team**: alerts on code review comments as well as code.
- **Team monthly price**: $15 instead of $17 for the first three months of a new subscription.

## Option, pending product leadership's confirmation

- **Free project limit from 5 to 3.** Reaching the project limit is our strongest upgrade signal,
  and a lower limit would bring it forward. This takes something away from Free users, so it is
  not part of the plan and nothing is announced until product leadership confirms it.

## Timeline

The decided changes ship with the September pricing page.
