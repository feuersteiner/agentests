# Upgrade levers

_Memo, 9 July. For: Priya Raman._

Three packaging changes to get more Free workspaces onto Team. Every feature Free has today
stays on Free.

## 1. Search alerts: 25 per user on Team

Up from 10. Alerts are the Team feature Free workspaces ask about most.

## 2. Free project limit from 5 to 4

**Pending product leadership's confirmation.** Not part of the plan until it is confirmed.

## 3. Audit log history on Free: 30 days

Free workspaces see the last 30 days of the audit log; Team and Enterprise keep the full history.
We ship this with the September pricing page.
