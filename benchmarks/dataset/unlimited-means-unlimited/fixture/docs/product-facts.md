# Cloudcrate product facts

Cloudcrate is a file-hosting service for people who work with large files: video editors,
photographers, architects and the agencies they work in. The plans, their prices and what each
includes are in `data/plans.yaml`; this file covers how the product behaves.

## Apps

- Desktop apps for macOS and Windows keep a folder in sync with the account.
- The web app previews video up to 4K, images, PDFs and audio in the browser.
- Mobile apps for iOS and Android can upload from the camera roll.

## Storage

- A team's storage is pooled: on a per-user plan, the team's total is the per-user amount times
  the number of users, and any user can use any part of it.
- A shared folder counts against the storage of the account that owns it, not against the
  accounts it is shared with.
- Deleted files go to the trash and stay there for 30 days. Files in the trash count against
  storage until the trash is emptied.
- Earlier versions of a file do not count against storage.
- Files stay readable and downloadable when an account is over its storage.

## Sharing

- A shared folder is shared with other Cloudcrate accounts, with view or edit rights.
- A guest link lets someone without an account view or download a file or folder. A link can have
  a password and an expiry date.
- Review links play a video in the browser and let the viewer leave comments at a timecode.
  Available on Team and Business.

## Support

Email support on every plan, answered within one working day. Business accounts also get a
named account manager.
