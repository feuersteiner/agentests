# Hand over the files, keep the versions

Cloudcrate keeps your project folders in sync between your machines and lets you send finished
work to a client as a link.

[Start a 14-day free trial](/signup)

## Send work as a link

Make a guest link to a file or a folder. Your client opens it in the browser, with no account.
Add a password or an expiry date if the work is not public yet.

## Go back to an earlier version

The Personal plan keeps earlier versions of every file for 30 days, so you can restore last
Tuesday's cut.

## What Personal includes

200 GB of storage, files of up to 5 GB each and 10 guest links, for $8 a month.
