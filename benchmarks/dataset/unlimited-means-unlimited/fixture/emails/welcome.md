Thanks for creating a Cloudcrate account. Your 14-day free trial has started, and here is how to
get going:

1. **Install the desktop app.** [Download it](https://cloudcrate.example/download) for macOS or
   Windows and sign in.
2. **Pick a folder to sync.** Anything you put in it appears on your other machines and in the
   web app.
3. **Send your first guest link.** Right-click a file, choose **Guest link** and paste the link to
   a client.
