# A newsletter tool for small publishers

Write an issue in the editor, schedule it and send it to your list. Every issue also gets a page
in your public archive.

[Start a 14-day free trial](/signup)

## Three plans

Starter is $9 a month for up to 1,000 subscribers and four sends a month. Growth is $29 a month
for up to 10,000 subscribers. Business is $79 a month with unlimited subscribers, unlimited sends
and five editor seats.

## Bring your list

Import subscribers from a CSV file. We store the date each person signed up, so your consent
records come with you.
