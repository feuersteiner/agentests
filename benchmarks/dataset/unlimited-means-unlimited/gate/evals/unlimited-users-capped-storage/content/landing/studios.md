# Add people without changing plan

Cloudcrate Business has no upper limit on users. Add a freelancer for a week or a whole new team,
and they get the same shared folders, review links and single sign-on as everyone else.

## What Business includes

Unlimited users, 2 TB of storage for each of them, files of up to 250 GB and unlimited guest links.
