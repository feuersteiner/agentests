# Two-step sign-in

## Turn it on

Open **Settings → Security** and choose **Two-step sign-in**. Scan the QR code with an
authenticator app and enter the six-digit code to confirm.

## Recovery codes

We show ten recovery codes when you turn two-step sign-in on. Store them somewhere safe, such as
a password manager. Each code works once.

## Too many wrong codes

There is a limit of five wrong codes in ten minutes. After that, sign-in is locked for 15 minutes.

## Lost your phone?

Sign in with a recovery code, then set up a new authenticator app. If you have no recovery codes
left, your admin can reset two-step sign-in for you on Team and Business accounts.
