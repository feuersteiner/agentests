# One place for the whole edit, from rushes to final master

Built for video agencies: upload files of up to 250 GB each, keep a year of versions, and get 2 TB
of storage per user on Business. Clients review cuts in the browser and comment at the timecode.

[Start a 14-day free trial](/signup)

## One folder per project

Give each project a shared folder. Everyone on the team sees the same files, and the desktop app
keeps them in sync on each machine.

## Review links with comments at the timecode

Send a client a review link. The video plays in their browser, and their comments land on the
frame they are about.

## Controls for the admin

On Business, sign in with single sign-on and see who opened, changed or shared a file in the
audit log.
