# Storage FAQ

## How much storage does each plan include?

Personal includes 200 GB. Team includes 1 TB per user and Business 2 TB per user. On Team and
Business the storage is pooled: a team of five on Business shares 10 TB.

## What happens when I run out of space?

On Personal and Team, uploads are refused until you free space, upgrade or add users. On Business,
uploads keep working above 2 TB per user but are slowed to 1 Mbps until the team is back under
the limit. On every plan your files stay readable and downloadable.

## What is the largest file I can upload?

5 GB on Personal, 50 GB on Team and 250 GB on Business.

## Do files in a shared folder count against my storage?

Only if you own the folder. A shared folder counts against the storage of the account that owns
it, not the accounts it is shared with.

## Do deleted files and earlier versions count against my storage?

Deleted files stay in the trash for 60 days and count against your storage until you empty the
trash. Earlier versions of a file do not count.
