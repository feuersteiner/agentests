# Shared folders and guest links

## Share a folder with other accounts

Open the folder, choose **Share** and enter the email addresses of the people to share with. Give
each person view or edit rights. They need a Cloudcrate account.

## Send a guest link

A guest link lets someone without an account view or download a file or a folder. Open the item,
choose **Guest link** and copy the link. You can set a password and an expiry date.

## How many guest links can I have?

Personal accounts can have 10 active guest links and Team accounts 100. Business accounts have
unlimited guest links. An expired or deleted link no longer counts.

See also the [storage FAQ](/help/storage-faq).
