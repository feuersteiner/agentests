# Pricing

Three plans, billed per user each month. You can change plan or cancel at any time, and every plan
starts with a 14-day free trial.

|                 | Personal    | Team             | Business         |
| --------------- | ----------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Price           | $8 a month  | $12 per user a month | $20 per user a month |
| Users           | 1           | 3 to 50          | 5 or more        |
| Storage         | 200 GB      | 1 TB per user    | 2 TB per user    |
| Largest file    | 5 GB        | 50 GB            | 250 GB           |
| Version history | 30 days     | 90 days          | 365 days         |
| Guest links     | 10          | 100              | Unlimited        |
| Single sign-on  | No          | No               | Yes              |
| Audit log       | No          | No               | Yes              |

On Business, uploads slow to 1 Mbps once the team is over 2 TB per user. On Personal and Team,
uploads stop at the limit until you free space or upgrade.

## Questions about billing

**Can I change plan later?** Yes. Upgrades apply at once and are charged pro rata; downgrades
apply at the end of the billing month.

**Do you offer refunds?** If you cancel in the first 30 days of a paid plan, we refund the month.
