# Storage on the Business plan

## Is Business storage unlimited?

No. Each Business user adds 2 TB to the team's pool, so a team of eight has 16 TB to share.

## What happens above the limit?

Uploads are not refused, but they are slowed to 1 Mbps until the team frees space or adds users.
Downloads and sharing work as usual.

## Can we buy more storage without adding users?

Not at the moment. Write to your account manager if your team is regularly close to its limit.
