# Unlimited means unlimited

## The rule

Do not call a plan feature "unlimited" or "no limits" when the plan terms impose a cap, fair-use limit or throttling; state the real limit instead of qualifying an unlimited claim in small print.

Cloudcrate's Business plan storage has a fair-use cap of 2 TB per user, after which uploads are throttled. So customer-facing copy never calls storage unlimited, or describes it with a synonym such as "endless", "as much as you need" or "never run out", with or without a footnote, and wherever it says how much storage Business has, it states the 2 TB per-user limit. A feature the plan terms do not cap, such as guest links on Business, may be called unlimited.

Apply it to every page and email you write or edit, for the whole task: headlines, subject lines,
front matter, tables, body copy and footnotes.

## Why

A customer who reads "unlimited" plans around it: an agency moves its whole archive over and
finds its uploads slowed to a crawl. A footnote that takes the claim back does not fix the claim;
advertising rules say a qualification may clarify a claim but must not contradict it. The real
figure is a good one, so say it.

## Gotchas

- The plan data labels Business storage `unlimited` and keeps the cap in a separate fair-use
  field. Copying the label into a table cell or a headline is the usual way this goes wrong.
  Write the figure from the fair-use field instead.
- "Unlimited storage*" with "*fair use policy applies" at the bottom is the same claim.
- So are the synonyms: "limitless", "endless space", "store everything", "never think about
  space again", "all the room you will ever need".
- Leaving storage vague ("generous storage") to dodge the word is not stating the limit. When the
  copy describes Business storage, it gives the number.
- Check each "unlimited" against the plan terms one feature at a time. A feature with no cap
  keeps the word.

## Examples

A social ad for the Business plan.

Compliant:

> **Cloudcrate Business:** 2 TB of storage per user, 250 GB files and unlimited guest links.
> Over 2 TB per user, uploads slow down until you free space or add users.

Violating:

> **Cloudcrate Business:** unlimited storage\*, 250 GB files and unlimited guest links.
>
> \*Fair use policy applies.

## Before you commit

Read each page and email you changed, front matter and footnotes included. Find every statement
about how much a plan gives. For each one, check the plan terms for a cap, a fair-use limit or
throttling, and if there is one, replace the claim with the figure. Then read it once more for
synonyms of "unlimited".
