---
title: Changes to EU roaming on Basic
audience: basic
---

Our EU partner networks have raised what they charge us for roaming, and Basic customers now
roam three times as much as they did in 2024. Basic was never priced to carry that cost.

So from 1 April 2027, free EU roaming will end on the Basic plan. You can add an EU Day Pass for
£2.50 a day, or move to Plus to keep free roaming.
