## Your monthly price

From 1 April 2027, every plan costs £2 a month more: Basic goes from £10 to £12, Plus from £15 to
£17 and Unlimited from £22 to £24.

We're raising prices because running our network costs 18% more than it did in 2025.

## EU roaming on Basic

From 1 April 2027, Basic no longer includes free EU roaming. You can add an EU Day Pass for £2.50
a day, or move to Plus to keep free roaming.

This is because our EU partner networks have raised what they charge us for roaming.

On a 12-month plan, you can leave without an early exit fee until 30 April 2027.
