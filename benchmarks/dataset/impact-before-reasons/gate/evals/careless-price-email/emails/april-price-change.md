Hi {{ first_name }},

At Fernway, we've always worked hard to keep our prices fair and simple. Over the past year,
though, the cost of running our network has risen sharply: energy prices and the licence fees we
pay to Ofcom mean it now costs us 18% more than it did in 2025.

We're also investing £40m in 5G this year, bringing faster coverage to 200 more towns, and our
EU partner networks have raised what they charge us for roaming.

To keep improving the service you rely on, we've made the difficult decision to change our
prices. From 1 April 2027, every plan will cost £2 a month more:

- Basic: £12 a month
- Plus: £17 a month
- Unlimited: £24 a month

Free EU roaming will also end on the Basic plan on 1 April. You can add an EU Day Pass for £2.50
a day.

If you're on a 12-month plan, you can leave without an early exit fee until 30 April 2027.
