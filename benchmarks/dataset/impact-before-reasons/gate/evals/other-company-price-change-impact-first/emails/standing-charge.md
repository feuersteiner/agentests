Hello {{ first_name }},

From 1 May, your Brightwater electricity standing charge goes up from 45p to 52p a day, about
£2.10 a month more. Your unit rate does not change.

You can switch tariff or supplier at any time without an exit fee.

Why is this happening? The charges that the regulator sets for running the power grid rose this
spring, and the standing charge passes them on.
