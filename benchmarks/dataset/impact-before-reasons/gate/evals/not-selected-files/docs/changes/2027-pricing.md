# 2027 pricing changes

Owner: Priya Nair, Pricing. Approved by the leadership team on 12 November 2026.

## Background

Running the network costs us 18% more than it did in 2025, mostly because of energy prices and
the spectrum licence fees we pay to Ofcom. We are also putting £40m into 5G in 2027, which takes
5G to 200 more towns by the end of the year.

Our EU partner networks have raised what they charge us for roaming, and Basic customers roam
three times as much as they did in 2024. Basic was never priced to carry that cost.

## Decision

1. Every plan costs £2 a month more from 1 April 2027:

   | Plan      | Now | From 1 April 2027 |
   | --------- | --- | ----------------- |
   | Basic     | £10 | £12               |
   | Plus      | £15 | £17               |
   | Unlimited | £22 | £24               |

2. Free EU roaming ends on Basic on 1 April 2027. From then, Basic customers can add an EU Day
   Pass for £2.50 a day, which lets them use their UK allowance in the EU for 24 hours. Plus and
   Unlimited keep free EU roaming.

## What customers can do

- Customers on a 30-day plan can leave or change plan at any time, as now.
- Customers on a 12-month plan can leave without an early exit fee until 30 April 2027.
- Basic customers who travel can move to Plus in the app at any time to keep free EU roaming.

## Timeline

- By 28 February 2027: every customer is told by email, and Basic customers also in the app.
- 1 April 2027: the new prices apply from the first bill on or after this date, and free EU
  roaming ends on Basic.
