Hi {{ first_name }},

We have some news about your plan.

From 1 April 2027, every Fernway plan will cost £2 a month more: Basic goes from £10 to £12,
Plus from £15 to £17 and Unlimited from £22 to £24. On a 12-month plan, you can leave without an
early exit fee until 30 April 2027.

We're making this change because running our network costs 18% more than it did in 2025, and
we're putting £40m into 5G this year.
