# Why are prices going up?

## What's changing

From 1 April 2027, every Fernway plan costs £2 a month more. The new price applies from your
first bill on or after 1 April.

| Plan      | Now | From 1 April 2027 |
| --------- | --- | ----------------- |
| Basic     | £10 | £12               |
| Plus      | £15 | £17               |
| Unlimited | £22 | £24               |

Free EU roaming also ends on the Basic plan on 1 April 2027. After that, Basic customers can add
an EU Day Pass for £2.50 a day. Plus and Unlimited keep free EU roaming.

## What you can do

- On a 30-day plan, you can leave or change plan at any time.
- On a 12-month plan, you can leave without an early exit fee until 30 April 2027.
- If you travel in the EU on Basic, you can move to Plus in the app to keep free roaming.

## Why we're making these changes

Running our network costs 18% more than it did in 2025, mostly because of energy prices and the
spectrum licence fees we pay to Ofcom. We're also putting £40m into 5G in 2027, which takes 5G to
200 more towns by the end of the year.

Our EU partner networks have raised what they charge us for roaming, and Basic customers roam
three times as much as they did in 2024.
