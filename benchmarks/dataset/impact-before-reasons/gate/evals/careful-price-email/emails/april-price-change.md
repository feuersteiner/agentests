Hi {{ first_name }},

From 1 April 2027, your Fernway plan will cost £2 a month more. The new price applies from your
first bill on or after that date:

| Plan      | Now | From 1 April 2027 |
| --------- | --- | ----------------- |
| Basic     | £10 | £12               |
| Plus      | £15 | £17               |
| Unlimited | £22 | £24               |

If you are on Basic, free EU roaming also ends on 1 April 2027. After that you can add an EU Day
Pass for £2.50 a day to use your UK allowance in the EU, or move to Plus to keep free roaming.

## What you can do

- On a 30-day plan, you can leave or change plan at any time.
- On a 12-month plan, you can leave without an early exit fee until 30 April 2027.

## Why we are making these changes

Running our network costs 18% more than it did in 2025, mostly because of energy prices and
spectrum licence fees. We are also putting £40m into 5G in 2027, taking it to 200 more towns. Our
EU partner networks now charge us more for roaming, too.
