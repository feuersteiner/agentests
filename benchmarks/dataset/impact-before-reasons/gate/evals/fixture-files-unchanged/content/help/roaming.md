# Using your phone abroad

## In the EU

Every Fernway plan includes roaming in the EU. You can use your UK data, calls and texts in any EU
country, Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein at no extra cost, for up to 60 days in a row.

## Outside the EU

Add a World Day Pass in the app for £5 a day. It lets you use your UK allowance for 24 hours from
your first call, text or use of data.

## Before you travel

Check that roaming is turned on in your phone's settings and in the app under **Settings >
Roaming**.
