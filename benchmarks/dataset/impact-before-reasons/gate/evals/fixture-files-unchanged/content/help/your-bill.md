# Your monthly bill

We take your plan price by Direct Debit on the same day each month, your bill day. You can see
your bill day in the app under **Billing**.

Your bill shows your plan price and anything you added that month, such as a World Day Pass or a
Data Boost.

## Moving your bill day or your plan

To move your bill day, open **Billing > Bill day** in the app. You can move it once every three
months.

You can switch plans in the app under **Plan**. Changes to your plan apply from your next bill
day.
