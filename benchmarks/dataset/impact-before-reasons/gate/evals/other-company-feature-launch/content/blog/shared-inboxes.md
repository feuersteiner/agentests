# Shared inboxes are here

Until now, every Tallyhive seat had its own inbox, so teams forwarded customer emails to each
other and replies got lost. Many of you asked for a better way.

From 3 March, you can create a shared inbox for your team on any Tallyhive plan, at no extra
cost. Everyone in the inbox sees each email, can assign it to a teammate and can see who replied.

Open **Settings > Inboxes** to create your first one.
