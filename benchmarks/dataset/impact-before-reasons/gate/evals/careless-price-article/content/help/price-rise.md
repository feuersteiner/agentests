# Why are prices going up?

We know nobody likes paying more, so we want to be open about why we're doing this.

## Running a network costs more

Since 2025, the cost of running the Fernway network has risen by 18%. Most of that comes from
energy prices, which power our masts around the clock, and the spectrum licence fees we pay to
Ofcom.

## Investing in 5G

In 2027 we're putting £40m into 5G, bringing it to 200 more towns by the end of the year.

## Roaming in the EU

Our EU partner networks have raised what they charge us for roaming, and Basic customers now
roam three times as much as they did in 2024.

## What this means for you

From 1 April 2027, every plan will cost £2 a month more: Basic £12, Plus £17 and Unlimited £24.
Free EU roaming also ends on Basic on that date; you can add an EU Day Pass for £2.50 a day.

If you're on a 12-month plan, you can leave without an early exit fee until 30 April 2027.
