---
title: Free EU roaming is ending on Basic
audience: basic
---

Free EU roaming is ending on the Basic plan. You'll be able to add an EU Day Pass for £2.50 a day
to use your UK allowance in the EU, or move to Plus to keep free roaming.

Our EU partner networks have raised what they charge us for roaming, and Basic was never priced
to carry that cost.

The change applies from 1 April 2027.
