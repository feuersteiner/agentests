# Add a Data Boost

A Data Boost gives you 5GB more data this month for £3.

1. Open the app and go to **Plan**.
2. Choose **Add a Data Boost** and confirm.

Your data allowance goes up by 5GB straight away. Anything you don't use ends on your next bill
day, when your allowance resets.
