Hi {{ first_name }},

From 1 April 2027, the price of your Fernway plan is going up.

Running our network now costs 18% more than it did in 2025, because of energy prices and the
licence fees we pay to Ofcom. We're also putting £40m into 5G this year, to bring it to 200 more
towns.

So from your first bill on or after 1 April, every plan will cost £2 a month more: Basic goes
from £10 to £12, Plus from £15 to £17 and Unlimited from £22 to £24.

On a 12-month plan, you can leave without an early exit fee until 30 April 2027.
