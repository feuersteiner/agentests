# Next week's rota, done in minutes

Tessellate is the shift-scheduling app for cafés, shops, clinics and every other small team that
works in shifts. Drag shifts onto the week, see who is free, and publish the rota to everyone's
phone.

## What you can do

- **Build the week**: copy last week's rota, then drag shifts to change it.
- **See who is free**: staff add their availability and time off, and Tessellate warns you before
  you book someone twice.
- **Swap shifts**: staff offer a shift, a colleague takes it, and you approve the swap with one tap.
