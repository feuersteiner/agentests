# Privacy policy

Larkspur Yoga Ltd, 22 Quay Street, Manchester M3 3JE, runs class bookings through this website and
our app.

## What we use your data for

- **Bookings and memberships**: your name, email, phone and payment details, to book your classes
  and take payment (contract).
- **Analytics**: which classes and pages you look at, to plan the timetable (our legitimate
  interests).
- **Marketing**: our weekly email of new classes and offers (our legitimate interests for members,
  consent for everyone else).

## Sharing

Our booking software provider and our payment provider, on our instructions.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, and to
withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@larkspuryoga.example to use any of these rights.
