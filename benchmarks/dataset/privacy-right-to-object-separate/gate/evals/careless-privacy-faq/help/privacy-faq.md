# Privacy FAQ

## Who controls my data?

Tessellate Ltd, 14 Weaver Street, Leeds LS1 4AB. Contact privacy@tessellate.example.

## What data do you collect?

Account details, rota data, billing details, support messages, usage data, sign-in and payment
signals, and newsletter sign-ups.

## Why do you use my data?

To run the app and bill you, for product analytics and fraud prevention (our legitimate
interests), and to send product news and the newsletter.

## Do you sell my data?

No. We share it only with our hosting, payment and email providers.

## How long do you keep it?

Account data for the life of your account plus 30 days, invoices for 7 years, usage data for 13
months and sign-in signals for 12 months.

## What are my rights?

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to and port your data, and to
withdraw consent. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time. Email privacy@tessellate.example to
use your rights, or complain to the ICO.
