# Shift Notes: how we use your details

When you sign up for Shift Notes, Tessellate Ltd (14 Weaver Street, Leeds LS1 4AB) uses your
email address and first name to send you the newsletter once a month. We use them for nothing
else, and we never sell them.

## Why we can use them

You give us your consent when you sign up. Our email provider sends the newsletter for us, on our
instructions.

## How long we keep them

Until you unsubscribe. We then delete your details within 30 days.

## Your right to object

You can object to the newsletter at any time, and we will stop sending it straight away. Click
"Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any issue, or email privacy@tessellate.example.

## Your other rights

You can ask for a copy of your details, or ask us to correct or delete them, by emailing
privacy@tessellate.example. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at
ico.org.uk. Our full [privacy notice](/privacy) covers everything else we do.
