# Privacy notice

This notice explains how Tessellate Ltd ("Tessellate", "we") uses personal data when you visit
tessellate.example, use the Tessellate app or get our emails.

## Who we are

Tessellate Ltd, 14 Weaver Street, Leeds LS1 4AB, United Kingdom, decides how and why your personal
data is used: we are its controller. Our privacy team answers questions about this notice at
privacy@tessellate.example.

## What we collect

- **Account details**: your name, work email address, password (we store only a hash of it) and
  your role in your team.
- **Rota data**: the shifts, availability and time-off requests that you and your team add.
- **Billing details**: the billing contact, the company address and the last four digits of the
  card. Our payment provider holds the full card details.
- **Support messages**: what you write to us and what we reply.
- **Usage data**: the features you use, the screens you open in the app, and your device and
  browser.
- **Sign-in and payment signals**: IP address, sign-in times, and failed sign-ins and payments.
- **Newsletter sign-ups**: your email address and first name, if you sign up for Shift Notes, our
  monthly newsletter.

## What we use it for, and why

| What we do                                                               | Data we use                       | Lawful basis               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Run your account and the app                                             | Account details, rota data        | Contract                   |
| Bill you and keep invoices                                               | Billing details                   | Contract; legal obligation |
| Answer your support messages                                             | Account details, support messages | Contract                   |
| Product analytics: learn which features are used so we can improve them | Usage data                        | Our legitimate interests   |
| Fraud prevention: stop fake sign-ups, account takeovers and card fraud   | Sign-in and payment signals       | Our legitimate interests   |
| Marketing emails to customers: product news and tips                     | Account details                   | Our legitimate interests   |
| Shift Notes, our monthly newsletter                                      | Newsletter sign-ups               | Your consent               |

Where we rely on our legitimate interests, we have weighed them against your interests and rights
and keep a record of that assessment.

## Your right to object

You can object at any time to our use of your data for product analytics or fraud prevention.
Email privacy@tessellate.example and say what you object to. We will stop unless we have
compelling reasons to carry on that outweigh your interests, such as stopping fraud on your
account, and we will tell you what they are.

You can also object to marketing at any time, and then we always stop. Use the unsubscribe link at
the bottom of every marketing email and newsletter, turn off "Product news" under Settings >
Notifications in the app, or email privacy@tessellate.example.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the companies that run parts of Tessellate for
us, under contracts that let them use it only on our instructions:

- our hosting provider, which stores Tessellate's data in data centres in the UK and Ireland;
- our payment provider, which takes card payments;
- our email provider, which sends app notifications, marketing emails and the newsletter.

We also share data when the law requires it, for example with HMRC for tax.

## How long we keep it

- Account details and rota data: while your account is open, and 30 days after it closes.
- Invoices: 7 years, as tax law requires.
- Support messages: 2 years after the conversation closes.
- Usage data: 13 months.
- Sign-in and payment signals: 12 months.
- Newsletter sign-ups: until you unsubscribe.

## Questions and complaints

Write to privacy@tessellate.example with any question about this notice or about your personal
data. If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's
Office at ico.org.uk.

## Changes to this notice

We update this page when we change how we use personal data, and we email customers about any
important change.
