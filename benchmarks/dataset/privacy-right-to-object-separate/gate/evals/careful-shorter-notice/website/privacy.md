# Privacy notice

Tessellate Ltd, 14 Weaver Street, Leeds LS1 4AB, makes the Tessellate shift-scheduling app and
controls the personal data it uses. Questions: privacy@tessellate.example.

## What we collect and why

| Data                                                   | What we use it for                          | Lawful basis               |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Name, work email, password hash, role, rota data       | Running your account and the app            | Contract                   |
| Billing contact, company address, card's last 4 digits | Billing you and keeping invoices            | Contract; legal obligation |
| Support messages                                       | Answering you                               | Contract                   |
| Features used, screens opened, device and browser      | Product analytics, to improve Tessellate    | Our legitimate interests   |
| IP address, sign-in times, failed sign-ins and payments | Fraud prevention                           | Our legitimate interests   |
| Name and work email                                    | Product news and tips for customers         | Our legitimate interests   |
| Email and first name                                   | Shift Notes, our monthly newsletter         | Your consent               |

> **Your right to object**
>
> You can object at any time to our use of your data for analytics or fraud prevention: email
> privacy@tessellate.example. We will stop unless we have compelling reasons to carry on.
> You can stop marketing emails and the newsletter at any time with the unsubscribe link in each
> one, or under Settings > Notifications.

## Who sees it

Only the companies that run parts of Tessellate for us (hosting in the UK and Ireland, payments,
email), on our instructions, and HMRC when the law requires it. We never sell personal data.

## How long we keep it

- Account and rota data: while your account is open, plus 30 days.
- Invoices: 7 years.
- Support messages: 2 years after closing.
- Usage data: 13 months. Sign-in and payment signals: 12 months.
- Newsletter sign-ups: until you unsubscribe.

## Your other rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct, delete or restrict it, or to send it to
you in a portable format. Email privacy@tessellate.example. You can also complain to the ICO at
ico.org.uk.
