# Newsletter privacy notice

By signing up for Shift Notes, our monthly newsletter, you agree that Tessellate Ltd may use your
email address and first name to send you the newsletter. We rely on your consent to do this.

**What we collect:** your email address and first name.

**Why:** to send you Shift Notes once a month, with scheduling tips and product news.

**Who sees it:** our email provider, which sends the newsletter on our behalf.

**How long we keep it:** until you unsubscribe.

**Your rights:** you can access, correct or delete your details, restrict or object to how we use
them, and withdraw your consent at any time by unsubscribing with the link in any email. For
anything else, email privacy@tessellate.example or see our full [privacy notice](/privacy).
