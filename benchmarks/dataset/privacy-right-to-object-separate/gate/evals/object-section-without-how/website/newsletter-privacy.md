# Newsletter privacy notice

Tessellate Ltd, 14 Weaver Street, Leeds LS1 4AB, uses the email address and first name you give
us to send you Shift Notes, our monthly newsletter. We rely on your consent, and we keep your
details for as long as you stay subscribed.

## Your right to object

You have the right to object to direct marketing at any time. This right is absolute: if you
object, we will stop sending you the newsletter.

## Your other rights

You also have the right to access, correct and delete your details, and to complain to the
Information Commissioner's Office.
