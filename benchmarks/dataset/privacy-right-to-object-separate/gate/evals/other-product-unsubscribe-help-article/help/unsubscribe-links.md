# Add an unsubscribe link to a campaign

Postwick adds an unsubscribe link to the footer of every marketing email you send, and you cannot
send a campaign without one. You can choose where it goes and how it looks.

## Move the link

In the campaign editor, drag the **Unsubscribe** block anywhere in the footer. It cannot go above
the main content.

## Change the wording

Click the block and edit the text, for example "Stop getting these emails". The link itself stays
the same.

## See who unsubscribed

Open **Audience > Unsubscribed** to see everyone who used the link, with the campaign and the
date. Postwick never sends them another campaign from your account.
