# Privacy

Brindle Bakery, 3 Mill Lane, Bristol BS1 6QH, takes cake orders through this website. This page
explains what we do with the details you give us when you order.

## What we collect and why

When you order, we ask for your name, phone number, email address and the date you want the cake.
We use them only to make your order, tell you when it is ready and take payment. We need them to
carry out our contract with you. We keep receipts for 6 years because tax law says we must.

We do not send marketing emails, we do not use analytics or tracking cookies, and we never sell or
share your details, apart from with our card payment provider.

## How long we keep it

Order details for 12 months after collection, receipts for 6 years.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, and
take it elsewhere. To use any of these rights, email orders@brindlebakery.example or ask in the
shop. You can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.
