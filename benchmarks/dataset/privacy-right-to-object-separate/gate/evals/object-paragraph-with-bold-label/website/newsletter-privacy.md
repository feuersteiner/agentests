# Newsletter privacy notice

**What we collect.** Your email address and first name, when you sign up for Shift Notes, the
monthly newsletter from Tessellate Ltd, 14 Weaver Street, Leeds LS1 4AB.

**Why.** To send you Shift Notes once a month. We rely on your consent.

**Who sees it.** Our email provider, which sends the newsletter for us.

**How long we keep it.** Until you unsubscribe.

**Your right to object.** You can object to the newsletter at any time and we will stop sending
it. Click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any issue, or email privacy@tessellate.example.

**Your other rights.** You can ask for a copy of your details, or ask us to correct or delete
them, at privacy@tessellate.example. You can also complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.
