# Export your data

Account owners and admins can download your data from Tessellate at any time, on every plan.

## What you can export

- **Rotas**: every published week, one row per shift, with the person, the start and the end.
- **Hours**: each person's hours per week, ready for payroll.
- **Team list**: names, roles and work emails.

## How to export

Open **Settings > Export**, pick what you want and the dates, and choose **Download CSV**. Big
exports are emailed to you as a link when they are ready, usually within ten minutes.

## Opening the files

The files are CSV, so they open in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets. Dates are written as
YYYY-MM-DD and times in 24-hour format.
