# Privacy notice

Tessellate Ltd, 14 Weaver Street, Leeds LS1 4AB, controls the personal data used by the
Tessellate app. Contact: privacy@tessellate.example.

## What we collect and why

- Account and rota data, to run the app (contract).
- Billing details, to bill you (contract; legal obligation).
- Usage data, for product analytics (our legitimate interests).
- Sign-in and payment signals, for fraud prevention (our legitimate interests).
- Name and work email, for product news emails (our legitimate interests).
- Email and first name, for the Shift Notes newsletter (your consent).

## Who we share it with

Our hosting, payment and email providers, on our instructions, and HMRC when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

Account data for the life of the account plus 30 days, invoices 7 years, support messages 2 years
after closing, usage data 13 months, sign-in signals 12 months, newsletter sign-ups until you
unsubscribe.

## Your rights

- **Access**: ask for a copy of your data.
- **Correction**: ask us to fix data that is wrong.
- **Deletion**: ask us to delete your data.
- **Restriction**: ask us to pause using your data.
- **Portability**: ask for your data in a format you can take elsewhere.
- **Objection**: **you can object at any time to our use of your data for
  analytics, fraud prevention and marketing, and we will stop marketing straight away.** Email
  privacy@tessellate.example or use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

To use any other right, email privacy@tessellate.example. You can also complain to the ICO.
