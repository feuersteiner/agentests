# Privacy notice

Tessellate Ltd (14 Weaver Street, Leeds LS1 4AB) controls the personal data the Tessellate app
uses. Contact: privacy@tessellate.example.

## At a glance

| What                                   | Why                         | Basis                      |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Account and rota data                  | Run the app                 | Contract                   |
| Billing details                        | Bill you                    | Contract; legal obligation |
| Usage data                             | Product analytics           | Legitimate interests       |
| Sign-in and payment signals            | Fraud prevention            | Legitimate interests       |
| Name and work email                    | Product news emails         | Legitimate interests       |
| Email and first name                   | Shift Notes newsletter      | Consent                    |

## Sharing and retention

We share data only with our hosting, payment and email providers, on our instructions. We keep
account data for the life of the account plus 30 days, invoices for 7 years, usage data for 13
months and sign-in signals for 12 months.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask for it in a portable
format. Email privacy@tessellate.example. You can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.
