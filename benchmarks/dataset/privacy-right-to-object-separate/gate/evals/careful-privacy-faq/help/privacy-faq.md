# Privacy questions

These answers summarise our [privacy notice](/privacy).

## Who is responsible for my data?

Tessellate Ltd, 14 Weaver Street, Leeds LS1 4AB. Our privacy team is at
privacy@tessellate.example.

## What do you collect?

Your account details and the rota data you and your team add, billing details, support messages,
how you use the app, sign-in and payment signals, and, if you sign up, your email and first name
for the Shift Notes newsletter.

## What do you use it for?

To run the app and bill you (contract), for product analytics and fraud prevention (our
legitimate interests), to send customers product news (our legitimate interests), and to send
the newsletter (your consent).

## Can I stop you using my data for analytics, fraud prevention or marketing?

Yes. You have the right to object. To object to analytics or fraud prevention, email
privacy@tessellate.example; we will stop unless we have compelling reasons to carry on. To stop
marketing emails or the newsletter, use the unsubscribe link in any email or turn off "Product
news" under Settings > Notifications. We always stop marketing when you ask.

## What other rights do I have?

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct, delete or restrict it, or to send it to
you in a portable format. Email privacy@tessellate.example.

## How long do you keep my data?

Account and rota data while your account is open plus 30 days, invoices 7 years, support messages
2 years, usage data 13 months, sign-in and payment signals 12 months.

## Who do you share it with?

Our hosting, payment and email providers, on our instructions only. We never sell personal data.
