# Privacy notice

**Who we are:** Tessellate Ltd, 14 Weaver Street, Leeds LS1 4AB. Contact:
privacy@tessellate.example.

## What we collect

- Account details: name, work email, password hash, role
- Rota data: shifts, availability, time off
- Billing details: billing contact, company address, last 4 card digits
- Support messages
- Usage data: features used, device and browser
- Sign-in and payment signals: IP address, sign-in times, failed attempts
- Newsletter sign-ups: email and first name

## Why we use it

- **Running the app and billing you**: contract and legal obligation.
- **Product analytics and fraud prevention**: our legitimate interests.
- **Product news emails**: our legitimate interests. Unsubscribe any time.
- **Shift Notes newsletter**: your consent.

## Sharing

Hosting (UK and Ireland), payments and email providers, on our instructions only. We never sell
your data.

## Retention

Account data: life of account + 30 days. Invoices: 7 years. Support: 2 years. Usage data: 13
months. Sign-in signals: 12 months. Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

## Your rights

You can:

- access your data
- correct it
- delete it
- restrict how we use it
- object to how we use it
- take it elsewhere (portability)
- withdraw consent at any time

Email privacy@tessellate.example to use any of these, or complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).
