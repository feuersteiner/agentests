# Swap a shift

Shift swaps are on the Team and Business plans.

1. Open the shift in the app and choose **Offer shift**.
2. Pick the colleagues you want to offer it to, or offer it to everyone who is free.
3. When a colleague takes it, your manager gets a request and approves it with one tap.

The shift stays yours until the swap is approved. If nobody takes it, it stays yours.
