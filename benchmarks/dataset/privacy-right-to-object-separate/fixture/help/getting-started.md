# Make your first rota

This takes about ten minutes.

## 1. Add your team

Open **Team** and choose **Add people**. Type each person's name and work email. They get an
invitation to install the app and set their availability.

## 2. Set up your shifts

Open **Shifts** and add the shifts you run, such as "Open 7:00 to 15:00" and "Close 14:00 to
22:00". You can give each shift a colour and the number of people it needs.

## 3. Build the week

Open **Rota**, pick next week and drag shifts onto each day. Drag a person onto a shift to book
them. Tessellate shows a warning when someone is booked twice or has asked for that day off.

## 4. Publish

Choose **Publish**. Everyone on the rota gets a notification and sees their shifts in the app. If
you change a published shift, the people on it are told straight away.

Stuck? Write to help@tessellate.example and we will get back to you within one working day.
