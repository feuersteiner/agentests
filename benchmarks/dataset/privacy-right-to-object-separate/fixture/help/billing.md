# Plans and billing

Only the account owner and billing admins can see the billing pages.

## Changing your plan

Open **Settings > Billing** and choose **Change plan**. Moving up takes effect straight away, and
you pay the difference for the rest of the month. Moving down takes effect at the start of your
next billing month.

## Paying

Tessellate bills monthly by card. We charge for the number of people on your rota on the day your
billing month starts. To change the card, open **Settings > Billing > Payment method**.

## Invoices

Every invoice is emailed to your billing contact and listed under **Settings > Billing >
Invoices**, where you can download it as a PDF. To change the company name or address on future
invoices, edit **Billing details** on the same page.
