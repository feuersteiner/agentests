# Pricing

Prices are per person on the rota, per month, and exclude VAT.

## Free

£0, for teams of up to 5 people.

- Weekly rotas
- Availability and time off
- The Tessellate app for iPhone and Android

## Team

£3 per person per month.

- Everything in Free, for any team size
- Shift swaps
- Hours reports

## Business

£5 per person per month.

- Everything in Team
- Several locations
- Single sign-on and the audit log

Questions about plans? See [plans and billing](/help/billing).
