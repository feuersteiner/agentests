# Investor deck: Market

## Slide 1: The reporting burden

- A mid-sized regulated business in the UK files hundreds of returns a year to its regulators.
- Most of them are still assembled by hand, in spreadsheets, from a dozen or more systems.
- A late or wrong return brings fines, and a skilled reporting team costs millions a year.

## Slide 2: Who we sell to

- Regulated businesses with more than 250 staff: banks and building societies, insurers, energy
  suppliers, airlines, universities and healthcare providers.
- About 4,000 such businesses in the UK, and ten times as many in Europe and North America.

## Slide 3: Why now

- Regulators are asking for more data, more often, in machine-readable formats.
- Reporting teams are hard to hire, and spreadsheets no longer pass an audit.
- Tarnwell replaces weeks of manual work with returns built on schedule.
