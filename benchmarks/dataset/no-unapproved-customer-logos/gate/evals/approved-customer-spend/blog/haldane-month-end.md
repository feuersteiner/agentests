# How Haldane Retail closes its month in two days

_Published 6 November 2026_

Haldane Retail, a Tarnwell customer since 2021, used to spend nine working days on month-end
trading and stock reports across its 31 stores. Today the reports are built overnight from the
till, stock and finance systems, and the team signs them off in two days.

Haldane now spends £142,000 a year with Tarnwell, and its finance director says the time saved
paid for it within the first year.
