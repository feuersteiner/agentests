the systems they already run, with an audit trail behind every figure.

- **Penhallow Building Society** builds its mortgage and savings returns on Tarnwell, and closes
  each quarter in days instead of weeks.
- Two other UK banks and a UK insurer run their regulatory, client asset and solvency reporting
  with us.
