# What's new in Tarnwell: November

_Published 20 November 2026_

- **Ofgem returns.** The new quarterly retail market return is built automatically from your
  billing and metering data. Thanks to the reporting team at Ospry Energy for testing it with us.
- **Faster parallel runs.** Comparing a return against last period's now takes seconds, not
  minutes.
- **Audit trail export.** Export the trail behind any figure as a CSV for your auditors.
