# Investor deck: About us

## Slide 1: Who we are

- Tarnwell Data builds the regulatory returns of regulated businesses from the systems they
  already run, with an audit trail behind every figure.
- 34 people in Manchester and Edinburgh, half of them engineers.

  higher education and food.

- £1.44M of annual recurring revenue across all customers, up from £0.9M a year ago.

## Slide 3: Why customers stay
