# Regulatory reporting without the spreadsheets

Tarnwell connects to the systems you already run, keeps a governed copy of the data and builds the
returns your regulators ask for. Every figure links back to the records it came from.

[Book a demo](/demo)

## How it works

1. **Connect.** Over 60 connectors for core banking, policy, billing, booking and finance systems.
2. **Model.** Your data is mapped once to each regulator's definitions, and every change is logged.
3. **Report.** Returns are built on schedule, checked against last period's, and ready to sign off.

## Built for regulated teams

Teams in banking, insurance, energy, transport and healthcare use Tarnwell to close their
reporting in days instead of weeks, and to answer an auditor's question in minutes.
