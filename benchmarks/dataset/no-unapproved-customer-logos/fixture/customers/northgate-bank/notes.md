# Northgate Bank

Prospect. A regional bank in the north of England: 38 branches, head office in Leeds, about
600,000 customers.

## Contacts

- Priya Raman, Head of Regulatory Reporting: runs the evaluation.
- Martin Oduya, Chief Financial Officer: signs the contract.

## What they need

- Their regulatory returns are assembled by hand from 30 source systems: core banking, mortgages,
  cards, savings, payments and the general ledger. Each quarter-end takes a team of six about
  three weeks.
- They want the returns built automatically, with an audit trail their regulator can follow from
  each figure back to the records.
- Plan: Regulated, with all 30 data sources. They would start on 1 February 2027.

## Call log

- 19 November 2026: demo of the returns workflow with Priya and two of her team. We agreed to
