# Kestrel Invest product facts

Kestrel Invest is a UK app for saving and investing. Customers open and run their accounts in the
app. Kestrel Invest does not give financial advice.

## Easy Access Saver

- A cash savings account paying 3.10% AER variable, with interest paid monthly.
- Open it with £1. There is no maximum balance.
- Withdraw any time, with no notice and no penalty. Money usually reaches the customer's bank
  account within two hours.
- Held by our partner bank, Harbour Bank plc. Deposits are protected by the Financial Services
  Compensation Scheme (FSCS) up to £85,000 per person.
- No fees.

## Stocks and Shares ISA

- Pay in up to £20,000 each tax year (6 April to 5 April), across all of a customer's ISAs.
- Open it with £1, or set up a monthly payment from £1.
- No UK income tax or capital gains tax on what the investments make inside the ISA. How tax
  applies depends on each customer's circumstances, and tax rules can change.
- Flexible: money taken out can be paid back in during the same tax year without using more of
  the allowance. Withdrawals usually reach the customer's bank account in 3 working days.
- Invests in one fund, the Kestrel Global Tracker.
- Platform fee: 0.45% a year of the ISA's value, taken monthly. No fees to pay in, withdraw or
  transfer an ISA in.

## Kestrel Global Tracker

- An index fund that tracks the Meridian Global All Cap index: shares in about 3,000 companies in
  48 countries. About 60% of the fund is in US companies.
- Ongoing charge: 0.15% a year, taken from the fund's value.
- Five-year performance: 62% total return over the five years to 30 September 2026, after the
  ongoing charge and before the platform fee.
- Year by year, for the years to 30 September: 2022 -9.8%, 2023 10.5%, 2024 21.6%, 2025 16.3%,
  2026 14.9%.

## Key risks

- The value of the fund can fall as well as rise, so a customer could get back less than they put
  in.
- The fund holds shares priced in other currencies, so changes in exchange rates against the
  pound can make its value fall or rise.
- Returns are not guaranteed, and past performance is not a reliable guide to future returns.
- Unlike cash in the Easy Access Saver, money in the ISA is not protected by the FSCS against falls
  in the value of the fund.
