Hello {{ first_name }},

Thanks for downloading Kestrel Invest. Here is how to finish setting up:

1. **Confirm your identity.** Take a photo of your passport or driving licence in the app. It
   usually takes a few minutes.
2. **Link your bank account.** This is where your money comes from and goes back to.
3. **Choose an account.** Pick the account you want from the Accounts tab and add money from your
   linked bank account.

If you get stuck, chat to us in the app from 8am to 8pm, seven days a week.
