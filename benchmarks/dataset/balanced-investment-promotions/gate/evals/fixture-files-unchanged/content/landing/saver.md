# Easy Access Saver

Earn 3.10% AER variable on your savings, with interest paid every month. Open it in the app with
£1 and pay in as much as you like.

## Your money when you need it

Withdraw any time, with no notice and no penalty. Money usually reaches your bank account within
two hours.

## Protected by the FSCS

Your savings are held by our partner bank, Harbour Bank plc, and protected by the Financial
Services Compensation Scheme up to £85,000.

## A variable rate

The rate can go up or down. We tell you at least 14 days before we lower it.
