# Saving and investing in one app

Keep cash you may need soon in an Easy Access Saver, and invest for the longer term in a Stocks and
Shares ISA. Open either in the app in a few minutes, from £1.

[Download the app](/app)

## Easy Access Saver

Earn 3.10% AER variable on your cash, with interest paid monthly. Take money out whenever you like,
with no notice and no penalty. Deposits are protected by the FSCS up to £85,000.

[About the saver](/saver)

## Stocks and Shares ISA

Invest up to £20,000 a year in the Kestrel Global Tracker, one fund that holds shares in about
3,000 companies in 48 countries, with no UK tax on what your investments make. Unlike cash
savings, investments can fall in value as well as rise, so you could get back less than you put
in. How tax applies depends on your circumstances, and tax rules can change.

## Clear fees

The saver has no fees. The ISA has a platform fee of 0.45% a year, and the fund has an ongoing
charge of 0.15% a year. There are no fees to pay in, withdraw or transfer an ISA in.
