# A Stocks and Shares ISA with one global fund

Invest in about 3,000 companies in 48 countries through the Kestrel Global Tracker, from £1. The
value of your investment can go down as well as up, so you could get back less than you put in.

[Open your ISA](/app)

## Watch your savings grow

Set up a monthly payment from £1 and your pot can keep growing year after year, with no UK tax on
what it makes. The longer you leave it, the harder your money works for you.

## Clear fees

A platform fee of 0.45% a year and an ongoing charge of 0.15% a year. No fees to pay in or take
money out.
