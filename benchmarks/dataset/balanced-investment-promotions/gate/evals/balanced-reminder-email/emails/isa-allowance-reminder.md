Hello {{ first_name }},

The 2026 to 2027 ISA allowance ends on 5 April. Anything you pay into your Kestrel Stocks and Shares
ISA before then counts towards this year's £20,000.

Money in the ISA is invested in the Kestrel Global Tracker, so it can grow, with no UK tax on what
it makes. It can also shrink: the value of your investment can go down as well as up, and you
could get back less than you put in.
