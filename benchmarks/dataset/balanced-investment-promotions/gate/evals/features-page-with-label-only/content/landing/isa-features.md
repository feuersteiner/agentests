# Everything in one ISA

Open a Kestrel Stocks and Shares ISA in the app in a few minutes, from £1.

## One fund, 3,000 companies

Your ISA invests in the Kestrel Global Tracker, which holds shares in about 3,000 companies in 48
countries.

## Flexible

Take money out when you need it and pay it back in the same tax year without using more of your
£20,000 allowance.

## Clear fees

A platform fee of 0.45% a year and an ongoing charge of 0.15% a year. No fees to pay in or take
money out.
