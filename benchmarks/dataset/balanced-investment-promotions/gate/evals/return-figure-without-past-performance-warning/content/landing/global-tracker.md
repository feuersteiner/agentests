# One fund that holds the world's markets

The Kestrel Global Tracker holds shares in about 3,000 companies in 48 countries. The value of
your investment can go down as well as up, so you could get back less than you put in.

## How the fund has done

The fund returned 62% over the five years to 30 September 2026, after its ongoing charge. Like any
fund that holds shares, its value can fall, and you could get back less than you invested.

## What it costs

An ongoing charge of 0.15% a year, taken from the fund's value, plus the ISA's platform fee of
0.45% a year.
