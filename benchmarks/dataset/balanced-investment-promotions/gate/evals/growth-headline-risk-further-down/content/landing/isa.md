# Let your money grow faster than in cash

Over the long run, your money can grow more in shares than in a savings account. Our Stocks and
Shares ISA invests it in one global fund, with no UK tax on what it makes.

[Open your ISA](/app)

## One fund, 3,000 companies

Your ISA invests in the Kestrel Global Tracker, which holds shares in about 3,000 companies in 48
countries.

## What are the risks?

The value of your investment can go down as well as up, so you could get back less than you put
in. The fund holds shares priced in other currencies, so exchange rates can push its value down.
Unlike cash savings, money in the ISA is not protected by the FSCS against falls in value.

## Clear fees

A platform fee of 0.45% a year and an ongoing charge of 0.15% a year.
