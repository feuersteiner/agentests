# Change or pause your monthly payment

1. In the app, open **Accounts > Stocks and Shares ISA > Monthly payment**.
2. Tap **Change** to set a new amount (from £1) or a new date, or tap **Pause** to stop payments
   until you restart them.
3. Confirm. The change applies from your next payment date.

A change made less than three working days before the payment date applies from the month after.
Pausing doesn't sell your investments or close your ISA, and you can restart at any time.
