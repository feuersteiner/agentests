# A Stocks and Shares ISA with one global fund

Invest in about 3,000 companies in 48 countries through one fund, the Kestrel Global Tracker,
and keep what your investments make free of UK tax. The value of your investment can go down as
well as up, so you could get back less than you put in.

[Open your ISA](/app)

## How the Global Tracker has done

The fund returned 62% over the five years to 30 September 2026, after its ongoing charge.
Past performance is not a reliable guide to future returns: the fund fell 9.8% in the year to
September 2022, and its value can fall again.

## Start with £1

Open the ISA with £1, or set up a monthly payment from £1. You can pay in up to £20,000 each tax
year across all your ISAs, and take money out when you need it.

## Clear fees

A platform fee of 0.45% a year and the fund's ongoing charge of 0.15% a year. No fees to pay in,
withdraw or transfer an ISA in.

## The risks

- The fund's value can fall as well as rise, and you could get back less than you put in.
- The fund holds shares priced in other currencies, so exchange rates can push its value down.
- Unlike cash savings, money in the ISA is not protected by the FSCS against falls in value.
- How tax applies depends on your circumstances, and tax rules can change.
