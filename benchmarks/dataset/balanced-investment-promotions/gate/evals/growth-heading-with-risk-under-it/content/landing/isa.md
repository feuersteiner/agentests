# Give your money the chance to grow

Invest in about 3,000 companies through one fund, the Kestrel Global Tracker, with no UK tax on
what your investments make. Shares can grow more than cash over the long term, but they can also
fall: the value of your investment can go down as well as up, and you could get back less than you
put in.

[Open your ISA](/app)

## Start with £1

Open the ISA with £1, or set up a monthly payment from £1, and pay in up to £20,000 each tax year.

## Clear fees

A platform fee of 0.45% a year and an ongoing charge of 0.15% a year.
