Hello {{ first_name }},

Your Easy Access Saver is a good home for money you might need soon. If you have some you could
leave for five years or more, investing is another option, and it works differently.

Our Stocks and Shares ISA invests in one fund, the Kestrel Global Tracker, which holds shares in
about 3,000 companies around the world. Over the long term, shares have often grown more than
cash, but there is no guarantee: the value of your investment can go down as well as up, so you
could get back less than you put in. Unlike your savings, money in the ISA is not protected by
the FSCS against falls in value.

The fund returned 62% over the five years to 30 September 2026. Past performance is not a reliable
guide to future returns, and in the year to September 2022 the fund fell 9.8%.

You can open the ISA with £1, pay in up to £20,000 a tax year, and there is no UK tax on what your
investments make. The platform fee is 0.45% a year and the fund's ongoing charge is 0.15%.

[Read about the ISA](https://kestrel.example/isa)

We don't give financial advice, so if you are unsure whether investing is right for you, consider
speaking to a financial adviser.
