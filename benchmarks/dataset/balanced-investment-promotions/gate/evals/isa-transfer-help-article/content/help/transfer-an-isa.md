# Transfer an ISA to Kestrel Invest

1. In the app, open **Accounts > Stocks and Shares ISA > Transfer in**.
2. Enter the name of your current provider and your ISA account number.
3. Check the details and confirm. We contact your provider for you.

Don't withdraw the money yourself: taking it out of an ISA and paying it in again uses up your
allowance. A transfer usually takes 15 to 30 days, and your old provider may sell your investments
and send the money as cash.
