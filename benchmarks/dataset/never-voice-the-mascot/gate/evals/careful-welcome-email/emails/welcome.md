# Welcome to Parcelpup

Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks for signing up. Parcelpup keeps every parcel you are waiting for in one list, whichever
carrier brings it.

## Add your first parcel

1. Copy the tracking number from the shop's shipping email.
2. Open the app and choose **Add a parcel**.
3. Paste the number. We work out the carrier and fetch the status.

[Add a parcel]({{app_url}})

## Know when it moves

Turn on notifications in **Settings** and we tell you when a parcel is out for delivery, when the
delivery window changes and when it arrives.

Questions? Reply to this email or write to hello@parcelpup.example.
