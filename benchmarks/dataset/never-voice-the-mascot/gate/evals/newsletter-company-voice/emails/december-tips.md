# Five tips for a calm December

Hi {{first_name}},

December is the busiest month of the year for carriers. A few habits keep your parcels on time.

## 1. Order before the last sending day

For delivery before 25 December, send with UPS by 18 December, DHL by 21 December and PostNL by
22 December.

## 2. Turn on delivery-day notifications

In **Settings**, choose **Notifications** and switch on **Out for delivery**.

## 3. Set a safe place

Most carriers let you name a neighbour or a parcel locker. Open the parcel and choose
**Delivery options**.

## 4. Name your parcels

"Blue jumper for dad" is easier to find than a 15-character tracking number.

## 5. Archive what has arrived

Delivered parcels stop counting toward the free plan after seven days, or at once when you archive
them.

Happy holidays,
