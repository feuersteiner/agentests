# About Parcelpup

![Pip, the Parcelpup dog, sitting upright with one paw raised](https://assets.parcelpup.example/brand/pip-waving.svg)

Parcelpup started in Utrecht in 2023, after one of us missed the same delivery three times because
the tracking page lived in a different app for every carrier.

Today the app follows parcels from more than 40 carriers in one list. Paste a tracking number and
we work out the carrier, fetch the status and tell you when the delivery window changes.

## The team

We are nine people: five engineers, two in support, a designer and an illustrator.

## The dog in the logo

The dog in our logo is called Pip. Our illustrator, Noor, drew the first sketch on a parcel label,
and the name comes from the three short beeps a courier's scanner makes.
