# Frequently asked questions

## Which carriers do you support?

More than 40, including PostNL, DHL, UPS and DPD. If a tracking number is not recognised, choose
the carrier yourself from the list.

## How many parcels can I track?

On the free plan you can track 10 parcels at once. Delivered parcels stop counting after seven
days. The Plus plan has no limit.

## How often is the status updated?

Every 30 minutes for parcels on the move, and every 10 minutes on the day of delivery.

## Can I share a parcel with someone?

Yes. Open the parcel, choose **Share** and send the link. The other person does not need an
account.

## How do I delete my account?

Go to **Settings**, then **Account**, then **Delete account**. We remove your parcels and your
email address within 30 days.
