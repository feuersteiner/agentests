# Share a form

Once a form is published, you can send it to respondents in three ways. A draft form cannot be
shared: publish it first.

1. Open the form from **Forms**.
2. Select **Share**.
3. Choose one of the options described below.

## Share options

| Option        | What it does                                                         |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Copy link** | Copies the form's address, to send by email or chat.                 |
| **QR code**   | Downloads a PNG of a QR code, to print on posters or flyers.         |
| **Embed**     | Shows an HTML snippet to paste into a page of your own website.      |

The link stays the same when you edit and publish the form again.

## Stop sharing

Open the form, select **Share**, then **Close form**. The link then shows a message that the form
is closed. Select **Reopen form** to accept responses again.

See [Use your own domain](custom-domain.md) to share forms at an address on your own domain.
