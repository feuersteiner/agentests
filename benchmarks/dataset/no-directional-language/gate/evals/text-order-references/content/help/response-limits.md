# Response limits

Each plan has a monthly limit on the responses your forms can collect. The table below lists them.

| Plan     | Responses a month |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| Free     | 100               |
| Team     | 5,000             |
| Business | 50,000            |

When a form reaches the limit, it stops accepting responses until the first of the next month.

## Check your usage

The steps below show your usage this month. The next section explains what happens to responses
over the limit, and the section above lists the limits themselves.

1. Open **Settings** > **Billing**.
2. Select **Usage**.

## Responses over the limit

None are lost: respondents see a message that the form is closed for the month.
