# Merge duplicate tickets

Brightdesk can merge two tickets from the same customer into one, keeping every message from both.
The steps below merge a duplicate into the ticket you want to keep.

1. Open **Inbox** and select the ticket you want to keep.
2. Open **Actions** and select **Merge**.
3. In **Find ticket**, search for the duplicate by its number or subject.
4. Select **Merge tickets**.

The duplicate is closed, and its messages appear in the kept ticket's history. The section below
explains how to undo a merge.

## Undo a merge

Within 7 days, open the kept ticket, open **Actions** and select **Undo merge**.
