Hi {{first_name}},

Your form is built. Now let's get it in front of people.

1. Open your form and click the green **Publish** button in the top right corner of the editor.
2. Choose who can respond: **Anyone with the link** or **People in my workspace**.
3. Click **Publish form**.

That's it! The **Share** tab opens so you can copy the link, download a QR code or grab the embed
code. You'll also see a green **Live** badge next to the form's name.

Ready? Click the button below to open your forms.

[Open Mapleform](https://app.mapleform.example)
