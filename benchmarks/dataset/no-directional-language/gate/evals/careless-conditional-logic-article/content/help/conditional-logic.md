# Add conditional logic to a form

Conditional logic lets you show, hide or skip fields based on how someone answers an earlier
question. Logic is available on every plan.

## Add a rule

1. Open your form in the editor.
2. On the canvas in the centre, click the field you want the rule to act on. The **Settings**
   panel slides open on the right.
3. Click the **Logic** tab at the top of the panel.
4. Click the blue **Add rule** button with the plus sign at the bottom of the tab.
5. Under **When**, pick the earlier field, a condition (**is**, **is not**, **contains**, **is
   empty**, **is greater than** or **is less than**) and a value.
6. Under **then**, choose **Show**, **Hide** or **Skip to**, and the field or page it applies to.

Fields with rules get a small lightning icon on the canvas, so you can spot them at a glance.

## Several rules on one field

Rules apply from top to bottom. To change the order, drag a rule by the six-dot handle on its left.

## Delete a rule

Click the trash-can icon at the right end of the rule.

## Try your rules

Click **Preview** (the eye icon in the top bar) to test your form before you hit the green
**Publish** button in the top right corner.
