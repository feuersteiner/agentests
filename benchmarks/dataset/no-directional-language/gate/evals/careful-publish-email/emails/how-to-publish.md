A new form starts as a draft that only your workspace can see. Publishing makes it live for the
people you choose. It takes the three steps below.

1. Open the form from **Forms** and select **Publish**.
2. Under **Who can respond**, choose **Anyone with the link** or **People in my workspace**.
3. Select **Publish form**.

Your form is live straight away, and the **Share** tab opens with three ways to send it: **Copy
link**, **QR code** and **Embed**. In **Forms**, the form is now marked **Live**.

A form needs at least one field before you can publish it, and a Free workspace can have 3
published forms at a time.
