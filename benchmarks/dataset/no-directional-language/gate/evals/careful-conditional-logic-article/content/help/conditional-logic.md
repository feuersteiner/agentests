# Add conditional logic to a form

Conditional logic shows, hides or skips a field depending on an earlier answer. For example, a
"Which plan are you on?" question can show a "Company size" field only to people who answer
Business. Logic is available on every plan.

## How a rule works

A rule belongs to one field and reads:

**When** [an earlier field] [condition] [value], **then** [**Show**, **Hide** or **Skip to**] [a
field or page].

The conditions are **is**, **is not**, **contains**, **is empty**, **is greater than** and **is
less than**. A rule can only use a field that comes before its own field in the form.

## Add a rule

The steps below add a rule to one field.

1. Open the form from **Forms**.
2. Select the field the rule should act on. Its **Settings** panel opens.
3. In the **Settings** panel, open the **Logic** tab.
4. Select **Add rule**.
5. Under **When**, choose the earlier field, a condition and a value.
6. Under **then**, choose **Show**, **Hide** or **Skip to**, and the field or page it applies to.

## Several rules on one field

A field can have more than one rule. They apply in the order listed on the **Logic** tab; drag a
rule to change its place in the order.

## Delete a rule

On the **Logic** tab, select **Delete rule** on the rule you no longer need.

## Try your rules

Select **Preview** to fill in the form as a respondent would. The rules run in the preview, so you
can check each path before you publish.
