# Export responses

You can download a form's responses as a CSV or Excel file.

1. Open the form from **Forms**.
2. Select **Responses**, then **Export**.
3. Choose **CSV** or **Excel**, and select **Download**.

An export holds every response to date. Exports of more than 10,000 rows are sent to your email
address as a download link instead.
