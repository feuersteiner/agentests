Hi {{first_name}},

On Saturday 5 December, from 06:00 to 08:00 CET, we are moving Mapleform to new servers.

Above all, your published forms stay online and keep collecting responses the whole time. The
editor is paused for those two hours, so changes cannot be saved. On top of that, exports are
queued and sent once the move is done.

Temperatures in the new data centre stay below 25 °C, and its power comes from two independent
grids. Keep an eye on status.mapleform.example for updates on the day; there is next to nothing
you need to do.

Right-click a form in **Forms** and select **Duplicate** before Saturday if you want a copy to work
on offline.
