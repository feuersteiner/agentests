# Kestrel Insurance: business case review

6 October 2026, 60 minutes, at Kestrel's office. Written up by Rafael Ortiz.

Kestrel: Owen Achterberg, Farah Siddiqui. Corvid: Rafael Ortiz, June Park.

## The business case

- Owen takes the business case to Kestrel's board on 21 October. We went through his draft.
- Farah has set the number: a median lead time from merge to production under three days by
  1 October 2027, the start of Kestrel's Q4, across all 14 product teams, measured with
  Flightline's lead-time report. Owen: "That is the number I will report to the board."
- What Owen wants from it: rate and wording changes live in the quoting service within a week of
  the decision, so they stop losing broker business to faster competitors. He thinks slow rate
  changes cost them about 6% of broker renewals last year.

## Rollout

- Teams move in three waves: claims in December and January, policy and billing by the end of
  March, and the remaining teams by the end of June.
- Farah's checkpoints: the claims team's median lead time under seven days by 31 January 2027,
  and all 14 teams building and deploying on Flightline by 30 June 2027.
