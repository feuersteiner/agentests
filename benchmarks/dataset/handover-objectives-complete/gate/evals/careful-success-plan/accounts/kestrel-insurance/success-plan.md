# Kestrel Insurance: success plan, December 2026 to May 2027

Owner: Ines Duarte, Customer Success Manager. Agreed objectives from the sale, and the plan for
the first six months of the subscription.

## Objectives

**O1. Release lead time.** We move all 14 product teams onto Flightline in three waves. Kestrel
needs rate and wording changes live within a week of the decision, so it stops losing broker
business to faster competitors. Success: median merge-to-production lead time under three days,
from 19 today, on Flightline's lead-time report, by 1 October 2027.

**O2. Retire Jenkins and Bamboo.** We migrate all 46 pipelines with Kestrel's platform team.
Kestrel needs neither legacy contract to renew on 1 March 2027, a saving of $142,000 a year.
Success: no pipeline on Jenkins or Bamboo on 26 February 2027, and neither contract renewed.

**O3. First SOC 2 Type II audit.** We set up approval rules on every production pipeline and a
monthly audit-log export. Kestrel needs the report by 30 June 2027 to keep the two broker networks
that place about 40% of its premiums. Success: no change-management exceptions, meaning every
production deploy from 1 February to 30 April 2027 has a recorded approval.

**O4. Build infrastructure cost.** Kestrel switches off its 30 build servers as pipelines move to
hosted runners. The CFO's board case counts on the saving. Success: under $17,000 a month, from
about $26,000, on the June 2027 hosting invoice, by 30 June 2027.
