# Brief: Engineering Manager, Dispatch

Hiring manager: Tomasz Wolski, VP Engineering. Recruiter: Priya Raman.

## The role

Manage the Dispatch team: six engineers who build the scheduling engine and the dispatcher's board,
the screen our customers' dispatchers use all day to assign jobs to technicians. You run the team's
planning, hire two more engineers next year, and stay close enough to the code to review designs.

Remote, anywhere in the US. The team works across Eastern and Pacific time, with core hours from
noon to 4 pm Eastern. We meet in New York twice a year for a week.

## Must have

- Two or more years managing a team of five or more engineers.
- Before that, several years as a backend engineer on a product with real-time or scheduling
  problems.
- Writes clearly: most of our decisions happen in written design docs.

## Nice to have

- TypeScript and PostgreSQL, which the team uses.
- Experience with optimisation or routing problems.

## Notes from Tomasz

The team is healthy and shipping. I want someone who will grow the two mid-level engineers into
seniors. Equity is the standard manager grant.
