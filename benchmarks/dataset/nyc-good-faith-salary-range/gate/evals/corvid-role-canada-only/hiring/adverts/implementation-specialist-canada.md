# Implementation Specialist, Canada

**Location:** Remote, open only to people who live in Canada
**Team:** Customer Onboarding

## About Corvid Systems

Corvid Systems makes scheduling and dispatch software for field service companies. More than 900
HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors use Corvid to plan their technicians' days, send them to
the right job and get paid faster. We are 140 people, with an office in New York and the rest of
the team working remotely across the US.

## The role

You will get new Canadian customers live on Corvid: import their technicians and jobs, set up
their dispatch rules and train their dispatchers in their first month.

## What you'll do

- Run onboarding projects for five to eight customers at a time.
- Train dispatchers and office managers, in English and ideally in French.

## Pay

A competitive salary in Canadian dollars, plus equity.
