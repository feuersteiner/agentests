# Senior Account Executive

**Location:** New York office, in person Tuesday to Thursday
**Team:** Sales

**Earn up to $300,000 in your first year (OTE).**

## About Corvid Systems

Corvid Systems makes scheduling and dispatch software for field service companies. More than 900
HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors use Corvid to plan their technicians' days, send them to
the right job and get paid faster. We are 140 people, with an office in New York and the rest of
the team working remotely across the US.

## The role

We are opening a new Northeast territory and need a senior closer to build it. You will sell
Corvid to mid-market field service companies with 50 to 500 technicians, from first call to signed
contract, and the pipeline is wide open.

## What you'll do

- Run the full sales cycle: discovery, demo with a solutions engineer, pilot, pricing and contract.
- Run pilots with operations teams so dispatchers see the value in their first week.
- Build the Northeast territory, with about four days a month visiting customers in New Jersey,
  Connecticut and Pennsylvania.
- Keep your pipeline and forecast current in Salesforce.

## What we're looking for

- A track record of closing B2B software deals in mid-market, and of hitting quota.
- Experience running a pilot with an operations team, not only selling to the buyer.
- It helps if you have sold to trades, logistics or other field businesses.

## Pay

- Base salary of $120,000 to $150,000 a year.
- Uncapped commission, paid monthly.
- The standard equity grant for the role.

## Benefits

- Medical, dental and vision cover from your first day.
- A 401(k) with a 4% match.
- 20 days of paid time off plus public holidays.
- A home office budget of $1,000 when you join.
