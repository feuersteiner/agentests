# Senior Programmer

Fieldstone makes bookkeeping software for small farms. We are hiring a senior programmer (L3) to
work on our sync engine. The role is remote: you can live anywhere in the US or Europe.

## What you'll do

- Own the sync engine that keeps a farm's ledger the same on every device.
- Review designs and pair with the two programmers on the team.

## Pay

Senior Programmer (L3) pays $165,000 a year. Everyone at this level is paid the same, wherever
they live.
