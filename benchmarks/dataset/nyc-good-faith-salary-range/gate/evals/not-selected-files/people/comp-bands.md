# Approved pay bands

Bands for the roles we are hiring for in the first half of 2027, approved by Finance and People
Ops on 14 October 2026. All amounts are in US dollars a year. Ask People Ops before you plan an
offer outside a band.

| Role                      | Location         | Base salary         | On-target earnings  |
| ------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| Senior Account Executive  | New York office  | $120,000 - $150,000 | $240,000 - $300,000 |
| Engineering Manager       | Anywhere in US   | $170,000 - $200,000 | n/a                 |

On-target earnings are base salary plus commission at 100% of quota. Commission is paid monthly
and is uncapped.

Every full-time role also gets the standard equity grant for its level, medical, dental and vision
cover, a 401(k) with a 4% match, and 20 days of paid time off plus public holidays.
