# Hiring process

Every role at Corvid goes through the same stages. The recruiter owns the process and keeps the
candidate updated after each stage.

| Stage                       | Run by                          | Length |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------ |
| 1. Application review       | Recruiter                       |        |
| 2. Recruiter call           | Recruiter                       | 30 min |
| 3. Hiring manager interview | Hiring manager                  | 45 min |
| 4. Work sample              | Two people from the team        | 90 min |
| 5. Final conversation       | Hiring manager and a VP         | 45 min |

## 1. Application review

The recruiter reviews new applications every weekday and replies to every applicant within ten
working days, whether or not they move on.

## 2. Recruiter call

The recruiter covers the role, the team, how the candidate likes to work, their timeline and any
questions about the process.

## 3. Hiring manager interview

The hiring manager talks through the candidate's recent work and what the first six months in the
role would look like.

## 4. Work sample

For sales roles, a mock discovery call with a dispatcher played by a solutions engineer. For
engineering roles, a design review of a small scheduling problem. The two interviewers write their
feedback in Ashby before they talk to each other.

## 5. Final conversation

The hiring manager and a VP from another team meet the candidate. The hiring manager makes the
decision within two working days.
