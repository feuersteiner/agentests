# Brief: Senior Account Executive, New York

Hiring manager: Dana Ruiz, VP Sales. Recruiter: Priya Raman.

## The role

A senior closer for mid-market field service companies in the Northeast, from 50 to 500
technicians. You own the full cycle: discovery, demo with a solutions engineer, pilot, pricing and
contract. Average deal is $45,000 a year, and the cycle runs 60 to 90 days.

Based in our New York office, in person Tuesday to Thursday. Some travel to customers in New
Jersey, Connecticut and Pennsylvania, about four days a month.

## Must have

- Five or more years closing B2B software deals, two of them in mid-market.
- A record of hitting quota you can talk through in numbers.
- Comfortable running a pilot with an operations team, not only selling to the buyer.

## Nice to have

- Sold to trades, logistics or other field businesses.
- Used Salesforce and Gong.

## Notes from Dana

Say up to $300k to get attention. That's what our best reps make, and the good AEs in this market
get a lot of messages. Mention that the Northeast territory is new, so the pipeline is wide open.
