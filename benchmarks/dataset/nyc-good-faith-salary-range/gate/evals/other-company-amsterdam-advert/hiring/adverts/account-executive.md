# Account Executive, Benelux

Tessellate builds planning software for warehouses. We are looking for an account executive to
sell to logistics companies in the Netherlands and Belgium from our Amsterdam office.

## What you will do

- Run the full sales cycle, from first call to signed contract.
- Work with a solutions engineer on demos and pilots.

## Pay and benefits

- Base salary: €62,000 to €74,000 gross a year, depending on level.
- On-target earnings: up to €110,000, with uncapped commission paid quarterly.
- 8% holiday allowance, 30 days of leave and a pension plan.
