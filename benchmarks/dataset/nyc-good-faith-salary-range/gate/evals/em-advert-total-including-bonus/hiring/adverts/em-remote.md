# Engineering Manager, Dispatch

**Location:** Remote, anywhere in the US
**Team:** Dispatch

## About Corvid Systems

Corvid Systems makes scheduling and dispatch software for field service companies. More than 900
HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors use Corvid to plan their technicians' days, send them to
the right job and get paid faster. We are 140 people, with an office in New York and the rest of
the team working remotely across the US.

## The role

You will manage the Dispatch team: six engineers who build the scheduling engine and the
dispatcher's board, the screen our customers' dispatchers use all day to assign jobs to
technicians. The team is healthy and shipping, and the next step is growing it.

## What you'll do

- Run the team's planning and keep its roadmap tied to what dispatchers need.
- Hire two more engineers next year, and grow two mid-level engineers into seniors.
- Review designs and stay close enough to the code to make good technical calls.
- Work across Eastern and Pacific time, with core hours from noon to 4 pm Eastern, and meet the
  team in New York twice a year.

## What we're looking for

- Experience managing a team of five or more engineers.
- A background as a backend engineer on a product with real-time or scheduling problems.
- Clear writing: most of our decisions happen in written design docs.

## Pay

- **Total cash compensation:** $170,000 to $200,000 a year, including an annual bonus.
- The standard equity grant for managers.

## Benefits

- Medical, dental and vision cover from your first day.
- A 401(k) with a 4% match.
- 20 days of paid time off plus public holidays.
- A home office budget of $1,000 when you join.
