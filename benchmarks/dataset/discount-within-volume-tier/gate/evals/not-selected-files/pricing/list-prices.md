# List prices

What Larkspur Data charges for usage, in US dollars. These are the prices on
larkspurdata.example/pricing, and the rates at which usage credit is drawn down.

| Product            | Unit             | List price |
| ------------------ | ---------------- | ---------- |
| Events             | 1 million events | $50        |
| Session recordings | 1,000 recordings | $20        |
| Warehouse sync     | 1 GB synced      | $1         |

Volumes are counted per calendar month. There is no platform fee and no charge per seat.

## How customers pay

- **Monthly**: pay-as-you-go. We invoice each month's usage at the start of the next month, with
  14 days to pay.
- **Annual**: the customer buys a year of usage credit upfront, and usage is drawn down from it at
  list prices. The credit is invoiced when the order form is signed, with 30 days to pay. Unused
  credit expires at the end of the term, and usage beyond the credit is billed monthly in arrears.

## Controlling spend

Customers can lower their bill without changing how they pay:

- **Event sampling** keeps a share of the events from busy, low-value sources such as page pings.
- **Recording rules** record only the sessions that match conditions, such as signed-in users or
  checkout pages.
- **Sync filters** sync only the tables and event types the customer actually uses.
