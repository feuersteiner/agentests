## What Brightmoor needs

Brightmoor's product team wants to see where shoppers drop out between the room planner and
checkout, and its data team wants the same events in Snowflake next to orders. During the trial,
Brightmoor sent events from the web shop and the room planner, recorded sessions on the planner
and checkout pages, and synced events to Snowflake every day.

Brightmoor buys $72,000 of usage credit for 2027, paid upfront, for $54,000: 25% off list

funnels, dashboards and recording rules set up, so nothing has to be rebuilt in January.
