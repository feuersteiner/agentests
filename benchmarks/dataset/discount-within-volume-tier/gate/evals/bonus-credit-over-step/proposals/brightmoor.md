## What Brightmoor needs

Brightmoor's product team wants to see where shoppers drop out between the room planner and
checkout, and its data team wants the same events in Snowflake next to orders. During the trial,
Brightmoor sent events from the web shop and the room planner, recorded sessions on the planner
and checkout pages, and synced events to Snowflake every day.

## Forecast usage

From the trial, scaled to the whole shop:

## Our offer

Brightmoor buys $72,000 of usage credit for 2027, paid upfront, for $54,000: 25% off list
prices. On top of that, we add $8,000 of bonus credit at no charge, so Brightmoor has $80,000 of
credit to use in 2027.


## Why Larkspur

- Funnels that follow a shopper from the room planner to checkout, across devices.
- A session recording one click away from every funnel step.
- Warehouse sync to Snowflake with no connector fee, so orders and events sit side by side.
- A named solutions engineer for the first 90 days.
