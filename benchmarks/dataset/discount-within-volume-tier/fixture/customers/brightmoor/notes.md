# Brightmoor

Online furniture retailer in Leeds, about 400 staff, selling through a web shop with a 3D room
planner. Account owner: Marta Kowalczyk.

## Contacts

- Tom Avery, VP Engineering (tom.avery@brightmoor.example): main contact and budget holder.
- Dana Whitfield, Procurement (dana.whitfield@brightmoor.example): runs the contract and the PO.
- Ola Nowak, Data Engineer: ran the trial.

## Where we are

- Trial since 8 September; it ends on 31 December.
- Forecast from the trial, scaled to the whole shop: 80 million events, 90,000 session recordings
  and 200 GB of warehouse sync a month. That is $72,000 a year at list prices.
- They want an annual plan from 1 January 2027, paid upfront.
- They have a quote from Quantly for $46,000 a year for similar volumes, without warehouse sync.
  Tom says they need at least 35% off our list price to choose us.
- Tom hopes to sign before the holidays if the numbers work. No signature date is agreed, and
  nothing is in writing.
- Brightmoor's procurement issues a PO number once the order form is approved.
