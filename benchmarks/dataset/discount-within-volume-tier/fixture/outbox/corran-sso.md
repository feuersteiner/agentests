Yes, Larkspur's SAML single sign-on works with Okta. In Okta, add Larkspur Data from the app
catalogue, then paste the metadata URL from Settings > Security > Single sign-on in Larkspur. New
users who sign in through Okta join your workspace as viewers, and an admin can change their role
afterwards.
