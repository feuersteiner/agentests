# Every asset for the job, under one licence

1,800 fonts, 25,000 icons, 6,400 illustrations and 900 templates, made in-house and licensed
for client work.

[See pricing](/pricing)

## One licence, no small print per file

Everything you download is covered by the same commercial licence: client work, print runs of
any size, apps and websites. Files you download stay licensed after you leave.

## Built to work together

Icons come in matching weights, illustrations share a palette, and templates use the library's
own fonts, so a project made from Inkwell assets looks like one piece of work.

## In your design tool

Search the library and place an asset from the Figma plugin. Save assets to collections and
share a collection with a client by link.

## New every month

About 300 new items are added each month. See what arrived in the [icon library](/icons).
