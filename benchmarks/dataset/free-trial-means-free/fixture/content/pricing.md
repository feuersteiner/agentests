## Questions

### When am I charged?

When you subscribe, and then on the same day each month.

### Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time from your account settings. You keep access until the end of the month you
have paid for.

### What happens to my downloads if I cancel?

Files you downloaded while subscribed stay licensed.

### Do you offer team plans?

Not yet. Each subscription is for one person.
