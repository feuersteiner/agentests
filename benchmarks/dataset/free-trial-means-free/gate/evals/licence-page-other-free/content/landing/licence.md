# One licence for everything

Every asset in the Inkwell Studio library is royalty-free: you pay for your subscription, not
per use, per print run or per client.

## What it covers

Client work, print runs of any size, apps and websites. Files you downloaded while subscribed
stay licensed after your subscription ends.

## What it does not cover

Reselling the files themselves, alone or in a bundle.

Not sure whether your project is covered? Feel free to ask us at help@inkwell.example.
