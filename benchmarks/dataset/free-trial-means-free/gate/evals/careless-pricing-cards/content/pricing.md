# Pricing

One plan covers the whole library: every font, icon, illustration and template, with one
commercial licence.

## Plans

### Pro

**£12 a month**

- Unlimited downloads from the whole library
- One commercial licence that covers client work
- One seat
- Cancel at any time
- 30-day money-back guarantee

[Start your free trial](/signup)

## Questions

### When am I charged?

When you subscribe, and then on the same day each month.

### Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time from your account settings. You keep access until the end of the month you
have paid for.

### What happens to my downloads if I cancel?

Files you downloaded while subscribed stay licensed.

### Do you offer team plans?

Not yet. Each subscription is for one person.
