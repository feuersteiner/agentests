# Career levels

Every role at Fieldstone, in engineering, design, support or operations, sits on one of five
levels. The level sets the title, the scope of the work and the salary.

| Level | Title            | Salary, gross a year | Scope                                                         |
| ----- | ---------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| L1    | Junior <role>    | €58,000              | Well-defined tasks, with support from a more senior colleague. |
| L2    | <role>           | €72,000              | Features or projects delivered on their own, within a team.    |
| L3    | Senior <role>    | €88,000              | Larger projects; shapes the team's choices and mentors others. |
| L4    | Lead <role>      | €106,000             | Sets the direction of a team or area and answers for results.  |
| L5    | Principal <role> | €128,000             | Shapes direction across the company.                          |

## Moving up

Your manager proposes a promotion to the next level, and the levels review agrees it. The review
meets in June and December.

## Benefits

- 27 days' holiday a year, plus the public holidays where you live.
- A €1,500 home-office budget when you join, and €500 a year after that.
- €1,000 a year for training, books and conferences.
- A laptop of your choice, replaced every three years.
- A company retreat twice a year.
