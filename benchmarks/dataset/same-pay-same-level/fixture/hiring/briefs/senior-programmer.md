# Brief: Senior Programmer, Clinic Calendar team

Level: L3. Hiring manager: Joost Verhoeven, Lead Programmer. Recruiter: Sanne de Wit.

## The team

The Clinic Calendar team builds the booking calendar that physiotherapists and front-desk staff
keep open all day: appointments, rooms, recurring treatment plans and reminders. The team is four
programmers, a designer and Joost.

## What they will do

- Own the recurring-appointments engine, which books a course of treatments in one go.
- Move the two-way sync with Google and Outlook calendars onto the new events service.
- Review code and mentor the team's two programmers.

## What we need

- Five or more years building web applications, at least two of them with TypeScript and
  PostgreSQL.
- Experience with calendars, scheduling or time zones is a plus.
- Clear writing: most of our decisions are made in writing, in pull requests and short proposals.

## Working

- Fully remote, from anywhere in Europe between UTC-1 and UTC+3.
- Full time, 40 hours a week, with core hours from 10:00 to 15:00 CET.
- Start as soon as possible, ideally by February 2027.

## Interview stages

1. A 30-minute call with the recruiter.
2. A 90-minute technical interview: pairing on a small change to a booking API.
3. A 60-minute conversation with the hiring manager and a designer.
4. References from two recent managers.
