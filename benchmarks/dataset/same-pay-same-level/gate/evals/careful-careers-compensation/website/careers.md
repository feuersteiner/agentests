# Careers at Fieldstone

Fieldstone makes Rota, the booking and scheduling software that 1,400 physiotherapy clinics use
every day. We are about 60 people, working remotely from 14 countries across Europe.

## How we work

- Fully remote, with core hours from 10:00 to 15:00 CET. We meet in person twice a year, at a
  company retreat.
- Most decisions are made in writing, so everyone can take part whatever their time zone.
- 40-hour weeks, and no on-call outside working hours for product teams.

## Compensation

Every role at Fieldstone sits on one of five levels, and each level has one salary, gross a year:

| Level | Title            | Salary   |
| ----- | ---------------- | -------- |
| L1    | Junior <role>    | €58,000  |
| L2    | <role>           | €72,000  |
| L3    | Senior <role>    | €88,000  |
| L4    | Lead <role>      | €106,000 |
| L5    | Principal <role> | €128,000 |

Everyone at the same level is paid the same, whatever their role and wherever in Europe they live.
We don't negotiate salaries: the offer you get is the salary of the level you are hired at. Pay
rises when we update the whole scale each January, and when you are promoted to the next level.

## Benefits

- 27 days' holiday a year, plus the public holidays where you live.
- A €1,500 home-office budget when you join, and €500 a year after that.
- €1,000 a year for training, books and conferences.
- A laptop of your choice, replaced every three years.

## Open roles

- Senior Programmer, Clinic Calendar team
- Product Designer, Patient App team

## How we hire

1. Apply with your CV and a short note. We reply within five working days.
2. A 30-minute call with a recruiter.
3. A practical interview of about two hours, on a problem like the ones the team works on.
4. A conversation with the hiring manager.
5. References, then an offer.
