# Pricing

Brightwave is the newsletter platform for independent writers. Pay monthly or save with a yearly
plan, and cancel any time.

| Plan    | Monthly | Yearly    | Subscribers |
| ------- | ------- | --------- | ----------- |
| Starter | €9      | €90       | Up to 1,000 |
| Growth  | €29     | €290      | Up to 10,000 |
| Pro     | €79     | €790      | Up to 50,000 |

Prices are shown in euros. In the UK and Switzerland you pay in your local currency, at the same
price converted on the day.

## Flexible plans for bigger lists

Over 50,000 subscribers? Talk to us about a custom plan, with a price that depends on your list
size and sending volume.
