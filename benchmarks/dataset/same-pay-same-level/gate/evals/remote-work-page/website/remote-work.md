# Working remotely at Fieldstone

We have no office. Everyone at Fieldstone works from home or a co-working space, from anywhere in
Europe between UTC-1 and UTC+3.

## Contracts and taxes

If you live in the Netherlands, Fieldstone B.V. employs you directly. Everywhere else, you are
employed through our partner Remotely, which writes a local contract, pays your salary through
local payroll and handles tax and social security in your country. Your salary and benefits are
the same either way.

## Time zones and holidays

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 CET, so everyone overlaps for five hours a day. You get 27 days'
holiday a year plus the public holidays of the country you live in.

## Co-working

If you prefer a desk outside your home, we pay up to €150 a month for a co-working membership.
