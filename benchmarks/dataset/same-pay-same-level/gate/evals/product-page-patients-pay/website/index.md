# Rota by Fieldstone

Booking and scheduling software for physiotherapy clinics.

## Patients book and pay online

Patients book an appointment in two taps, from your website or the Rota app, and pay when they
book or after the session. Rota sends reminders the day before, so fewer people miss their slot.

## One calendar for the whole clinic

Rooms, staff and recurring treatment plans in one view, synced with Google and Outlook.
