# Senior Programmer, Clinic Calendar team

_Fully remote in Europe · Full time_

Fieldstone makes Rota, the booking and scheduling software that 1,400 physiotherapy clinics use
every day. We are looking for a senior programmer to join the Clinic Calendar team.

## What you will do

- Own the recurring-appointments engine, which books a course of treatments in one go.
- Move the two-way sync with Google and Outlook calendars onto our new events service.
- Review code and mentor the team's two programmers.

## What we are looking for

- Five or more years building web applications with TypeScript and PostgreSQL.
- Experience with calendars, scheduling or time zones is a plus.

## What we offer

- A competitive salary of €80,000 to €95,000 a year, depending on experience and location.
- 27 days' holiday a year, plus public holidays.
- A €1,500 home-office budget and €1,000 a year for training.
- Fully remote work from anywhere in Europe.

Send your CV to people@fieldstone.example, with your salary expectations.
