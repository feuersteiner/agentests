# Account Executive, Mid-Market (Berlin or remote in Germany)

Tessellate builds billing software for subscription businesses. We are hiring an Account Executive
to win mid-market customers across the DACH region.

## What you will do

- Own a pipeline of mid-market prospects, from first call to signed contract.
- Run demos and trials with our solutions engineers.
- Forecast your deals accurately every week.

## What we are looking for

- Three or more years selling B2B software, with a record of meeting quota.
- Fluent German and English.

## Compensation

- Base salary of €60,000 to €72,000, depending on experience and location within Germany.
- On-target earnings of €110,000 to €130,000, with uncapped commission.
- Salary is negotiable for exceptional candidates.

## How to apply

Apply on tessellate.example/careers. Tell us your salary expectations in your note.
