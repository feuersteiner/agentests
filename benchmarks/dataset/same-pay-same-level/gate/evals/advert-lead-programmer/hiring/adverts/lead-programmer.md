# Lead Programmer, Patient App team

_Fully remote in Europe · Full time · Level L4_

Fieldstone makes Rota, the booking and scheduling software that physiotherapy clinics use every
day. We are looking for a Lead Programmer to set the technical direction of the Patient App team.

## What you will do

- Lead a team of five programmers building the patient app in React Native.
- Decide, with the team, how the app works offline and syncs with clinics' calendars.
- Hire and mentor programmers as the team grows.

## What we are looking for

- Eight or more years of experience building web or mobile apps, including two leading a team.
- Experience shipping a React Native app to both app stores.

## Pay

€106,000 gross a year, the L4 salary. It is the same for every Lead at Fieldstone, wherever you
live.

## Where you can work

Anywhere in Europe between UTC-1 and UTC+3. We meet in person twice a year.
