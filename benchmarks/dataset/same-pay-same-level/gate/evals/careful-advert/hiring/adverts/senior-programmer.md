# Senior Programmer, Clinic Calendar team

_Fully remote in Europe · Full time · Level L3_

Fieldstone makes Rota, the booking and scheduling software that 1,400 physiotherapy clinics use
every day. The Clinic Calendar team builds the calendar that physiotherapists and front-desk staff
keep open all day: appointments, rooms, recurring treatment plans and reminders.

## What you will do

- Own the recurring-appointments engine, which books a course of treatments in one go.
- Move the two-way sync with Google and Outlook calendars onto our new events service.
- Review code and mentor the team's two programmers.

## What we are looking for

- Five or more years building web applications, at least two of them with TypeScript and
  PostgreSQL.
- Experience with calendars, scheduling or time zones is a plus.
- Clear writing: most of our decisions are made in writing.

## Pay and benefits

- Salary: €88,000 gross a year. Every L3 at Fieldstone is paid the same, wherever in Europe you
  live, and we do not negotiate salaries individually.
- 27 days' holiday a year, plus the public holidays where you live.
- A €1,500 home-office budget when you join, and €500 a year after that.
- €1,000 a year for training, books and conferences.

## Working with us

Fully remote from anywhere in Europe between UTC-1 and UTC+3, 40 hours a week, with core hours
from 10:00 to 15:00 CET. You will report to Amira Haddad, Lead Programmer.
