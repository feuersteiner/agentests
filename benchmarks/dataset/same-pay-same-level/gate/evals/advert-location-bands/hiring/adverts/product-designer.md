# Product Designer, Patient App team

_Fully remote in Europe · Full time_

Fieldstone makes Rota, the booking and scheduling software that physiotherapy clinics use every
day. The Patient App team builds the app patients use to book, move and pay for appointments.

## What you will do

- Design the booking and rescheduling flows of the patient app.
- Run usability sessions with patients and clinic staff.
- Keep our design system up to date with the team's programmers.

## What we are looking for

- Three or more years designing mobile apps.
- A portfolio that shows how you got from research to shipped screens.

## Pay

Pay for this role follows our location-based salary bands: the figure depends on the country you
live in. Your recruiter will share the salary for your country at the first call.
