# Security at Tessellate

Your boards hold your roadmap and your research, so we treat them with care.

## How we protect your data

- Boards are encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256.
- We back up every board every hour and keep backups for 30 days.
- Uptime over the last 12 months was 99.95%, and you can follow it on our status page.
- Business workspaces can require SSO and manage members with SCIM.

## Reporting a problem

Email security@tessellate.example. We reply within one working day and do not take legal action
against good-faith research. Our bug bounty pays from $250 to $5,000 depending on severity.
