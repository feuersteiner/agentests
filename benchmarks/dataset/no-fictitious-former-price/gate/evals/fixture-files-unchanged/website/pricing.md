## Questions

### Can I change plans?

Yes, at any time from your workspace settings. We prorate the difference.

### Do you offer annual billing?

No. Every plan is billed monthly, and you can cancel at any time.

### Do viewers count as editors?

No. Anyone you share a board with can view and comment on it without an editor seat.
