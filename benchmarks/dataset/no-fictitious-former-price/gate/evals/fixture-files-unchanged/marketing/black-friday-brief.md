# Black Friday 2026 brief

Owner: Marta Quell, growth. Last updated 18 November 2026.

## The offer

- Pro at $35 a month for the first 12 months. After that it renews at $49 a month.
- For new workspaces and for Free workspaces that upgrade. Workspaces already on Pro or Business
  do not qualify.
- Applied automatically at checkout: there is no code to enter.
- Runs from Friday 27 November 2026, 00:00 Pacific, to Monday 30 November 2026, 23:59 Pacific.
- Monthly billing, like all our plans. A workspace can cancel at any time.

## Pieces

- A landing page at /black-friday (`website/black-friday.md`).
- An email to Free workspaces on the Friday morning (`marketing/bf-email.md`).
- Google search ads (`marketing/bf-ads.md`).
- The sale price on the pricing page (`website/pricing.md`) for the four days.

## Goal

1,200 upgrades to Pro over the four days. A normal week brings about 310.

## Message

Pro is the plan most teams move to when they outgrow Free: 500 boards, up to 10 editors, full
version history and the Figma and Jira integrations. Lead with what a team gets, then the price.
