The October price test showed Pro at $79/month to new signups for nine days, to measure how
sensitive they were to price. Upgrades fell by 41%, so we rolled back to $49/month on 15 October.
The 37 workspaces that upgraded at $79 were moved to $49 and refunded the difference. Existing
Pro workspaces stayed on $49 throughout.
