# Plan the product on one canvas

Tessellate is an online whiteboard for product teams. Sketch flows, map user journeys and run
retros together, in real time, on a canvas the whole team can see.

## What teams use it for

- Roadmaps that the whole company can read.
- User journey maps with sticky notes, arrows and screenshots.
- Retros and planning sessions with timers and voting.

## Works with your tools

Bring in frames from Figma and turn sticky notes into Jira issues. Both integrations come with
Pro.
