# Everyday Cleanser

A low-foam gel that lifts off sunscreen and make-up without leaving skin tight. Oat extract and
glycerin keep it mild enough for morning and evening.

## How to use

Massage a pea-sized amount onto damp skin for 30 seconds, then rinse with lukewarm water.

## Size

A 200 ml pump bottle. When it is empty, top it up from the 200 ml Everyday Cleanser refill pouch:
unscrew the pump, pour, and screw the pump back on.

## Ingredients

Aqua, glycerin, cocamidopropyl betaine, sodium lauroyl sarcosinate, Avena sativa (oat) kernel
extract, panthenol, citric acid, sodium benzoate, potassium sorbate.
