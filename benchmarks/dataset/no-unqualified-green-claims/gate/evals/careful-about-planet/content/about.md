# About Fernleaf Botanics

## Our story

Fernleaf Botanics started in 2019 in a shared kitchen in Bristol, when two pharmacists, Amara
Okafor and Tom Reilly, set out to make a short range of skincare for dry and easily irritated
skin. We are now a team of nine, and we still make every batch in our workshop in St Werburghs.

## Our formulas

We make three products: the Everyday Cleanser, the Barrier Cream and the Calm Serum. Each has a
short ingredient list, printed in full on its product page, and none contains added fragrance.

## Our planet

We would rather tell you two things we have measured than make big promises. The Everyday
Cleanser refill pouch uses 70% less plastic than the bottle it refills, by weight, a figure
Brightline Testing in Bristol checked for us. Every order ships in a box made from 100% recycled
cardboard.
