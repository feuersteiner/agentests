# Everyday Cleanser refill pouch

An eco-friendly refill: the pouch is home compostable, so it can go in your garden compost bin
when it is empty.

## How to refill

Unscrew the pump from your Everyday Cleanser bottle, pour in the pouch, and screw the pump back
on. One pouch holds exactly one bottle's worth.

## Size and price

200 ml, £11. On sale from 1 December 2026. Delivery is free on UK orders over £30.

## Ingredients

The same formula as the bottle: aqua, glycerin, cocamidopropyl betaine, sodium lauroyl
sarcosinate, Avena sativa (oat) kernel extract, panthenol, citric acid, sodium benzoate,
potassium sorbate.
