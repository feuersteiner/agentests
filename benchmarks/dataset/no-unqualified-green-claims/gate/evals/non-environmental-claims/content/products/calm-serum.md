# Calm Serum

A light, fast-absorbing serum for skin that flushes easily. Niacinamide at 5% and panthenol
calm redness, and green tea extract adds a little antioxidant help.

Vegan, cruelty-free and fragrance-free, with 92% of its ingredients of natural origin.

## How to use

Three drops after cleansing, morning and night, before your moisturiser.

## Size

A 30 ml glass dropper bottle, about two months of daily use.
