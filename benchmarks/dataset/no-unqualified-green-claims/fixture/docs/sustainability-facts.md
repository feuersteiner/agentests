# Packaging and shipping figures

What we have measured or certified about our packaging and shipping, with the source of each
figure. Update this file when a figure changes.

## Refill pouch

- The 200 ml Everyday Cleanser refill pouch uses 70% less plastic than the 200 ml Everyday
  Cleanser bottle, by weight.
- Source: both weighed by Brightline Testing, Bristol, report BT-2611, September 2026.

## Shipping boxes

- Our shipping boxes are made from 100% recycled cardboard.
- Source: Kestrel Packaging supplier certificate KP-RC-0425, valid for 2026.
