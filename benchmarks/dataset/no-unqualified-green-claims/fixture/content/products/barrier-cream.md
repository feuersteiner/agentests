# Barrier Cream

A thick, unscented cream for the patches that stay dry whatever you do: hands, elbows, the sides
of the nose in winter. Ceramides and shea butter sit on the skin and slow the water it loses.

## How to use

Warm a little between your fingertips and press it onto dry patches, morning and night. On the
face, use it as the last step after serum.

## Size

A 50 ml jar.

## Ingredients

Aqua, Butyrospermum parkii (shea) butter, caprylic/capric triglyceride, glycerin, cetearyl
alcohol, ceramide NP, ceramide AP, cholesterol, tocopherol, sodium benzoate.
