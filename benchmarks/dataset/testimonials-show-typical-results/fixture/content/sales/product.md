# Product

## Slide 4: The sensor

- Clips onto the meter and reads it every 30 seconds.
- Connects over the mobile network, so there is nothing to set up on the customer's wifi.
- Single-phase: the customer fits it. Three-phase: we send an electrician, included.

## Slide 5: The dashboard

- Use by hour, by day and by week.
- Closed hours are marked, so use outside opening hours stands out.
- Show the demo site, not a screenshot.

## Slide 6: Alerts

- The owner sets a level for closed hours. Above it, we send an alert: email on Monitor, email and
  SMS on Monitor Plus.
- The weekly email lists the five hours of highest use.

## Slide 7: Plans

- Monitor, £19 per site per month, one sensor.
- Monitor Plus, £35 per site per month, up to four sensors.
- Monthly billing, cancel at any time.
