# Product facts

Copy takes its facts from this file. Ask the product team before changing it.

## What Kilowatch does

- A clip-on sensor reads the electricity meter every 30 seconds. No electrician is needed for a
  single-phase supply; a three-phase supply needs one visit, which is included.
- The dashboard shows use by hour, by day and by week, and marks the hours the business is closed.
- An alert goes out when use during closed hours is above the level the owner sets.
- A weekly email lists the five hours with the highest use.

## Plans

- Monitor: £19 per site per month. One sensor, 12 months of history, email alerts.
- Monitor Plus: £35 per site per month. Up to four sensors, so single machines can be tracked,
  12 months of history, email and SMS alerts.
- Both plans are billed monthly and can be cancelled at any time.

## Hardware

- The sensor is included in the plan and stays Kilowatch's property.
- The sensor has a 2-year warranty.
- It connects over the mobile network, so it does not need the business's wifi.
