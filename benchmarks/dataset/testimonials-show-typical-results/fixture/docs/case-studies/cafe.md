# Harbour Café, Whitby

Interview with Efua Mensah, co-owner, 28 October 2026. Monitor, one sensor, customer since
September 2025. Efua has approved the quotes below for publication.

## The business

A forty-seat café, open 08:00 to 17:00, closed on Mondays. Espresso machine, two display fridges,
a dishwasher, a small kitchen.

## What they found

- The espresso machine and the dishwasher's booster heater stayed on overnight and all Monday.
- The outdoor heaters were on a switch nobody owned.

## The result

Electricity bill down 27% over the first twelve months.

## Quotes

> "Kilowatch took 27% off our electricity bill, mostly from things that were on when we were shut."

> "It took me ten minutes a week. I am not an energy person."
