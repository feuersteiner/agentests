# Inkwell Print, Derby

Interview with Gareth Pugh, owner, 4 November 2026. Monitor, one sensor, customer since October
2025. Gareth has approved the quotes below for publication.

## The business

A print shop with four staff, open 09:00 to 17:30 on weekdays. Two digital presses, a laminator, a
guillotine.

## What they found

- The laminator was left warming all day for two or three jobs. It is now switched on per job.
- Nothing else of note: the presses already slept when idle.

## The result

Electricity bill down 9% over the first twelve months.

## Quotes

> "About 9% off the bill. Not dramatic, but it covers the subscription and a bit more."

> "What I like is knowing. Before, the bill was just a number that arrived."
