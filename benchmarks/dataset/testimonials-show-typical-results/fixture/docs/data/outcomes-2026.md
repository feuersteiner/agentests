# Customer outcomes 2026

Prepared by the data team, October 2026.

## What was measured

Every site that had Kilowatch for a full twelve months ending on or before 30 September 2026:
1,312 sites. For each site we compared the electricity bill for those twelve months with the bill
for the twelve months before, at the same unit price, so that tariff changes do not count as a
saving or a loss.

## First-year saving on the electricity bill

- Median saving: 6%.
- 70% of customers saved under 10%.

## Source

The figures come from the billing export of 2 October 2026. Ask the data team for the notebook.
