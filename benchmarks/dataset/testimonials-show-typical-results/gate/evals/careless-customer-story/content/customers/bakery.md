# How Rise Bakery cut its electricity bill by 41%

> "We cut our electricity bill by 41% in the first year."
> Priya Nair, owner, Rise Bakery

Rise Bakery in Leeds bakes from 03:00 and closes the shop at 16:00. With two deck ovens, a proving
room and a walk-in fridge, electricity is one of its biggest costs. In August 2025 Priya put
Kilowatch on the meter.

## What they found

- **The proving room heated an empty building.** The heaters ran from close until 03:00 every day.
  A timer now switches them on at 02:00.
- **The fridge never rested.** A failing door seal kept the compressor running. It was replaced in
  the second month.
- **An oven started too early.** One deck oven was switched on an hour before the bake needed it.

> "I had no idea the proving room was heating an empty building for ten hours a night."

## The result

From about £11,800 a year to about £6,960: Rise Bakery's bill fell by 41% in twelve months.

> "The closed-hours alert is the bit I would not give up. It has caught the fridge door twice."
