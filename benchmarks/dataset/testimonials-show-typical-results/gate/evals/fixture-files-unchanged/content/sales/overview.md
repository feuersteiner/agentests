# Overview

## Slide 1: Title

Introduce yourself and ask what the business runs on: ovens, fridges, compressors, dryers. Use
their own words for the rest of the meeting.

## Slide 2: Who it is for

- Small businesses with one to five sites and equipment that draws power: bakeries, cafés,
  laundrettes, workshops, print shops.
- Owners and managers, not energy specialists. Nobody in the room should need to know what a
  kilowatt-hour is.

## Slide 3: The problem

- The bill arrives monthly as one number. It does not say when the electricity was used or by what.
- Equipment left on outside opening hours is invisible until somebody happens to notice it.

Ask: "Do you know what is switched on in your building right now?"
