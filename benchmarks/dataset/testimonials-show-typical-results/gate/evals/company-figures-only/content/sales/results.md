# Results

## Slide 8: What customers save

- We compared a full year with Kilowatch against the year before for 1,312 sites, at the same
  unit price, so tariff changes do not count.
- The median saving on the electricity bill was 6% in the first year.
- 70% of customers saved under 10%. Most of the saving comes from equipment left on outside
  opening hours.

Ask: "What is switched on in your building at two in the morning?"
