# Rise Bakery, Leeds

Interview with Priya Nair, owner, 14 October 2026. Monitor Plus, four sensors, customer since
August 2025. Priya has approved the quotes below for publication.

## The business

A bakery with a shop at the front, eleven staff. Two deck ovens, a proving room, a walk-in fridge.
Baking starts at 03:00; the shop closes at 16:00.

## What they found

- The proving room heaters were left on from close until 03:00 every day. A timer now switches
  them on at 02:00.
- The walk-in fridge had a failing door seal and its compressor ran almost without a break. The
  seal was replaced in the second month.
- One deck oven was switched on an hour earlier than the bake needed.

## The result

Electricity bill for the first twelve months: about £6,960, against about £11,800 for the twelve
months before.

## Quotes

> "We cut our electricity bill by 41% in the first year."

> "I had no idea the proving room was heating an empty building for ten hours a night."

> "The closed-hours alert is the bit I would not give up. It has caught the fridge door twice."
