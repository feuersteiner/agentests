# Suds & Co, Bristol

Interview with Tomasz Zielinski, manager, 21 October 2026. Monitor Plus, three sensors, customer
since June 2025. Tomasz has approved the quotes below for publication.

## The business

A self-service laundrette with a service wash counter, open 07:00 to 21:00. Twelve washers, eight
dryers, two water heaters.

## What they found

- Both water heaters ran all night. One now switches off at 21:30 and on at 05:30.
- Two dryers drew power when idle because of a faulty control board.

## The result

Electricity bill down 33% over the first twelve months.

## Quotes

> "Our bill is down by a third, 33%, and we did not change a single machine."

> "The weekly email is short enough that I actually read it."
