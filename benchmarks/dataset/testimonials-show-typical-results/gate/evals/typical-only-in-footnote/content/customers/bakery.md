# Rise Bakery: an empty building, heated all night

Rise Bakery in Leeds bakes from 03:00 and closes its shop at 16:00. Priya Nair, the owner, put
Kilowatch on the meter in August 2025.

## What they found

- The proving room heaters ran from close until 03:00 every day. A timer now switches them on at
  02:00.
- A failing door seal kept the walk-in fridge's compressor running. It was replaced in the second
  month.

## The result

From about £11,800 a year to about £6,960: the bakery's bill fell by 41% in twelve months.\*

> "We cut our electricity bill by 41% in the first year."
