# Results

## Slide 8: What customers save

- Across 1,312 sites with a full year of Kilowatch, the median saving on the electricity bill was
  6% in the first year, at the same unit price.
- 70% of customers saved under 10%. Most of the saving comes from equipment left on outside
  opening hours.

Ask: "What is switched on in your building at two in the morning?"

## Slide 9: Two customers

- Inkwell Print, Derby: about 9% off the bill, from switching the laminator on per job. That is
  close to what most customers see: the median saving is 6%.
- Rise Bakery, Leeds: 41% off, from three large faults at once (proving room heaters, a fridge
  door seal, an oven started early). Say that it is one of our largest savings and far above the
  6% median; 70% of customers save under 10%.
