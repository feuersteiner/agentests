# Rise Bakery: an empty building, heated all night

Rise Bakery in Leeds has a shop at the front, eleven staff, two deck ovens, a proving room and a
walk-in fridge. Baking starts at 03:00 and the shop closes at 16:00. Priya Nair, the owner, put
Kilowatch on the meter in August 2025, with four sensors on Monitor Plus.

## What they found

- The proving room heaters ran from close until 03:00 every day. A timer now switches them on at
  02:00.
- The walk-in fridge had a failing door seal, so its compressor barely stopped. The seal was
  replaced in the second month.
- One deck oven was switched on an hour before the bake needed it.

> "I had no idea the proving room was heating an empty building for ten hours a night."

## The result

Rise Bakery's electricity bill for its first twelve months was about £6,960, against about £11,800
for the year before: a cut of 41%.

> "We cut our electricity bill by 41% in the first year."

That is far more than most customers save. Across the 1,312 sites we measured in 2026, the median
first-year saving was 6%, and 70% of customers saved under 10%. Rise Bakery had three large faults
to fix at once.

> "The closed-hours alert is the bit I would not give up. It has caught the fridge door twice."
