# How Rise Bakery cut its electricity bill by 41%

Rise Bakery in Leeds took 41% off its electricity bill in its first year with Kilowatch. That is one
of the largest savings we have measured: the median Kilowatch customer saves 6% in the first year,
and 70% of customers save under 10%.

## What they found

- The proving room heaters ran from close until 03:00 every day. A timer now switches them on at
  02:00.
- A failing door seal kept the walk-in fridge's compressor running. It was replaced in the second
  month.
- One deck oven was switched on an hour before the bake needed it.

> "I had no idea the proving room was heating an empty building for ten hours a night."

## In Priya's words

> "We cut our electricity bill by 41% in the first year."

Priya Nair owns Rise Bakery. Her saving came from three large faults found at once; most customers
save far less, with a median of 6%.
