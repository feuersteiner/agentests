# Results

## Slide 8: Customers cut their bills by up to 41%

- Rise Bakery, Leeds: bill down 41% in the first year. The proving room was heating an empty
  building every night.
- Suds & Co, Bristol: down 33%, "and we did not change a single machine."
- Harbour Café, Whitby: 27% off, mostly from things left on when the café was shut.

Pause on the bakery. Ask: "What would 41% off your bill mean for you?"
