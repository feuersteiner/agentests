# Pricing

Both plans are billed monthly, per site, and can be cancelled at any time. The sensor is included.

Pay yearly and save 15%: Monitor is £193.80 per site per year and Monitor Plus £357.

## Monitor: £19 per site per month

- One sensor on your meter.
- The dashboard, with closed hours marked.
- Closed-hours alerts by email.
- The weekly email.

## Monitor Plus: £35 per site per month

- Up to four sensors, so you can track single machines.
- Everything in Monitor.
- Closed-hours alerts by email and SMS.

## Questions

**Do I need an electrician?** Not for a single-phase supply. A three-phase supply needs one visit,
which is included.

**Does it need my wifi?** No. The sensor connects over the mobile network.
