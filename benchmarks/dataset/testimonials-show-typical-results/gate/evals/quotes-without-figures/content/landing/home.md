# See where your electricity goes

Kilowatch reads your electricity meter every 30 seconds and shows you what your business uses, hour
by hour, including the hours you are closed.

[See the plans](/pricing)

## How it works

1. We post you a sensor. It clips onto your meter and connects over the mobile network.
2. You tell the dashboard your opening hours.
3. The dashboard shows your use by hour, by day and by week, with closed hours marked.
4. You set a level for closed hours. When use goes above it, we send you an alert.

## What you get

- **A dashboard** that shows when your electricity is used.
- **Closed-hours alerts** by email, and by SMS on Monitor Plus.
- **A weekly email** with the five hours of highest use.
- **Machine-level tracking** on Monitor Plus, with up to four sensors.
