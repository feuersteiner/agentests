# Fitting the sensor

1. Find your electricity meter. The sensor works on 98% of the meters fitted in UK businesses.
2. Clip the sensor over the pulse light on the front of the meter.
3. Open the dashboard. The first reading arrives within two minutes.

If the battery drops below 20%, the dashboard shows a warning and we post you a new sensor at no
cost. A three-phase supply needs an electrician; that visit is included, worth about £90.
