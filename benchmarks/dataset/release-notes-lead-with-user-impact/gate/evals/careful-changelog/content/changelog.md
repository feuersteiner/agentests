# What's changed

The changes in Mapleform, sprint by sprint, as the form builder's "What's changed" panel shows
them. The newest sprint is at the top.

## Sprint 48 (2026-11-21)

- You can now let respondents save a multi-page form and finish it later. They get a link that
  works for 30 days.
- You can now start from 12 new templates for schools, such as a trip consent form and a club
  sign-up sheet.
- You can now use the Google Sheets integration on every plan.
- You can now receive up to 250 submissions a month on the Free plan, up from 100.
- Your dropdown search now finds options with accents, such as "Zürich", when you type "Zurich".
- You can now transfer ownership of a workspace when you have two-factor authentication turned on.

## Sprint 47 (2026-11-07)

- You can now duplicate a whole page of a multi-page form, not just one field at a time.
- Your submissions inbox now remembers the filter you used last.
- You no longer need to log in again when you switch between workspaces.

## Sprint 46 (2026-10-24)

- You can now add a signature field to any form.
- You can now see who last edited a form, under its name in the form list.
