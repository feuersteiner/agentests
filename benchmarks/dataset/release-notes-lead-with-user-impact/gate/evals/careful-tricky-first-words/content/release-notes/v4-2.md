# Mapleform 4.2

1. Updates you make to a published form now reach respondents straight away, without publishing
   it again.
2. Webhook settings now show your last 20 deliveries and whether each one arrived.
3. Changes you make in the builder now autosave every 10 seconds, so you lose at most a few
   seconds of work if your browser closes.
4. Removing a field no longer deletes the answers it collected. You can still see them in your
   inbox and exports.
5. Fixed-width forms now print on one page with their layout intact: choose A4 or Letter in the
   print settings.
