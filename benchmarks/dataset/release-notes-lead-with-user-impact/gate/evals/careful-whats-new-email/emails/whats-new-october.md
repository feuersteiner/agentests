Hi {{ first_name }},

We shipped two releases in October. Here is what they change for you.

## Page breaks

You can now split a long form into pages. Add a page break from the field menu, and respondents
see a progress bar at the top of each page.

## Excel export

You can now export submissions as an Excel file as well as CSV, from the Export menu in your
submissions inbox. Since 4.1.1, accented letters such as é and ñ come through correctly.

## Slack

You no longer need to reconnect Slack after renaming a channel. Notifications keep going to the
renamed channel.

## Dates

You can now type a date into a date field, such as 06/10/2026, instead of picking it from the
calendar.

## Large forms

You can open forms with more than 200 fields in the builder again. They had stopped loading after
4.1, and we fixed that in 4.1.1.

Thanks for building with Mapleform. We read every reply to this email.
