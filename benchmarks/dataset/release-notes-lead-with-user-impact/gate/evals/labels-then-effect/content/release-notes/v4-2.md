# Mapleform 4.2

- **File uploads.** You can now add a file-upload field to any form. Respondents attach PDF, JPG,
  PNG or DOCX files of up to 10 MB each.
- **Bulk delete.** You can now delete up to 500 submissions at once from your inbox.
- **Slack.** You can now send notifications to private channels.
- **Large forms.** Forms with 100 or more fields open faster in the builder.
- **Webhooks.** Your failed webhook deliveries are now retried three times: after 1 minute,
  10 minutes and 1 hour.
- **Print view.** You can no longer print submissions from the Print view. Use Export > PDF
  instead.
- **Conditional logic.** Your rules now apply to questions after a page break. Before 4.2 they
  were skipped there.
- **CSV export.** Your exports no longer drop the last row of a form with a page break.
