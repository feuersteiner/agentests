Hi {{ first_name }},

Here is what changed in Mapleform in October.

## Page breaks

You can now split a long form into pages, and respondents see a progress bar at the top of each
page.

## Excel export

Added Excel as an export format next to CSV, in the Export menu of the submissions inbox.

## Slack

Improved the Slack integration so that notifications keep going to a channel after it is renamed.

## Large forms

Fixed a problem that stopped forms with more than 200 fields from loading in the builder.
