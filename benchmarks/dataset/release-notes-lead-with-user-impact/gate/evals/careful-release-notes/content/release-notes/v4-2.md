# Mapleform 4.2

Released on 17 November 2026.

## New

- You can now add a file-upload field to any form. Respondents can attach PDF, JPG, PNG or DOCX
  files of up to 10 MB each.
- You can now delete up to 500 submissions at once: select them in your inbox and choose Delete.
- You can now send Slack notifications to private channels as well as public ones.

## Changed

- Forms with 100 or more fields open faster in the builder.
- Your webhooks are now retried three times if a delivery fails: after 1 minute, 10 minutes and
  1 hour.
- You can no longer print submissions from the Print view. Use Export > PDF instead, which has
  done the same job since 4.0.

## Fixed

- Your conditional logic now works on questions after a page break. Before, those rules were
  skipped.
- Your CSV exports no longer drop the last row of a form with a page break.
