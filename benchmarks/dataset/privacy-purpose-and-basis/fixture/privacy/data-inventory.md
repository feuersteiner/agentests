# Data inventory

Every use Tessellate makes of personal data, kept by the privacy lead, Priya Nair. It covers the
website, the web app and the mobile apps. Add a use here before it goes live.

Last reviewed: 2026-11-10.

## 1. Account creation and running the account

- **What we do:** create the account, sign the user in, and store and sync their boards,
  comments and workspace membership.
- **Personal data:** name, work email address, password hash, profile picture if added, the
  boards and comments the user creates.
- **Lawful basis:** contract.
- **Shared with:** our hosting provider (EU data centres).
- **In the privacy notice since:** 2023-04.

## 2. Billing

- **What we do:** take payment for paid plans, send invoices and receipts, and keep invoices.
- **Personal data:** billing name, billing address, VAT number, the last four digits of the
  card, invoice history. Full card details stay with our payment provider.
- **Lawful basis:** contract (taking payment); legal obligation (keeping invoices under UK tax
  law).
- **Shared with:** our payment provider; our accountants.
- **In the privacy notice since:** 2023-04.

## 3. Product analytics

- **What we do:** record which features are used, in which order, and where people give up, in
  the web app and the mobile apps.
- **Personal data:** account ID, usage events, device and browser type, approximate location
  from the IP address (country only).
- **Lawful basis:** legitimate interests.
- **Legitimate interest:** understanding how Tessellate is used so that we can fix confusing
  steps and decide what to build next. Assessed in LIA-2026-03; users can opt out under
  Settings > Privacy.
- **Shared with:** our analytics provider (EU hosting).
- **In the privacy notice since:** not yet. Live since 2026-11-04.

## 4. Marketing emails, including the newsletter

- **What we do:** send the monthly newsletter and occasional product announcements to people who
  ask for them.
- **Personal data:** name, email address, which emails were opened.
- **Lawful basis:** consent, given by an unticked box at signup or on the newsletter page.
  Every email has an unsubscribe link.
- **Shared with:** our email delivery provider.
- **In the privacy notice since:** not yet. First newsletter sent 2026-11-18.

## 5. Fraud prevention

- **What we do:** check sign-ins and payments for signs of stolen accounts, card testing and
  abuse of free workspaces, and block them.
- **Personal data:** IP address, sign-in history, device identifiers, payment outcomes.
- **Lawful basis:** legitimate interests.
- **Legitimate interest:** protecting our customers' accounts and Tessellate from fraud and
  abuse. Assessed in LIA-2025-01.
- **Shared with:** our payment provider's fraud screening.
- **In the privacy notice since:** 2025-06.
