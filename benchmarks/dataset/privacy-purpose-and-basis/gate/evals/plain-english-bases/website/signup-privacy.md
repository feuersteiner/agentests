## Your privacy, in plain English

**Your name, email and password** set up your account and sign you in. We can't give you
Tessellate without them, so we use them to provide the service you're signing up for.

**Our newsletter** only reaches you if you tick the box below. We send it because you asked us
to, and one click in any email stops it.

That's all we do with the details on this page. For everything else, read our
[privacy notice](/privacy).
