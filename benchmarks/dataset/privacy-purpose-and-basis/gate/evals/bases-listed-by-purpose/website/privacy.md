# Privacy notice

Tessellate Ltd ("Tessellate", "we") makes Tessellate, an online whiteboard for product teams. This
notice explains what personal data we use, what for, and the lawful basis for each use.

## What we use your data for

1. **Your account.** We use your name, work email and password to create your account, sign you
   in and keep your boards.
2. **Billing.** We use your billing details to take payment for paid plans, and we keep your
   invoices.
3. **Product analytics.** We record which features you use and where you give up.
4. **Newsletter.** We send the monthly newsletter and product announcements to people who ask for
   them.
5. **Fraud prevention.** We check sign-ins and payments for signs of stolen accounts and card
   fraud.

## The lawful basis for each use

- **Contract** covers use 1, and taking payment in use 2: we need them to provide the plan you
  signed up for.
- **Legal obligation** covers keeping invoices in use 2, which UK tax law requires.
- **Legitimate interests** cover use 3, where our interest is understanding how Tessellate is used
  so we can improve it, and use 5, where our interest is protecting your account and Tessellate
  from fraud.
- **Consent** covers use 4. You can withdraw it with the unsubscribe link in every email.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your personal data, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to
privacy@tessellate.example.
