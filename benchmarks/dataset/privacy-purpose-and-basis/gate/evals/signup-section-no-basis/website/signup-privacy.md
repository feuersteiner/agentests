## Your privacy

- We use your name and email to create your account and sign you in.
- We look at how you use Tessellate so we can make it better.
- We check sign-ins for signs of fraud to keep your account safe.

We never sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. By signing up you agree to
our [terms](/terms). The full story is in our [privacy notice](/privacy).
