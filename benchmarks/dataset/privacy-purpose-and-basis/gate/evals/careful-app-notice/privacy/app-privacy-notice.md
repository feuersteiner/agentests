# Privacy notice for the Tessellate mobile apps

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This notice is for Tessellate for iOS and Android, made by Tessellate Ltd ("we"). The apps use the
same account as the web app. They have no payments, no push notifications, no access to your
contacts or precise location, and no advertising.

## What we use your data for, and on what basis

| What we do | Personal data | Lawful basis |
| ---------- | ------------- | ------------ |
| Create your account, sign you in, and store and sync your boards and comments | Name, work email, password, your boards and comments | Contract: we need it to provide the app you signed up for |
| Bill you for a paid plan you bought on our website | Billing name and address, VAT number, last four card digits, invoices | Contract, to take payment; legal obligation, to keep invoices under UK tax law |
| Product analytics: record which features you use and where you give up | Account ID, usage events, device type, country | Legitimate interests: understanding how the apps are used so we can fix confusing steps and decide what to build next. Opt out under Settings > Privacy |
| Send the newsletter and product announcements, if you asked for them | Name, email address, which emails you open | Consent, which you can withdraw with the unsubscribe link |
| Prevent fraud: check sign-ins for stolen accounts and abuse | IP address, sign-in history, device identifiers | Legitimate interests: protecting your account, other customers and Tessellate from fraud and abuse |

## Who we share it with

Our hosting provider (EU data centres), analytics provider, email delivery provider and payment
provider, acting on our instructions.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your personal data, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to
privacy@tessellate.example. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office
(ico.org.uk).
