# Privacy notice

Tessellate Ltd ("Tessellate", "we") makes Tessellate, an online whiteboard for product teams. This
notice explains what personal data we collect when you use tessellate.example, the web app or our
mobile apps, and how we use it.

## What we collect

- Account details: your name, work email address and password.
- Billing details: your billing name and address, VAT number and the last four digits of your
  card.
- Usage data: the features you use, your device and browser type, and your country.
- Email engagement: which of our emails you open.
- Security data: your IP address, sign-in history and device identifiers.

## How we use your data

- To create and run your account and keep your boards.
- To take payment for paid plans and send invoices.
- To understand how Tessellate is used and improve it.
- To send you our newsletter and product announcements.
- To detect and prevent fraud and abuse.

## Our legal bases

We process your personal data under the UK GDPR on the following lawful bases:

- **Contract:** where processing is necessary to perform our contract with you.
- **Legal obligation:** where the law requires us to process data.
- **Legitimate interests:** where processing is in our legitimate interests and these are not
  overridden by your rights.
- **Consent:** where you have given us your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

## Who we share it with

Our hosting, payment, analytics and email delivery providers, and our accountants.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your personal data, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to
privacy@tessellate.example. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office
(ico.org.uk).
