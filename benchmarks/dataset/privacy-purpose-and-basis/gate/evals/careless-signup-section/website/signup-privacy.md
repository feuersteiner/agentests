## Your privacy

We use your name and email to set up your account, keep it secure, see how Tessellate is used so
we can improve it, and, if you tick the box, send you our newsletter.

We only do this where the law allows: to provide the service you signed up for, with your
consent, or where we have a legitimate interest.

We never sell your data. The full story is in our [privacy notice](/privacy).
