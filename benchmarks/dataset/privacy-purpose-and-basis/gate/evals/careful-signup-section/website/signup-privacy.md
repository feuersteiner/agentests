## Your privacy

- **Your account.** We use your name, email and password to set up your account and sign you in.
  We need them to give you Tessellate, so this is part of our contract with you.
- **How you use Tessellate.** We see which features you use so we can fix the confusing bits.
  That's in our legitimate interests in making Tessellate better, and you can switch it off in
  Settings.
- **Our newsletter.** Only if you tick the box. That's your consent, and you can unsubscribe from
  any email.
- **Keeping accounts safe.** We check sign-ins for signs of fraud. That's in our legitimate
  interests in protecting your account and Tessellate from fraud and abuse.

The full story is in our [privacy notice](/privacy).
