# Private boards

Some boards are not ready for the whole company. Mark a board as private and only the people you
invite can find it, open it or see its comments.

- **Privacy by default for drafts.** New boards in a personal space start private.
- **Invite by name.** Add teammates one by one, as viewers or editors.
- **Move it when it is ready.** Make the board visible to your workspace with one click.

Private boards come with every plan, including Free. [Create your workspace](/signup).
