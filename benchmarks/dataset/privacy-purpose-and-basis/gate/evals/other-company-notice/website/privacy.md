# Privacy notice

Fernway Cycles Ltd ("Fernway", "we") sells and services bicycles from our shop in Bristol and at
fernway.example. This notice explains what personal data we use, what for, and on what basis.

## Your orders and repairs

We use your name, address, phone number and order history to deliver your bike, book repairs and
handle returns. **Lawful basis:** contract, because we need these details to do what you ordered.

## Website analytics

With your permission, we use analytics cookies to see which pages you visit and how you found us.
**Lawful basis:** consent, which you give or refuse in the cookie banner and can change at any time
from the link in the footer.

## Service reminders

We email you when your bike is due for its free six-week check and its yearly service.
**Lawful basis:** legitimate interests: keeping the bikes we sell safe to ride and offering the
servicing customers expect from us. You can turn the reminders off from any email.

## Keeping records for tax

We keep invoices for six years. **Lawful basis:** legal obligation, under UK tax law.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, and ask us to correct or delete it, at privacy@fernway.example.
