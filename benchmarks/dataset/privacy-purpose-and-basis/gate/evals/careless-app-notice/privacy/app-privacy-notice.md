# Privacy notice for the Tessellate mobile apps

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This notice is for Tessellate for iOS and Android, made by Tessellate Ltd ("we"). The apps use the
same account as the web app. They have no payments, no push notifications, no access to your
contacts or precise location, and no advertising.

## What we collect and why

| Data | What we use it for |
| ---- | ------------------ |
| Name, work email, password | Creating your account and signing you in |
| Your boards and comments | Storing and syncing your work across devices |
| Usage events, device type, country | Understanding how the apps are used and improving them |
| Email address, email opens | Sending the newsletter and product announcements you asked for |
| IP address, sign-in history, device identifiers | Detecting and preventing fraud |

Billing for paid plans happens on our website and is covered by our website privacy notice.

## Legal bases

Under the UK GDPR we rely on the following lawful bases:

- Performance of a contract
- Compliance with a legal obligation
- Our legitimate interests in running and improving a secure service
- Your consent

## Who we share it with

Our hosting provider (EU data centres), analytics provider, email delivery provider and payment
provider, acting on our instructions.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your personal data, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to
privacy@tessellate.example. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office
(ico.org.uk).
