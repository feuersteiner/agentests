# Security at Tessellate

Your boards hold your team's plans, so we treat them with care.

- **Encryption.** Your data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256.
- **EU data centres.** Boards are stored in two EU regions, with daily backups kept for 30 days.
- **Single sign-on.** Business workspaces can require sign-in through their identity provider.
- **Independent testing.** An outside firm tests the app every year; we fix what it finds.
- **Status.** See uptime and incidents at status.tessellate.example.

Found a vulnerability? Write to security@tessellate.example. For how we use personal data, read
our [privacy notice](/privacy).
