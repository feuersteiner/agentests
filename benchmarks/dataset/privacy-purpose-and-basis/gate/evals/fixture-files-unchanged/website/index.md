# Plan, sketch and decide on one board

Tessellate is an online whiteboard for product teams. Run a retro, map a customer journey or
plan the next quarter on an endless canvas, with sticky notes, shapes, comments and votes.

- **Real-time boards.** Everyone sees every change as it happens.
- **Templates.** Start from a retro, a roadmap or a story map instead of a blank page.
- **Comments and votes.** Agree on what matters without another meeting.

Free for up to three boards. [Create your workspace](/signup).
