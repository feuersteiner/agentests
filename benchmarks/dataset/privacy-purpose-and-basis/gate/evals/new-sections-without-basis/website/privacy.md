# Privacy notice

Tessellate Ltd ("Tessellate", "we") makes Tessellate, an online whiteboard for product teams. This
notice explains what personal data we use when you visit tessellate.example or use Tessellate,
what we use it for, and why the law allows us to.

## Creating and running your account

When you sign up we ask for your name, your work email address and a password. We use them to
create your account, sign you in, and keep your boards and comments in your workspace. We need
this information to give you the service you signed up for, so our lawful basis is our contract
with you.

## Billing

If you buy a paid plan, we use your billing name and address, your VAT number and your payment
details to take payment and send you invoices. Our payment provider holds your full card details;
we only see the last four digits. We rely on our contract with you to take payment, and on our
legal obligation under UK tax law to keep invoices.

## Preventing fraud

We look at sign-in attempts, IP addresses and payment outcomes to spot stolen accounts and
fraudulent payments, and to block them. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests: protecting
your account, and Tessellate, from fraud and abuse.

## Product analytics

We record which features you use, in which order, and where you give up, in the web app and the
mobile apps, together with your device and browser type and your country. This helps us
understand how Tessellate is used and make it better. You can opt out at any time under
Settings > Privacy.

## Our newsletter

We use your name and email address to send you our monthly newsletter and occasional product
announcements, and we note which emails you open so that we can send you more of what you find
useful. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

## Who we share it with

We use a hosting provider with data centres in the EU and a payment provider. They use your data
only on our instructions.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, and ask us to correct it or delete it. Write to
privacy@tessellate.example and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we
handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
