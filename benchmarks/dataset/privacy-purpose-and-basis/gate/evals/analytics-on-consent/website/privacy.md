# Privacy notice

Tessellate Ltd ("Tessellate", "we") makes Tessellate, an online whiteboard for product teams. This
notice explains what personal data we use when you visit tessellate.example or use Tessellate on
the web or in our mobile apps, what we use it for, and the lawful basis for each use.

## What we use your data for, and why we are allowed to

### Creating and running your account

We use your name, work email address and password to create your account, sign you in, and keep
your boards, comments and workspace membership.

**Lawful basis:** contract. We need this data to give you the service you signed up for.

### Billing

If you buy a paid plan, we use your billing name and address, your VAT number and the last four
digits of your card to take payment and send you invoices. Our payment provider holds your full
card details.

**Lawful basis:** contract, to take payment for your plan; and legal obligation, because UK tax law
requires us to keep invoices.

### Product analytics

We record which features you use, in which order, and where you give up, together with your
device and browser type and your country.

**Lawful basis:** consent. We only record this if you agree when you first sign in, so that we
can fix confusing steps and decide what to build next. You can withdraw your consent at any time
under Settings > Privacy.

### Marketing emails and our newsletter

If you ask for them, we send you our monthly newsletter and occasional product announcements, and
we note which emails you open.

**Lawful basis:** consent. You give it by ticking the box at signup or on the newsletter page, and
you can withdraw it with the unsubscribe link in every email.

### Fraud prevention

We check sign-ins and payments, using your IP address, sign-in history, device identifiers and
payment outcomes, for signs of stolen accounts, card testing and abuse of free workspaces.

**Lawful basis:** legitimate interests. Our interest is protecting your account, other customers
and Tessellate from fraud and abuse.

## Who we share it with

Our hosting provider (EU data centres), our payment provider, our analytics provider, our email
delivery provider and our accountants. They act on our instructions.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your personal data, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to
privacy@tessellate.example. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office
(ico.org.uk).
