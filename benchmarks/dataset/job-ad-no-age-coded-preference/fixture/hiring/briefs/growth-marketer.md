# Brief: Growth Marketer

From Daan Visser, Head of Growth. Budget approved by Finance on 4 November; no advert yet.

## Why we're hiring

Installs of the Tessellate Shifts app have been flat at about 9,000 a month since the summer. Most
new users are shift workers who find us on social media, and nobody owns paid social full time.

## What they'll do

- Plan and run paid campaigns on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, about €15k a month in total.
- Make short videos with the design team; test hooks, captions and landing pages.
- Report every week on installs, cost per install and week-one retention.
- Run the "bring your team" referral campaign with the sales team.

## Who I want

Someone young and hungry who gets TikTok, ideally a recent grad. 2-4 years max so they're not set
in their ways. A digital native who'll fit right in with the crew. Must have run paid campaigns
with a real budget and be able to show the numbers. Knows TikTok Ads Manager and Meta Ads Manager,
and can cut a video in CapCut.

## Practical

- Amsterdam office, hybrid: Tuesday and Thursday in the office.
- Salary band: €48,000 to €56,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance.
- Reports to me. Start as soon as possible.
- Process: a call with me, a take-home campaign plan, then a chat with Lotte (CEO).
