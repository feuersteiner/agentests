# Backend Engineer, Planning Engine

- Level: medior to senior (L3 to L4)
- Team: Planning Engine, six engineers
- Hiring manager: Joris van Dam, Engineering Manager
- Location: Amsterdam, Keizersgracht office; hybrid, three days a week in the office
- Salary band: €68,000 to €86,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance
- Contract: permanent, 40 hours a week

## The work

The Planning Engine turns a warehouse's orders, staff availability and labour rules into shift
plans. The engineer builds and runs the Kotlin services behind it, on PostgreSQL and Kafka, and
works with the solver team on how plans are scored.

## Must have

- Four or more years building backend services in Kotlin or Java
- Experience running services in production: on-call, monitoring, incident follow-up
- SQL and data modelling in PostgreSQL

## Nice to have

- Event streaming with Kafka
- Scheduling or optimisation problems
- Dutch (not required; the team works in English)
