# Account Manager

Amsterdam, Keizersgracht office, hybrid (three days a week in the office). Permanent, 40 hours.
Salary: €52,000 to €60,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance and a quarterly bonus.

## The role

We're looking for a confident salesman to look after our warehouse customers. He will own a book
of 40 accounts, make sure every site gets value from Tessellate, and grow each account over time.

## What you'll do

- Visit customer sites and run a review with each account every quarter.
- Find accounts that could plan more sites with Tessellate, and agree the expansion with them.
- Work with support on any account that is at risk.

## What you bring

- At least three years of managing B2B software accounts.
- He has a track record of growing accounts and can show the numbers.
- Clear, honest communication with customers at every level.

## About the team

The customer team is five people who sit with sales and support.
