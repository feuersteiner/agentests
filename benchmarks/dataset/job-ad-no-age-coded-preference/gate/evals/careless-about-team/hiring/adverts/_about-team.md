# About the team

We're a young, international crew of 26 in Amsterdam, building shift planning software that more
than 60,000 shift workers use every week. Average age: 27. Most of us joined straight from
university or a first job, so you'll be surrounded by people at the same stage as you.

We ship every day, own our problems from start to finish, and write things down in Notion. It's a
work hard, play hard kind of place: Friday drinks, the Mario Kart league and the ski trip. Nobody
cares about titles, and the best argument wins.

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, and the rest is up to you.
