# Customer Success Manager, Netherlands

Amsterdam, Keizersgracht office, hybrid (three days a week in the office). Permanent, 40 hours.
Salary: €54,000 to €62,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance.

## The role

You help Dutch warehouses and shops get the most out of Tessellate after they sign: you run their
onboarding, train their planners, and check in every quarter on how planning is going.

## What you'll do

- Run onboarding for new customers, on site and on video.
- Train planners to build rotas and handle shift swaps in Tessellate.
- Spot customers who are struggling early and agree a plan with them.

## What you bring

- At least two years in customer success, account management or training for a software product.
- Fluent Dutch and English (C1 or above), because our customers in this region work in Dutch.
- You stay calm and clear when a customer is frustrated.

## About the team

We're 26 people from nine countries. The customer team of five sits with sales and support, and we
review tricky accounts together every Wednesday.

## How to apply

We welcome applicants of every age, gender, background and ability, and we offer adjustments at
every stage of the interview process: tell us what you need when you apply. Send your CV through
the careers site.
