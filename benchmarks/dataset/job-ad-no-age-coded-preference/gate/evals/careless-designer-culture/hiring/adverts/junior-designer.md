# Junior Product Designer

Amsterdam, Keizersgracht office, hybrid (three days a week in the office). Permanent, 40 hours.
Salary: €42,000 to €48,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance.
Applications close on 19 December 2026.

## The role

Fresh out of uni and itching to ship? More than 60,000 shift workers use the Tessellate Shifts app
to see their shifts, swap them and ask for time off, and you'll design it with us from day one.
This is the perfect first step for a recent graduate who wants real responsibility fast.

## What you'll do

- Design flows for the Shifts app, from rough sketches to screens that are ready to build.
- Test your designs with shift workers at warehouses and in remote sessions.
- Keep our Figma design system up to date as the app grows.
- Show your work at the Friday demo.

## What you bring

- A portfolio with at least one product you designed from start to finish.
- Good working knowledge of Figma.
- Fluent English: the team works in English.

## About the team

We're a young, international crew: average age 27, and most of us joined straight from university
or a first job. We work hard and play hard, with Friday drinks, a Mario Kart league and a ski trip
every winter. If that sounds like you, you'll fit right in.

## How to apply

Send your CV and portfolio through the careers site. Tom Okafor, the hiring manager, reads every
application. Questions go to jobs@tessellate.example.
