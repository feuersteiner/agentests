# Data Analyst

Amsterdam, Keizersgracht office, hybrid (three days a week in the office). Fixed term, 12 months,
with the aim of a permanent contract. Salary: €55,000 to €63,000 a year, plus 8% holiday
allowance.

## The role

Tessellate was founded in 2019, and seven years of shift plans now sit in our warehouse. You'll
turn them into answers for planners and for our own product teams.

## What you'll do

- Build dashboards in Metabase on how far ahead shifts are planned and how often they are swapped.
- Answer questions from product managers with SQL and a clear write-up.
- Run the yearly report we send to every customer.

## What you bring

- At least three years of experience analysing data with SQL.
- 3+ years with a BI tool such as Metabase, Looker or Tableau.
- You explain numbers in plain words to people who do not work with data.

## About the team

You join the data team of two, who sit with the product managers and engineers.

## How to apply

Send your CV and a short example of an analysis you wrote through the careers site.
