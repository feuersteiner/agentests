# Junior Product Designer

Amsterdam, Keizersgracht office, hybrid (three days a week in the office). Permanent, 40 hours.
Salary: €42,000 to €48,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance.
Applications close on 19 December 2026.

## The role

Tessellate makes shift planning software for warehouses and shops. Planners use our web app to
build rotas, and more than 60,000 shift workers use the Tessellate Shifts app to see their shifts,
swap them and ask for time off. As a Junior Product Designer you join the design team and work on
the Shifts app with a product manager and the mobile engineers.

## What you'll do

- Design flows for the Shifts app, from rough sketches to screens that are ready to build.
- Test your designs with shift workers, in person at warehouses and in remote sessions.
- Keep our Figma design system up to date as the app grows.
- Show your work at the Friday demo and give feedback on other people's work.

## What you bring

- A portfolio with at least one product you designed from start to finish, from a job, an
  internship, a course or a project of your own.
- Good working knowledge of Figma.
- You can explain why you made a design decision, and change it when testing shows it does not
  work.
- Fluent English: the team works in English. Dutch is not needed.

## About the team

The design team is four designers led by Tom Okafor, Head of Design. We sit with the product
managers and engineers of the Shifts app, review each other's work twice a week, and every
designer spends at least one day a month with users at a warehouse or shop.

## How to apply

Send your CV and portfolio through the careers site. Tom Okafor, the hiring manager, reads every
application, and we reply within five working days. Questions go to jobs@tessellate.example.
