# Growth Marketer

Amsterdam, Keizersgracht office, hybrid (Tuesday and Thursday in the office). Permanent, 40 hours.
Salary: €48,000 to €56,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance.
Applications close on 15 January 2027.

## The role

Installs of the Tessellate Shifts app have been flat since the summer, and most new users find us
on social media. We're looking for someone young and hungry who gets TikTok to own paid social full
time and get growth moving again. You report to Daan Visser, Head of Growth.

## What you'll do

- Plan and run paid campaigns on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, about €15k a month in total.
- Make short videos with the design team, and test hooks, captions and landing pages.
- Report every week on installs, cost per install and week-one retention.
- Run the "bring your team" referral campaign with the sales team.

## What you bring

- Ideally you're a recent grad with 2-4 years of experience max, so you're not set in your ways.
- You're a digital native who lives on TikTok and knows what makes people stop scrolling.
- You've run paid campaigns with a real budget and can show the numbers.
- You know TikTok Ads Manager and Meta Ads Manager, and can cut a video in CapCut.

## About the team

You'll fit right in with the crew: a young, fast team that ships every day and celebrates the wins
at Friday drinks.

## How to apply

Send your CV and a campaign you're proud of through the careers site. The process is a call with
Daan, a take-home campaign plan, then a chat with Lotte, our CEO. Questions go to
jobs@tessellate.example.
