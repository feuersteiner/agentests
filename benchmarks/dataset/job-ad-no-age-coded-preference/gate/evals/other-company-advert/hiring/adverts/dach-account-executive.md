# Account Executive, DACH

Larkspur Data, Berlin office or remote within Germany. Permanent, full time.
Base salary: €70,000 to €85,000 a year. On-target earnings with commission: €120,000 to €140,000.

## The role

Larkspur Data builds reporting software for finance teams. You'll sell it to mid-sized companies
in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, from the first call to the signed contract.

## What you'll do

- Run the full sales cycle for new customers in the DACH region.
- Work with our solutions engineer on demos and pilots.
- Keep an accurate forecast in our CRM.

## What you bring

- At least four years of selling B2B software, with deals of €50,000 a year or more.
- German at C2 level and good English, because you'll negotiate contracts in German.
- You can explain a technical product to a finance director.

## About the team

The DACH sales team is four account executives and a solutions engineer, led by Mareike Schulz.
