# Offsite notes, October 2026

Notes from the session "How would you describe Tessellate to a friend?" at the October offsite in
Zandvoort, written up by Lotte for anyone who writes about the company.

## Tessellate in numbers

- 26 people: 14 in engineering, 4 in design, 8 in growth, sales and operations.
- 9 nationalities; everyone works in English.
- Average age 27. Most of us joined straight from university or a first job.
- More than 60,000 shift workers use the Shifts app every week.
- 2 office dogs, Bram and Pip.

## What people said

- "We ship every day, and small teams own a problem from start to finish."
- "You get real responsibility in your first month."
- "We write things down: decisions, specs and post-mortems all live in Notion."
- "Everyone spends time with users. Engineers and designers visit warehouses too."
- "Work hard, play hard: Friday drinks, the Mario Kart league and the ski trip."
- "Nobody cares about titles; the best argument wins."

## What we want to keep as we grow

- Small teams with a clear owner for each problem.
- Honest feedback, given kindly.
- Flexible hours: core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, and the rest is up to you.
