# About the team

Tessellate is 26 people in Amsterdam, from nine countries, building shift planning software for
warehouses and shops. More than 60,000 shift workers use our Shifts app every week.

We work in small teams, and each team owns a problem from start to finish. We ship every day,
show our work at the Friday demo, and write our decisions, specs and post-mortems down in Notion so
anyone can pick them up. Everyone spends time with the people who use our software, engineers and
designers included.

We give honest feedback, kindly, and the best argument wins, whoever makes it. Core hours are 10:00
to 16:00, and the rest of your day is up to you. Everyone works in English.
