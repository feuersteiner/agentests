# Growth Marketer

Amsterdam, Keizersgracht office, hybrid (Tuesday and Thursday in the office). Permanent, 40 hours.
Salary: €48,000 to €56,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance.

## The role

Most new users of the Tessellate Shifts app find us on social media, and nobody owns paid social
full time yet. As our Growth Marketer, you will. You report to Daan Visser, Head of Growth.

## What you'll do

- Plan and run paid campaigns on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, about €15,000 a month in total.
- Make short videos with the design team, and test hooks, captions and landing pages.
- Report every week on installs, cost per install and week-one retention.

## What you bring

- You're ideally two to four years out of university, with some paid social experience behind you.
- Hands-on experience with TikTok Ads Manager and Meta Ads Manager.
- You can edit a short video, for example in CapCut.

## About the team

The growth team is three people who sit with sales and design.

## How to apply

Send your CV and two campaigns you ran, with their results, through the careers site.
