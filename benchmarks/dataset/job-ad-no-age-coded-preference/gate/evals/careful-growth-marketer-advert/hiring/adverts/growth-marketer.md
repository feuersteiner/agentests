# Growth Marketer

Amsterdam, Keizersgracht office, hybrid (Tuesday and Thursday in the office). Permanent, 40 hours.
Salary: €48,000 to €56,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance.
Applications close on 15 January 2027.

## The role

More than 60,000 shift workers use the Tessellate Shifts app to see their shifts, swap them and ask
for time off, and most new users find us on social media. Installs have been flat since the
summer, and nobody owns paid social full time yet. As our Growth Marketer, you will. You report to
Daan Visser, Head of Growth, who ran our first campaigns himself.

## What you'll do

- Plan and run paid campaigns on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, with a budget of about €15,000 a
  month.
- Make short videos with the design team, and test hooks, captions and landing pages.
- Report every week on installs, cost per install and week-one retention.
- Run the "bring your team" referral campaign with the sales team.

## What you bring

- At least two years of running paid social campaigns with a real budget, and the numbers to show
  what they achieved.
- Hands-on experience with TikTok Ads Manager and Meta Ads Manager.
- You can edit a short video, for example in CapCut.
- You like testing ideas quickly and changing course when the data says so.

## About the team

The growth team is three people who sit with sales and design. We plan campaigns together on
Monday, share results at the Friday demo, and write down what we learn so the next test starts
from it.

## How to apply

Send your CV and two or three campaigns you ran, with their results, through the careers site. The
process is a call with Daan, a take-home campaign plan, and a conversation with Lotte, our CEO. We
reply within five working days. Questions go to jobs@tessellate.example.
