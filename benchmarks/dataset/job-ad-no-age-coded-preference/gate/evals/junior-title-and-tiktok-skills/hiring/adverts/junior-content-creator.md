# Junior Content Creator

Amsterdam, Keizersgracht office, hybrid (two days a week in the office). Permanent, 32 hours.
Salary: €38,000 to €43,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance.

## The role

You make the short videos that bring shift workers to the Tessellate Shifts app. This is a junior
role: you work from briefs written by our Growth Marketer and learn how a paid social channel is
run, with time each week set aside for training.

## What you'll do

- Film and edit short videos for TikTok and Instagram Reels, three or four a week.
- Write captions and test different hooks for the same video.
- Interview shift workers at warehouses about how they use the app.

## What you bring

- A few videos you made and edited yourself, for any channel, that you can show us.
- You know how TikTok and Instagram Reels work, and you notice why some videos hold attention.
- You can edit in CapCut, Premiere Pro or a similar tool.

## About the team

You join the growth team of three. We move fast, try a lot of ideas, and share what worked at the
Friday demo.

## How to apply

Send your CV and links to two or three of your videos through the careers site.
