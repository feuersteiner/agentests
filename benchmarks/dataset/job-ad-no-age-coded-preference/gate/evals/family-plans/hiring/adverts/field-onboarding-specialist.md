# Field Onboarding Specialist

Amsterdam, with travel to customer sites in the Netherlands and Belgium. Permanent, 40 hours.
Salary: €46,000 to €52,000 gross a year, plus 8% holiday allowance and a company car.

## The role

You set up Tessellate at new warehouses: you visit each site, import the staff and shift patterns,
and train the planners until they can build a rota on their own.

## What you'll do

- Spend three or four days a week at customer sites, and the rest in our Amsterdam office.
- Import staff lists and shift patterns, and check them with the site manager.
- Train planners, in person and on video.

## What you bring

- At least one year of experience training people to use a software product.
- A driving licence, because most sites are not near a train station.
- Because of the travel, this role suits someone without children at home and with no plans to
  start a family in the next couple of years.

## About the team

The onboarding team is four people who sit with customer success.
