# Campaign Manager, Benelux launch

Amsterdam, Keizersgracht office, hybrid (two days a week in the office). Fixed term, 12 to 18
months, depending on how the launch goes. Salary: €50,000 to €58,000 a year, plus 8% holiday
allowance.

## The role

Tessellate has run in the Netherlands for seven years and launches in Belgium next spring. You'll
plan and run the launch campaigns.

## What you'll do

- Keep 2 to 4 campaigns live at any time across TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn.
- Plan a budget of €20,000 to €30,000 a month with the Head of Growth.
- Report every two weeks on installs and sign-ups by region.

## What you bring

- At least two years of planning and running paid campaigns.
- You can read a campaign report and decide what to change.

## About the team

You join the growth team of three, who sit with sales and design.

## How to apply

Send your CV and one campaign you ran, with its results, through the careers site.
