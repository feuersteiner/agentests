# Q2 retro: growth team

_1 July 2026_

## Numbers

| Measure                                            | Q1    | Q2    |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----- | ----- |
| Trials started                                     | 1,310 | 1,420 |
| Trials that connected a data source on day one     | 38.0% | 41.0% |
| Trials that became paid workspaces                 | 11.2% | 11.8% |
| New paid workspaces with a second user in 30 days  | 52%   | 49%   |

The weekly numbers are in `data/q2-weekly.csv`.

## What went well

- The welcome email series went out in April. Trials that opened the second email connected a
  data source on day one 1.6 times as often as those that did not.
- The pricing page test settled the annual discount at two months free.

## What did not go well

- Stores without a web shop connector cannot get their data in. 41 trials asked support for a
  CSV import in Q2, and none of them became paid.
- Half of the trials that ended without paying never connected a data source, so they only ever
  saw empty dashboards.
- Fewer new workspaces added a second user after "Invite teammates" moved into settings in May.
