# Growth team

The growth team looks after sign-up, the 14-day trial and the first month of a paid workspace at
Larkspur Data.

## People

- Ines Duarte, Head of Growth.
- Priya Menon, product manager: the trial and the pricing page.
- Tom Ellery, senior engineer: sign-up and the data connectors.
- Sam Okafor, product designer: the onboarding screens and in-app messages.

## How we work

- At the end of each quarter we hold a retro and a brainstorm, written up in `planning/`, and
  write the next quarter's plan in this folder.
- Numbers review every Monday, from the trial dashboard.
- Questions go to #growth.
