# Growth team: Q3 2026 plan

_3 July 2026_

Based on the [Q2 retro](../../planning/q2-retro.md) and the
[Q3 brainstorm](../../planning/q3-brainstorm.md).

## Objective 1: Every trial sees its own numbers on day one

Owner: Priya Menon

Why now: half of the trials that ended without paying in Q2 never connected a data source, and 41
trials asked support for a CSV import.

Measure: trials that connect a data source on day one, from 41% to 50%.

What we'll ship:

- Guided setup checklist on the home screen of a new workspace: Priya Menon
- CSV import for stores without a web shop connector: Tom Ellery

## Objective 2: More trials become paid workspaces

Owner: Ines Duarte

Why now: trial-to-paid conversion only moved from 11.2% to 11.8% in Q2.

Measure: trial-to-paid conversion, from 11.8% to 13.5%.

What we'll ship:

- In-app reminders in the last week of the trial that show what the workspace has used: Sam Okafor
- Seven more trial days for a workspace that connects a second data source: Ines Duarte

## Objective 3: New workspaces bring their team

Owner: Sam Okafor

Why now: new paid workspaces with a second user in 30 days fell from 52% to 49% after "Invite
teammates" moved into settings.

Measure: new paid workspaces with a second user in 30 days, from 49% to 55%.

What we'll ship:

- "Invite teammates" back on the dashboard: Sam Okafor (Ines Duarte helping)
- Ask who else should get a daily report when a workspace sets one up: Tom Ellery

## Not this quarter

The referral programme and the mobile app stay parked, as the brainstorm says.
