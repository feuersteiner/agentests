# Q2 retro: growth team

_1 July 2026_

## How the Q2 objectives went

| Objective                                   | Target | Result |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------ |
| More trials connect a data source on day one | 45%    | 41%    |
| More trials become paid workspaces          | 13%    | 11.8%  |
| Launch the welcome email series             | April  | April  |

## What we shipped

- The welcome email series, in April.
- The pricing page test, which settled the annual discount at two months free.
- "Invite teammates" moved into settings, in May.

## What we learned

- Trials that opened the second welcome email connected a data source on day one 1.6 times as
  often as those that did not.
- 41 trials asked support for a CSV import, and none of them became paid.
- Fewer new workspaces added a second user after the invite button moved.
