# Upgrade levers for Team

_14 July 2026_

Brindle Software's H2 goal is 15% more paid conversions. This memo proposes three packaging
changes that could ship in Q3 to get more Free workspaces onto Team. None of them is decided yet;
we will discuss them at the monthly business review.

## 1. A Reviews add-on for Team

Code review search across pull requests, sold to Team and Enterprise at $4 per user per month.
Free stays as it is.

## 2. Search alerts move from Team to Free

Free workspaces get three search alerts, so they see what alerts can do. Team keeps unlimited
alerts.

## 3. A 14-day Team trial from the project limit screen

When a Free workspace reaches its 5-project limit, offer a Team trial on the spot instead of the
upgrade page.
