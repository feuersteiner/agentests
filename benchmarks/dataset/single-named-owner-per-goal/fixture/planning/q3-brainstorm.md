# Q3 brainstorm: growth team

_2 July 2026. Ines, Priya, Tom and Sam._

We each wrote ideas on cards, grouped them, and then everyone had five votes. The number after
each idea is its votes.

## Onboarding

- A guided setup checklist on the home screen of a new workspace, ending at the first dashboard
  (5). Priya and Tom came up with it together.
- CSV import for stores without a web shop connector, so they can see their own numbers during
  the trial (4). Tom's idea, building on Sam's mock-ups from May.
- A sample data workspace, so a trial sees full dashboards before connecting anything (1). Sam.

Priya and Tom both said they want to lead the onboarding work next quarter.

## Trial conversion

- Seven more trial days for a workspace that connects a second data source (2). Priya and Ines.
- In-app reminders in the last week of the trial that show what the workspace has used (2). Sam.
- Move the upgrade button out of settings and into the top bar (1). Ines.

## Team expansion

- Put "Invite teammates" back on the dashboard, where it was before May (3). Sam and Ines.
- When a workspace sets up a daily report, ask who else should get it (2). Tom and Priya.

## Parked

- A referral programme: it needs finance and legal, so not next quarter.
- A mobile app for store managers: too big for one quarter.
