# One named owner for every objective and every item to ship

## The rule

In a quarterly plan, every objective and every item the team will ship has exactly one named person as owner, not a team name, not two people, and not "TBD" or "shared".

Other people may be named as helping, as long as one person is clearly the owner, and each item has its own owner named next to it. A template for quarterly plans asks for one person's name as the owner of each objective and of each item to ship.

Apply it to every plan, set of objectives or plan template you write or edit, even when it is one
step of a longer task.

## Why

When a team or two people own something, each assumes the other is driving it, and nobody is
asked when it slips. One accountable name per objective and per item makes it clear who reports
progress and who decides. Plenty of people can help; only one person answers for it.

## Gotchas

- Brainstorm notes credit ideas to several people. Credit is not ownership: pick one owner, and
  list the others as helping if they will.
- Two people who both want to lead something are not co-owners, and neither is work someone asks
  to be split. Give one of them the objective and the other one of its items, or name the other
  as helping.
- Items under an objective need their own owner, even when it is the objective's owner.
- No team names ("Reporting team", "Design"), no "Owners:", no "Dana/Luis", no "TBD", "shared" or
  "whoever picks it up". If you cannot tell who should own something, choose one person and say
  so in the summary of your work rather than leaving it open.
- In a template, write the placeholder for one person: `Owner: <one person's name>`, never
  `Owner(s)` or `<team>`, and give every item to ship its own owner placeholder.

## Examples

Compliant:

> **Objective 2: Faster first report** (Owner: Dana Whit)
>
> We'll ship:
>
> - Report scheduling wizard: Luis Ortega
> - Saved filters on the report page: Dana Whit (helping: Mei Chen)

Violating:

> **Objective 2: Faster first report** (Owners: Dana Whit and Luis Ortega)
>
> We'll ship:
>
> - Report scheduling wizard: Reporting team
> - Saved filters on the report page: TBD

## Before you commit

Read each plan or template you changed. For every objective and every item to ship, point to the
one person named as its owner. Fix any that has none, a team, two people, "TBD" or "shared", then
check again.
