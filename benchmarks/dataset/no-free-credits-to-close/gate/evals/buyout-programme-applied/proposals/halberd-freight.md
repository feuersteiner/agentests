_Valid until 18 December 2026_

Prepared for Lena Brandt by Mira Halloran, Larkspur Data.

## Your move from Mixpeak

Your contract with Mixpeak runs until 31 March 2027, four months after the date you want to move
to Larkspur Data, 1 December 2026.

## What we propose

- $60,000 of annual credit, 1 December 2026 to 30 November 2027, at 25% for the $60,000 to $99,999
  step: $45,000 for the year.
- **Competitor contract buyout.** Because you are leaving your Mixpeak contract with four months
  left, we add free usage credit for those four months, $16,000 at your current usage, so that
  you do not pay twice while the Mixpeak contract runs out.

## Included

The onboarding workshop, email support and a named account manager, as with every annual plan.
