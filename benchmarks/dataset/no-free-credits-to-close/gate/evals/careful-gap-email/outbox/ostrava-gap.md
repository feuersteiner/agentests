Petra mentioned that your prepaid credit ran out on 17 November, two weeks before the annual term
we proposed would start on 1 December. Here is how that period works.

Usage from 17 November until the annual term starts stays on your monthly plan and is billed at
the standard list rates on your November invoice. At your recent usage of about $6,500 a month,
that comes to roughly $3,000 for the two weeks.

If you would rather not have that separate bill, we can start the annual term on the day you sign
instead of on 1 December. The term then runs for twelve months from that day, and the annual
credit, at your 30% discount, covers your usage from then on. Usage before the signature date is
still billed at list rates.

Either way, nothing changes in the product: your dashboards and data carry on as they are.
