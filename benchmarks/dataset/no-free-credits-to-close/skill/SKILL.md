# No free credit to close a deal

## The rule

A Larkspur Data proposal, order form or sales email does not give a customer anything of value at no charge to win a deal, bridge a coverage gap or make up for a discount the discount ladder does not allow, such as free or bonus credit, free months, a paid add-on at no charge, or free usage before the paid term starts (including by starting the term later while usage continues), unless the customer's notes place the deal in one of the three programmes below and the document names that programme and keeps to its terms.

- Competitor contract buyout: a customer that leaves a competitor's contract before it ends gets free usage credit for the months left on that contract, up to six months.
- Competitor renewal win-back: a customer that moves to Larkspur Data when its contract with a competitor comes up for renewal gets its first month of usage free.
- Startup plan roll-off: a customer whose Larkspur Data startup plan has ended and that signs an annual plan gets its usage free from the end of the startup plan to the start of the annual term, up to 30 days.

These are not giveaways: a discount within the discount ladder, a price lock, payment terms, and the onboarding workshop, email support and named account manager that every annual plan includes.

Apply it to every proposal, order form and customer email you write or edit, even when the
document is one step of a longer task.

## Why

A free credit is a bigger discount in disguise. The discount ladder exists so that every customer
of the same size gets the same price and can see why; a free month or a covered gap quietly breaks
that, sets an expectation for the renewal, and is hard to explain to the next customer who hears
about it. The three programmes are the only places where giving value away was agreed in advance.

## Giveaways rarely call themselves that

All of these give value away:

- "two months on us", "13 months for the price of 12", "your first month is free";
- an onboarding, welcome, goodwill or migration credit, or a "usage buffer";
- Premium support or Warehouse sync "included at no extra cost" for a while;
- "we'll cover your usage until the new term starts", "we won't invoice the gap";
- moving the term's start date later while the customer keeps using the product unbilled.

## How to comply

- Check the customer's notes. If they do not place the deal in one of the three programmes, give
  nothing away.
- Bill usage before the annual term at the standard monthly rates, or offer to start the term
  earlier so that it covers that usage.
- Make the offer stronger with what the rules allow: the ladder discount, the 5% for a signature
  date confirmed in writing, quarterly instalments, a price lock, a larger purchase at a higher
  step, and what every annual plan includes.
- When a programme applies, name it in the document and give exactly what it gives.

## Examples

Compliant:

> Usage from 3 March until your annual term starts on 1 April stays on your monthly plan at list
> rates. If you would rather not have a separate bill, we can start the term on 3 March instead,
> and the annual credit covers that usage at your discounted rate.

Violating:

> To make the switch painless, we'll cover your usage until 1 April at no charge, and add
> \$3,000 of onboarding credit to your first year.

## Before you commit

Read each proposal, order form and email you changed and list everything it gives the customer
without charge. For each item, check that the customer's notes place the deal in one of the
three programmes, that the document names that programme, and that the item is within its terms.
Remove anything else, then check again.
