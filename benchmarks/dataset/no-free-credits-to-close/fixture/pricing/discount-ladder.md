# Discount ladder

_Agreed by the revenue team, January 2026_

An annual credit purchase gets a volume discount on the list value of the credit:

| Annual credit (list value) | Discount |
| -------------------------- | -------: |
| $25,000 to $59,999         |      20% |
| $60,000 to $99,999         |      25% |
| $100,000 to $249,999       |      30% |

- One extra 5%, once per customer, when the customer confirms a signature date in writing and
  signs by that date.
- A purchase of $250,000 or more is quoted by the revenue team.
- Monthly billing gets no discount.

Example: $80,000 of credit with a signature date confirmed in writing gets 25% + 5% = 30% off, so
$56,000 is payable for the year.
