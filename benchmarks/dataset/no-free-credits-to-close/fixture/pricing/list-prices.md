# List prices

_Updated 1 September 2026_

Larkspur Data is billed by usage, paid for in credit: one dollar of credit covers 20,000
analytics events or 200 session recordings.

## Ways to pay

- **Monthly.** Usage is billed at list rates at the end of each month. A monthly customer can also
  buy a prepaid credit pack of $5,000, $10,000 or $20,000 at list rates; usage beyond the pack goes
  back on the monthly invoice.
- **Annual.** The customer buys a year of credit, at least $25,000 of list value, with the volume
  discount in `discount-ladder.md`. The term runs for twelve months from its start date, and
  credit left at the end of the term expires. The credit is invoiced when the term starts,
  payable within 30 days; with $50,000 or more of credit, the customer can pay in four quarterly
  instalments instead, at the same total.

## Included with every annual plan

- An onboarding workshop in the first month: two hours, remote, for up to ten people.
- Email support, answered within one business day.
- A named account manager.

## Add-ons

Sold with annual plans only, and not discounted.

| Add-on          | Price per year | What it adds                                         |
| --------------- | -------------: | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Premium support |         $9,000 | Phone and Slack support, answered within four hours  |
| Warehouse sync  |         $6,000 | A daily export of raw events to Snowflake or BigQuery |
