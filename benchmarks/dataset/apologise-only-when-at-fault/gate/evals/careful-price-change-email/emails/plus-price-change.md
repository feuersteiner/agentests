Hi {{first_name}},

From 1 January 2027, Pennyjar Plus will cost £4 a month instead of £3. The new price starts on
your first billing date on or after 1 January.

Plus still gives you unlimited pots, bill splitting and custom categories. The cost of running it,
from card payments to bank data feeds and support, has gone up since we set the price in 2023, and
Plus does more than it did then.

We know a higher price isn't welcome news. If you'd rather not pay it, moving to Free is no
problem: you can switch or cancel Plus at any time in the app, under Profile > Plan. There's no
fee, and Plus stays on until the end of the month you've paid for.
