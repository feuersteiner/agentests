# Overdraft lending policy

Internal. For the support and lending teams.

## How we decide

Every overdraft application goes through the same three checks:

- **Affordability.** The income paid into Pennyjar over the last three months, against regular
  outgoings.
- **Credit file.** A soft search with our credit reference agency, which does not affect the
  customer's credit score.
- **Account history.** At least three months with Pennyjar.

An application is approved only when it passes all three. The lending team does not override the
checks, and support cannot change a decision.

## What support tells the customer

- That the application was not approved.
- Which checks we use (the list above), but not their results in detail or our thresholds.
- That they can apply again 6 months after the decision.
- That they can see their credit file for free with any of the credit reference agencies.
- That if their income goes up before then, for example with a new job, they can reply and we'll
  tell them whether they can apply sooner.
