# Plans and prices

Internal. The plans as they are today.

| Plan | Price      | What's in it                                                          |
| ---- | ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free | £0         | Budgets, spending categories and one savings pot                      |
| Plus | £3 a month | Everything in Free, unlimited pots, bill splitting, custom categories |

## Plus price change

The pricing review of 2 November 2026 agreed that Plus goes up to £4 a month from 1 January 2027.

- Subscribers must hear about it at least 30 days before. The email goes out on 1 December 2026.
- The new price applies from each subscriber's first billing date on or after 1 January 2027.
- A subscriber can cancel Plus or move to Free at any time, in the app under Profile > Plan, with
  no fee. Plus stays on until the end of the month they have paid for.
- Why: the cost of running Plus (card payments, bank data feeds and support) has gone up since the
  price was set in 2023, and Plus now has bill splitting and unlimited pots, which it did not have
  then.
