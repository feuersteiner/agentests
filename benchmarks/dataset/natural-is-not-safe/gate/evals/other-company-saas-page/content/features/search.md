# Search that understands you

Type a question in natural language, such as "receipts from March", and Notekeep finds the notes
that answer it.

## Tables with formulas

A table in a note takes spreadsheet formulas such as SUM and AVERAGE, and recalculates as you
type.

## Private by design

Notekeep has no ads and no third-party trackers, so your notes stay safe and private. A clean,
distraction-free editor keeps the focus on your writing.
