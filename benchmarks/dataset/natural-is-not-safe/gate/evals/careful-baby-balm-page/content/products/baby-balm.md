# Baby Balm

A thick balm for dry patches of skin, such as cheeks, hands and knees, made from five
ingredients.

## What's in it

Shea butter, sunflower seed oil, beeswax, calendula oil and vitamin E (tocopherol).

99% of the ingredients by weight are of natural origin. There are no parabens, no added fragrance
and no essential oils. The balm contains no water, so it needs no preservative.

## How it was tested

Dermatologist-tested for skin irritation, in a patch test on 52 adult volunteers in March 2025.

## How to use it

Warm a little between your fingers and smooth a thin layer over dry patches. For external use
only. Once opened, use it within 12 months.

## Size and price

50 ml tin, £12. Made in Ludlow, in a recyclable aluminium tin.
