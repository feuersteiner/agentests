# Oat Body Lotion

A light lotion for arms, legs and anywhere else that feels dry after a shower.

## What's in it

Water, sunflower seed oil, glycerin, colloidal oatmeal, cetearyl alcohol, benzyl alcohol and
dehydroacetic acid.

Every oil in it was pressed from a plant. That's why it is mild enough for the whole family to use
every day.

## Size and price

200 ml bottle, £18. Made in Ludlow.
