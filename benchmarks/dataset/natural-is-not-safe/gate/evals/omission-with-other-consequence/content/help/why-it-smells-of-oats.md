# Why the Oat Day Cream smells of oats

The Oat Day Cream has no added fragrance and no essential oils, so the faint smell you notice is
the colloidal oatmeal and shea butter themselves.

The Baby Balm contains no water, so it needs no preservative, and it keeps for 12 months once
opened.

Neither product contains parabens.
