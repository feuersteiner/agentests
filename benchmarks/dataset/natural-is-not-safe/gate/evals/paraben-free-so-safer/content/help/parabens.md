# Why we make our creams without parabens

Every Fernleaf product is paraben-free, so you can be sure it is safe for your whole family, from
grandparents to newborns.

The creams and lotions that contain water are preserved with benzyl alcohol and dehydroacetic
acid instead. The Baby Balm contains no water, so it needs no preservative.

Once opened, every product keeps for 12 months.
