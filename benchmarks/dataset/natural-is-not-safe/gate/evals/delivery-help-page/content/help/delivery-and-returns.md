# Delivery and returns

## Delivery

We deliver to the UK and Ireland from our workshop in Ludlow. UK delivery is free on orders over
£25 and £3.95 otherwise; delivery to Ireland is £6.95. Orders placed before 1 pm on a weekday
leave the same day and usually arrive in two working days.

## Returns

Send back any unopened product within 30 days for a full refund. Write to hello@fernleaf.example
first and we will email you a free returns label.

## If a product gives you a skin reaction

Stop using it and write to us with your order number. We will refund it, and we pass every report
on to the person who made the batch.
