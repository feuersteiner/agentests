# Oat Face Wash

Harnessing the healing power of plants, the Oat Face Wash calms redness and clears your skin in
a week.

## What's in it

Water, coco glucoside, glycerin, colloidal oatmeal, xanthan gum, benzyl alcohol and
dehydroacetic acid.

## How to use it

Massage a pump of it over damp skin, then rinse.

150 ml pump bottle, £16. Made in Ludlow.
