# Frequently asked questions

## Where do you deliver?

We deliver to the UK and Ireland. UK delivery is free on orders over £25 and £3.95 otherwise.
Delivery to Ireland is £6.95. Orders placed before 1 pm on a weekday leave Ludlow the same day.

## Can I return a product?

Yes. Send back any unopened product within 30 days for a full refund. Write to
hello@fernleaf.example first and we will send you a free returns label.

## Are your products vegan?

The Oat Face Wash, Oat Day Cream and Oat Body Lotion are. The Baby Balm and the Calendula Hand
Cream contain beeswax, so they are not.

## Where can I find the full ingredients?

On each product's page, under "What's in it", and printed on the pack.

## How long does a product keep once opened?

Twelve months. The open-jar symbol on the pack says the same.

## Can I recycle the packaging?

Yes. Our tins and tubes are aluminium, our jars are glass and our bottles are recyclable plastic.
Rinse them out and put them in your household recycling.

## Is Fernleaf safe for sensitive skin?

Everyone's skin is different, so we can't promise that a product will suit yours. What we can
tell you is what is in each product and how it has been tested.

- The full ingredients are on every product page and on the pack. If you know an ingredient your
  skin reacts to, check the list for it.
- None of our products contains parabens. The Baby Balm and the three oat products have no added
  fragrance or essential oils; the Calendula Hand Cream is scented with lavender oil.
- The Baby Balm is dermatologist-tested for skin irritation, in a patch test on 52 adult
  volunteers. Our other products have not been through skin testing.
- Natural ingredients can cause reactions too. Before you use a new product, try a little on the
  inside of your arm and wait 48 hours. If you have a skin condition, ask your doctor or
  pharmacist first.

If a product doesn't agree with your skin, stop using it and write to hello@fernleaf.example.
