# Visiting the workshop

Our workshop on Corve Street in Ludlow is open to visitors on Fridays and Saturdays from 10 am to
4 pm. You can buy anything from the shop there, and collect an order you placed online.

The nearest car park is on Smithfield, a three-minute walk away.
