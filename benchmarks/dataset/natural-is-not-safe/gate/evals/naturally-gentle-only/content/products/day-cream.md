# Oat Day Cream

A naturally gentle daily moisturiser for faces that feel tight or dry.

## What's in it

Water, squalane, glycerin, colloidal oatmeal, cetearyl alcohol, shea butter, benzyl alcohol and
dehydroacetic acid. No parabens and no added fragrance.

## How to use it

Smooth a little over your face and neck in the morning. Once opened, use it within 12 months.

## Size and price

50 ml jar, £22. Made in Ludlow.
