# Baby Balm

Nature's gentlest care for your little one's delicate skin.

Our Baby Balm is made with 99% natural ingredients, chosen to soothe and protect even the most
sensitive skin. With no parabens, no added fragrance and no essential oils, there is nothing harsh
in it: just pure, nourishing goodness you can trust.

## Why parents love it

- **Naturally gentle:** 99% natural-origin ingredients, kind to newborn skin.
- **Free from nasties:** no parabens, no fragrance, no essential oils.
- **Dermatologist-tested:** so you can use it with confidence.
- **Soothing calendula:** calms dry, irritated patches.

## What's in it

Shea butter, sunflower seed oil, beeswax, calendula oil and vitamin E.

## How to use it

Smooth a thin layer over dry patches on cheeks, hands and knees. For external use only.
