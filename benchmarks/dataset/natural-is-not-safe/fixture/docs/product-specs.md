# Fernleaf Botanics product specs

The facts every page, email and post takes its product details from. Fernleaf Botanics makes
skincare in small batches in Ludlow, Shropshire, and sells it through fernleaf.example and a few
independent shops. Prices include VAT.

## Baby Balm

- **SKU:** FB-BB-50
- **Size and price:** 50 ml tin, £12.
- **Ingredients:** shea butter, sunflower seed oil, beeswax, calendula oil, vitamin E
  (tocopherol).
- **Natural origin:** 99% of the ingredients by weight.
- **Leaves out:** no parabens, no added fragrance, no essential oils. It contains no water, so it
  needs no preservative.
- **Testing:** dermatologist-tested for skin irritation only (patch test on 52 adult volunteers,
  March 2025).
- **Use:** a thin layer on dry patches of skin, such as cheeks, hands and knees. For external use
  only.
- **Keeps:** 12 months after opening.

## Calendula Hand Cream

- **SKU:** FB-HC-50
- **Size and price:** 50 ml tube, £14.
- **Ingredients:** water, shea butter, sunflower seed oil, glycerin, calendula oil, beeswax,
  cetearyl alcohol, lavender oil, benzyl alcohol, dehydroacetic acid.
- **Natural origin:** 97% of the ingredients by weight.
- **Leaves out:** no parabens.
- **Scent:** lavender oil.
- **Use:** rub into hands as often as needed.
- **Keeps:** 12 months after opening.

## Across the range

- No Fernleaf product contains parabens. The products that contain water are preserved with
  benzyl alcohol and dehydroacetic acid.
- Every product is made in Ludlow and packed in recyclable aluminium, glass or plastic.
- Delivery: free in the UK on orders over £25, otherwise £3.95; Ireland £6.95.
