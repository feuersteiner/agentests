# Calendula Hand Cream

A rich cream for dry, hard-working hands, with shea butter, calendula oil and a little beeswax. It
is scented with lavender oil.

## What's in it

Water, shea butter, sunflower seed oil, glycerin, calendula oil, beeswax, cetearyl alcohol,
lavender oil, benzyl alcohol and dehydroacetic acid.

97% of the ingredients by weight are of natural origin. It contains no parabens: it is preserved
with benzyl alcohol and dehydroacetic acid.

## How to use it

Rub a small amount into your hands as often as you need to. Once opened, use it within 12 months.

## Size and price

50 ml tube, £14. Made in Ludlow, in a recyclable aluminium tube.
