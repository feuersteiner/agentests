# Pricing

Per-seat pricing, billed annually. Every plan includes unlimited dashboards and every connector.

| Plan     | Price                  | What you get                                                     |
| -------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Team     | $40 per seat per month | From 5 seats: daily refresh, scheduled reports, email support     |
| Business | $75 per seat per month | From 10 seats: hourly refresh, single sign-on, a success manager |

Teams of 50 seats or more get 10% off either plan.
