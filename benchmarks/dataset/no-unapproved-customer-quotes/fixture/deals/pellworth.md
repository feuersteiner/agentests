# Pellworth

- Account executive: Ravi Mehta
- Stage: discovery
- Contacts: Helen Marsh, Chief Operating Officer; Owen Tate, Head of Finance

## Who they are

Pellworth sells homeware from 38 stores in the south of England and a web shop that makes up
about a third of its sales.

## Discovery call, 12 November 2026

- Store managers get a weekly sales PDF on Tuesday, when the week is half gone.
- The web shop runs on Shopify, the tills on Lightspeed, and stock sits in a Postgres database
  the warehouse team runs.
- Finance builds the Monday trading pack by hand in Excel, which takes Owen's team most of Monday.
- They want a daily dashboard for each store and one trading view for head office.
- Helen asked how other retailers use Larkspur.
