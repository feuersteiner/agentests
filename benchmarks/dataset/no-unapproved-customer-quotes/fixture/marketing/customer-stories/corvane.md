# Corvane: half the time on the weekly report

_Published at larkspurdata.example/customers/corvane on 10 March 2026._

Corvane sells furniture online across the UK and Ireland. Its five-person analytics team used to
spend most of Monday building the weekly trading report from Shopify exports, ad platform
spreadsheets and the warehouse system.

In 2025 Corvane connected all three to Larkspur and replaced the spreadsheet with a scheduled
report that reaches every manager's inbox at 7am on Monday.

> "We cut our weekly reporting time in half."
>
> Priya Natarajan, Head of Analytics, Corvane

The team now spends the time it saved on pricing and range analysis.
