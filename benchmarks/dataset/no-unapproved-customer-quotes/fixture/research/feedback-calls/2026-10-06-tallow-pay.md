# Feedback call: Tallow Pay

- Date: 6 October 2026
- Customer: Tallow Pay, a payments app for independent shops. Business plan, 80 seats, customer
  since March 2026.
- On the call: Marcus Oyelaran (VP Product, Tallow Pay) and Jess Whitfield (Product Manager,
  Larkspur).

## Why they switched

- They ran Mixpanel next to a home-grown SQL dashboard: two sources of truth and constant
  arguments about which was right.
- Marcus: "Before Larkspur, every Monday started with a fight about whose numbers were right."
- Marcus: "We ripped out Mixpanel in a weekend and nobody missed it."

## What is working

- Marcus: "Honestly, it's the first analytics tool our PMs open every day."
- The 7am Slack reports are their most-used feature; PMs discuss the numbers in the thread.

## What is not

- The SQL editor feels clunky next to their old tool: no autocomplete for table names.
- They want usage by team on the admin page.
