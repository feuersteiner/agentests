# Feedback call: Mossgiel Foods

- Date: 21 October 2026
- Customer: Mossgiel Foods, a Scottish food brand that sells online, in its own shops and
  wholesale. Team plan, 18 seats, customer since May 2026.
- On the call: Callum Reid (COO, Mossgiel Foods) and Jess Whitfield (Product Manager, Larkspur).

## Why they switched

- They were about to hire analysts. Callum: "We'd have hired two analysts if we hadn't found you."
- They wanted wholesale orders and online orders in one place.
