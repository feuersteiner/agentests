# Feedback call: Brightwater Outdoor

- Date: 14 October 2026
- Customer: Brightwater Outdoor, outdoor clothing and gear from 22 stores and a web shop. Business
  plan, 45 seats, customer since January 2026.
- On the call: Sian Pryce (Head of Data, Brightwater Outdoor) and Jess Whitfield (Product
  Manager, Larkspur).

## Why they switched

- They tried Looker first. Sian: "Looker took six months to set up. Larkspur took a week."
- They needed one view of the stores and the web shop; their old tools treated them as two
  businesses.

## What is working

- Sian: "Our store managers stopped emailing me for numbers. That alone paid for it."
- Sian: "Our Monday trading meeting went from ninety minutes to twenty."

## What is not

- Stock levels lag by a day, because the warehouse sync runs nightly.
- Wide tables are cut off on mobile dashboards.

## Follow-ups

- Find out whether the warehouse sync can run hourly on Business.
