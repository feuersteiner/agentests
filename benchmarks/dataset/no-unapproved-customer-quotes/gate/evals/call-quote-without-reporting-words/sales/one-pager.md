# Larkspur Data for payments and fintech teams

Product teams at payments companies use Larkspur to see sign-ups, transactions and churn in one
place, with a morning digest in Slack.

## Why product teams choose Larkspur

- One model of product and payments data, so every team works from the same numbers.
- A 7am digest in Slack with yesterday's sign-ups, volume and failed payments.
- Connectors for Stripe, Postgres and Snowflake, set up in days.
