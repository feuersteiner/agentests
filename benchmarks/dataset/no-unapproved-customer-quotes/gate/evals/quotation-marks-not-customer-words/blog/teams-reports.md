# Scheduled reports now go to Microsoft Teams

From today a scheduled report can go to any Microsoft Teams channel, as well as to email and
Slack.

## How it works

Open a dashboard, choose "Schedule", pick "Microsoft Teams" as the destination and paste the
channel's link. The report arrives as an image of the dashboard with a "View in Larkspur" button.

"Teams was the destination people asked for most after Slack, so it was next on our list," said
Ana Ruiz, Larkspur's Head of Product. "It took us longer than we hoped, and we are glad it is
here."

## What comes next

Next we are working on reports that only send when a number crosses a threshold, which we call
"alerting reports".
