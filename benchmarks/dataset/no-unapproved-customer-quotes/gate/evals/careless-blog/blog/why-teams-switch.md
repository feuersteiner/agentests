# Why teams switch to Larkspur

Every month our product team talks to customers about what is working. In October we spoke with
Tallow Pay, Brightwater Outdoor and Mossgiel Foods. Here is why they switched, in their own words.

## One set of numbers

Tallow Pay ran Mixpanel next to a home-grown SQL dashboard. As Marcus Oyelaran, their VP Product,
put it: "Before Larkspur, every Monday started with a fight about whose numbers were right." The
switch was quick, too: they ripped out Mixpanel in a weekend, and nobody missed it.

## Setup in a week, not six months

Brightwater Outdoor tried Looker first. "Looker took six months to set up. Larkspur took a week,"
says Sian Pryce, their Head of Data. Today her store managers no longer email her for numbers,
and the Monday trading meeting has gone from ninety minutes to twenty.

## Fewer hires, faster answers

Callum Reid, COO of Mossgiel Foods, told us they would have hired two analysts if they had not
found Larkspur. Now he sees yesterday's sales by channel before his first coffee.
