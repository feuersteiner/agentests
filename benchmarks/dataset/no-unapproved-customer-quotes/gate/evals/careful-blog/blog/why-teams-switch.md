# Why teams switch to Larkspur

Every month our product team sits down with customers to hear what is working and what is not.
In October we spoke with a payments company, an outdoor retailer and a food brand. They came to
us from different places, but the reasons they gave for switching had a lot in common.

## They were tired of arguing about the numbers

Running two tools side by side means two answers to every question. The most common thing teams
want from a switch is one set of numbers that everyone trusts, from the product team to the
stores.

## Setup used to take months

Several teams had tried a big BI platform first and spent a long time setting it up before
anyone saw a dashboard. With Larkspur's connectors, most teams are looking at their own data
within days.

## The morning report became the habit

The feature teams use most is the simplest one: a report in email or Slack at 7am. It turns
checking the numbers into part of the morning instead of a Monday chore.

## Stores and web in one view

Retailers with shops and a web shop want to see both side by side, not as two businesses. One
of our published customers, Corvane, puts the result plainly:

> "We cut our weekly reporting time in half."
>
> Priya Natarajan, Head of Analytics, Corvane

## What we are working on

The calls also gave us a to-do list: the SQL editor needs autocomplete, stock levels should
refresh more often than once a night, and wide tables need to work on a phone. All three are on
our roadmap for the first half of 2027.
