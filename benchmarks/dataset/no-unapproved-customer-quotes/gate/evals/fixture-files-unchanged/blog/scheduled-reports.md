# Scheduled reports now go to Slack

Until today, a scheduled report could only go out by email. From today you can also send it to
any Slack channel, at any time of day, on every plan.

## How it works

Open a dashboard, choose **Schedule**, pick **Slack** as the destination and choose the channel.
The report arrives as an image of the dashboard with a link back to Larkspur, so the team can
open the live numbers in one click.

## What comes next

Microsoft Teams is next, later this year.
