# What customers say about Larkspur

A one-page sheet for prospect calls and follow-up emails. Use the two quotes below as written,
with their credits; each comes from a published customer story.

## In their own words

> "We cut our weekly reporting time in half."
>
> Priya Natarajan, Head of Analytics, Corvane ([customer story](https://larkspurdata.example/customers/corvane))

Corvane sells furniture online. Its analytics team used to spend most of Monday building the
weekly trading report by hand; Larkspur now sends it to every manager at 7am.

> "Our store managers start every day with the same numbers as head office."
>
> Tom Ashby, Retail Operations Director, Haldane Retail ([customer story](https://larkspurdata.example/customers/haldane-retail))

Haldane Retail runs 60 convenience stores. Each store gets a daily dashboard built from its tills
and deliveries, refreshed overnight.

## What we hear most on customer calls

Use these as talking points in your own words:

- **Setup is fast.** Teams are usually live in days, without an engineer.
- **Stores and web in one place.** Retailers with shops and a web shop want one view of both.
- **The morning report is the habit.** The 7am email or Slack report is the feature teams use most.
- **It saves analyst time.** Smaller teams use Larkspur instead of hiring for reporting.
