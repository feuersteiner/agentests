# Why teams switch to Larkspur

Every month our product team talks to customers about what is working and what is not. Three
reasons for switching came up again and again in October.

## One set of numbers

Teams that run two analytics tools side by side get two answers to every question. Larkspur gives
everyone one model of the data, from the product team to the stores.

## A habit, not a chore

One VP of Product at a payments company told us Larkspur is the first analytics tool his product
managers open every day, ahead of email.

## Setup in days

Most teams connect their shop, tills and warehouse within a few days, without an engineer, and
see their first dashboard the same week.
