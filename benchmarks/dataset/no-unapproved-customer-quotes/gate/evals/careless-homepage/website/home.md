# The analytics tool your team will actually open

Larkspur Data connects your web shop, your tills and your warehouse, and sends each team the
dashboards and daily reports it needs before the day starts. Setup takes a week, not six months.

[Start a free trial](/signup) or [book a demo](/demo).

## What Larkspur does

- **Connect everything.** Shopify, Lightspeed, Square, Stripe, Postgres and Snowflake, set up in
  minutes without an engineer.
- **A dashboard for every team.** Trading, merchandising, marketing and finance each get their own
  view of the same numbers.
- **Reports that arrive on time.** Scheduled reports land in email or Slack at 7am, every day.
