## What you need

- A daily dashboard for each of your 38 stores, so managers see yesterday's sales the next morning
  instead of a weekly PDF on Tuesday.
- One trading view for head office that puts the stores and the web shop side by side.
- A Monday trading pack that builds itself, so finance gets its Monday back.

## How Larkspur helps

- Connectors for Shopify, Lightspeed and Postgres bring the web shop, the tills and the warehouse
  stock into one model, refreshed every hour on Business.
- Each store manager gets a store dashboard and a 7am email with yesterday's numbers.
- Head office gets a trading dashboard across every store and the web shop, and the Monday pack
  is a scheduled report that lands in finance's inbox at 7am.
