# Haldane Retail: one set of numbers for 60 stores

_Published at larkspurdata.example/customers/haldane-retail on 18 June 2026._

Haldane Retail runs 60 convenience stores across the north of England. Before Larkspur, store
managers got a weekly PDF from head office and kept their own spreadsheets in between, so the
stores and head office rarely agreed on the numbers.

Haldane now sends each store a daily dashboard built from its tills and deliveries, refreshed
overnight.

> "Our store managers start every day with the same numbers as head office."
>
> Tom Ashby, Retail Operations Director, Haldane Retail
