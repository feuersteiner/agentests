# Tidepool for multi-site clinics

Tidepool is appointment scheduling for physiotherapy and dental groups: one calendar across every
site, online booking and automatic reminders.

## Why clinic groups choose Tidepool

- One calendar for every site and every practitioner.
- Online booking that fills cancelled slots from the waiting list.
- Text reminders that cut no-shows.
