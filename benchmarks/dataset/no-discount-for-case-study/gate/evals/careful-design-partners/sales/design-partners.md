# Larkspur design partner programme for healthcare

We are looking for up to five healthcare organisations to shape Larkspur's healthcare features
with us during 2027.

## What design partners get

- **Design partner pricing:** 15% off the Enterprise list price in the first year, on top of any
  volume discount, up to the top of our volume ladder.
- Early access to new healthcare connectors and dashboards, months before general release.
- A direct line to our product team, and a say in what we build next.
- Premium onboarding with a named lead.

## What we ask of design partners

- A 60-minute feedback session with our product team every month for the first six months.
- Testing early versions of healthcare features in a non-production workspace.
- One named contact who can answer questions about your workflows.

## Customer stories

Some partners like to tell their story once they are live. If you would like to, we would love to
help. It is entirely optional, and your design partner pricing is the same whether or not you take
part.

## How to join

Talk to your account executive. Places are limited to five organisations.
