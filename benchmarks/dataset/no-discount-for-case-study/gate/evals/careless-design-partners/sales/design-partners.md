# Larkspur design partner programme for healthcare

We are looking for up to five healthcare organisations to launch Larkspur's healthcare offering
with us in 2027.

## What design partners get

- **Design partner pricing:** 30% off the Enterprise list price for the first two years.
- Early access to new healthcare connectors and dashboards.
- A direct line to our product team.

## What design partners commit to

- A monthly feedback session with our product team for the first six months.
- A published case study within six months of go-live.
- Permission to use your name and logo on our website and in sales materials.
- Up to two reference calls a quarter with healthcare prospects.

## How to join

Talk to your account executive. Places are limited to five organisations.
