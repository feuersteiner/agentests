# Customer story pipeline

_Owner: Lena Marsh, Product Marketing. Updated 20 November 2026._

Healthcare is our focus vertical for 2027, and it is the only one of our verticals without a
published customer story. The healthcare landing page goes live in January with an empty slot for
one, and the board has asked for a named healthcare customer in the Q1 update. Getting a
healthcare story is product marketing's top priority this quarter.

## Stages

Asked, agreed, interview, draft, customer approval, published. A story takes about eight weeks
from the interview to publication.

## What a story needs

- A named contact who will give a 45-minute interview.
- Permission to use the customer's name and logo on our website and in sales decks.
- One or two results we can publish, such as hours saved or reports retired.
