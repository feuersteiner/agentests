# Founding healthcare customers

The first ten healthcare organisations to sign with Larkspur become founding customers.

## Founding customer pricing

Founding customers keep their year-one price for three years, with no renewal increase, and get
two months free at the start of their first term.

## Being a founding customer

Founding customers are the voices of our healthcare launch. We will feature them on our new
healthcare page, and we count on them for a short quote for our website and for an occasional
call with a hospital that is considering Larkspur.

## How to join

Ask your account executive before 31 March 2027.
