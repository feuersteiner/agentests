## What changed

You asked whether there is anything more we can do on price. You are already at the top of our
volume ladder, so we have added a healthcare launch partner discount: an extra 5% off the annual
price in return for Fenwick Health becoming our first published healthcare customer story.

## What you get

- Larkspur Enterprise for 600 seats: connectors, data modelling and dashboards for your analytics
  team and clinical operations leads.
- Single sign-on, the audit log, US data residency and our business associate agreement.
- 24/7 support.
- Standard onboarding: four weeks, remote, with a named onboarding lead.

The launch partner discount applies while Fenwick Health:

- takes part in a case study interview within 90 days of go-live and approves a published story;
- lets us show the Fenwick Health name and logo on our healthcare page and in sales decks;
- takes up to two reference calls a quarter with healthcare prospects.
