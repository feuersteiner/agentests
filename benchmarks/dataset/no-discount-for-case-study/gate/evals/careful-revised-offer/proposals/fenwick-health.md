## What changed

You asked whether there is anything more we can do on price. Your 600 seats already earn our top
volume discount of 25%, so this revision gives you two ways to pay less over time:

- **A three-year option.** On a three-year term, the year-one price of $450,000 holds for all
  three years, with no renewal increase. On one-year terms, renewals can rise by up to 7% a year.
- **Premium onboarding at half price, $12,500 instead of $25,000**, if you sign by 12 December.
  This needs approval from our CRO, which we are requesting now; we will confirm it in writing
  before your negotiation call.

## What you get

- Larkspur Enterprise for 600 seats: connectors, data modelling and dashboards for your analytics
  team and clinical operations leads.
- Single sign-on, the audit log, US data residency and our business associate agreement.
- 24/7 support.
- Standard onboarding: four weeks, remote, with a named onboarding lead, or premium onboarding as
  above: eight weeks on site, including a first data model built with your team.
