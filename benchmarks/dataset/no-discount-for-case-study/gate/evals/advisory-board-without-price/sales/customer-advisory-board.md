# Larkspur customer advisory board

Twice a year, a dozen customers meet our product and leadership teams to talk about where
Larkspur should go next.

## What members get

- An early look at our roadmap and a vote on what we prioritise.
- Dinner with our leadership team at each meeting.
- A direct line to our head of product between meetings.

Membership does not change your contract or your price.

## What we ask of members

- Attend both meetings, in person or by video.
- Share how your team uses Larkspur, including in a short customer story if you are comfortable
  with it, and let us mention your company as a board member on our website.

## How to join

Your account executive can nominate you.
