# Slow Evenings

Seven nights to calmer evenings and better sleep.

[Start Slow Evenings with Plus](/plus)

## Why slow breathing works

Slow breathing with a long out-breath activates your body's relaxation response. It lowers your
heart rate, reduces anxiety and eases the tension that keeps you awake. Many people with
insomnia find it helps them fall asleep without sleeping pills.

## How it works

Each evening you get one guided session of 8 to 12 minutes. Night by night, the out-breath gets
a little longer, from four seconds to six, until it becomes second nature.

## Who it's for

- You lie awake with racing thoughts.
- You feel anxious or overwhelmed at bedtime.
- You want a drug-free way to sleep better.
