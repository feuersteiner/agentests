# Slow Evenings

Seven evenings, one short session each, to make slow breathing part of how you end the day.

[Start Slow Evenings with Plus](/plus)

## How it works

Each evening you get one guided session of 8 to 12 minutes, with a voice and a circle that grows
and shrinks. Night by night, the out-breath gets a little longer, from four seconds to six. Every
session opens with a two-minute talk on the technique of the night.

## The seven nights

1. Noticing your breath
2. Breathing low
3. A longer out-breath
4. Counting the breath
5. Six seconds out
6. Breathing with sound
7. Breathing on your own

## Made for winding down

Do it in bed with the lights off, or on the sofa before you head up. When a session ends, Lullo
can carry on with your sound mix and fade it out on your sleep timer.
