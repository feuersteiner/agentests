# Your own mix for bedtime

Pick up to three sounds, set the volume of each and save the mix for tomorrow night.

## What you can listen to

- **Rain and water:** light rain, rain on a tent, a stream, waves on shingle.
- **Steady noise:** brown noise, pink noise, a desk fan, a washing machine.
- **Places:** a night train, a crackling fire, a library after closing.

There are more than a hundred recordings, and the free plan includes 20 of them.

## Set it and forget it

The sleep timer fades your mix out after 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or lets it play all night.
With Plus, download your mixes and listen without a connection, on a plane or in a tent.
