# Listen to Lullo offline

With Lullo Plus you can download sounds, mixes and breathing exercises and play them without a
connection.

1. Open the sound, mix or exercise you want.
2. Tap the download arrow next to its name. A tick shows when it is saved.
3. To see everything you have saved, open **Library** and choose **Downloads**.

Downloads use about 20 MB per hour of sound. To remove one, swipe left on it in **Downloads**.

Downloads stay on your phone while your Plus subscription is active. If Plus ends, they stop
playing until you subscribe again.
