# Dozeleaf One-a-Night

Dozeleaf One-a-Night is a pharmacy medicine licensed by the MHRA for the short-term relief of
temporary insomnia in adults, such as after a change of time zone or a stressful week.

Take one tablet 20 minutes before bed. Do not take it for more than seven nights in a row.

Ask our pharmacist before you buy if you are pregnant, take other medicines or have a long-term
health condition. If your sleeplessness lasts more than two weeks, see your doctor.

Always read the label.
