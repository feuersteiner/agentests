# Why listeners choose Lullo

Nearly half of our listeners came to Lullo because they had trouble getting to sleep. After two
weeks of using it at bedtime, 64% said they fell asleep more easily.

## What they use

Most start with a sound mix and the sleep timer, then add a breathing exercise. Box breathing is
the most played.
