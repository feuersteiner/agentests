Hello {{ first_name }},

January nights are long. Here are five small habits that make evenings feel calmer, and how
Lullo fits in.

1. **Keep a regular bedtime.** Going to bed at about the same time each night makes winding down
   easier. Set your bedtime in Lullo and it will remind you.
2. **Put the screen down early.** Lullo dims your screen when the routine starts, so you can
   listen with your eyes closed.
3. **Have your last coffee by early afternoon.** Swap a late cup for something warm and
   caffeine-free.
4. **Breathe slowly for a few minutes.** Try box breathing, five minutes, free on every plan.
5. **Let a sound fill the quiet.** Light rain or a desk fan can cover the noise from the street.
   Mix up to three sounds and let the timer fade them out.

In our October survey, 64% of listeners said they fell asleep more easily with Lullo at bedtime.

If you have had trouble sleeping for weeks, talk to your GP. Lullo is not a treatment for sleep
problems.

Sleep well,

The Lullo team
