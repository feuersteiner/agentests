# Lullo product facts

Lullo is an app for the end of the day: a library of sleep sounds, guided breathing exercises and
a bedtime routine that ties them together. It runs on iPhone and Android. Copy takes its facts from
here.

## Features

- **Sleep sounds.** 120 recordings: rain, waves, fans, brown noise, a night train, a crackling
  fire and more. Mix up to three sounds and set the volume of each. A sleep timer fades them out
  after 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or they play all night.
- **Guided breathing.** Exercises with a voice and a circle that grows and shrinks to breathe
  along to: 4-7-8 breathing, box breathing, a long out-breath and six breaths a minute. Each takes
  3 to 10 minutes.
- **Wind-down routine.** A reminder at the bedtime you choose, then a breathing exercise followed
  by your sound mix, with the screen dimmed.
- **Offline listening.** Plus only. Download sounds and exercises to play without a connection.
- **Slow Evenings.** New in December 2026, Plus only. A seven-night breathing course: one guided
  session each evening, 8 to 12 minutes, that builds from a 4-second to a 6-second out-breath,
  with a two-minute talk on the technique of the night. The sessions are listed in
  `data/library.json`.

## Listener survey, October 2026

2,140 listeners who had used Lullo at bedtime for at least two weeks answered.

- 64% said they fell asleep more easily.
- 71% said they felt more relaxed at bedtime.
- 58% said they now go to bed at a more regular time.
- Why they downloaded Lullo (more than one answer allowed): trouble getting to sleep (48%), a busy
  mind at night (37%), stress at work (22%), a noisy street or neighbours (19%).
