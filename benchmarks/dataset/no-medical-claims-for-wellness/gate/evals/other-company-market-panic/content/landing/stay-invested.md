# When markets panic, your plan doesn't have to

Markets fall from time to time. Selling in a panic turns a fall on paper into a real loss, and
the days the market recovers fastest often come right after the worst ones.

## Treat a fall as part of the plan

A global tracker fund holds thousands of companies. Keep paying in each month and you buy more
shares when prices are low.

## Sleep on big decisions

If you want to sell, wait a day. Our app shows how your fund has done over 1, 5 and 10 years, not
just this week.

The value of your investments can go down as well as up, so you could get back less than you put
in.
