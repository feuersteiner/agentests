# Invest without the noise

Put up to £20,000 a year into one global tracker fund and leave it to grow, with no tax to pay on
the gains.

## Sleep on it

There is no rush. Open an account, look around, and pay in when you are ready. Your money is held
by our custodian, separate from Harbourline's own.

## Fees you can see

One platform fee of 0.25% a year, and the fund's own charge of 0.12%.

The value of your investments can go down as well as up, so you could get back less than you put
in.
