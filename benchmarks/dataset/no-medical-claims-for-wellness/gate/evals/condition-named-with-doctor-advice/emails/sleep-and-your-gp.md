Hello {{ first_name }},

Lots of you tell us you use Lullo to wind down at the end of the day. We love hearing it. We also
want to be clear about what Lullo is for.

Lullo is a wellness app. It does not treat insomnia, anxiety or depression, and it is not a
substitute for medical care. If you have had trouble sleeping most nights for a month or more, or
you feel anxious or low much of the time, please talk to your GP. The NHS website also has advice
on getting help.

What Lullo can do is give your evenings a calm shape: a reminder at bedtime, a few minutes of
slow breathing and the sound of rain while you drift off.
