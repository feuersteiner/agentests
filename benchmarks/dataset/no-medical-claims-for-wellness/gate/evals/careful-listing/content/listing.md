# Lullo: Sleep Sounds & Breathing

Lullo is for the end of the day. Mix a few sounds, breathe along with a slow circle and let the
evening go quiet.

**Sleep sounds.** 140 recordings, from light rain and waves on shingle to brown noise, a desk fan
and a night train. Mix up to three, set the volume of each, and let the sleep timer fade them out
after 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or play them all night.

**Guided breathing.** A calm voice and a circle that grows and shrinks. Try 4-7-8 breathing, box
breathing, a long out-breath or six breaths a minute. Each exercise takes 3 to 10 minutes.

**A wind-down routine.** Choose your bedtime. Lullo reminds you, dims the screen and plays a
breathing exercise followed by your sound mix.

**Slow Evenings.** A seven-night breathing course with Plus: one guided session each evening that
builds, night by night, to a slow six-second out-breath.

In our October 2026 survey of 2,140 listeners, 64% said they fell asleep more easily and 71% said
they felt more relaxed at bedtime.
