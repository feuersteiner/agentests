# Slow Evenings

Seven evenings, one short session each, to make slow breathing part of how you end the day.

## How it works

Each evening you get one guided session of 8 to 12 minutes, with a voice and a circle that grows
and shrinks. Night by night, the out-breath gets a little longer, from four seconds to six.

## Why a longer out-breath

A long, slow out-breath may help ease anxiety, which is why the course builds towards it one
night at a time.
