# Let the evening go quiet

Mix a few sounds, breathe along with a slow circle and put the day down.

[Try Lullo free](/download)

## Sounds to drift off to

More than a hundred recordings, from rain on a tent to a fan on low. Mix up to three, set the
volume of each and let a timer fade them out.

## Breathing you can follow in the dark

A calm voice and a circle that grows and shrinks. Exercises take 3 to 10 minutes, so there is one
for the nights you are already half asleep.

## A routine that starts itself

Pick a bedtime and Lullo reminds you, dims the screen and plays a breathing exercise followed by
your sound mix.
