# Quillnote for students

Lecture notes, reading notes and essay drafts in one place, on your laptop and your phone.

## 40% off your first year of Plus

Sign up with your school or university email address and enter the code **STUDENT40** when you
choose annual billing. Your first year of Plus costs $57.60 instead of $96 paid monthly. Later
years are billed at the usual annual price, $76.80.

The code works until the end of 30 September 2027.

## What Plus gives you

- **Every note on every device.** Sync across up to 5 devices.
- **Offline access.** Read and write in a lecture hall with no signal.
- **Linked notes.** Type `[[` to link a lecture to the reading it covers.
- **Version history.** Go back to any version of a note from the past year.

[Sign up with your school email](https://quillnote.example/signup?code=STUDENT40)
