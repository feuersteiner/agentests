# Quillnote for students

Lecture notes, reading notes and essay drafts in one place, on your laptop and your phone.

## 40% off your first year of Plus

Sign up with your school or university email address and enter the code **STUDENT40** when you
choose annual billing. Your first year of Plus costs $57.60 instead of $96 paid monthly.

The code works until the end of 30 September 2027. Only 500 codes are available, and they are
going fast.

## What Plus gives you

- **Every note on every device.** Sync across up to 5 devices.
- **Offline access.** Read and write in a lecture hall with no signal.
