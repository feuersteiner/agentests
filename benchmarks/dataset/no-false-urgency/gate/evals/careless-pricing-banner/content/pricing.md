# Pricing

Start on Free. Move to Plus when you want every note on every device.

| | Free | Plus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0 | $8 a month, or $76.80 a year |
| Notes | 100 | Unlimited |
| Devices | 1 | 5 |
| Offline access | No | Yes |
| Version history | 7 days | 1 year |

## Paying yearly

Plus costs $8 a month when you pay monthly. Pay yearly and it is $76.80 a year, which is 20% less.
You can switch between monthly and yearly billing in Settings, under Billing.

## Free trial

Every new account gets Plus free for 14 days. When the trial is over, the account moves to Free
and your notes stay where they are. No card is needed to start.

## Questions

**Can I cancel?** Yes, in Settings, under Billing. Plus stays on until the end of the period you
paid for.

**What happens to my notes if I go back to Free?** They are all kept. You can read and export
every note, and edit the 100 most recent.
