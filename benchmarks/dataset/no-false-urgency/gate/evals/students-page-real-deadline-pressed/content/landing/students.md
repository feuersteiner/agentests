# Back to school with Quillnote

## 40% off your first year of Plus, until 30 September

The student code **STUDENT40** is a limited-time offer: it stops working after 30 September.

costs $57.60.

- **Every note on every device.** Sync across up to 5 devices.
- **Offline access.** Read and write in a lecture hall with no signal.
