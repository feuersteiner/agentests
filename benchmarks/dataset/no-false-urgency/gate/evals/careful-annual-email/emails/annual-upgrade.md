You pay for Quillnote Plus month by month, at $8 a month. That comes to $96 over a year.

If you pay yearly, Plus costs $76.80 a year, which is 20% less. Nothing else changes: unlimited
notes, 5 devices, offline access and a year of version history.

To switch:

1. Open Settings and choose Billing.
2. Choose **Pay yearly**.

The yearly price is our standing price for annual billing, so there is no deadline. Switch
whenever it suits you, and you can go back to monthly billing at the end of any year.
