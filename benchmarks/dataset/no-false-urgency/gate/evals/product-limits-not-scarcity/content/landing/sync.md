# Your notes on every device

Sync is only in Plus. Free accounts are limited to 1 device and 100 notes; Plus accounts sync
across up to 5 devices, with no limit on notes.

- **Offline first.** Changes you make offline sync when you are back online.
- **Version history.** Free keeps the last 7 days of each note. Plus keeps 1 year.
- **Conflicts.** If two devices change one note before syncing, Quillnote keeps both versions
  and asks you which to keep.

Plus costs $8 a month, or $76.80 a year when you pay yearly.
