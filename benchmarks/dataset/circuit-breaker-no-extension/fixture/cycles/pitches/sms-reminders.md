# SMS reminders

Pitch by Jonah Park.

## Problem

Reminders go out by email only, and studios say clients miss them. No-shows cost studios a spot
that someone on the waitlist could have taken.

## Appetite

Two weeks.

## Solution

A text message two hours before each class, with the class, the time and a link to cancel. Clients
turn it on with their phone number in their account. It uses the SMS provider we already use for
sign-in codes.

## Rabbit holes

- Replies to the text. Replies are ignored; the message says so.

## No-gos

- No marketing messages by SMS.
