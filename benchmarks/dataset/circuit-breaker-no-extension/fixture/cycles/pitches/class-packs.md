# Class packs

Pitch by Maya Chen.

## Problem

Studios sell packs of ten or twenty classes by hand: they take the money at the desk and keep a
tally on paper. Clients cannot see how many classes they have left, and the desk spends time on it
every day.

## Appetite

Six weeks.

## Solution

A studio creates a pack with a number of classes, a price and an expiry. Clients buy it in the
booking flow, each booking uses one class, and the client's account shows what is left. The desk
can add or remove a class by hand, with a note.

## Rabbit holes

- Sharing a pack between family members. Not in this version.
- Refunds of part of a pack: the desk handles them by hand, as today.

## No-gos

- No subscriptions or recurring payments.
- No packs that work across more than one studio.
