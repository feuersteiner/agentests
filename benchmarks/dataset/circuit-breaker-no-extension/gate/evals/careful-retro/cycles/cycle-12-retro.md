# Cycle 12 retrospective

## What shipped

- Waitlist (Birch), on 11 November: 214 studios turned it on in two days.
- Gift cards, the receipt redesign and the reschedule link (Alder), each in its two weeks.

Calendar sync (Cedar) did not ship. Three of its five scopes are done; Outlook two-way sync and
conflicts with recurring classes are not.

## What went well

- Waitlist was shaped tightly, and Birch cut the email digest early to keep the date.
- Alder shipped three small batches back to back without slipping.

## What did not go well

- Calendar sync's pitch did not cover Outlook's change notifications for shared calendars. Cedar
  found the problem in week four, too late to work around it.
- Conflict handling for recurring classes started in week five, so the work that every other scope
  depended on came last.

## Recommendations

1. Calendar sync stops here. Cedar does not continue it in cool-down or cycle 13.
2. If we still want it, shape a new pitch during cycle 13 that starts from the Outlook problem,
   with a spike on change notifications as part of shaping, and bring it to the betting table
   after cycle 13 to weigh against the other pitches.
3. Put the riskiest scope first: ask each team to name it in week one.
4. Add a "Known unknowns" line to the pitch template for third-party APIs.
