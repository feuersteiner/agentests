# Calendar sync: what happens next

To the product team, 23 November 2026.

Calendar sync did not ship in cycle 12. Cedar got a lot done: Google and Outlook connections,
export of classes to both, and Google busy times blocking bookings. Two scopes are not done:
Outlook two-way sync, held up by Microsoft's change notifications dropping events from shared
calendars, and conflicts with recurring classes. Without the conflict handling the finished
scopes cannot ship on their own, so nothing has gone out.

I know it feels close. Cedar thinks the rest would take about two weeks. But the project had its
cycle, and it stops here. Cedar does not keep working on it in cool-down or in cycle 13.

What happens next:

- The `calendar-sync` branch and the `calendar_sync` flag stay as they are, so nothing is lost.
- During cycle 13, Maya and Priya will shape the remaining problem into a new pitch, using what
  Cedar learned about Outlook. That pitch goes to the betting table after cycle 13 with the other
  pitches, and the table will decide whether to bet on it.
- Cedar's bet for cycle 13 comes from the cycle 13 betting table, like every team's.

Thanks to Priya, Sam and Lena for the work so far.
