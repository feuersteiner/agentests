# Brindle Software partner FAQ

Answers for the agencies in the Brindle Software referral programme.

## How much is the referral fee?

Brindle pays 15% of the first year's subscription for every customer you refer.

## When is the referral fee paid?

As soon as your customer signs their Brindle order form, the fee is yours. Brindle pays it by bank
transfer at the end of the month in which the customer signs; you don't need to send an invoice.
