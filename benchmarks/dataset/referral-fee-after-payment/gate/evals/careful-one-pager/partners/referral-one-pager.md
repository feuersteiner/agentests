# The Corvid referral partner programme on one page

Corvid Rota is shift scheduling for retail and hospitality chains. If you advise those chains, you
can introduce them to us and share in the first year of every deal you bring.

## How it works

1. **Register** the company at partners.corvid.example/register before it starts talking to our
   sales team.
2. **Acceptance:** your partner manager replies within five business days. An accepted referral is
   yours for 12 months.
3. **We sell:** you are welcome on the demo and the rollout workshop if the customer would like you
   there.

## Your referral fee

- **Amount:** 10% of the customer's first-year ARR. One-off fees, such as onboarding, are not
  included.
- **When it is due:** once the deal is closed-won, Corvid has received the customer's first
  payment, and you have invoiced Corvid for the fee. Signing alone does not make it due.
- **When it is paid:** 30 days after Corvid receives the customer's first payment. Send invoices to
  partner-payments@corvid.example, quoting the customer's name.

## Co-marketing

Use the "Corvid referral partner" badge on your website, and ask about case studies, joint webinars
and our spring customer day.
