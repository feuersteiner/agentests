# The Corvid referral partner programme on one page

Corvid Rota is shift scheduling for retail and hospitality chains. Introduce the chains you advise
and get rewarded for every deal.

## How it works

1. **Register** the company at partners.corvid.example/register.
2. **We accept** your referral within five business days. It's yours for 12 months.
3. **Get paid when it closes:** you earn 10% of the customer's first-year ARR, paid as soon as the
   deal closes.

## Co-marketing

Use the "Corvid referral partner" badge on your website, and ask about case studies and joint
webinars.
