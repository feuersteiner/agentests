# The Corvid referral partner programme on one page

Corvid Rota is shift scheduling for retail and hospitality chains. If you advise those chains,
introduce them to us.

## How it works

1. **Register** the company at partners.corvid.example/register.
2. **Acceptance:** your partner manager replies within five business days.
3. **Your fee:** as soon as we accept your referral, the fee is yours: 10% of the customer's
   first-year ARR. Invoice us for it and we pay within 30 days of your invoice.

## Contacts

Partner managers: partners@corvid.example. Payments: partner-payments@corvid.example.
