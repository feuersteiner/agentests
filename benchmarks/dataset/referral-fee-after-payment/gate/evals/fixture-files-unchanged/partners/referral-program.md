# Corvid referral partner programme

The referral partner programme is for consultancies, payroll bureaus and other advisers who work
with retail and hospitality chains and introduce them to Corvid Rota. A partner signs our referral
partner agreement once; this document summarises how the programme works under it.

## Registering a referral

Register a referral with the form at partners.corvid.example/register before the customer has
started talking to our sales team. Give the company's name, the person you spoke to, roughly how
many sites they run and what they need. One registration covers one company.

## Acceptance

Your partner manager replies within five business days. We accept a referral when the company is
not already in our pipeline and has not been a customer in the last 12 months. An accepted
referral is yours for 12 months: if the company buys Corvid Rota in that time, the deal counts as
your referral.

## Referral fee

- **Amount:** 10% of the referred customer's first-year ARR, the annual recurring revenue in the
  first year of its subscription. One-off fees, such as onboarding, are not included.
- **When it is due:** no fee is due for an accepted referral until all three of these have
  happened: the deal is closed-won, Corvid has received the customer's first payment, and you have
  invoiced Corvid for the fee.
- **When it is paid:** Corvid pays the fee 30 days after it receives the customer's first payment.
- **Invoices:** send them to partner-payments@corvid.example, quoting the customer's name.
- A deal that is lost, or a customer that never pays, earns no fee.

## Co-marketing

Partners may use the "Corvid referral partner" badge on their website and in proposals. Ask your
partner manager for case studies, a joint webinar, or a seat at our spring customer day.
