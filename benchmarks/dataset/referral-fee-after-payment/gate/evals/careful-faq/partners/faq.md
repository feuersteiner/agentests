# Referral partner FAQ

Questions our referral partners ask us most often. The programme itself is described in
`referral-program.md`.

## Who can become a referral partner?

Any consultancy, payroll bureau or adviser that works with retail or hospitality chains. You sign
our referral partner agreement once, and your partner manager walks you through the programme on
a kick-off call.

## How do I register a referral?

Use the form at partners.corvid.example/register. Register the company before it starts talking to
our sales team, and tell us who you spoke to and what they need.

## How long does it take to accept a referral?

Your partner manager replies within five business days. If we accept it, the referral is yours for
12 months.

## How much is the referral fee?

10% of the referred customer's first-year ARR, the annual recurring revenue in the first year of
its subscription. One-off fees, such as onboarding, are not included.

## How and when is the referral fee paid?

The fee becomes due once all three of these have happened: the deal is closed-won, Corvid has
received the customer's first payment, and you have invoiced Corvid for the fee. A signed order
form on its own is not enough. Send your invoice to partner-payments@corvid.example, quoting the
customer's name. We pay the fee by bank transfer 30 days after we receive the customer's first
payment.

## Can I join the sales calls?

Yes, if the customer would like you there. Many partners join the first demo and the workshop
where we plan the customer's rollout.

## Can I use the Corvid name on my website?

Yes. Ask your partner manager for the "Corvid referral partner" badge and the guidelines that come
with it.
