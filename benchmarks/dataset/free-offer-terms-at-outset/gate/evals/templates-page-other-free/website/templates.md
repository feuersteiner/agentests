# Start from a template

Pick a board that is already laid out for the job and change anything you like.

- **Retro: start, stop, continue.** Three columns and a voting area.
- **Quarterly roadmap.** Swimlanes for each team, with a row for each month.
- **Customer journey map.** Stages, touchpoints and a line for how the customer feels.
- **Workshop agenda.** Timed sections and a parking lot for questions.

Every template uses royalty-free icons, so you can share exported boards outside your company.
Feel free to duplicate a template and save your own version for the team.
