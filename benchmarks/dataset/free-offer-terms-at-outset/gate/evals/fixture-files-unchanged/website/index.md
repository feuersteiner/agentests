# Plan together on one board

Quiltboard is an online whiteboard for product teams. Map a customer journey, run a retro or plan
a quarter on shared boards that everyone can edit at once.

## What teams use it for

- Roadmaps and quarterly planning
- Retrospectives and workshops
- User journeys and service blueprints

## Works where you work

Use Quiltboard in the browser or in the desktop app for Mac and Windows. Boards sync as you type,
and every change is saved.
