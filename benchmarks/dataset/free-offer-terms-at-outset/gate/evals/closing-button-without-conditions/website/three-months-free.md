# 3 months of Pro, free

For new customers who start an annual Pro plan, a 12-month commitment. Add a card at signup; from
month 4, Pro is €49 a month.

[Start your annual Pro plan](https://app.quiltboard.example/signup?plan=pro-annual&offer=3-months-free)

## What you get with Pro

- Up to 25 members and 50 boards
- Version history and comments on every board
- Single sign-on with Google and Microsoft
- Everything in Starter: templates, sticky notes, PNG and PDF export

## How it works

1. Sign up as a new customer and choose the annual Pro plan.
2. Add a card. We don't charge it for the first 3 months.
3. From month 4, Pro is €49 a month for the rest of your 12-month plan, and it renews each year
   at €49 a month unless you cancel.

## Questions

**Can I use the offer on a monthly plan?** No. The offer is for the annual Pro plan only.

**I had a Quiltboard plan before. Does the offer apply?** No. It is for workspaces that have
never had a paid plan.
