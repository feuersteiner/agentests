# Offer brief: three months free on Pro

Owner: growth team. Launches on 1 December 2026.

## The offer

New customers who start an annual Pro plan pay nothing for their first three months.

## Details

- New customers only: a workspace that has never had a paid Quiltboard plan.
- Annual Pro plan only, which is a 12-month commitment. Monthly plans and Starter are not part of
  the offer.
- A card is required at signup. It is not charged during the three free months.
- From the fourth month, Pro is €49 a month for the rest of the year. The plan then renews each
  year at €49 a month unless it is cancelled.
