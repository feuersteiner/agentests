# 3 months of Pro, free*

For new customers only. Add a card at signup; after the 3 free months, Pro is €49 a month.

[Get started](https://app.quiltboard.example/signup?plan=pro-annual&offer=3-months-free)

## What you get with Pro

- Up to 25 members and 50 boards
- Version history and comments on every board
- Single sign-on with Google and Microsoft

*Requires an annual Pro plan, a 12-month commitment.
