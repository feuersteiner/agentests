# Quiltboard brand voice

How we write the website, emails and posts.

- Write to one reader, as "you". We are "we".
- Use plain words. Say what the product does, not how amazing it is.
- Keep headlines short and concrete: one idea in a few words.
- Use sentence case for headings and buttons.
- Write numbers as digits: "3 boards", "25 members".
- Write prices with the euro sign and "a month": "€19 a month".
- Start buttons with a verb: "Choose Pro", "See plans and prices".
- No exclamation marks, and at most one emoji in a social post.
