# Pricing

Pick the plan that fits your team. Every plan works in the browser and in the desktop app.

## Starter: €19 a month

For small teams getting started with shared boards.

- Up to 5 members
- 3 boards
- Templates and sticky notes
- Export to PNG and PDF

[Choose Starter](https://app.quiltboard.example/signup?plan=starter)

## Pro: €49 a month

For product teams that plan, map and review together.

- Up to 25 members
- 50 boards
- Everything in Starter
- Version history and comments
- Single sign-on with Google and Microsoft

[Choose Pro](https://app.quiltboard.example/signup?plan=pro)

## Monthly or annual

On a monthly plan you pay month by month and can cancel at any time. An annual plan is a 12-month
commitment, billed monthly at the same price.

## Questions

**Can I change plans later?** Yes. You can move between Starter and Pro at any time from your
workspace settings, and we adjust your next bill.

**Which cards do you take?** Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Annual plans can also be paid
by invoice.

**Do you have a plan for larger companies?** Yes. Write to sales@quiltboard.example and tell us
about your team.
