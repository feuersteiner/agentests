# Staff picks on your front page

Put your booksellers' choices where readers see them first.

## Shelves you curate

Make a shelf for your best-selling titles, one for the latest arrivals and one for whatever your
most loyal readers keep asking about. Each shelf holds up to 12 books.

## A note on each pick

Add a short note to each pick, the way you would on a shelf talker. Notes can be up to 280
characters.

## On the Growth plan

Staff picks come with Growth, which is $49 a month. Shops with more than 2,000 titles need
Growth in any case, because Starter stops at 2,000.
