# Boards

Every project gets a board. Cards move through the columns you set, and each card holds its
owner, due date, checklist, files and comments.

## Load time

Boards load twice as fast as in Trackhub and Taskwell: a 500-card board took a median of
0.8 seconds in Orbitly, 1.6 in Trackhub and 1.7 in Taskwell (Northgate Labs, March 2026).
