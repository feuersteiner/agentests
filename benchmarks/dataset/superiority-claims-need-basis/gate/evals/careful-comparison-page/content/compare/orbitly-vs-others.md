# Orbitly, Trackhub and Taskwell

If you are choosing a project tool, you are probably looking at more than one. This page says
what we know from testing and what Orbitly includes, so you can line it up against the others
yourself.

## What has been tested: board load time

Northgate Labs, an independent testing firm, timed how long a 500-card board takes to load in
three tools in March 2026. Orbitly did not pay for the test or take part in it.

| Tool     | Median board load time |
| -------- | ---------------------- |
| Orbitly  | 0.8 seconds            |
| Trackhub | 1.6 seconds            |
| Taskwell | 1.7 seconds            |

Boards loaded twice as fast in Orbitly as in Trackhub and Taskwell. The test covered board load
time only, so we make no claim about any other screen.

## What Orbitly includes

- Boards, with a timeline view of the same cards on every plan.
- Automations: 250 runs a month on Team, no cap on Business and Enterprise.
- A workload view and time tracking on Business and Enterprise.
- Slack on every plan; GitHub and Google Drive from Team up.
- Free guest accounts for clients: 5 on Team, 50 on Business.

## What Orbitly costs

Free for up to 5 people. Team is $9 and Business is $16 per person per month, with 15% off when
you pay for a year. Enterprise is by quote.

## What to check for yourself

We have not tested Trackhub's or Taskwell's features or prices, and they change. Ask each
vendor what its plans include, what guests cost and where your data is stored, then try a board
of your own in each.
