# Pricing

Prices are per person per month, billed monthly. Pay for a year up front and take 15% off.
Whichever plan you pick, Orbitly costs less than Trackhub for the same team.

## Free

For up to 5 people and 3 boards. Boards, the timeline and the Slack integration.

## Team: $9 per person

For up to 25 people. Unlimited boards, 250 automation runs a month, and the Slack, GitHub and
Google Drive integrations.

## Business: $16 per person

For up to 200 people. Everything in Team, plus unlimited automation runs, the workload view and
time tracking.
