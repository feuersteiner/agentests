# Timeline view

Any board can be shown as a timeline. Each card with a start and a due date becomes a bar on a
calendar, grouped by column or by owner.

## Reschedule by dragging

Drag a bar to move its dates, or drag an edge to make it longer or shorter. The card's due date
changes with it, and its owner gets a notification.

## Dependencies

Link two cards when one has to finish before the other starts. Move the first and Orbitly asks
whether to move the second by the same number of days.

## Export

Export the timeline as a PNG image to put in a client report.

The timeline is on every plan, including Free.
