# Orbitly Enterprise

Project management for agencies and product teams, with the controls your security and
procurement teams ask for.

## What your teams get

- One board per project, with a timeline view of the same cards.
- Automations with no cap on runs.
- A workload view of each person's open cards per week, and time tracking with a weekly CSV export.
- Free guest accounts for clients, with no limit.

## What IT and security get

- SAML single sign-on with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace, and SCIM provisioning.
- An audit log of sign-ins, permission changes and exports, kept for 24 months.
- A choice of data region: EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia).
- A SOC 2 Type II report, available under NDA.
- A 99.9% monthly uptime commitment, with service credits when it is missed.

## Tested board load time

In Northgate Labs' independent test of March 2026, a 500-card board loaded in a median of
0.8 seconds in Orbitly, against 1.6 seconds in Trackhub and 1.7 seconds in Taskwell.

## Getting started

A named onboarding manager for the first 90 days, and support answered within 4 working hours.
No seat limit; invoicing by purchase order from 50 seats. Pricing is by quote.
