# Guest accounts

Invite a client to one board as a guest. Guests see that board only, can comment on cards and
upload files, and do not count as paid seats: 5 guests on Team, 50 on Business, no limit on
Enterprise.

Taskwell charges a full seat for every client you invite, and Trackhub has no guest access at
all.
