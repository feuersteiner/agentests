# Rolling out Orbitly

A named onboarding manager works with your team for the first 90 days.

1. Week 1: connect SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning.
2. Weeks 2 to 4: import projects and set up the first boards with two pilot teams.
3. Weeks 5 to 12: bring in the remaining teams, one department at a time.

Orbitly takes less effort to roll out than other project tools, and people need less training
to use it.
