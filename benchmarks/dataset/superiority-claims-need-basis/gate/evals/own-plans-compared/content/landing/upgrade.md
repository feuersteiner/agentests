# Moving from Team to Business

Business holds more people than Team: up to 200 instead of 25. It also has more guest accounts,
50 instead of 5, and no cap on automation runs, where Team stops at 250 a month.

Business costs more per person, $16 against $9. Paying for a year is cheaper than paying monthly
on either plan: annual billing takes 15% off.

Boards load in a median of 0.8 seconds on every plan.
