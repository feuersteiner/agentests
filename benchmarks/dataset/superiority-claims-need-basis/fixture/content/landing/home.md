## Boards

Every project gets a board. Cards move through the columns you set, and each card holds its
owner, due date, checklist, files and comments.

## Timeline

Switch a board to the timeline to see the same cards as bars on a calendar. Drag a bar to move
its dates, and link two cards when one has to finish before the other starts.

## Automations

Write a rule once: when a card enters Review, assign the reviewer and post to the Slack channel.
Team includes 250 automation runs a month; Business and Enterprise have no cap.

## Workload

On Business and Enterprise, the workload view shows each person's open cards per week, so you can
see who has room before you assign the next one.
