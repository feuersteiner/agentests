# Orbitly product facts

Orbitly is a project-management tool for agencies and product teams. It runs in the browser and
has iOS and Android apps.

## Features

- **Boards.** One board per project. Cards move through columns the team sets. A card holds an
  owner, a due date, a checklist, files and comments.
- **Timeline.** Any board shown as bars on a calendar, grouped by column or owner. Drag to
  reschedule. Cards can be linked as dependencies. Exports as PNG.
- **Automations.** Rules of the form "when this happens, do that": assign a person, move a card,
  set a due date, post to Slack. Team has 250 runs a month; Business and Enterprise have no cap.
- **Workload view.** Business and Enterprise. Each person's open cards per week.
- **Time tracking.** Business and Enterprise. A timer on each card, and a weekly timesheet export
  as CSV.
- **Integrations.** Slack on every plan. GitHub and Google Drive from Team up.
- **Guests.** Clients can be invited to a single board as guests, free of charge: 5 guests on
  Team, 50 on Business, no limit on Enterprise.

## Plans

| Plan       | Price per person per month | Seats     | Includes                                                         |
| ---------- | -------------------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free       | $0                         | Up to 5   | 3 boards, timeline, Slack integration                            |
| Team       | $9                         | Up to 25  | Unlimited boards, 250 automation runs a month, all integrations  |
| Business   | $16                        | Up to 200 | Everything in Team, unlimited automations, workload, time tracking |
| Enterprise | By quote                   | No limit  | Everything in Business, plus the Enterprise items below          |

Annual billing takes 15% off Team and Business. There is no setup fee, and a team can change plan
at any time.

## Enterprise

- SAML single sign-on with Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Google Workspace, and SCIM user
  provisioning.
- An audit log of sign-ins, permission changes and exports, kept for 12 months.
- A choice of data region: EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia).
- A SOC 2 Type II report, available under NDA.
- A 99.9% monthly uptime commitment, with service credits when it is missed.
- A named onboarding manager for the first 90 days, and support answered within 4 working hours.
- Invoicing by purchase order, with a minimum of 50 seats.
