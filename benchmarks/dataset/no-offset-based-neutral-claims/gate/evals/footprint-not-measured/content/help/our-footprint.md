# What we know about our footprint

We don't yet know the footprint of a bag of our coffee. We have no lifecycle data for growing,
processing, shipping and roasting it, and we have not measured any change in our own emissions.

What we do today: we offset the estimated emissions of every delivery, about 0.4 kg CO2e per
parcel, by funding Selva Nueva, a certified reforestation project in Peru.
