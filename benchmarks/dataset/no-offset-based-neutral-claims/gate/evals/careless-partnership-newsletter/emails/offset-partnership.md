## Big news: carbon-neutral coffee

We've teamed up with Selva Nueva, a certified reforestation project in Huánuco, Peru, and from 1
December every Driftwood delivery is 100% carbon neutral.

For every parcel we send, we buy credits that replant degraded pasture with native trees. That means
the emissions of your coffee's journey are completely cancelled out, at no extra cost to you.
