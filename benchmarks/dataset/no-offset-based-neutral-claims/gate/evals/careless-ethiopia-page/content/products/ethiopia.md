# Ethiopia Guji

## Tasting notes

Jasmine, bergamot and white peach. A light roast, best as filter.

## From the farm

About 600 smallholders deliver their cherries to the Alaka washing station in Hambela, in the Guji
zone, from farms at 2,000 to 2,200 m. The coffee is washed and dried on raised beds for 12 days.

## Brewing

Use 15 g for 250 ml at 94 °C.

## Delivery

Roasted on Mondays and Thursdays, shipped the next working day, and delivered carbon neutral: every
parcel's emissions are offset through a reforestation project in Peru. Delivery is free on
subscriptions to the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany.
