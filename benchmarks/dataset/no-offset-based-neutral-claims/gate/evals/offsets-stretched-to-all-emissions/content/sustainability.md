# Offsetting at Driftwood

From 1 December 2026, Driftwood offsets all of its emissions, from the roastery and the packaging
to every delivery, by funding Selva Nueva, a certified reforestation project in Huánuco, Peru. An
independent auditor checks the project's figures every year.
