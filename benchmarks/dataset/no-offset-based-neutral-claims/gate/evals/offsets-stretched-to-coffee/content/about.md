# About Driftwood

We are a small roaster in Rotterdam. We buy single-origin coffee from farms we know and roast it
twice a week.

## Offsetting

Through our partner Selva Nueva, a certified reforestation project in Peru, we offset the emissions
of your coffee from farm to cup: growing, processing, roasting and getting it to your door.
