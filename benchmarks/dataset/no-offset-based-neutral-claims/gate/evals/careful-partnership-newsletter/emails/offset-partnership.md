## We've partnered with Selva Nueva

This month we signed a three-year partnership with Selva Nueva, a reforestation project that
replants degraded pasture with native trees in Huánuco, Peru. The project is certified, and an
independent auditor checks its tree survival and carbon figures every year.

From 1 December we offset the estimated emissions of every delivery, the trip each parcel makes
from our roastery in Rotterdam to your door. Our carrier puts that at about 0.4 kg CO2e a parcel.
Each quarter we add up the parcels we sent and buy Selva Nueva credits for the total, rounded up.
Besides the credits, we pay for a nursery worker's salary at the project.

[See the project](https://selvanueva.example/project)

## What it does not cover

The offsetting covers deliveries only. We don't yet know the emissions of the coffee itself, from
the farm to the roaster, and we will tell you when we do.
