# Climate questions

**Is Driftwood carbon neutral?** No, and we don't claim to be. We offset the estimated emissions of
our deliveries by funding a reforestation project in Peru, but offsetting does not take away the
emissions of the coffee itself, and we have not measured those yet.

**Is my delivery climate neutral?** We don't describe it that way. What we can say is that we buy
credits for the estimated emissions of every parcel we send.
