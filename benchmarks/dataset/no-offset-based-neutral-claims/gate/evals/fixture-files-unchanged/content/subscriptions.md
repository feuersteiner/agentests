# How the subscription works

Pick a coffee, choose how many 250 g bags you want and how often, and we send them freshly roasted.

## How often

Every two weeks or every four weeks. You can change it from your account up to two days before
your next delivery.

## Roasting and delivery

We roast on Mondays and Thursdays and ship the next working day, so your coffee is never more than
a few days old when it arrives. Delivery is free on subscriptions to the Netherlands, Belgium and
Germany. Parcels fit through a standard letterbox.

## Pausing and cancelling

Skip a delivery, pause for up to three months or cancel from your account at any time. There is no
minimum term.
