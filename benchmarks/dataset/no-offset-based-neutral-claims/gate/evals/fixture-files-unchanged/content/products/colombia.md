# Colombia Huila

## Tasting notes

Red apple, panela and milk chocolate. A medium roast that works as filter and as espresso.

## From the farm

The Rojas family grows Caturra and Castillo at 1,750 m on finca El Mirador, near La Plata in Huila,
under the shade of guamo trees. The cool mountain climate slows ripening, which gives the cherries
time to build sweetness. The coffee is washed and dried on the farm.

## Brewing

For filter, use 15 g for 250 ml at 94 °C. For espresso, start with 18 g in and 36 g out.
