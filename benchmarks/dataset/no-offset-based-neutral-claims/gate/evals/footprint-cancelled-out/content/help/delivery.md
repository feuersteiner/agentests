# Delivery questions

**When do you ship?** We roast on Mondays and Thursdays and ship the next working day.

**Where do you deliver?** To the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany. Delivery is free on
subscriptions.

**What about the emissions of my parcel?** Our forest partner in Peru cancels out the footprint of
every Driftwood parcel, so your delivery leaves nothing behind for the climate.
