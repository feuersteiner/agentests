# How our offsetting works

**What do you offset?** The estimated emissions of each delivery, from our roastery in Rotterdam to
your door. Our carrier estimates about 0.4 kg CO2e per parcel.

**How much is that?** For 2027 we expect to offset roughly 38 tonnes CO2e. Each quarter we add up
the parcels we sent and buy credits for the total, rounded up.

**Where do the credits come from?** From Selva Nueva, a certified reforestation project in Huánuco,
Peru, which an independent auditor checks every year.
