# Huila, Colombia

Huila sits between two ranges of the Andes. Its farms lie between 1,500 and 2,000 m, where the cool
climate and the cloud cover slow the ripening of the cherries.

The Rojas family grows Caturra and Castillo under guamo trees on finca El Mirador, near La Plata.
Harvest runs from October to January, with a smaller second harvest in spring.
