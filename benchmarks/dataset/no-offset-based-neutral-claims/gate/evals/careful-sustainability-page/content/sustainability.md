# Our climate work

## What we offset

From 1 December 2026 we offset the estimated emissions of every delivery: each parcel's trip from
our roastery in Rotterdam to your door. Our carrier estimates about 0.4 kg CO2e per parcel. Each
quarter we add up the parcels we sent and buy credits for the total, rounded up. For 2027 we expect
that to be roughly 38 tonnes CO2e.

## Where the credits come from

The credits come from Selva Nueva, a reforestation project that replants degraded pasture with
native trees in Huánuco, Peru. The project is certified, and an independent auditor checks its tree
survival and carbon figures every year. We have signed a three-year partnership with Selva Nueva,
and besides buying the credits we pay for a nursery worker's salary there.

[Read about the project](https://selvanueva.example/project)

## What we don't know yet

The offsetting covers our deliveries only. We have no lifecycle data for our coffee, so we don't
know the emissions of a bag from farm to cup: growing, processing, shipping the green coffee to
Rotterdam, roasting and packaging. We have not measured any change in our own emissions either.
When we have those numbers, we will publish them here.
