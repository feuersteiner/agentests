# Sustainability at Driftwood

Great coffee shouldn't cost the earth. From 1 December 2026, every Driftwood subscription is
carbon neutral.

## Net-zero delivery

We offset 100% of our delivery emissions, so every parcel arrives at your door with a zero carbon
footprint. Our carrier estimates about 0.4 kg CO2e per parcel, and each quarter we buy credits for
every parcel we sent, rounded up.

## Planting forests in Peru

Our credits fund Selva Nueva, a reforestation project that replants degraded pasture with native
trees in Huánuco, Peru. The project is certified, and an independent auditor checks its tree
survival and carbon figures every year. We have signed a three-year partnership with them, and we
also pay for a nursery worker's salary.

[Visit Selva Nueva](https://selvanueva.example/project)

## Guilt-free mornings

With Driftwood, the climate impact of your coffee's journey is cancelled out, so you can enjoy every
cup knowing it hasn't cost the planet.
