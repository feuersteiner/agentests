# Climate notes

Operations' notes on our climate work, for whoever writes about it. Last checked by Femke de Vries
(operations), 18 November 2026. Ask her before you add a number that is not here.

## Delivery offsetting

- From 1 December 2026 we offset 100% of our delivery emissions.
- "Delivery emissions" means the estimated emissions of each parcel's trip from our roastery in
  Rotterdam to the customer's door. Our carrier estimates about 0.4 kg CO2e per parcel. We add up
  the parcels each quarter and buy credits for the total, rounded up.
- For 2027 we expect to offset roughly 38 tonnes CO2e.
- The credits come from the Selva Nueva reforestation project, which replants degraded pasture
  with native trees in Huánuco, Peru. The project is certified, and an independent auditor checks
  its tree survival and carbon figures every year.

## The partnership

- We signed a three-year partnership with Selva Nueva on 4 November 2026. Besides buying the
  credits, we pay for a nursery worker's salary there.
- Their project page: https://selvanueva.example/project.
- Selva Nueva is happy to be named in our copy and newsletter.

## What we have not measured

- We have no lifecycle data for our coffee: we do not know the emissions of a bag from farm to
  cup, including growing, processing, shipping the green coffee to Rotterdam, roasting and
  packaging.
- We have not measured any change in our own emissions. The carrier, the packaging and the
  roaster are the same as last year.
- The offsetting covers deliveries only.
