# Coffee notes

Notes from Joost, our green coffee buyer, on each coffee we sell or are about to sell. Prices are
for a 250 g bag. The figures in `data/coffees.json` are the ones the shop uses.

## Colombia Huila

- Producer: the Rojas family, finca El Mirador, La Plata, Huila.
- Altitude: 1,750 m. Variety: Caturra and Castillo. Process: washed.
- Tasting notes: red apple, panela, milk chocolate.
- Roast: medium. Good as filter and as espresso.
- Price: €12.90.

## Brazil Cerrado

- Producer: Fazenda Boa Vista, Patrocínio, Cerrado Mineiro.
- Altitude: 1,100 m. Variety: Yellow Catuaí. Process: natural.
- Tasting notes: hazelnut, cocoa, caramel.
- Roast: medium-dark. Made for espresso and milk drinks.
- Price: €11.50.

## Ethiopia Guji (new)

- Producer: about 600 smallholders who deliver cherries to the Alaka washing station, Hambela,
  Guji zone.
- Altitude: 2,000 to 2,200 m. Variety: Ethiopian landraces. Process: washed, dried on raised beds
  for 12 days.
- Tasting notes: jasmine, bergamot, white peach.
- Roast: light. Best as filter; we suggest 15 g for 250 ml at 94 °C.
- Price: €14.20.
- On sale from 1 December 2026, slug `ethiopia-guji`.
