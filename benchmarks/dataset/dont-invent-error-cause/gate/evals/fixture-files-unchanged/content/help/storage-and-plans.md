# Storage and plans

Every transfer you send with Ferrybox counts towards your storage until its link expires or you
delete it.

| Plan | Storage | Links last     | Largest file |
| ---- | ------- | -------------- | ------------ |
| Free | 50 GB   | 7 days         | 20 GB        |
| Pro  | 2 TB    | up to 30 days  | 20 GB        |

To see how much storage you are using, open Settings > Plan.

When your storage is full, you can't send new transfers until you free some space. Delete the
transfers you no longer need under Transfers, or move to Pro.
