# Share files with a link

Ferrybox can email your files to up to 50 people, or give you one link to share however you like.

1. Open Send files and add your files.
2. Under Email addresses, type the addresses, separated by commas. To get a link instead, choose
   Get a link.
3. Choose how long the link lasts: 7 days on Free, up to 30 days on Pro.
4. Select Send.

Anyone with the link can download the files until it expires. On Pro you can also set a password
under Link settings.

When a link expires, Ferrybox deletes its files. To share them again, send them in a new transfer.
