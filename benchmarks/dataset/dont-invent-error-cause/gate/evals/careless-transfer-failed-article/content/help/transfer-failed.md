# Why did my transfer fail?

When Ferrybox can't send a transfer, it shows a message with an error code. Find the code below to
see what happened and what to do.

## Your storage is full (E_QUOTA_FULL)

Every transfer counts towards your storage until its link expires or you delete it: 50 GB on Free,
2 TB on Pro. Delete the transfers you no longer need under Transfers, or move to Pro.

## A file is over 20 GB (E_FILE_TOO_LARGE)

Each file can be up to 20 GB, on every plan. Split a larger file into parts of 20 GB or less, for
example with a split archive, and send the parts together.

## A file type is blocked (E_FILE_TYPE_BLOCKED)

Ferrybox never sends .exe, .bat, .cmd, .scr or .msi files, even inside a .zip file. Remove the
file and send the rest.

## The link has expired (E_LINK_EXPIRED)

Links last 7 days on Free and up to 30 days on Pro. When a link expires, its files are deleted, so
ask the sender to send them again.

## Too many recipients (E_TOO_MANY_RECIPIENTS)

A transfer can go to up to 50 email addresses. Send it as several transfers, or choose Get a link
and share the link yourself.

## Other common reasons

If you saw E_UPLOAD_500 or E_UNKNOWN, one of these is usually the cause:

- **An unstable internet connection.** Large uploads fail when your Wi-Fi drops, even for a
  moment. Use a wired connection if you can.
- **Your computer went to sleep.** Keep it awake until the upload finishes.
- **A VPN, firewall or browser extension.** Turn them off and try again, or try another browser.
- **Busy servers.** Now and then our servers are under heavy load. Wait a few minutes and try
  again.

If none of these helps, contact support at support@ferrybox.example.
