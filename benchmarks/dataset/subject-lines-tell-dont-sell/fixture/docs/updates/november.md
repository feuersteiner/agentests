# Product updates: November 2026

What shipped this month, from the product team. Check anything not listed here with the product
team before it goes in an email.

## Scheduled sends

- Shipped on 4 November, on every plan.
- When a campaign is ready, you can pick a date and time to send it instead of sending it at once.
- Optionally, each contact gets the email at that time in their own time zone. Contacts without a
  known time zone get it at the account's time zone.
- A scheduled campaign can be edited or cancelled until 10 minutes before it sends.

## New template editor

- Shipped on 18 November, on every plan. The old editor stays available until 31 January 2027.
- Emails are built from blocks: text, image, button, product, divider and footer.
- Any block can be saved and reused in other emails.
- A phone view shows how the email will look on a phone while you edit it.

## Smaller changes

- Campaign reports can be exported as a CSV file, with the opens, clicks and unsubscribes of each
  contact.
- Fixed: the unsubscribe link in test sends opened a "page not found" error. Test sends now use a
  working link.
