# New plans from 1 January 2027

On 1 January 2027 Wrenmail replaces its two plans, Basic and Pro, with three plans: Starter, Growth
and Scale. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax.

| Plan    | Price a month | Contacts | Emails a month | Support                 |
| ------- | ------------- | -------- | -------------- | ----------------------- |
| Starter | $15           | 1,000    | 10,000         | Email                   |
| Growth  | $39           | 3,000    | 50,000         | Email and chat          |
| Scale   | $99           | 25,000   | 250,000        | Email, chat and phone   |

Starter covers up to 1,000 contacts, Growth up to 5,000 and Scale up to 25,000. Every plan includes
scheduled sends, the template editor and abandoned-cart emails. Scale adds phone support and a
dedicated sending IP address.

## Current customers

- Basic customers move to Starter on 1 January and keep their current price, $12 a month, until
  their first renewal after 1 April 2027.
- Pro customers move to Growth on 1 January and keep their current price, $29 a month, until their
  first renewal after 1 April 2027.
- The move is automatic. A customer who wants a different plan can change it at any time under
  Settings > Billing.
