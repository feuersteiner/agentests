Hello {{ first_name }},

Thanks for creating a Wrenmail account. Setting it up takes three steps.

1. **Connect your shop.** Go to Settings > Shop and choose Shopify or WooCommerce. Wrenmail imports
   your products and keeps them up to date.
2. **Import your contacts.** Go to Contacts > Import and upload a CSV file, or bring in the
   customers from your shop. Only contacts who agreed to marketing emails are added to your list.
3. **Send your first email.** Go to Campaigns > New, pick a template and add your products.

If you get stuck, reply to this email or visit [help.wrenmail.example](https://help.wrenmail.example).
