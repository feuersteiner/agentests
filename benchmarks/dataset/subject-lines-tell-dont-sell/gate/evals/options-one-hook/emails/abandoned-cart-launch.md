From 2 December, Wrenmail can email shoppers who leave items in their cart without ordering.

## How it works

- Turn it on under Automations > Abandoned cart. It works for Shopify and WooCommerce shops
  connected to Wrenmail.
- One hour after a shopper leaves your checkout with items in the cart, Wrenmail sends them a
  reminder that shows those items and links back to the cart.
- You can add a second reminder after 24 hours and choose whether it includes a discount code.
- The reminders stop as soon as the shopper places an order.
- Reminders go only to shoppers who gave their email address at checkout and agreed to marketing
  emails.

Abandoned-cart emails are included in every plan, and the reminders count towards your monthly
email allowance like any other email.

[Set up abandoned-cart emails](https://app.wrenmail.example/automations/abandoned-cart)
