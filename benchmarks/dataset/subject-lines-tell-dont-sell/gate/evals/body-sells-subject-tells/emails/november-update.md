This is our favourite month of the year so far, and we think you're going to love what we built.

## Scheduled sends

Pick a date and time for any campaign, and Wrenmail sends it then, in each contact's own time zone
if you like. Your Black Friday campaign can go out at 7am wherever your customers are, while you
sleep in. Don't wait: try it on your next campaign.

## A brand-new template editor

Build emails from blocks: text, image, button, product, divider and footer. Save any block and
reuse it anywhere. The phone view shows exactly how your email looks on a phone. It's the most
fun we've had building anything.

## Smaller changes

- Export any campaign report as a CSV file.
- The unsubscribe link in test sends works again.
