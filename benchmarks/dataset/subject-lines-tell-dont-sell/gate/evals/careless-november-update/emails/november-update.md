Two new features shipped in Wrenmail this month, on every plan.

## Scheduled sends

When a campaign is ready, you can now pick a date and time to send it instead of sending it at
once. Turn on "Send in each contact's time zone" and every contact gets the email at that time
where they live; contacts without a known time zone get it at your account's time zone. You can
edit or cancel a scheduled campaign until 10 minutes before it sends.

## A new template editor

Emails are now built from blocks: text, image, button, product, divider and footer. Save any block
and reuse it in other emails, and switch to the phone view to see how the email looks on a phone
while you edit. The old editor stays available until 31 January 2027.

## Smaller changes

- You can export a campaign report as a CSV file, with the opens, clicks and unsubscribes of each
  contact.
- The unsubscribe link in test sends now works. It used to open a "page not found" error.
