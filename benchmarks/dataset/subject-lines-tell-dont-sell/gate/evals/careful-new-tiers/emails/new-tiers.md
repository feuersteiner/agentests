On 1 January 2027 Wrenmail replaces its two plans, Basic and Pro, with three plans: Starter, Growth
and Scale. Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax.

| Plan    | Price a month | Contacts | Emails a month | Support               |
| ------- | ------------- | -------- | -------------- | --------------------- |
| Starter | $15           | 1,000    | 10,000         | Email                 |
| Growth  | $39           | 5,000    | 50,000         | Email and chat        |
| Scale   | $99           | 25,000   | 250,000        | Email, chat and phone |

Every plan includes scheduled sends, the template editor and abandoned-cart emails. Scale adds
phone support and a dedicated sending IP address.

## What happens to your plan

- If you are on Basic, you move to Starter on 1 January and keep paying $12 a month until your
  first renewal after 1 April 2027.
- If you are on Pro, you move to Growth on 1 January and keep paying $29 a month until your first
  renewal after 1 April 2027.

You do not need to do anything. If you would like a different plan, you can change it at any time
under Settings > Billing.
