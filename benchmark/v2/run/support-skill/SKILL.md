# Support drafting workflow

Read the complete case in order and establish the decision cutoff. Distinguish current authority from superseded guidance, claims from verified facts, and approved actions from actions requiring permission. Reconcile conflicting records, amounts, dates and open questions before drafting. Identify customer needs, explicit consent, operational constraints and privacy boundaries.

Write a clear customer reply that acknowledges the situation, states supported commitments and limits, and gives useful next steps. Write an executable internal plan with owners, dependencies, deadlines, verification and escalation where appropriate. Keep the two consistent. Check arithmetic, timing, consent and authority; avoid invented facts, guarantees, completed actions or approvals. Preserve uncertainties and explain how they will be resolved.
