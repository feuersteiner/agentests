# Six-arm producer study

The unit is a newly generated support reply and internal plan for one of ten complex support situations. Each situation is run three times under each of six conditions: the common prompt; prompt plus support workflow; prompt plus agentest with Luna; prompt plus agentest with Jev; and each of those two gates combined with the workflow. This gives 180 isolated producer trials. The 50 existing candidate pairs are grader controls, not new producer cases or labels. All producers use gpt-6.1-terra.

Every producer sees the same complete 20 ordered records and normative criteria at the case cutoff, and the same neutral production instruction. Reviewer task instructions, existing candidates, source and summary files are excluded. The skill is an agent-authored general workflow supplied explicitly as context in the three relevant arms; no physical skill tool invocation is claimed. Arm identities are control metadata, not producer or judge instructions.

The four gated conditions automatically submit drafts to the shipped core binary pass-probability gate at 0.8. A rejected draft may be revised using only finding ID, criterion and score feedback, without provider identity or gold labels. Each trial has at most three submissions. Ungated trials have one draft. Thus the maximum is 420 drafts and 360 gate calls. This introduces a compute and attempt-count confound: record attempts, usage, latency and costs, and interpret differences accordingly.

Judge the last draft of every trial, including rejected last submissions, using the existing full graded adapter and rubric response schema with gpt-6.1-terra. The judge receives exactly one candidate and the complete case context, without arm, transcript, gate outcome, repeat, producer identity or workflow. Submission acceptance is a separate operational metric, never a quality grade. The intended final assessment count is 180; report errors and missing judgments explicitly. These are provisional model-judge diagnostics on ten unheldout situations, not human labels or generalizable estimates.

Before any paid calls, freeze hashes of case inputs, prompts, workflow, arm descriptors, runtime and judge contract; preserve the full snapshot privately in ignored results. Use a recorded seed to interleave the paired conditions and repetitions, and isolate conversations per trial. Preserve failures and retries, and report actual denominators and unavailable costs rather than imputing zero. Credentials stay in the harness process and outside prompts and evidence.
