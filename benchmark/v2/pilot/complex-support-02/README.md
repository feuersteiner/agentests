# Security incident during enterprise renewal

This source-grounded fictional situation concerns Northline Access and Alder
Freight at **2025-05-14 12:00 UTC**. A support-system compromise intersects with
an unsigned enterprise renewal, an official premature assurance, partial log
coverage, known attachment retrieval, sales security overclaims, customer
protection and confidential evidence. Production availability and complete
exposure assessment are different questions. All businesses, people, dialogue,
contracts, authority and candidate actions are synthetic.

The primary public inspiration is the 2023 Okta support incident, including
[Okta's root-cause account](https://sec.okta.com/articles/2023/11/unauthorized-access-oktas-support-case-management-system-root-cause/),
[its later scope update](https://sec.okta.com/articles/october-security-incident-recommended-actions/),
[BeyondTrust's affected-company account](https://www.beyondtrust.com/blog/entry/okta-support-unit-breach)
and [Cloudflare's affected-company account](https://blog.cloudflare.com/how-cloudflare-mitigated-yet-another-okta-compromise/).
Company findings and customer accounts remain attributed; none becomes Northline
policy or proves another customer's impact. Current retrieval date, source dates,
redirects, claim limits and reuse basis are in [sources.json](sources.json).
Only brief original factual paraphrases appear in E01, with zero quoted words;
local hashes cover those paragraphs, **not original webpages**. No transcript
redistribution permission is asserted. No raw records or real identifiers appear.

[manifest.json](manifest.json) freezes the complete ordered context, five neutral
completed reply/plan alternatives, task, normative rubric, output schema and
separate provenance/reviewer records. There is **one situation and five
alternatives**, not five independent customer cases. The six stable dimensions
use cited ordinal 0–3 quality categories and separate confidence; no average can
hide a supported critical breach. The response schema requires evidence IDs and
exact reply/plan span IDs for every dimension and integrated judgment.

[summary.json](summary.json) is a provenance/navigation aid.
[Reviewer expectations](reviewer/expectations.json) contain provisional ranges,
rationales, disagreements and alternative routes. Both stay outside packets.
Candidate expectations are **synthetic, provisional and not human reviewed**;
agent content review is not human review or empirical evaluator validation.
Only the task, normative rubric/schema, complete ordered context and one
candidate reply/plan are assessment inputs. Other candidates, expected ranges,
source-selection metadata and author diagnoses are excluded. Input-only
preparation does not open reviewer files; full validation separately checks
their integrity and references.

Offline validation and deterministic packet preparation use the benchmark-only
workbench. See the [suite methods](../../README.md) for current single-case and
suite commands. Preparation writes only ignored results, never fixture inputs.
No model call, measured evaluation or structured ordinal execution adapter is
implemented or authorized by this fixture. Core binary findings and passing
probabilities cannot be relabeled as these ordinal grades. Context measurements
are exact UTF-8 bytes and whitespace-delimited words in the manifest; packet
measurements are recorded by offline preparation. Token counts remain null
because no named tokenizer and encoding were used.
