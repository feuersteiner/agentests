# Accessibility complaint before launch

LanternPath and Rivermere Skills Trust are fictional. This single situation
combines an unusable evidence-and-declaration workflow, uncertain repair timing,
unsafe assistance proposals, misleading accessibility claims, finite support
capacity, saved drafts, a partner-population dispute and a public opening deadline.
The decision cutoff is **2026-11-23 15:00 UTC**. Twenty ordered records provide
the full context; five completed reply/plan alternatives remain one business
situation. All dialogue, contracts, people, authority and operations are synthetic.

Primary technical grounding uses [WCAG 2.2 complete-process scope](https://www.w3.org/TR/2024/REC-WCAG22-20241212/#conformance-reqs),
[WAI Easy Checks](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/preliminary/),
[interim repairs](https://www.w3.org/WAI/planning/interim-repairs/),
[accessibility statements](https://www.w3.org/WAI/planning/statements/) and
[user evaluation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/involving-users/).
Verified retrieval date: November 23, 2026. Source-specific publication/update
limits and attribution are in [sources.json](sources.json). E01 contains short
original factual paraphrases, whose hashes identify local paragraphs rather than
original webpages. No copied transcripts, screenshots, real applicants or
certification are supplied. The public guidance does not establish applicable
law, fictional contractual authority or conformance of a product.

[manifest.json](manifest.json) freezes ordered records and contract bytes.
[task.json](task.json), [rubric.json](rubric.json) and the
[response schema](response.schema.json) require six independent ordinal 0–3
judgments, exact evidence and candidate-span citations, separate confidence and
an integrated ready/revise/hold-for-review disposition. Critical breaches cannot
be averaged away. Readiness concerns the reply and plan, not a completed repair.
The context includes current and superseded procedures, incomplete tests, signed
conditional authority and newly disclosed information that the signer had not
reviewed. No route is established by a dashboard color alone.

[Reviewer expectations](reviewer/expectations.json) remain **synthetic,
provisional and not human reviewed**. They contain distinct operational routes,
case-specific alternatives, ranges and genuine interpretation disagreements.
Those files and [summary.json](summary.json) stay outside assessment packets.
Every packet contains the same complete ordered context and exactly one pair;
other candidates, provenance selection notes and reviewer material are excluded.
Public content is method and synthetic fixture material, not measured evidence.

From the repository root, with shared dependencies installed:

```sh
bun --no-env-file --no-install benchmark/v2/workbench/dossier-cli.ts validate --case complex-support-06
bun --no-env-file --no-install benchmark/v2/workbench/dossier-cli.ts prepare --case complex-support-06
```

Validation writes nothing. Preparation remains offline, does not open reviewer
files and writes only to a fresh ignored directory under
`benchmark/v2/results/prepared/`. Run full validation separately for provenance
and reviewer integrity. No model calls, credential loading, scores, paid runs or
inference adapter are part of this fixture. Graded evaluation is not implemented;
a structured execution adapter and human review remain separately scoped work.
Counts measure actual UTF-8 document bytes and nonempty whitespace-delimited
words, with no token estimate. Complete packet sizes include JSON overhead and
contracts; shared preparation records them in its inventory. See
[private-evidence rules](../../../evidence/EVIDENCE.md) for future run artifacts.
