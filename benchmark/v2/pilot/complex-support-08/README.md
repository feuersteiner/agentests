# Account suspension appeal: continuity under confidential enforcement

RelayQuay and Lattice Field Services are fictional. This source-grounded dossier
contains one situation, twenty ordered records and five complete alternative
customer reply/internal plan pairs. The decision cutoff is **November 12, 2026,
13:00 UTC**. Suspicious credential use and a package-distribution allegation
intersect with legitimate Dispatch work, a defective notice, current and retired
procedures, conditional access authority, conflicting technical receipts,
confidential detection methods and sales pressure.

All business dialogue, policies, contracts, names, permissions and candidates are
synthetic. Public factual inspiration appears only through brief attributed
original paraphrases in E01. Sources were actually retrieved November 23, 2026;
current public policy is not backdated into fictional authority. The first-party
sources are [GitHub appeal and reinstatement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-appeal-and-reinstatement),
[GitHub active malware or exploits](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-active-malware-or-exploits),
[GitHub preventing unauthorized access](https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/keeping-your-account-and-data-secure/preventing-unauthorized-access),
[Discord Warning System](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/18210965981847-Discord-Warning-System)
and [Discord appeal guidance](https://discord.com/safety/360043712172-how-you-can-appeal-our-actions).
No transcript redistribution license is asserted; no raw pages, screenshots,
credentials or real customer identifiers are included.

[Sources](sources.json) records direct and retrieval URLs, stable identifiers,
dates, update uncertainty and local paraphrase hashes. A local SHA-256 is **not an
original webpage hash**. Undated maintained pages explicitly declare
`publication-date-unknown`. [Manifest](manifest.json) freezes ordered context,
candidates and contracts. [Task](task.json), [rubric](rubric.json) and
[response schema](response.schema.json) define six cited 0–3 ordinal dimensions,
separate confidence and integrated ready/revise/hold-for-review dispositions.
Critical breaches block readiness without erasing other dimensional strengths.
A draft may be ready while account and appeal work remain pending.

[Reviewer expectations](reviewer/expectations.json) remain **synthetic,
provisional and not human reviewed**. They contain case-specific alternatives,
cited dimensional ranges and disagreements, excluded from assessment packets.
[Summary](summary.json) is also reviewer/provenance navigation, not assessment
input. Each packet contains the same complete ordered context and one completed
reply/plan pair, never other candidates or expected judgments. This fixture is
not a real customer dataset or empirical evaluator validation.

From the repository root with existing locked dependencies:

```sh
bun --no-env-file --no-install benchmark/v2/workbench/dossier-cli.ts validate --case complex-support-08
bun --no-env-file --no-install benchmark/v2/workbench/dossier-cli.ts prepare --case complex-support-08 --out benchmark/v2/results/prepared/complex-support-08-review
```

Validation is offline and writes no artifact; it checks input, provenance,
reviewer hashes and references. Preparation never opens reviewer files and must
be paired with separate full validation for complete integrity. Its five packets
and size/hash inventory go only to a fresh ignored directory under
`benchmark/v2/results/prepared/`. Existing destinations are refused. The suite
inventory records complete serialized packet bytes separately from context and
reply/plan text. Token counts remain null because no tokenizer is configured.
