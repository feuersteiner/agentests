# Failed enterprise implementation: the launch-readiness gap

This source-grounded fictional dossier concerns a promised enterprise launch,
unreliable order and warehouse integrations, disputed responsibilities and a
customer's contractual retailer deadline. RelayGrid, Vela Instruments, Northstar
Connect, Atlas Parts and every business record and candidate are synthetic.
The decision cutoff is **2025-06-12 14:00 UTC**. It contains one situation with
five completed reply/internal-plan alternatives, not five independent cases.

The evidence separates transport delivery from business commit and acceptance,
component responsibility from joined coordination, customer dependency delay
from unsupported blame, and approval from actual execution. Current and stale
launch procedures, a forecast incentive, customer signatory limits, bounded
operational exceptions, access controls and real staffing limits interact with
the June 16 customer deadline. The final implementation milestone has not been
issued or collected. The board update and retailer notice cannot wait for a
complete fault allocation.

Living technical documentation is current retrieval context, not proof of

[manifest.json](manifest.json) freezes ordered documents, candidates, normative
contracts and separate reviewer/provenance material. The six stable dimensions

from grade and cited evidence/span references required by
[response.schema.json](response.schema.json). Critical breaches block readiness
without hiding the other dimensional strengths. A draft can be ready while the
implementation remains unresolved.

and not human reviewed**. They contain two distinct defensible routes, concrete
